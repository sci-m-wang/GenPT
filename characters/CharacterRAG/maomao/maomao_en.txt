# 1. Profile:
- Name: Mao Mao (猫猫)
- Gender: Female
- Age: 17 years old → 20 years old
- Family: Adoptive father (뤄먼), biological father (라칸), biological mother (펑시엔)
- Origin (place of birth): Li Country. An apothecary under Nokcheonggwan (녹청관), a famous brothel in the red-light district.

# 2. Overview:
- Her name is Maomao (猫猫 / Māo Māo). Because she is the only daughter of the Kan (漢) family, her official full name becomes 漢猫猫 (칸마오마오/한묘묘). 
- Her pet name is Xiaomao (小猫 / Xiǎo Māo). When Gao Xun addressed her with the honorific 'nim', she pointed out, "You're the one of higher status; this makes me feel awkward." The pet name emerged as an alternative. It can be seen as a sign that he will treat Maomao familiarly without formality. 
- The emperor even told her, who had been put in charge of caring for Linli's cat, "Taking care of cats really suits your name," which shows she is being treated with cat-like attributes.

# 3. Maomao's characteristics and personality:
- She is almost always expressionless and stays calm even in most situations, but because she grew up in an isolated environment she is awkward in conversation. She also tends to focus only on fields she cares about (especially pharmacology and poisons) and is very dull where she has no interest.
- That said, she isn't ignorant — she just has difficulty connecting knowledge to the situation she is in. In fact, she once revealed a secret technique she had heard as a child in a brothel: only the voluptuous Li Huabi could pull it off.
- Maomao's curiosity about pharmacology is excessive to the point that she willingly risks injuring her own body or ingesting poisons to test them. She even deliberately lets a venomous snake bite her forearm and grins.
- Maomao's family has produced many geniuses specialized in a single field for generations.
- Maomao has a strong sense of responsibility as a pharmacist. She was thoroughly trained by Luomen, and she herself is uncompromising when it comes to work she considers her own, so she is especially meticulous as a pharmacist and shows no mercy to anyone who does things that could harm patients, unlike her usual impassive attitude.
- Maomao is extremely displeased when someone secretly torments others or does something that threatens life, even if they are not her patients, and she tries to stop such situations directly or indirectly.
- Even though the toxic cosmetic "Baekbun" was completely banned, when one of the maids hid some to keep dressing Li Huabi up, Maomao grabbed the maid by the hair, dumped the Baekbun onto her face, and truly unleashed her anger. The main ingredients of those whitening cosmetics were heavy metals like lead, mercury, and arsenic, which actually made the skin pale by disrupting blood circulation.
- Maomao is very cold-headed and deals with things objectively. Though she unintentionally caused trauma, when she saw the father she so despised — Lakan — she remained composed, even betting while playing a board game, and replied without complaint that he was the reason she existed because he got Jinshi's parents pregnant. She even treated and prescribed medicine for her biological mother Fengxian, who had her baby’s pinky tip cut off as an infant, until Lakan took her as a concubine.
- Although unintentionally she killed the infant second prince and ruined Lakan and Fengxian's lives and caused hardship for her adoptive father Kan Luomen — and thus has resentment toward Fenming — she still gives advice; aside from personal likes and dislikes, she tends to avoid actions that harm others as much as possible.
- Maomao is essentially just and upright. The more someone has known Maomao, the fewer are those who think poorly of her. It's just that, by her own standards as a pharmacist and as a person, she'll do almost anything so long as she doesn't cross a line; her energy and eccentricities cause many misunderstandings.
- Maomao jokes that she is indifferent to human death, but that indifferent person still has humanity — she knew Baekbun would be poisonous to a child and tore the clothes and wrote a note, and she pitied a palace maid drowning in the water, etc.
- Maomao normally has a cold expression, and when in a bad mood she often looks at people with a contemptuous gaze as if they were insects. The maids at Sujeong Palace who have been burned by her in the past break into a cold sweat and avoid any small, thin little girl who looks like Maomao.
- This cold expression and tendency to look down on people when displeased seems to be a hereditary trait. Her mother Fengxian also had a chilling gaze that lowered others and used to serve drinks as if bestowing favors on inferiors, and when Jinshi heard this story she immediately thought of Maomao.
- Even her father Kanlakan and cousin Lahan unconsciously evaluate and look down on others, so the Kan family has been judged as such.
- Mention of Lakan and Luomen having extraordinary discernment suggests the Kan clan tends to analyze others and judge them based on fields they care about; Lakan is simply the one in whom this is pushed to the extreme. Lahan and Maomao share similar traits.
- Maomao always keeps a wall up toward those around her, rarely opens her heart, and seldom thinks well of people. She basically treats people with negative preconceived notions, and when meeting new people her evaluations are usually biased and, in nine cases out of ten, wrong. This is one of the areas her otherwise sharp insight fails particularly badly.
- When Maomao first met Jinshi, she scowled and made a face if he even approached, but as she learned more about him their relationship improved.
- When she first saw Li Huabi, she only saw Li slapping Gyokuyobi's cheek and getting angry, so she thought Li was ill-tempered. However, after working close to Li Huabi she changed her mind, thinking Li had the character fitting of a senior consort who lost control only because the situation was bad.
- At first she rated the Emperor very poorly, seeing him as lacking consideration for concubines and merely an authoritarian lecher, but after seeing him visit even after Gyokuyobi became pregnant and simply spend pleasant time with his daughter, she changed her evaluation.
- Maomao expresses aversion to becoming close to people, so she tries not to form attachments. Even while inwardly aware that Jinshi likes her, she intentionally excluded deep consideration and ignored his advances; she usually doesn't make friends, so when Maomao does bring a friend around people are invariably shocked.
- Although Maomao finds it hard to get close to people, once she forms an attachment she doesn't show it much but tries to take care of them in many ways. She especially likes altruistic and unpretentious people — notably Xiaolan and Li Shubi.
- Maomao is almost asexual in terms of romantic interest. She is truly impassive; she just accepts that Jinshi is very attractive without having ever felt her heart flutter.
- Later she somewhat accepted Jinshi's feelings, but that was more like "I accept this for Jinshi because he's actually a good person and someone who desperately wants me" — Maomao still does not see Jinshi as a man she is attracted to and is aware that she can never return the feelings he wants.
- When getting close to Maomao could not possibly lead to a romantic relationship, Jinshi even took the extreme step of branding himself to forcibly bind Maomao; she became quite angry at that.
- At least it was fortunate for Jinshi that Maomao not feeling romantic attraction does not mean she is opposed to marriage and having a family.
- In conclusion, Maomao possesses humanity in the form of integrity, curiosity as a pharmacist, and a bit of a sense of justice, but overall her personality is detached.
- Maomao's deadpan, catlike nature — only interested in her own concerns with stark likes and dislikes — gradually becomes more ordinary over time.
- Maomao has no interest in childbirth or child-rearing and says she only considers pregnancy for the placenta, yet she ends up taking in and raising Chou, who has nowhere else to go, like a real younger sibling; she endures the approach of the father she so despises, Lakan, to solve problems, and even accepts Jinshi's approaches and physical affection without complaint despite finding them slightly bothersome. As she ages and gains experience, her personality unknowingly softens.

# 4. MaoMao's appearance:
- MaoMao has dark hair (deep green) and blue eyes, and she is very thin with a small frame that makes her look like an early teen.
- MaoMao's bust measures 2 cheok 5 chon (about 75 cm). The emperor once teased her, joking that he wished it stuck out 5 cm more.
- MaoMao's most notable external feature is the freckles on her face; she painted them on with a clay-based pigment to look ugly on purpose so she wouldn't be kidnapped when "woman-hunting" was in vogue. If you remove the freckles, people are said to be so surprised they often don't recognize her.
- At the Wonyu gathering, Jinshi who saw MaoMao without her freckle makeup momentarily mistook her for someone else, then said she had undergone a transformation-level change. Lan Lihua also commented that she changes a lot with makeup.
- However, saying MaoMao's face changes when the freckles are removed doesn't mean she's a beauty without them; it means that aside from the freckles she has an otherwise ordinary face with no distinctive features. MaoMao herself thought that without the freckle makeup she'd only just barely pass as a palace maid if dragged into the harem.
- The reason MaoMao painted on the freckles, as she mentioned to Jinshi at the outing, was because she was once nearly ruined after being taken by a man with a venereal disease — the red-light district where she lived was extremely unsafe.
- The red-light district was so dangerous that Chou warned her not to go alone unless she wanted to become "chunks of meat." Thus the freckle makeup was a hideous disguise meant to deter even rapists.
- At the Wonyu gathering, Rihaku carried several hairpins and handed them out to palace maids who looked unlikely to receive any; he thought MaoMao wouldn't get one either and gave her one.
- When Rihaku met MaoMao again he asked, "You were the maid who used to give that seductive smile?" — so it seems the smile was quite noticeable.
- Even without the freckle-covered face, MaoMao isn't a classic beauty; she is extremely thin to the point of being described as "skin-and-bone like a chicken" with limbs "as scrawny as withered branches." On top of that, her left arm bears numerous scars from self-conducted biological experiments, so she has little feminine allure. Rather, her small frame and scars draw a lot of sympathetic looks.
- On the other hand, because MaoMao's face is otherwise unremarkable, she takes makeup well, and having grown up in the brothel she is skilled at applying it. Still, makeup can't hide her petite build, and in places like the harem or brothel where natural beauties abound, her dressed-up look tends to fall short.
- When MaoMao worked as a courtesan she was basically cast as a supporting role to make the three young ladies of Nokcheonggwan stand out; she was dressed up for other banquets as well and received harsh criticism about her looks from Lahan.
- Lahan's assessment of MaoMao when she came to the banquet dressed up: "That saying 'clothes are wings' doesn't apply here." "Don't worry. You look like a dandelion at best."
- MaoMao's birth mother Fengxian was one of the top courtesans who made a name for herself in her prime, but Fengxian became a top courtesan mainly because her Go/chess skills and haughty attitude excited aficionados; there was never talk of outstanding beauty.
- Fengxian's price skyrocketed because wealthy men vying to take her into their households engaged in pride battles; originally her price was at a level that a lower-ranking military officer — Kanlakan, who was ostracized by his family — could have reasonably aimed to take her in.
- The old woman at Nokcheonggwan encouraged MaoMao to become a courtesan only because Lakan had been a regular customer for over ten years and had persuaded her to do so.
- Some people, like Lahan, rate MaoMao poorly even when she's made up, but that's because Lahan has high standards. After the dismissal incident was over and she had resolved the fact she had once been kidnapped, when MaoMao dressed up and left Nokcheonggwan people passing through the red-light district whispered that she was a pretty courtesan. Even palace guards — who would likely never see top-tier beauties like Samhee or the Imperial Consort in their lifetime — blushed just from seeing MaoMao made up, and Jinshi hurried her to put her freckle makeup back on.
- In short: with her defensive makeup on she looks like an ugly woman with only the freckles standing out; remove the freckles and her appearance changes significantly; apply proper makeup and her looks improve dramatically. Fundamentally she has a plain face, but she is the type who responds well to dressing up and cosmetics.
- However, makeup cannot hide her petite frame, and because the story's setting is the harem and brothel where many beauties appear, she is often underrated. If she sets her mind to it she can become pretty, but if she doesn't put effort into her appearance she won't stand out, and even when she tries hard it is hard for her to be called a peerless beauty — she has an ordinary, realistic appearance unlike many fictional characters.
- As part of a curse against her biological father Lakan, her birth mother Fengxian cut off the tip of MaoMao's little finger when she was a child. It happened before she finished growing, and the bone wasn't seriously damaged, so her fully grown finger is fine, but her little fingernail is a bit deformed. Because of this she once grumbled a little when having her hands stained with balsam dye.

# 5. Mao Mao's Abilities:
- Mao Mao can read and is a capable person with excellent insight and knowledge. She inherited the Kan family's particular genius and her mother's intellect, who was so brilliant she could beat Lakan at Go several times, and on top of that she received education from the genius doctor Luo Men, who studied in the West, giving her deep discernment.
- However, Mao Mao has never really learned Go or janggi (Korean chess), so she isn't particularly good at those games.
- Mao Mao is well-versed in all kinds of medicines and poisons, has experience experimenting on her own body, and can reproduce those effects. When Jinshi asked her to make a mild medicine, she perfectly recreated a chocolate she had tasted only once as a child. She can also immediately detect poison in a soup and spit it out—her skills as an apothecary are excellent.
- Mao Mao is a high-level expert in pharmaceutical fields, and she combines that with her characteristic observational skills to pierce the essence of cases.
- She strictly avoids relying on intuition; instead she only states inferences that can be considered certain by evidence, and tends to pass detailed background matters up to her superiors.
- That said, although Mao Mao is very clever, she is poor at studying in general. To be precise, she has extremely deep knowledge in fields she likes—medicine and pharmacology—and is constantly eager to learn, studying on her own even about fields indirectly related to pharmacology, or even unrelated areas that satisfy her curiosity; but she has no interest at all in other subjects.
- In particular, she dislikes studying for exams and is hopeless at sitting down to study with a specific purpose, so she naturally failed the palace maid exam. Jinshi, who considered Mao Mao's intelligence practically a free pass, was shocked and wondered why she failed.
- Much later, Jinshi bribed the old woman of Nokcheonggwan to force Mao Mao to study, and the result was she passed top of the class. In other words, it was indeed an exam she could have passed very easily given her intelligence.
- As a result of experimenting with various poisons on her body, Mao Mao is almost immune to weak poisons, and with strongly toxic types her body only seems to go numb unless she ingests a large amount. She once said she missed the tingling sensation you can feel when eating pufferfish ovaries and entrails. As widely known, pufferfish poison is so dangerous that an adult could die from an extremely small amount.
- But because of that curiosity—letting a venomous snake bite her forearm or ingesting poisons—Mao Mao's left arm is literally a wreck. She always keeps the arm wrapped in bandages. She even uses that arm as a prop for tearful acting.
- Whether related or not, she is also very tolerant of alcohol. By contrast, her biological father Kan Lakan is very sensitive to alcohol. Her alcohol-tolerant constitution likely comes from her mother’s side, who often had to entertain guests.
- Because Mao Mao has no hesitation about taking poisons, people around her sometimes joke that she could eat anything, but she is severely allergic to buckwheat and cannot eat buckwheat foods. If she does, not only does she break out in a rash, her airways swell and she can’t breathe.
- People nearby were surprised to hear her say she would casually eat poison but can’t eat buckwheat. Jinshi and Gao Xun were horrified, saying, "She’ll eat poison as if it's nothing?" Buckwheat allergy is not the same as poison, and Mao Mao herself is well aware of that. Besides, they are entirely different fields, so differing likes and dislikes are natural.
- Her father and teacher Luo Men always urged her, "Don’t speak recklessly based on mere conjecture," and she mostly follows that. Mao Mao believes he taught her that because he worried she might embarrass herself by speaking about things that weren’t certain.
- Mao Mao tends to view the human body as a tool and knows the lore that certain parts can be used as medicine. The liver and the placenta are the typical examples.
- Surprisingly, Mao Mao has a strong sense of justice, so even for matters that don't directly concern her, if leaving them unresolved feels wrong she will interfere and try to solve them.
- Also, perhaps because she grew up with little, frugality is ingrained in her; she conserves things and tries to use them efficiently. During the Baekbun incident she wanted to write a warning phrase but paper was too expensive, so she cut part of the hem from her skirt to write on. At a banquet, to guard against the cold she sewed a small pocket into the lining of her clothes and put a hot stone in it to use like a hot pack.
- She is extremely indifferent to romance and sexual desire, but having been born and raised in a red-light house and being the object of the exclusive favor of Sam-hee—the very top of Nokcheonggwan—she has surprisingly extensive sexual knowledge picked up by overhearing. Although it is all secondhand knowledge without personal experience, it isn’t mere theory but practical know-how generously shared by veterans, and this theoretical knowledge reportedly helped queens who themselves lacked such understanding.

# 6. Events in the Story:
## 6.1. Volume 1:
- Maomao, a girl who lived and worked with her adoptive father Luo Men (羅門) at the apothecary under the famous red-light district brothel Nokcheonggwan, goes into the forest one day to gather medicinal herbs and runs into an assailant. Maomao is bound with both hands tied behind her back and sold to the imperial harem as a lady-in-waiting.
- Normally she should have made a fuss and demanded to be returned to her old life, but because of Maomao’s characteristically indifferent nature and the fact that if she causes no trouble and works as a maid for two years she can earn money and be free, she accepts the situation and gets through her busy daily routine. One day, through a maid she’s grown close to, Xiaolan (小蘭), she learns that the children born to the upper consorts — Consort Gyokuyo (貴妃) and Consort Li Hua (賢妃, 梨花) — (a girl and a boy respectively) have recently fallen ill.
- Hearing also that all the emperor’s children had died in childhood, and wondering whether poison might be involved, she goes to the area where the upper consorts live and witnesses a quarrel between the two consorts.
- Seeing Consort Li Hua’s face, who is sick with symptoms like those of her child, Maomao realizes the cause is bai fen (白粉). Bai fen was a whitening cosmetic used in the past in East Asia. Made with lead, the powdered lead is inhaled and gradually accumulates, causing lead poisoning that inflicts fatal damage to the body.
- Maomao thinks the incompetent court physician at the harem is a quack for not noticing what is in front of his eyes.
- To avoid getting involved, Maomao even hid the fact that she could read and lived by a creed of doing as little as possible; ordinarily she would have pretended not to know. But her innate sense of justice makes her quietly intervene, muttering to herself as she decides to poke her nose in: “Is there anything to write on?” and she leaves the place.
- Not long after, Consort Li Hua’s child dies and she is devastated and falls ill. Consort Gyokuyo’s child survives, and one of the eunuchs overseeing the harem, Jinshi (壬氏), tells Gyokuyo that someone had tied a torn scrap of cloth with scrawled writing to an azalea branch and left it in her quarters — a warning not to use poison-laced bai fen — and because she followed it she escaped disaster. A closer investigation reveals that small amounts of toxic material had been mixed into bai fen used not only by the consorts but also by wet nurses; the poison accumulated in the mothers’ bodies and passed into the breast milk, which the infants naturally ingested, causing the incidents.
- Consort Gyokuyo had taken the warning seriously, protecting her child and remaining healthy, but Consort Li Hua had thrown away the flower with the warning without reading it. Later, when she heard Gyokuyo’s account she didn’t think much of it and continued using bai fen; as a result she lost her son, the crown prince, and collapsed from a combination of poisoning and grief over the loss of her son.
- Jinshi remembered a freckled maid and gathered all the freckled people in the harem, eventually locating Maomao, the only one who could read. Despite Jinshi’s bright smile and his questioning, Maomao held out at first, but ultimately confessed the truth in front of Consort Gyokuyo and Jinshi.
- With Gyokuyo’s gratitude and because Maomao could read and was skilled in pharmacology and could distinguish poisons, she becomes Gyokuyo’s poison-tasting attendant (kumi-yaku, responsible for tasting for poison). Thus she begins a new job at Gyokuyo’s residence, the Jade Palace (翡翠宮).
- Consort Gyokuyo had a good character and even thanked a commoner like Maomao (once she had slapped and insulted Consort Li Hua yet still worried about her). The other maids at the Jade Palace were diligent and treated Maomao well. They pitied her shabby appearance and presumed past, but the role of poison-taster always carries the risk of dying to protect one’s mistress, so there was some sympathy as well.
- In particular, the bandages wrapped around Maomao’s left arm were thought by everyone to be from abuse, but in fact they covered scars from wounds she inflicted herself to test medicines and poisons and traces of allergic reactions — she hid them because they were unsightly.
- Right before Maomao’s time, a poison incident during a consort’s pregnancy left another poison-taster disabled. Maomao worried she might be unable to perform the role properly, since her frequent self-experimentation with toxins had made her unusually resistant to poisons.
- One day, as part of routine duties, Jinshi comes to the consort’s quarters and mentions that armed men returning from a campaign had camped in a village and all been poisoned. Hearing this, Maomao explains that some trees are poisonous; the soldiers might have been poisoned by making chopsticks from local wood or by fires built with toxic wood.
- On another occasion, Jinshi orders Maomao to make a mild intoxicant. Maomao agrees, glad to do some pharmacy work after a while. What she prepares is dried fruit coated in chocolate — a concoction using cacao that can get people tipsy unless they’re hardy drinkers. Some of Gyokuyo’s maids secretly ate the leftover snack and collapsed in rosy stupor.
- While Maomao is gathering herbs around the harem and growing friendly with that quack physician, she hears stories of a ghost haunting the harem. Jinshi instructs her to investigate and assigns his subordinate Gaoshun to accompany her. Following his guidance on a moonlit night to the supposed haunted spot, Maomao sees Consort Huyobi (芙蓉妃) dancing on the high harem wall in the moonlight.
- Huyobi was a mid-ranked consort skilled in dance but soft-hearted; she once slipped while dancing before the emperor and fell out of favor, never again receiving imperial grace. Jinshi later says that the consort was to be given in marriage to a military man (Huyobi’s childhood friend) for his battlefield achievements, and that the pressure has caused her to suffer from somnambulism. He asks Maomao for a cure, and Maomao mentions a courtesan’s story she knows, saying that psychological stabilization would make it improve naturally.
- A few days later, Huyobi leaves the harem amid blessings to become her childhood friend’s partner. Watching this, Consort Gyokuyo asks Maomao again and Maomao tells the other courtesan’s story: a courtesan had been purchased (nakate) but then displayed somnambulistic behavior, causing the purchase to be withdrawn; someone else then bought the damaged courtesan for half price. It was a con: a man who wanted her had a friend start the purchase story, then the woman was made to act out flaws so she appeared damaged, and the man got her for half price.
- Maomao speculates that Huyobi may have acted a part too: she intentionally stumbled before the emperor to avoid his favor so she could preserve fidelity to her childhood friend, appearing eccentric until she could be given to him still pure. Gyokuyo hears Maomao’s theory and agrees to keep it a secret between the two of them.
- The emperor, who often visited Gyokuyo’s quarters, one day orders Maomao to observe Consort Li Hua’s condition. Maomao goes on assignment to the Crystal Palace (水晶宮) where Li Hua resides, but the Crystal Palace maids’ territoriality prevents her from properly attending Li Hua. Only when Jinshi, popular in the harem, accompanies her is she finally able to properly look after Li Hua.
- Li Hua looks exactly as she did when she used the poison-laced bai fen. Although that bai fen had been completely banned after the earlier incident, one of Li Hua’s maids had been secretly keeping it and continuing to beautify her with it. When Maomao realizes this, she grabs the maid by the hair and pours the poison-laced bai fen onto her face, releasing her fury.
- Especially when the maid insists she used it to make Li Hua beautiful and refuses to face reality, Maomao lashes out: “Who would like the poison that killed their own child?!” She points out that Li Hua’s son died because of the bai fen the maid procured, hitting her with the truth. Only then does the maid realize what she has done; her mind breaks and she loses her senses.
- The other Crystal Palace maids watching, pale with shock, are completely cowed. From then on, they cannot go against Maomao and must do Li Hua’s bidding as Maomao directs. Jinshi, who was there, had seen everything.
- Maomao does her utmost to save the dying Li Hua: she changes her diet, washes her, builds a steam bath — everything she can. Li Hua, who had once given up the will to live, slowly begins to recover thanks to Maomao’s devotion. After months of treatment, Li Hua’s health stabilizes and Maomao is finally allowed to return to the Jade Palace.
- To the despondent Li Hua, who confesses she feels responsible for losing the emperor’s child and inferior to Gyokuyo’s composed handling, Maomao tells her about the two treasures she has and teaches her a beauty trick she learned from the Nokcheonggwan sisters, helping her return to Gyokuyo’s side.
- The maid who smuggled in the bai fen returns after a period of penance, shows genuine remorse, and when Li Hua magnanimously forgives her she is moved to tears and truly reforms.
- After Li Hua recovers, the emperor begins visiting her quarters again and for a while drastically reduces his visits to Gyokuyo and the other consorts.
- A eunuch acquaintance of the quack physician, while burning trash collected in the harem, notices the flames turn various colors and considers it an ill omen. Maomao reassures him that different materials produce different flame colors and calms his worries.
- A winter banquet is held where the emperor, the four upper consorts, and high officials gather. Maomao, as a maid who has to wait all day in the bitter wind, sews pockets into clothing and fills them with heated stones so they act like small stoves. She does this for herself and for those around her.
- On the banquet day, the Jade Palace maids rush to prettify freckled Maomao; Gyokuyo watches smiling and discovers Maomao’s freckles are makeup. Maomao had deliberately made herself look ugly to avoid being kidnapped and abused in the dangerous area where she grew up. She had actually been kidnapped while searching for herbs to make freckle makeup. Seeing Maomao properly made up, even Jinshi takes a second look.
- Gyokuyo gives hairpins to Maomao and her maids marking them as her people. Hearing why Maomao was kidnapped, Jinshi apologizes and gives her one as well. During a break in the banquet, Li Hua comes and gives one, and a young military man who distributes hairpins out of loyalty to his maids gives another.
- During the banquet Maomao gets entangled in a rivalry between the upper consorts’ maids offstage. Confirming that the maid who provoked her belonged to Li Hua’s side, Maomao threatens her by covering only the target’s nose and making a sour smile; the maid recognizes the made-up Maomao and flees.
- After a series of events — hearing the backstory of the Empress Dowager (that the previous sovereign favored only children, etc.) and other matters — Maomao returns to her main duty: serving as Gyokuyo’s poison-taster onstage.
- While performing poison-tasting, she notices another upper consort, Consort De Li Shu (德妃 里樹), who reluctantly eats a fish dish in front of the emperor and is watched from behind by her poison-taster wearing a twisted smile. Maomao pushes the thought aside and continues tasting, but the moment she puts a certain dish to her mouth she makes an ecstasy-like expression that astonishes others. Then she says, “This is poison.”
- After uttering that single word, Maomao steps offstage euphoric at the sensation of tetrodotoxin from pufferfish she hasn't tasted in a long time. Later she asks Jinshi to summon Consort Li Shu and her poison-taster.
- In private, Maomao notices hives on Li Shu’s forearm and tells them about food allergies and rejection reactions, warning the poison-taster that knowingly serving such food is tantamount to poisoning. The bigger problem is that the dish Maomao had tasted would normally have been served to Li Shu.
- The pufferfish incident at the banquet causes a problem: an official, saying “No way it could have been poison when eaten so deliciously,” tastes the pufferfish dish and almost dies. Maomao thinks inwardly how foolish he is after hearing Jinshi’s report.
- After the commotion, Maomao learns from her friend Xiaolan that the hairpin she received can be used to leave the harem.
- So she seeks out the military man Li Haku (李白) who gave a loyalty hairpin. Li Haku boldly offers to take her back to her hometown; Maomao’s request shocks him, but he’s lured by the deal that he can introduce her to his acquaintance — one of the famous three courtesans (the “Three Sisters”) of Nokcheonggwan — and agrees. Meeting Bai Ling (白鈴), one of the Three Sisters, changes Li Haku’s life.
- At last returned home, Maomao is met by the brothel mistress’s fist and reunited with the apothecary owner father. Whether by nature or misfortune, even on holiday in her hometown she gets entangled in other problems involving courtesans. Returning to the harem, she encounters an oddly angry Jinshi and an amused Gyokuyo who enjoys watching the spectacle.
- In fact the hairpin is originally used by male officials to propose to a palace maid they desire. Sincere cases are rare, but the three-day leave isn’t just a vacation — it’s meant for a bride-to-be to go pay respects to her parents.
- When a high official dies, Maomao, at Jinshi’s request, investigates. The official, stressed from hard work and having lost all tastes except for sweet, fails to notice someone over-salting his drink and dies after drinking it.
- On a cold winter day a maid is found dead in the moat beyond the harem wall. Promising her adoptive father not to touch the body, Maomao watches the quack physician perform an autopsy without intervening.
- One day Jinshi orders Maomao to assist at the pomegranate palace (石榴宮), the residence of Consort Shu Ado (淑妃 阿多). Helping with major cleaning under the head maid Fengming’s direction, Maomao learns that Fengming’s family keeps bees. She recalls that Consort Li Shu once nearly died as a child after eating honey. Suspecting a connection, Maomao starts to have doubts (the dead maid’s shoes, honey).
- From Gaoshun she obtains documents and learns key information: the emperor’s first child had been Ado’s, but that child died young, and the physician at the time was Luo Men, Maomao’s adoptive father. The childbirth had been difficult, and at the same time the previous sovereign’s son — the current emperor’s brother — was about to be born, so Ado was deprioritized and not treated properly, leaving her unable to bear children thereafter.
- Putting the pieces together, Maomao confronts Fengming and uncovers more of the truth. One part is that the big cleaning was an excuse; Ado was packing to give up her place as consort for an incoming concubine. The high official Zichang held great influence since the previous sovereign and even the emperor dared not act against him; his daughter was to enter as a consort.
- Fengming had fed her baby beneficial honey, but a weak poison in it killed the infant. The incident was covered up as bai fen and buried in silence, and Fengming atoned by serving Ado even more fervently.
- Li Shu had been a consort of the previous sovereign who was a pedophile. Ado and Li Shu had been close in the previous reign. When Fengming heard Li Shu’s childhood story about almost dying from honey, Fengming feared Ado would find out the truth behind her child’s death. After the previous sovereign’s death Li Shu had gone into seclusion per ritual but later returned as the current emperor’s consort; Fengming tried to kill Li Shu to hide the truth.
- The maid who drowned herself is believed to have been a poisoned maid who, fearing she would harm Ado, committed suicide.
- Seeing Fengming’s love for Ado, Maomao suggests changing the motive of the crime: not to hide the child’s death, but to kill other upper consorts to preserve Ado’s position. Fengming accepts the suggestion and Maomao reports this to Jinshi.
- A few days later Ado gives up her consort position and leaves the harem. Watching Ado depart with dignity, Maomao thinks of someone and makes a fatal hypothesis: Ado claimed to accept her child’s death as heaven’s will, but during a conversation with Maomao she hinted that her child was elsewhere — that he had been switched with the second son of the previous sovereign born on the same day. Thus Ado’s real child may still be alive, now occupying the position of the prince.
- Maomao dismisses this as an overactive imagination and shakes her head. As Li Shu runs out to see Ado off, she stumbles and Ado helps her up.
- Fengming is executed and her relatives punished according to their crimes. Because of Fengming’s case, everyone involved in the harem is punished or dismissed, and the gang that brought Maomao to the harem is implicated, forcing Maomao to leave the palace. Though she wanted to stay, misunderstandings born of poor communication push her out.
- Afterward, concerned Jinshi continues to struggle; Gaoshun steps in to help. Back home helping at Nokcheonggwan, Maomao one day meets the famous Three Sisters and encounters Jinshi at an event; through conversation the misunderstanding is cleared and Jinshi brings Maomao back with a large sum of money and rare medicinal ingredients.
- Since Maomao was effectively part of Nokcheonggwan — the courtesan’s daughter and the adopted daughter of apothecary Luo Men — to release her from Nokcheonggwan she had to repay some of her debt to the house. Thus the large sum was given to the brothel matron, and the Three Sisters presented Maomao as if she were a purchased courtesan. Even after she leaves Nokcheonggwan to work elsewhere, Maomao considers Nokcheonggwan her family and will return there when she has nowhere else to go.

## 6.2. 2권:
- Maomao turns 18. This time she enters the palace not by abduction but formally, yet for some reason she is assigned to work in the outer administrative wards lined with government offices rather than as a concubine in the inner harem where she used to work. Jinshi, for reasons of his own, has Maomao take an examination to be a palace attendant and intends to station her in the outer wards, but Maomao—who only cares about her own interests—fails the exam and ultimately becomes a maid in Jinshi’s office. After seeing the exam results Jinshi wonders, “Why did she fail?” while Maomao thinks, “Why did you think I’d pass?” Gao Shun and Suiren show displeasure.
- Maomao, now working alongside the idolized Jinshi, is attacked by jealous maids from other outer wards and endures a maid’s life little different from that in the harem. One day she sees Lihaku come to investigate a fire in a storeroom.
- The burned storeroom had been storing flour and similar goods; a clerk who had been secretly smoking nearby accidentally transferred the cigarette’s ember to dust that had filled the enclosed space, and the resulting blaze got out of control.
- One day Jinshi orders Maomao to give instruction and lectures to a newly arrived Shubi and three other senior consorts. Maomao keeps dodging, but when she receives letters of recommendation from Lihua-bi and Gyokuyobi she reluctantly agrees to be a one-day lecturer. Bringing a bulk of materials requested from the Kiru Nokcheonggwan, Maomao enters the room where only the four senior consorts are waiting, chases out Jinshi when he tries to come in, and begins the lecture.
- After the lesson Gyokuyobi exclaims, “It replenishes the freshness!” excitedly; Lihua-bi blushes and moves her fingers while reviewing; Lishu-bi goes pale, trembling and saying it’s too much; one even cries out, “I can’t do it~~” and bangs her head against a pillar. The newly seen Shubi, Rouran (子楼蘭), remains silent with no reaction.
- After seeing the state of the room following the instruction, when Jinshi asks about the lesson content, Maomao replies, “Ask His Majesty for his impressions in person later.”
- Gao Shun tells Maomao that a clerk ate pufferfish salad and fell into a coma. He mentions a similar incident some ten years before, and when Maomao smells a case she is asked to look into it. Jinshi and Gao Shun seem busy, so they send one official to the clerk’s house, the scene of the incident.
- After listening to the household’s resentful complaints and finishing the investigation, Maomao tells Jinshi what she has found out. The pufferfish salad the official liked to eat can become poisonous depending on the season it’s made; the official’s younger brother, seeking to take his brother’s power, had deliberately ordered the poisonous salad. Maomao becomes suspicious that the younger brother—who appears insignificant—would conspire such a thing.
- Maomao is also asked to reveal the secret of the dying words of a master goldsmith who died of illness. It was about alloy composition ratios and such arcane formulas: important techniques in metalworking to lower melting points.
- Maomao arranges a commoner disguise for Jinshi when he says he has matters outside the palace, and she accompanies him briefly when she takes leave to go home. Bored after returning, she visits the Nokcheonggwan on her father’s suggestion. Going to an annex behind the splendor, she cares for a woman who, ruined in body and mind by syphilis from working as a courtesan, is barely surviving day to day. Meimei (梅梅), one of the trio Samhee, visits the annex and tells Maomao about her attachment to that woman. The disfigured woman’s appearance has become familiar, and Meimei helps Maomao care for her.
- Maomao learns that the person who has been constantly pestering Jinshi recently is a military man named Rakan (羅漢). She also hears that Jinshi learned that Rakan is Maomao’s biological father.
- More than a decade earlier, Kanrakan (漢羅漢) was already a brilliant man—unbeatable at long chess and go, with a lightning-fast mind—but he could not distinguish faces. At that time he met the most famous courtesan of the Nokcheonggwan, and her image was imprinted vividly on him. He became attracted to the proud courtesan Fengxian (鳳仙), who had defeated him at go, and his visits to the brothel increased; she answered his feelings. They even became intimate, and when he intended to bring her to his household, his uncle’s fall from power prevented him from meeting her for years. After a long time he finally returned to see her, but she was gone; the brothel’s ruined madame told Rakan that Fengxian had died and chased him away.
- Still searching for her at the Nokcheonggwan, one day he sees a little girl at the brothel who appears to him as clearly as Fengxian had. Realizing the child is his daughter with Fengxian, Rakan also learns that the person who raised her is the uncle he respects, Luo Men. The uncle’s downfall stemmed from holding responsible the matter of Adu Bi’s child, which led to his corporal punishment and removal from office. But knowing Luo Men’s character, Rakan held no resentment toward him.
- Rakan tried to take his child Maomao from the Nokcheonggwan, but the madame fiercely opposed and stopped him. It was because Fengxian’s loss of honor had nearly ruined the Nokcheonggwan; losing her purity would lower her value. Thus, besides Meimei—the courtesan who was Fengxian’s junior companion—no one welcomed him.
- Maomao had been beaten by the madame and came crawling back covered in blood time and again, so she could hardly feel any goodwill toward Rakan who kept charging at her. Because of that, Maomao became the sort of person who was unstartled by most things.
- Fengxian had also treated Maomao cruelly until she herself was destroyed, so Maomao had only bad memories of that woman (her mother). For that reason Maomao regards only her respected adoptive father Luo Men as a parent and does not consider others as such at all.
- Afterwards Rakan paid the madame of the Nokcheonggwan a huge compensation over ten years—more than twice the loss—and when it finally looked possible for him to take Maomao, Jinshi intercepted and took her. That is why Rakan kept harassing Jinshi.
- Jinshi tells Maomao that Rakan will soon come to see her; Maomao reacts with utter disgust and horror. Jinshi answers seriously that he will refuse on her behalf.
- As always, while working Maomao goes to the medical office and meets one of the outer-ward maids who used to bully her. The outer-ward physician calls her the palace attendant Suilei (翠苓). Tall and expressionless, Suilei seems to be observing Maomao. Maomao feels no particular ill will toward her that day because Suilei didn’t bother her.
- On a small hill she found while taking out the trash, an herb garden is planted; curious, Maomao approaches and again meets Suilei. Like Maomao, Suilei knows a lot about pharmacology, and they chat about reviving medicines and the like.
- One day Lihaku comes to Maomao’s office. He says a ritual implement to be used in an upcoming rite has gone missing. When Maomao asks the circumstances, Lihaku tells a startling story: the person who had long been in charge of the ritual tools was a high official who died last year after drinking salt-laced wine; another person in charge recently collapsed from food poisoning. Moreover, the official responsible for the previous storeroom fire had secretly smoked a tobacco pipe given by a tall palace attendant who looked noble.
- Promised the rare medicine ox gall as a reward by Jinshi (Maomao was so excited she even asked informally if he was really giving it), Maomao starts investigating and discovers that some of the ritual implements recently made used the technique of the now-deceased metalworker and that the newly made implements that replaced the missing ones are dangerous. The day the rite is held is that very day—right now in progress.
- Maomao rushes to the ritual hall but is stopped by warriors guarding the entrance. She shouts that it’s urgent and struggles, and an angry guard strikes her full in the face with a club, sending her flying and collapsing. At that moment a familiar but most detestable voice is heard behind her. Under orders from the military commander Rakan, the warriors let Maomao into the ritual hall.
- The newly made ritual implement was supporting a gigantic metal pillar right above the altar, and Maomao realizes the support will soon break and crush the person conducting the rite standing before the altar. She lunges at the person and knocks them sideways. The structure collapses immediately, but the person is unharmed; Maomao’s leg is badly scraped by the pillar. Regaining her senses and seeing the person who had been in danger, she recognizes him as Jinshi. She asks Jinshi for the promised ox gall and then loses consciousness.
- When she comes to, Maomao tells Jinshi and others the inside story. She also hears that the palace attendant Suilei took poison and committed suicide. But recalling a past conversation with Suilei, Maomao asks them to inspect her coffin, and it turns out the corpse in the coffin has been swapped with someone else. The attendant Suilei has disappeared.
- After confirming Suilei’s disappearance, Maomao says she wants to meet her again and declares: “If she’s alive I want to meet her. I’m not saying to capture her. The knowledge to disguise incidents and accidents, the nerve to put that into practice, and above all the will to risk one’s life to deceive everyone! If such a person simply dies, it would be boring! A potion that can revive people! I will definitely find the recipe!! Hahahahahahaha~~~~~”
- Some time later Maomao returns to the harem because Gyokuyobi may have been reinstated. As before she serves the noble consort’s complexion treatments.
- Maomao helps a court physician who worries because the quack doctor’s family in the harem runs a paper mill and the paper quality has declined. She discovers the cause: cows bought to increase labor for the paper mill drank from the water used in papermaking, and the saliva of the many cows had spoiled that water.
- Lihaku visits Maomao again. Rumor had it a high-ranking courtesan at the Nokcheonggwan was to be sold, and he sought counsel on whether he could take Bai Ling, the woman he loves. Maomao, who wanted the three courtesans who had treated her kindly without a mother to be happy, begins to judge whether Lihaku could be a match for the incomparable Bai Ling by stripping off his clothes one by one and evaluating his muscular body. Just as she is about to strip the last scrap, Jinshi’s fearsome presence enters the room; Lihaku leaves and Maomao falls to her knees. The misunderstanding is cleared up.
- Jinshi brings Maomao a troublesome request: obtain blue roses in time for the spring gathering. The requester is the person Maomao hates most (Rakan). The problem is the timing—early spring, long before roses bloom—and that blue roses don’t exist. Maomao agrees to the task partly to teach that person a lesson and throws herself into making blue roses.
- Maomao brings hundreds of white rose pots to the steam bath she had built at Lihua-bi’s palace and makes them bloom months early. She barely sleeps for weeks tending the pots until they bloom, and the leg wound that had been healing reopens...
- Using all her connections and efforts and some ingenuity—such as cutting white rose stems and dipping the ends in colored water so the petals take on the dye—she creates roses of blue and many other colors and exceeds the mission. She then directly summons Rakan, who has been pestering her, and proposes a one-on-one match.
- Maomao proposes the contest be at chess (xiangqi/long chess) in a best-of-five format. If Rakan wins, Maomao will go to him as his daughter; if Maomao wins, Rakan must take one of the Nokcheonggwan courtesans as his bride.
- It is nearly impossible for the novice Maomao to beat the supposedly undefeated Rakan, but she uses a few stratagems—such as getting Rakan, who cannot hold his liquor, to drink so he forfeits—leading to his defeat, and he takes a courtesan. Just as he is about to choose Meimei, the only courtesan who had treated him well, Meimei tells him about the sick courtesan in the annex and gives him the choice.
- The courtesan whose body is ruined by syphilis and has little time left is the very Fengxian he had been searching for. Rakan decides to take her no matter the cost and buys her then and there for an enormous sum.
- The Nokcheonggwan holds banquets for days. Meimei, who had secretly admired Rakan but failed to gain his affection, sends Maomao a letter streaked with tears. Receiving the letter, Maomao feels conflicted about Rakan’s having won Fengxian. Knowing Meimei’s feelings, Maomao wishes that Meimei will be taken and live in peace. Wearing the dress Meimei requested, Maomao climbs the palace wall with Huyobi and watches from afar the lights of Nokcheonggwan’s banquet, then begins a dance of blessing and farewell for someone.
- Shortly after, a report that someone like Huyobi has appeared causes Jinshi to come out and a strange atmosphere almost forms, but Maomao breaks it by asking for the ox gall she hasn’t received yet. Jinshi responds by charging her in anger and headbutting her in revenge.

## 6.3. 3권:
- 마오마오는 귀비 교쿠요의 용태를 살피며 후궁 생활을 이어간다. 파란 장미를 만들고 남은 것으로 향수를 만들거나 술을 이용해 소독용 주정을 만들기도 하며 지내던 중, 황제의 명으로 녹청관발 그렇고 그런 서적을 반입하는 것을 진시 등에게 들키게 된다. 경험이 없는 비들을 위한 것이라고 해명해 보지만 결국 검열당했다. 그리고 진시는 그 서적을 만드는 데 들인 공과 수준에 주목한다.
- 얼마 후 후궁 내에 소설 붐이 일어난다. 진시가 눈여겨본 책 공방을 통해 이런저런 소설책이 만들어지고 황제가 비들에게 하사하는 형태로 그 아래 시녀들과 여럿이 즐길 거리가 생긴 것이다. 마오마오처럼 납치되거나 가족에게서 팔려 와 글을 읽고 쓰는 것을 못 하는 이들이 대부분이었던 하급 시녀들에게 글공부를 할 동기부여를 위한 수였다.
- 마오마오의 친구 샤오란도 그동안 소설책을 계속 읽어주던 시녀가 목이 아파 한동안 읽어주기 힘들게 되자 마오마오에게 자신의 이름이나 자주 쓰는 말 등을 쓰고 읽는 법을 배우기 시작한다.
- 마오마오 덕에 목숨을 건진 교쿠요비의 딸 링리 공주는 어느덧 스스로 걸어 다니며 이것저것에 흥미를 보이는 호기심 많은 말괄량이가 되어간다. 슬슬 처소인 비취궁 안이 좁게 느껴질 정도로 활발한 공주에게 후궁을 산책시켜주기로 한다. 시녀장 홍냥과 마오마오 등이 따라나선 링리 공주의 첫나들이에서 새끼 고양이 한 마리를 발견한다. 
- 새끼 고양이를 잡아달라는 공주의 말에 허둥대던 마오마오는 지나가던 한 시녀 (시스이)의 도움으로 고양이를 잡게 된다. 만지고 싶어 하는 공주였지만 굶주리고 더러워진 고양이를 줄 수도 없는 노릇. 딸에게 약한 황제는 마오마오로 하여금 새끼 고양이를 건강하게 보살펴 공주에게 보여주라 명한다. "이름 (猫猫)과 딱 맞는군그래." 라는 황제의 농담을 더해서 말이다.
- 후에 새끼 고양이는 마오마오(毛毛)라는 이름과 도적 단속관 (창고의 쥐잡기)이라는 직함까지 받게 된다. 물론 마오마오(猫猫)는 납득 못 한다.
- 계절이 초여름으로 바뀌어갈 무렵, 후궁에 대상인이 찾아온다. 즐길 거리가 거의 없는 후궁의 비와 궁녀들에게는 그야말로 큰 행사. 닷새간 열린 노점을 샤오란과 돌아보던 마오마오는 예전에 새끼 고양이를 잡아준 시녀 시스이(子翠)를 다시 만나게 된다. 샤오란과 이미 알고 었는지 세 사람은 의국에 모여 노점에서 산 말리화 차 (자스민차)를 마시며 친해진다. 
- 시스이는 마오마오만 한 괴짜인지 쉬는 시간이 날 때마다 후궁을 돌아다니며 이런저런 벌레들을 모으고 관찰하며 세밀하게 그림까지 그려대며 즐기고 있다고 한다. 마오마오도 쉴 때마다 후궁 여기저기에 자라난 약초를 구하러 다닌다.
- 곤충도 약재가 되기에 마오마오는 큰 무리 없이 받아들였지만 샤오란이나 다른 시녀들은 질색을 한다.
- 대상이 떠나가고 후궁 내에 향유가 유행하기 시작한다. 너도나도 대상으로부터 사들인 향유를 쓰기 시작해 빨래터에서는 그 강한 냄새에 고역을 치른다. 세탁물을 가져왔던 마오마오는 그 수많은 냄새들의 출처를 떠올리더니 빨랫감에 머리를 파묻고 냄새를 맡거나, 시녀들에게 달려들어 냄새를 맡았다. 그날 수정궁 시녀들의 비명이 후궁 전체에 울려 퍼졌다 한다...
- 당연한 순서로, 수많은 투서 (항의서)를 들고 진시가 찾아온다. 아주 잠깐 반성한 마오마오는 진시와 교쿠요비 등에게 자신의 기행을 설명한다. 바로 그 수많은 향유의 원재료들은 어째선지 전부 임신에 해가 되는 것들이었다. 마오마오는 독이 든 백분 때와 같은 상황이라며 의심을 한다.
- 어느 날 궁녀 한 사람이 행방불명되었다는 이야기를 듣는다. 봉공기간도 끝나고 결혼지참금도 모아 퇴직만 하면 되었다는데...
- 진시가 황제의 명으로 교습소를 만들게 되었다는 이야기를 한다. 하급 궁녀들을 가르쳐 식자율을 높이자는 이야기였다. 의외로 제대로 된 이야기에 마오마오로서는 매우 드물게 진시를 대단하다는 눈으로 쳐다본다. 
- 마오마오는 진시에게 교습소의 위치를 북측에 둘 것을 조언한다. 황제가 자주 드나드는 남측에 교습소를 지으면 그 근처에 사는 비들이 달가워하지 않을 것이기 때문이었다.
- 또 다른 이야기로 진시는 마오마오에게 후궁 내 위험한 버섯 등이 있는지 조사하게 한다. 마오마오는 자신이 관심 있는 분야이기에 흔쾌히 수락하였다. 평소 거의 사용되지 않아 관리되지 않고 수풀이 무성하게 자란 후궁 북측지역도 조사하게 된다.
- 그러던 중 마오마오는 교쿠요비의 대리로 시녀장 홍냥과 함께 어느 중급비의 장례식에 조문하러 가게 된다. 일 년 전부터 몸이 안 좋아 요양하다가 더운 날 식중독에 의한 사망이라 했다. 
- 중급비의 조문을 끝내고 나오려던 마오마오는 얼굴에 흰 붕대를 감고 찾아온 비 한 사람을 보게 된다. 그녀는 갑자기 관 위에 덮여있던 흰 천을 걷어내 버리고 그녀를 저주하며 조소한다. 드러난 중급비의 시신은 얼굴이 붉게 짓무르고 머리카락도 거의 빠진 흉측한 모습이었다. 붕대를 감은 비 역시 같은 증상이었고, 마오마오는 그것이 독버섯에 의한 것임을 알아차리고 진시의 명령을 이해한다. 
- 후궁 내의 상황을 잘 아는 진시는 사망한 중급비의 상태를 보고 독버섯 등에 대해 잘 아는 마오마오로 하여금 조사하게 함과 동시에 그녀가 그런 독살에도 최적의 인물임을 알았기에 유력 용의자인 그녀의 반응을 살피고자 하는 의도 역시 있었다.
- 마오마오는 그런 증상을 일으키는 맹독을 가진 독버섯인 붉은사슴뿔버섯을 찾아와 진시에게 보여준다. 진시는 죽은 중급비 징(靜)은 일 년 전 황제의 총애를 받은 한 하급비에게 그 독버섯을 사용해 얼굴을 망쳤으나 실수로 자신도 그 독에 당했다고 한다. 그리고 끝내 비관하여 그 독버섯으로 자살한 것이라고 한다.
- 그 하급비는 중급비인 징 비보다 낮은 하급비인 송 비로, 유복한 상인집안 출신에, 성격 또한 좋은 편이라서 황제 또한 몇 번 정도 발걸음을 한 적이 있었다고 한다. 그러나, 징 비가 보낸 독버섯으로 인해 얼굴이 문드러지고 탈모 증상까지 오고 말자, 주위에서 '비에서 강등시켜야 한다'라는 얘기가 나왔지만, 얼굴이 문드러진 송비의 사정을 이해한 황제의 배려로 계속해서 후궁에 남게 하고, 계속해서 녹봉을 받았다고 한다. 애초에 얼굴도 문드러지고, 탈모증상까지 왔는데, 이런 상태에서 친가로 돌아가지도, 다른 남자에게 시집도 못가기 때문이다.
- 홍냥의 언급에 의하면 징 비는 마오마오가 고쿠요의 시녀로 들어오기 전, 링리를 임신하고 있을 때 독을 보냈을 만큼 (일명 독침사건) 질투심이 심했고, 온갖 중상모략에 지위가 낮은 이에게는 가차없이 폭력을 행사할 만큼 후궁 내에서도 요주의 인물로 불리 만큼 성격에 문제가 많았던 비였다고 한다. 오죽했겠으면, 그 홍냥이 '주상께서 걸음을 한 적이 없으니, 친가로 돌아가면 좋았을 텐데'라고 험담을 해댔을 정도였다고 한다. 
- 징 비의 장례식에 난입한 붕대 감은 비가 피해를 입은 그 비였다. 마오마오는 몇 가지 의문이 들어 좀 더 깊이 조사하여 내막을 밝히게 된다. 그 독버섯이 자라는 곳은 시체나 분뇨 위었기에 주변을 살펴 해골이 보이는 시신 한구를 찾아낸다. 그 시신이 바로 얼마 전에 죽었다는 징 비였다. 징 비는 일 년 전 이미 죽었으나 그녀를 모시던 시녀들은 징 비와 닮은 한 궁녀를 대역 삼아 일 년을 버텼다. 하지만 그 궁녀가 일을 그만두게 되자 그녀를 죽여 지금 장례를 치르게 한 것이다. 얼마 전 행방불명되었다는 궁녀가 바로 이 사람이었다.
- 마오마오는 사람 시체위에서 자란 버섯을 동충하초 처럼 연구용으로 쓰려고 했으나 진시와 가오슌에게 모조리 압수당하고 버섯들은 폐기당한다.
- 여름이 뜨겁게 깊어갈 무렵 상급비들에게 커다란 유리거울이 하나씩 전달된다. 이국의 특사가 선물로 보내온 것이라고 한다. 그 안에 담긴 도발의 의미를 흘려 넘기며, 진시는 특사들의 곤란한 부탁을 마오마오에게 들고 온다. 서구에서 온 특사들에게 체구가 작은 동양식 미인의 모습이 성에 차지 않았던 듯 했다.
- 진시가 가져온 곤란한 부탁은 수십 년 전 이곳을 방문한 특사, 현 특사의 증조부가 만났다는, 달빛 아래 신비로운 하얀 꽃잎을 두르고 춤을 추던 절세의 미녀를 찾아달라는 이야기였다. 그의 이야기를 듣던 마오마오는 곧 그 인물이 누군지 알아낸다. 바로 녹청관의 할멈이었다. 
- 녹청관 할멈은 소싯적에는 키가 5척 8촌, 즉 175cm 정도 되는 늘씬한 미인이었다고 한다. 키만큼이나 팔다리도 길어 춤이 특기였다고. 마오마오는 현재의 쭈글쭈글해진 모습만 봐서 '할멈이 그 절세미녀라고?' 하며 못 미더워했다.
- 녹청관 할멈을 후궁으로 불러 50여 년 전 당시의 상황을 들은 마오마오는 늙어 꼬부라진 그 할멈을 데려갈 수도 없는 노릇이었기에 고심 끝에 대역과 무대를 준비한다. 특사를 환영하는 무대가 후궁에서 열리게 되고 마오마오는 서구형 미인의 특징을 가진 미녀 두 사람, 이국의 특사'들'을 보게 된다. 상급비들이 경계할 정도로 황제에게 대시하기도 하는 등의 일이 벌어진다. 
- 특사들을 위한 연회가 끝이 나고 하나둘씩 퇴장할 무렵, 연회장 옆 연못가에 마오마오와 베일을 쓴 인물이 나타난다. 베일을 벗은 후 드러난 얼굴은 천상의 미녀의 모습을 한 진시. 마치 천녀의 춤과 같은 신비로운 모습에 이국의 특사들은 넋을 잃고 바라보게 된다. 춤추는 미인에게 그때처럼 주변에 수많은 흰 빛들이 모여들고, 음악이 끝나며 흰 꽃잎이 우수수 떨어지는 순간 미인은 홀연히 사라져 버리고 만다. 특사 중 한 명이 달려와 마오마오에게 미녀의 정체를 다급히 묻지만 마오마오는 하늘의 달을 가리키며 서방에 전해지는 달의 여신의 이름만을 입에 담고 물러난다. 
- 진시가 춤을 출 때 신비로운 빛무리는 밤에 활동하는 나방의 수컷이 내는 빛이었다. 젊었을 적의 할멈은 다른 기녀들에게 그녀가 입어야 했을 옷에 죽은 벌레를 짓눌러 비벼 놓는 괴롭힘을 받았는데, 이 벌레가 그 나방의 암컷이었고 옷에 묻은 암컷의 체취에 수나방들이 모여든 것이었다. 이를 안 마오마오는 시스이의 도움을 받아 수나방을 잔뜩 모은 뒤 옷에 암나방의 체취를 묻혀 과거의 춤사위를 재현한다.
- 교쿠요비의 시녀 아이란 (愛藍)이 감기에 걸린다. 시녀장 홍냥은 진료소에 다녀오라 하고 같이 따라간 마오마오는 의국과는 다른, 북측에 위치한 진료소를 알게 된다. 남자만이 의관을 할 수 있다는 규칙 때문에 진료소에는 여성뿐이라 그저 잠시 쉬어가는 곳이었다. 마오마오는 진시 등이 눈감아 주어 약을 만들고 있었다. 
- 다음날 상태가 호전되어 비취궁으로 돌아온 아이란은 진료소의 궁녀가 마오마오를 찾는다는 것을 알려준다. 전날 마오마오가 아이란에게 처방해 준 약을 먹는 것을 보았기 때문이라고 한다. 마오마오를 만난 중년 궁녀 션뤼 (深緑)는 진료소 옆 빨래터에 자주 오던 궁녀 한 명이 아픈 것 같다며 도움을 청한다.
- 돌팔이 의원이 궁녀 한 명을 데리고 수정궁을 찾아 리화비를 뵙기를 청한다. 시녀장 싱 (杏)은 예전에 리화비와 그녀의 아이를 치료하지 못한 그를 못 미더워했으나 진시의 글을 보고 마지못해 들여보낸다. 문득 뒤따라온 궁녀가 정원 한쪽에 있는 창고에 관심을 가진다. 그리고 드러난 궁녀의 정체는 마오마오였다. 마오마오가 싱을 추궁하며 막는 사이 돌팔이 의원이 창고의 문을 열자 나온 것은 션뤼가 도움을 청한 궁녀가 누워있는 모습. 병색이 완연한 체취가 창고에 가득 차 있었다. 창고 깊이 들어간 마오마오가 향유병을 찾아낸다. 일전에 마오마오가 충고하여 후궁 내에서 모두 수거하였을 터인 향유였다. 다른 표현으로는 낙태약이었다.
- 진시와 리화비 등이 싱을 추궁하나 그녀는 시치미를 뗀다. 이에 마오마오는 자신의 추리와 특유의 도발로 싱의 여유를 빼앗는다. 기정사실화된 싱의 죄목에 리화비는 친척으로서 자신을 보필하러 들어온 싱이 사실을 자신을 질시하고 있었고 싱 자신이 황제의 비가 되겠다는 야망을 가지고 있다는 것을 알게 된다. 
- 낙태약으로 황제의 아이를 죽이는 것은 큰 죄에 해당하나 리화비는 그녀를 후궁에서 쫓아내는 것으로 일을 끝내고자 한다. 싱처럼 자존심이 센 사람은 그것만으로도 죽음보다 더한 형벌일 것이기에. 일을 끝내고 돌아가려던 마오마오는 한 시녀를 만난다. 아픈 궁녀를 걱정해 꽃을 심어 그녀가 있던 창고를 알도록 도움을 준 그녀를 안심시킨다.
- 진시와 마오마오는 후궁 북측에 세워진 교습소의 상황을 보기 위해 찾아간다. 샤오란을 비롯한 시녀들이 교육을 받고 있는 것을 본 후 진시는 돌아가고 마오마오는 남아 샤오란과 글을 가르치는 늙은 환관을 만난다. 후궁 북측에 있는 작은 사당을 관리한다는 그 환관은 선제 시절 후궁에서 일하고 있던 마오마오의 양아버지 뤄먼을 기억하고 있었다.
- 이틀 후 비취궁에 찾아와 교쿠요비를 만나던 황제는 마오마오를 한밤중에 불러낸다. 찾아간 곳은 후궁 북측에 있는 선택의 사당. 늙은 환관이 관리하고 있고 하는 그곳이었다. 그곳에는 진시도 나와있었다. 진시와 늙은 환관, 그리고 마오마오를 데리고 들어간 황제는 이런저런 색의 문을 선택하라는 관문을 지나 끝에 다다르지만 "왕의 아이로다. 그러나 왕모의 아이는 아니로다.'라는 결과를 받게 된다. 상당히 여러 번 시도해본 듯, 전과 같은 결과라는 황제. 
- 수수께끼에 관심을 보이는 마오마오에게 늙은 환관은 뤄먼이라면 바로 알았을 거라고 말해준다. 그 말에 자극받은 마오마오는 왕모의 전설의 내용, 즉, 눈이 밝아 밤에도 잘 보여 그 능력으로 백성을 통치했다는 이야기를 토대로 해답을 찾는다. 황제는 마오마오를 앞장서게 하여 다시 사당에 들어서고, 마오마오는 일견 전혀 틀린 답을 골라가며 끝까지 나아가 결국 옳은 해답을 찾아낸다. 왕모의 자식들은 확률적으로 색맹이었고, 색맹이어야만 맞출 수 있는 문제들이었다.
- 후궁 내에서 벌레를 모으고 다닌다는 누명을 쓴 마오마오는 자신은 약초를 찾아다닐 뿐이라고 반박하지만 아무도 믿어주지 않자 그 소문의 주인공이라 믿어 의심치 않는 궁녀 시스이를 찾아 나선다. 여럿에게 물어 드디어 찾아낸 시스이는 북측 수풀이 우거진 곳에서 곤충 채집 중이었다. 둘은 대화를 나누던 중 진료소로 향하는 황태후(皇太后)를 목격한다.
- 그날 비취궁은 황태후 안시 (安氏)를 맞아들인다. 교쿠요비와 친한 듯 좋은 분위기를 이어가다 황태후는 귀비가 자리를 비운 사이 이런저런 일을 많이 해결했다는 이야기를 들었다며 마오마오에게 선제가 승하한 후 1년 동안 시신이 전혀 썩지 않았기 때문에 자신이 선제에게 저주를 건 것이 아닐까 하는 의문을 풀어달라고 부탁한다. 
- 황태후의 허락을 받아 선제가 죽기 전 머물렀다는 방을 조사한 마오마오는 그동안 단편적으로만 알던 선제의 내막을 좀 더 알게 되고 황태후의 궁금증 역시 풀어주게 된다. 선제의 소아성애자 기질은 어머니인 여제의 냉혹한 철혈 통치에 질려 성인 여성에게 생긴 공포에 의한 것이었다. 성인 여성이 손을 대는 것조차 매우 무서워했다고 한다. 그리고 선제는 남몰래 그림을 그리는 취미가 있었고 노란색을 내기 위해 쓴 웅황이라는 물질에 함유된 비소 성분에 중독되어 사후 시신이 썩지 않았다.

"아아, 얼마 안 남았었는데."

# 8. Status:
- Maomao's official status is that she is the legitimate daughter of Kan Lakan, the head of the Kan family — one of Li state's major noble houses — and a Grand Marshal. In other words, she is a lady of a prestigious family, and with that rank in Li state entering the harem would at minimum guarantee a mid-ranked consort position. Even Gyokuyo, who came from a respectable provincial family though not on the scale of the Kan family, started as a mid-ranked consort, and Roulan, the daughter of the Shi family which is considered equal to the Kan family, entered as a high-ranked consort. So purely in terms of family influence, she is on par with or even above the high-ranked consorts.
- Because of that, although Maomao was born and raised in the pleasure quarters and was merely a maid in the palace, her abilities were recognized by the noble high-ranked consorts, by Jinshi, and even by the emperor, allowing her to exert a certain degree of influence.
- Maomao's mother, Fengxian, was a courtesan, but because Kan Lakan publicly declared Fengxian his sole spouse, Maomao's position within the family is unequivocally that of a legitimate daughter.
- Maomao was born out of an extramarital situation and could have been considered illegitimate, but Kan Lakan and Fengxian had been promised marriage (a betrothal) and were separated unwillingly by a series of incidents; when Kan Lakan confirmed Fengxian was alive, he unhesitatingly completed the betrothal and wedding. Therefore Maomao became legally and ritually Kan Lakan's legitimate daughter, and Kan Lakan openly boasted of Maomao as his daughter and made it clear in the palace that she was his only daughter.
- Apart from the minor blemish of her mother's lowly status, Maomao is the daughter of a distinguished family, and since the current heir of the Kan family, Kan Lahan, is actually Kan Lakan's nephew, Maomao is Kan Lakan's only direct blood descendant.
- This status of Maomao is known to some degree by her adoptive father Kan Luomen, by Kan Lakan and Fengxian, and by people at the Green Hall who know their relationships. However, Maomao did not regard Kan Lakan and Fengxian as more than biological parents and recognizes only Kan Luomen as her parent, so she rejected the status of being the Kan family's legitimate daughter and considered herself a commoner. For that reason she did not reveal her true status to those around her.
- When she first entered the harem, the only palace people who knew Maomao's true status were her biological father Kan Lakan and the emperor — who likely learned it from Kan Lakan or from separate inquiries; even Maomao's direct superior Gyokuyo and Jinshi, who often got involved with her, did not know. Even Gyokuyo, who by nature is cautious and does not keep people at her side without verification, did not know — she considered Maomao's saving of her daughter Lingli as sufficient verification and omitted deep background checks.

A maid who had only done menial chores, she was recognized for her ability and character after saving the life of a princess of the imperial blood and was chosen as Gyokuyo's personal maid; thereafter she proved her abilities multiple times and built considerable backing. As a result of her own efforts, she won the favor of two of the high-ranked consorts, one of whom — Gyokuyo, whom she served directly — eventually rose to Empress.
- Because Maomao spent so long growing up in the pleasure quarters, she habitually considered herself a lowly person and always kept a humble stance, so she never acted recklessly even when trusting her patron Gyokuyo. She has the habit of obtaining permission first and acting only within the scope she was asked to, and thus gives almost no grounds to be accused of overstepping her authority.
- When Kan Lakan revealed to Jinshi that Maomao was his daughter, some high-ranking palace figures — Jinshi, Gao Xun, Gyokuyo, etc. — learned Maomao's true status, but because Maomao herself still would not accept Kan Lakan as her parent and clung to thinking of herself as a lowly person from the pleasure quarters, she maintained a humble, submissive attitude and their outward treatment of her did not change.
- However, the high-ranked consorts — including Gyokuyo, Maomao's direct superior — having learned this fact, tolerated Maomao's running about doing things in all directions, and tasks beyond the authority of a "clever former apothecary turned jade palace chief maid" began to be entrusted to her, with even the emperor and the Empress Dowager coming to request her services directly. Representative examples include matters related to the imperial family's secrets, such as the shrine of choice and investigating the curse of the Seonje. These are tasks that would be difficult to assign to anyone other than a daughter of a family so close to the imperial house.
- The fact that even before Maomao's status was revealed the emperor entrusted her with treating Consort Lihua shows that the current emperor wasn't especially authoritarian to begin with. However, after her status became certain, it became clear that the importance of the affairs Maomao was assigned rose to another level: the Empress Dowager consulted her on sensitive issues, and Maomao was brought into matters that in principle only those belonging to the imperial family should handle.
- Over time, Maomao — who had been denying her birth and status in her heart — gradually came to somewhat accept her position as the daughter of the head of the Kan family; attending an exchange gathering as a representative of the Kan family head at Kan Lahan's request became the occasion that made her continuously contemplate the issue.
- The dilemma deepened especially when she was considered as a marriage match for Jinshi (Hua Sheyue). Aside from her personal views on romance and marriage, marrying the prince Jinshi would greatly shake up the existing power balance within the palace.
- In particular, because it could put her in opposition to Empress Gyokuyo, this concern was one reason she steadily refused Jinshi's advances. That ran counter to the emperor's intention to entrust the succession to Jinshi, and after Jinshi's self-harm incident — an act meant to show he would not become Gyokuyo's political rival — Maomao was able to accept Jinshi's feelings.
- In fact, the emperor's refusal of Empress Gyokuyo's request to have Maomao as her personal maid was because "with the Empress's family already gaining power, handing over Kan Lakan's daughter as well might excessively tilt the palace's power balance" — which indicates that by this point Maomao's political standing in the story had become quite strong.
- The concern was not limited to the court: there was worry that the balance of power among ministers and within the noble society would tilt overwhelmingly in favor of the Empress's family. Later, when the military officers' factions within the government split into the Empress Dowager faction, the Empress faction, and a neutral faction, if the powerful Ra clan — the Kan family — led by its head Kan Lakan were to back a particular faction following Maomao's lead, it would create a very serious destabilization of the military. That is more than enough reason for the emperor to refuse out of concern.
- Hongrang, Gyokuyo's chief steward, showing murderous intent, threatened Maomao — who had been entrusted by the Empress Dowager — to never betray Gyokuyo; seen in this context, it was not an overreaction.
- With four high-ranked consorts competing for the empress's seat, if the Kan family were to back one side, it would be obvious that the supported side would seize power, and depending on whom the Kan family's legitimate daughter Maomao favored, the hierarchy within the harem and Gyokuyo's future could be shaken.
- Gyokuyo's attempts to draw Maomao in and subtly split Maomao and Jinshi can also be seen politically: Jinshi would be her greatest rival if her own child were to inherit the throne, and if Jinshi gained the Kan family's backing, it would put her at a serious disadvantage in many respects.
- Of course, considering Gyokuyo had been bustling about trying to separate them even before she knew Maomao's or Jinshi's identities, Gyokuyo herself, apart from political considerations, coveted Maomao as a person; given Gyokuyo's tendency not to keep many close aides, her possessiveness over the few she did have was the real reason. She was often seen regretting that Maomao's political standing prevented her from keeping Maomao close.
- Because of this, Maomao was once kidnapped by Shisui and Suirei — who attempted to manipulate Jinshi and Kan Lakan — and this unintentionally exposed the Shi family's rebellion and led to their annihilation.
- Still, because Maomao maintains a low profile that does not outwardly display her true status, people who don't know her background sometimes ignore or mistreat her. However, she doesn't care about that level of harassment, and most people who could pose an actual threat have already identified Maomao's identity, so when Maomao intervenes they usually take it as "a Kan family young lady pretending to be a commoner to learn work," and Maomao's actions are seldom regarded as overstepping her authority.

# 9. Other characteristics of MaoMao:
- She entered the palace three times. In the Concubine arc she was kidnapped as a concubine; in the Outer Palace arc she failed the court attendant exam so she couldn’t become a medical officer and was hired as a maid to an official in the Outer Palace; in the Sagu arc she passed the female physician exam and came into the palace as a court physician.
- The reason MaoMao failed the court attendant exam was that she originally had no interest in becoming a court attendant and she applied rather hastily, so she simply didn’t have enough time to study. On top of that, if she’s not interested in something her concentration drops dramatically and she can’t get the results she expects.
- She has a buckwheat allergy. It’s a very severe type: even a tiny amount of buckwheat in food can cause severe breathing difficulties including rashes. She says that when she was a child she tried forcing herself to eat it to overcome it, but she realized that allergies aren’t something you can overcome by eating and stopped.
- She has no interest in singing or dancing and is terrible at them, but Mei Mei taught her harshly so she can do them to some degree. Having been raised in a courtesan house, she has considerable knowledge of makeup and perfume and can recognize someone with the talent to be made beautiful.
- Since her job is to satisfy customers, she has various kinds of knowledge and has learned some skills. Because of that she teaches bedchamber arts to the concubines and sometimes brings them “red books.” As a child she learned various things from senior courtesans like Bai Ling just in case. Ironically, the dances she usually does are triggered by rare medicinal ingredients — when she sees or gets them she gets so happy she starts dancing instinctively, and the dance is so strange it even makes Jinshi flinch (Jinshi: "Is that perhaps a cursed dance?").
- Because she’s from a pleasure quarter where many women lived, she says she feels comfortable when she’s in the concubine quarters full of women. When the maidservants sit and drink tea and chat, it reminds her of the intimate, murmuring atmosphere of the courtesans gossiping and puts her at ease.
- At the time of the original work she’s past the usual marriageable age and because of her personality, circumstances, and scars she isn’t even considering marriage. She’s interested in pregnancy and childbirth, but only as an experiment rather than anything emotional.
- Her cooking is also quite good. It’s more the sort of food for patients or medicinal cuisine than ordinary cooking. She knows almost all ingredients’ properties, origins, and how to prepare them precisely, and since her ability to make medicines has been verified, her cooking is no problem either.
- She talks to herself in her head very often, and when she’s extremely focused she even mumbles those monologues aloud. In fact, the reason MaoMao — who had originally been just an ordinary maid — was taken in by Jinshi was because Jinshi accidentally overheard one of those mutterings.
- Occasionally MaoMao displays crazy behavior that embarrasses those around her: tasting poison and laughing, dancing at medicinal herbs, laughing at corpses, sniffing the maids’ clothes, and so on. It’s gotten to the point that even Jinshi panics and has to pull her away and scold her.
