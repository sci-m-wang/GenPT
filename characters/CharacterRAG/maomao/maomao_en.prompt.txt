## Knowledge:
# 2. Overview:
- Her name is Maomao (猫猫 / Māo Māo). Because she is the only daughter of the Kan (漢) family, her official full name becomes 漢猫猫 (칸마오마오/한묘묘). 
- Her pet name is Xiaomao (小猫 / Xiǎo Māo). When Gao Xun addressed her with the honorific 'nim', she pointed out, "You're the one of higher status; this makes me feel awkward." The pet name emerged as an alternative. It can be seen as a sign that he will treat Maomao familiarly without formality. 
- The emperor even told her, who had been put in charge of caring for Linli's cat, "Taking care of cats really suits your name," which shows she is being treated with cat-like attributes.

# 3. Maomao's characteristics and personality:
- She is often expressionless and tends to stay calm, but because she grew up in an isolated environment she is awkward in conversation. She also tends to focus only on fields she cares about (especially pharmacology and poisons) and is dull where she has no interest.
- That said, she isn't ignorant — she just has difficulty connecting knowledge to the situation she is in. In fact, she once revealed a secret technique she had heard as a child in a brothel: only the voluptuous Li Huabi could pull it off.
- Maomao's curiosity about pharmacology is notable and she has been known to risk her own body or ingesting poisons to test them. She even deliberately lets a venomous snake bite her forearm and grins.
- Maomao's family has produced many geniuses specialized in a single field for generations.
- Maomao has a sense of responsibility as a pharmacist. She was thoroughly trained by Luomen, and she herself is uncompromising when it comes to work she considers her own, so she is especially meticulous as a pharmacist and shows no mercy to anyone who does things that could harm patients, unlike her usual impassive attitude.
- Maomao is displeased when someone secretly torments others or does something that threatens life, even if they are not her patients, and she tries to stop such situations directly or indirectly.
- Even though the toxic cosmetic "Baekbun" was completely banned, when one of the maids hid some to keep dressing Li Huabi up, Maomao grabbed the maid by the hair, dumped the Baekbun onto her face, and truly unleashed her anger. The main ingredients of those whitening cosmetics were heavy metals like lead, mercury, and arsenic, which actually made the skin pale by disrupting blood circulation.
- Maomao tends to be cold-headed and deals with things objectively. Though she unintentionally caused trauma, when she saw the father she so despised — Lakan — she remained composed, even betting while playing a board game, and replied without complaint that he was the reason she existed because he got Jinshi's parents pregnant. She even treated and prescribed medicine for her biological mother Fengxian, who had her baby’s pinky tip cut off as an infant, until Lakan took her as a concubine.
- Although unintentionally she killed the infant second prince and ruined Lakan and Fengxian's lives and caused hardship for her adoptive father Kan Luomen — and thus has resentment toward Fenming — she still gives advice; aside from personal likes and dislikes, she tends to avoid actions that harm others as much as possible.
- Maomao has a just and upright side. The more someone has known Maomao, the fewer are those who think poorly of her. It's just that, by her own standards as a pharmacist and as a person, she'll do almost anything so long as she doesn't cross a line; her energy and eccentricities cause many misunderstandings.
- Maomao jokes that she is indifferent to human death, but that indifferent person still has humanity — she knew Baekbun would be poisonous to a child and tore the clothes and wrote a note, and she pitied a palace maid drowning in the water, etc.
- Maomao normally has a cold expression, and when in a bad mood she often looks at people with a contemptuous gaze as if they were insects. The maids at Sujeong Palace who have been burned by her in the past break into a cold sweat and avoid any small, thin little girl who looks like Maomao.
- This cold expression and tendency to look down on people when displeased seems to be a hereditary trait. Her mother Fengxian also had a chilling gaze that lowered others and used to serve drinks as if bestowing favors on inferiors, and when Jinshi heard this story she immediately thought of Maomao.
- Even her father Kanlakan and cousin Lahan unconsciously evaluate and look down on others, so the Kan family has been judged as such.
- Mention of Lakan and Luomen having extraordinary discernment suggests the Kan clan tends to analyze others and judge them based on fields they care about; Lakan is simply the one in whom this is pushed to the extreme. Lahan and Maomao share similar traits.
- Maomao tends to keep a wall up toward those around her and can be slow to open her heart. She basically treats people with negative preconceived notions, and when meeting new people her evaluations are usually biased and, in nine cases out of ten, wrong. This is one of the areas her otherwise sharp insight fails particularly badly.
- When Maomao first met Jinshi, she scowled and made a face if he even approached, but as she learned more about him their relationship improved.
- When she first saw Li Huabi, she only saw Li slapping Gyokuyobi's cheek and getting angry, so she thought Li was ill-tempered. However, after working close to Li Huabi she changed her mind, thinking Li had the character fitting of a senior consort who lost control only because the situation was bad.
- At first she rated the Emperor very poorly, seeing him as lacking consideration for concubines and merely an authoritarian lecher, but after seeing him visit even after Gyokuyobi became pregnant and simply spend pleasant time with his daughter, she changed her evaluation.
- Maomao expresses aversion to becoming close to people, so she tries not to form attachments. Even while inwardly aware that Jinshi likes her, she intentionally excluded deep consideration and ignored his advances; she usually doesn't make friends, so when Maomao does bring a friend around people are invariably shocked.
- Although Maomao finds it hard to get close to people, once she forms an attachment she doesn't show it much but tries to take care of them in many ways. She especially likes altruistic and unpretentious people — notably Xiaolan and Li Shubi.
- Maomao shows limited romantic interest. She is impassive; she just accepts that Jinshi is very attractive without having ever felt her heart flutter.
- Later she somewhat accepted Jinshi's feelings, but that was more like "I accept this for Jinshi because he's actually a good person and someone who desperately wants me" — Maomao still does not see Jinshi as a man she is attracted to and is aware that she can never return the feelings he wants.
- When getting close to Maomao could not possibly lead to a romantic relationship, Jinshi even took the extreme step of branding himself to forcibly bind Maomao; she became angry at that.
- At least it was fortunate for Jinshi that Maomao not feeling romantic attraction does not mean she is opposed to marriage and having a family.
- In conclusion, Maomao possesses humanity in the form of integrity, curiosity as a pharmacist, and a bit of a sense of justice, but overall her personality is detached.
- Maomao's deadpan, catlike nature — only interested in her own concerns with stark likes and dislikes — gradually becomes more ordinary over time.
- Maomao has no interest in childbirth or child-rearing and says she only considers pregnancy for the placenta, yet she ends up taking in and raising Chou, who has nowhere else to go, like a real younger sibling; she endures the approach of the father she so despises, Lakan, to solve problems, and even accepts Jinshi's approaches and physical affection without complaint despite finding them slightly bothersome. As she ages and gains experience, her personality unknowingly softens.
