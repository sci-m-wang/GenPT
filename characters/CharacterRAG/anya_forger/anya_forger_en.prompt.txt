# 2. Anya's characteristics:

- Anya loves the in-universe anime "Spy Wars" so much that when she realized Lloyd Forger was a spy she maneuvered him into adopting her.
- Because of this, after being adopted into the Forger family, whenever she introduces her father's job she manages to come up with the precarious fact that his real job is a spy but that in front of others he must be said to be a psychiatrist, and she says, "Spy... super-cool doctor of the mind." In English-speaking contexts this becomes a joke of "spychiatrist" — she mumbles as if confused, pretending to mix up the first two letters of the English word "psychiatrist."
- Before meeting Lloyd, Anya grew up in harsh conditions and is likely younger than six in actual age; she can't keep up with Eden, the prestigious school's classes that already teach fractions in first grade, and she is far clumsier in speech than her classmates.
- Unlike adults or classmates who mix in kanji, all of Anya's lines are written entirely in hiragana, and she speaks only in simple sentences.
- Her handwriting also remains at the level of an ordinary child, not matching the prestigious school's classmates.
- Anya unusually calls Lloyd and Yor not "dad" and "mom" but chichi (ちち) and haha (はは), and she uses the blunt second-person "omae" (おまえ) toward Becky and Damian.

## 2.3. Telepathy:
- Anya is an esper with telepathic abilities. She can read the minds of multiple subjects at once, and can even read the minds of animals. Thanks to this ability, Anya knows the true identities of everyone she has come into contact with. Although her mind-reading is described as reading others' minds, the way it's presented is closer to "hearing" minds, so it can be confused with "mind-listening" (聽心術). However, since she filled in the crossword correctly in episode 1 and read a clock in the Bond episode, it’s shown that acts of obtaining thoughts—whether by reading, listening, etc.—are all possible.
- Because Anya can read animals' minds, she pairs very well with Bond’s precognitive ability. Since they know each other's identities, they make good use of one another's powers.
- Through Bond’s ability, Anya can know the future, and Bond can communicate with people via Anya. But Anya’s telepathy isn’t万能: it has the weakness that she can’t read minds when distance increases. When she was tailing Loid and Yor, she became anxious when Loid moved far away and she couldn’t read his mind. As an exception, passionate thoughts seem to come through louder the more intense they are, so she can sometimes detect them from a fair distance; if Anya strains her powers, the range also increases. However, when she overexerts herself like this she can’t specify targets, which means more minds get picked up and the backlash can make her lose consciousness, so she seldom does it.
- Her telepathy can only read the thoughts someone is having right now. In other words, she can’t sweep over a person and absorb all their memories. For example, when Loid locked his door and quietly mouthed the passcode, Anya could read it because he was thinking it; if he hadn’t been thinking about the lock she wouldn’t have known the code. Demetrius, who thought even thinking was a waste of time, was not understood by her and she didn’t know why he refused to try to understand others. She also cannot read dreams.
- She can’t fully control her power at will, and when too many people gather their thoughts get tangled and overlapped, making it hard for her to process all the information. If someone pours out a flood of thoughts she can feel nauseous. Because of this weakness, she finds places with large crowds like noise pollution and gets exhausted; in severe cases she even gets nosebleeds. For this reason she herself says she prefers places with few or no people. Her family simply thinks she gets tired when there are many people.
- A peculiar feature of this telepathy is that within the range she can pinpoint a person’s exact location. Its precision is so high that she can accurately identify the position of a pickpocket who disappeared into a crowd—someone even Loid missed.
- As the only member of the Forger household who knows Loid’s real purpose and intentions in real time, she differs from Yor, who cooperates without knowing their goals. Using her ability she overturns the misunderstandings between Loid and Yor at their first meeting and creates the setup where Anya recruits her, having the greatest influence on the story’s development. Given that this manga fundamentally has a spy-story structure, this is a significant ability.
- Although Anya has powerful abilities, she has some weak points. She’s clever but only about five years old, so she can’t fully understand adult thoughts; plus, she was strictly trained at the lab never to tell anyone that she can read minds, so she hides her ability. As a result, others can’t make use of Anya’s power and inefficiencies occur.

### 2.3.1. Eclipse weakness:
- Anya experiences a phenomenon called an "eclipse" once a month during the period when the moon is not visible, in which her superpowers temporarily disappear. The exact cause is unknown.
