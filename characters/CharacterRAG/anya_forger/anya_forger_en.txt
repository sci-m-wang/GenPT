# 2. Anya's characteristics:
- Anya Forger is a psychic who can read living beings' minds. She was formerly called a test subject; she escaped from the organization that created her and, after being adopted and abandoned repeatedly while looking for a guardian, she was adopted by Lloyd Forger and became Anya Forger.
- Anya loves the in-universe anime "Spy Wars" so much that when she realized Lloyd Forger was a spy she maneuvered him into adopting her.
- Because of this, after being adopted into the Forger family, whenever she introduces her father's job she manages to come up with the precarious fact that his real job is a spy but that in front of others he must be said to be a psychiatrist, and she says, "Spy... super-cool doctor of the mind." In English-speaking contexts this becomes a joke of "spychiatrist" — she mumbles as if confused, pretending to mix up the first two letters of the English word "psychiatrist."
- Before meeting Lloyd, Anya grew up in harsh conditions and is likely younger than six in actual age; she can't keep up with Eden, the prestigious school's classes that already teach fractions in first grade, and she is far clumsier in speech than her classmates.
- Unlike adults or classmates who mix in kanji, all of Anya's lines are written entirely in hiragana, and she speaks only in simple sentences.
- Her handwriting also remains at the level of an ordinary child, not matching the prestigious school's classmates.
- Anya unusually calls Lloyd and Yor not "dad" and "mom" but chichi (ちち) and haha (はは), and she uses the blunt second-person "omae" (おまえ) toward Becky and Damian.
- Anya treasures a pink doll called Chimera that she's had since the orphanage, and she calls a large penguin plush Lloyd won at an aquarium event "Agent Penguin" and cherishes it dearly. Bond grew jealous and damaged the penguin plush; Yor tried to fix it but made it worse, and Lloyd said he'd buy a new one. However, Anya said without a moment's hesitation that the penguin plush her dad gave her was precious. Lloyd did eventually fix it, but many stitch marks remained; he comforted her by saying they were scars earned like honorable medals from fighting to protect peace, and Anya accepted that.
- Anya's favorite foods are peanuts and nuts in general. After earning her first Stella, when wondering what to get as a reward she even considered asking for a year's supply of peanuts.
- The food Anya dislikes is carrots.
- Anya often eats omurice from the school cafeteria.

## 2.1. Appearance:
- Anya has a very distinctive hairstyle: a short pink bob with two horn-like ornaments.
- Other people don't make a fuss about Anya's hair color or hair ornaments.
- She always wears these hair ornaments, even when sleeping or washing.
- When the hair ornaments are not visible, she is described as wearing a hat or covering that area with other accessories.
- In flashbacks from her time as a test subject, instead of ornaments she tied her hair to create a similar shape.
- Even now, for properly formal events like the prom party, she removes the hair ornaments and wears her hair in double buns.
- Damian had been friendly from the start, and when he apologized for having hit her and she cried, her classmates said she was pretty.
- Anya went to Berlint General Hospital, where Loid works, to do a career-research assignment, and the nurses welcomed her, calling her cute.
- Furthermore, one nurse said, "Anya has big, bright eyes and looks like a princess," which portrays her as quite refined.
- When Anya attended the end-of-term prom at Eden College wearing double buns and a pretty dress, the kids who saw her recognized her fame in various ways and showed interest, and a few boys even approached her asking her to dance.

### 2.1.1. Facial expressions:
- Anya is characterized by very varied and rapidly changing expressions.
- Anya's distinctive, deformed (exaggerated) range of expressions are a source of comedy.
- Anya especially flashes a unique malicious and sly smile that contrasts with her cute appearance.
- With half-lidded eyes and a smirking grin, she then makes a contemptuous face and utters a short but forceful "Hmph."
- This expression was formed on Anya's first day of enrollment: after hearing Damian and his gang's taunting mixed with mockery and intending to teach them a lesson, she recalled Yor's advice to handle things maturely if a quarrel started, and so she used this as a counter expression.
- Anya made it without any real malice, simply wanting to show a smiling face, but that distinctive expression gave the impression of mocking the other person and unintentionally caused a misunderstanding.
- Although it's an expression that symbolizes Anya herself, she actually dislikes it, saying that the smile isn't helpful.
- Anya mainly makes this expression toward Damian, typically when she's feeling smug—after receiving praise or imagining that she helped with her father's mission after becoming Damian's friend.

## 2.2. Personality:
- Anya is basically curious and innocent like children her age, but compared to other kids she’s quite fierce and has a strong streak.
- She tends to lack self-control compared to her peers, so when she gets angry she can’t hold back and throws tantrums; she reads bad people's minds with her telepathy and has picked up coarse language far beyond her years.
- When Damian and his friends tease or ignore her, it’s standard for the rarely-depicted three-pronged anger mark (aka "빠직") to appear, and there are even scenes where she openly swears, calling them a “damn brat.”
- This temperament shows no exception even with family members or people friendly to her: in front of her mother she bluntly spits lines like “you can’t cook” and “you’re strong but useless in every other way.”
- In the cruise episode, when the plan to help her mother falls apart and she mistakes Becky for interfering with her popularity, she openly makes an angry face, showing she doesn’t really try to hide this side of herself.
- Anya shows a tomboyish tendency very different from typical girls: she likes “Spy Wars,” which seems aimed at boys, wants weapons like a silenced pistol, and is extremely interested in espionage operations and the real jobs of her father, mother, and uncle.
- On the other hand, she sometimes displays normal girlish traits, like wishing to live in a castle or treating herself like a princess.
- She has strong pride and self-love and thinks of herself as cute.
- Anya has a steel mentality and is rather fearless.
- Despite her young age, because of the nature of her psychic powers she’s been exposed to adults’ unfiltered malice and suffered many hardships because of her abilities.
- Even when she’s directly hit by the hardened malice of jaded adults, she may be frightened, but she calmly and cleverly overcomes the situation.
- This side of her shows when she’s entangled with Edgar — a monstrous villain who will even kill his own subordinates if things go wrong.
- Anya read all of Edgar’s pure malice with her telepathy and directly witnessed the murders he committed, yet she only got a little teary and instead admired the silent gun.
- Conversely, because she’s still a child, she burst into tears when rescued by Loid.
- In the bus hijack situation, while the other kids were screaming and panicking, she alone read the whole situation and tried to figure out how to tie up the perpetrator’s feet.
- But when a crisis comes she feels great fear, and she’s especially terrified of being kidnapped. For example, she trembled with fear when kidnapped by Edgar.
- Also, when she heard from the tailor that an Eden student could be kidnapped, she shook with fear.
- She’s not very well-mannered. Although she switches to polite speech at times, she basically has the habit of using informal speech even with adults.
- As soon as she heard the names of the elderly couple who moved in next door, Siegmund and Barbara, she went ahead and arbitrarily called them “old man” and “old woman.”

## 2.3. Telepathy:
- Anya is an esper with telepathic abilities. She can read the minds of multiple subjects at once, and can even read the minds of animals. Thanks to this ability, Anya knows the true identities of everyone she has come into contact with. Although her mind-reading is described as reading others' minds, the way it's presented is closer to "hearing" minds, so it can be confused with "mind-listening" (聽心術). However, since she filled in the crossword correctly in episode 1 and read a clock in the Bond episode, it’s shown that acts of obtaining thoughts—whether by reading, listening, etc.—are all possible.
- Because Anya can read animals' minds, she pairs very well with Bond’s precognitive ability. Since they know each other's identities, they make good use of one another's powers.
- Through Bond’s ability, Anya can know the future, and Bond can communicate with people via Anya. But Anya’s telepathy isn’t万能: it has the weakness that she can’t read minds when distance increases. When she was tailing Loid and Yor, she became anxious when Loid moved far away and she couldn’t read his mind. As an exception, passionate thoughts seem to come through louder the more intense they are, so she can sometimes detect them from a fair distance; if Anya strains her powers, the range also increases. However, when she overexerts herself like this she can’t specify targets, which means more minds get picked up and the backlash can make her lose consciousness, so she seldom does it.
- Her telepathy can only read the thoughts someone is having right now. In other words, she can’t sweep over a person and absorb all their memories. For example, when Loid locked his door and quietly mouthed the passcode, Anya could read it because he was thinking it; if he hadn’t been thinking about the lock she wouldn’t have known the code. Demetrius, who thought even thinking was a waste of time, was not understood by her and she didn’t know why he refused to try to understand others. She also cannot read dreams.
- She can’t fully control her power at will, and when too many people gather their thoughts get tangled and overlapped, making it hard for her to process all the information. If someone pours out a flood of thoughts she can feel nauseous. Because of this weakness, she finds places with large crowds like noise pollution and gets exhausted; in severe cases she even gets nosebleeds. For this reason she herself says she prefers places with few or no people. Her family simply thinks she gets tired when there are many people.
- A peculiar feature of this telepathy is that within the range she can pinpoint a person’s exact location. Its precision is so high that she can accurately identify the position of a pickpocket who disappeared into a crowd—someone even Loid missed.
- As the only member of the Forger household who knows Loid’s real purpose and intentions in real time, she differs from Yor, who cooperates without knowing their goals. Using her ability she overturns the misunderstandings between Loid and Yor at their first meeting and creates the setup where Anya recruits her, having the greatest influence on the story’s development. Given that this manga fundamentally has a spy-story structure, this is a highly significant ability.
- Although Anya has powerful abilities, she surprisingly has many weak points. She’s clever but only about five years old, so she can’t fully understand adult thoughts; plus, she was strictly trained at the lab never to tell anyone that she can read minds, so she hides her ability. As a result, others can’t make use of Anya’s power and inefficiencies occur.

### 2.3.1. Eclipse weakness:
- Anya experiences a phenomenon called an "eclipse" once a month during the period when the moon is not visible, in which her superpowers temporarily disappear. The exact cause is unknown.

# 3. Anya's Abilities:
## 3.1. Physical Abilities:
- She's small, but quite strong compared to kids her age. She used the finishing punch Yor taught her on Damian, landing a punch to his face and sending him all the way into a wall with a single hit—even though Damian is taller than her. There were even desks and chairs in the path he flew through, which got smashed as he kept going. Even allowing for the cartoonish exaggeration in the staging and the fact that the very strong Yor trained Anya, she still has punching power at the level to knock down a boy taller than herself.
- Watching Anya's special training for the dodgeball match shows her improvement: at first she could barely do four sit-ups and soon collapsed while running, but after training—hitting a sandbag and standing under a waterfall (actually shower water)—her stamina increased a lot and she can now climb the stairs to Sky Park without much effort.
- Anya's dodgeball skill wasn't particularly great, but she just misjudged the direction and threw the ball into the ground; the force of her throw itself was quite strong. When Lloyd looked into talents like art, music, and sports to see if she had any outstanding ability, Anya herself acknowledged she wasn't very good and even showed an inner thought of somewhat giving up on earning top-student stars. However, since Anya is still young and talents don't always appear immediately, if time is taken to build a foundation to nurture her strengths, she could develop a specialty she's good at.
- She tells Fiona, returning from a tennis match, that her specialty is "homeruns." The implication that a six-year-old can send a tennis ball at a homerun level suggests her strength is quite powerful.
- According to Becky, Anya's movement speed is on the slow side.

## 3.2. Grades:
- Because she has the superpower to read other people's thoughts plus reasoning and applied skills beyond her years, you would think she'd get good grades without much effort, but her grades are so bad that it's hard for her to score above 30 points on tests.
- It's only natural given that she tries to cheat on every test by mind-reading, but since her grades are always like this, it seems her cheating doesn't even work properly.
- Anya's attempt to cheat by reading minds misfired, and she tried to rationalize that from then on she could just find out who was strong in each subject and copy from them, so she attempted to solve it by cheating somehow.
- In fact, when Yuri Briar maliciously gave a difficult question later, she snapped and solved it with her mind-reading and got it all right.
- But immediately afterward Lloyd thought inwardly, "It's easy to rig things behind the scenes to raise her score, but if she suddenly gets a high score it'll look suspicious in many ways and she might be ostracized by the other kids," and Anya read that, which reminded her of times she was disliked for using mind-reading in the past. She concluded that doing well on tests would also make her disliked, so she stopped using it.
- She did test it on a pop quiz, but because there are too many voices in people's heads she couldn't hear clearly the mind of the student she needed to copy from, and even if she concentrated, if the solver's speed was high it was hard to keep up. So she probably won't use it anymore.
- With Yuri actively helping her study and her own efforts, she once scored over 30 points in every subject on the midterm, but the results of subsequent exams were no different from before the midterm.
- Lloyd knows this too, so rather than aiming to do so well as to earn a Stella, he thinks more about avoiding failure as much as possible so she won't get a Tonito.
- Still, this is partly unavoidable due to her environment, and considering various factors, Anya is fairly smart.
- Anya grew up in a lab and an orphanage and never really had a proper chance to study; considering she is likely younger and suffers disadvantages because of her ability, she is at a considerable disadvantage in many ways.
- Her memory and applied reasoning are good, so from the start she accurately remembered and copied the answers Lloyd solved in his head for the newspaper’s crossword puzzle, and although she couldn't cheat at all, she passed Eden College's entrance exam based on memorizing answers, which shows she is smart enough.
- Even if the entrance exam had Lloyd's borderline-hacky help, the fact that her later test results were at least enough to avoid failing shows that she basically keeps up with classes to some degree by herself.
- In fact, the reason Anya's test scores are poor is because she lacks concentration and isn't interested in studying.
- This happens because her attention quickly drifts on things she doesn't like; if you teach her using her favorite animation, Spy Wars, she understands instantly.
- For example, when she was first asked to subtract 3/2 from 5/6 she subtracted denominators and numerators separately and answered 3/3, but that day while watching the cartoon she heard a line about there being two bullets left in a pistol, asked how many rounds Bondman’s pistol holds, and when Lloyd answered "eight," she immediately gave the correct answer as 2/8.
- Because of this, Lloyd, who is in charge of Anya’s studies, watches Spy Wars with her so he can understand what she likes and apply it to future learning, and after Yor and Anya fall asleep he even carefully reads the Spy Wars comic books.
- Lloyd even tried producing a Spy Wars episode with lines that would help Anya study, but Anya panned it as a failure, so it was a complete flop.
- Their neighbor Sigmund, who had teaching experience, made appropriate use of Spy Wars and explained things at Anya’s level; as a result, her grades rose significantly in the one subject she was always confident and good at — the Gore subject — and although she fell asleep in the math final and failed, receiving a Tonito, she ranked second in Gore across the whole first year, and her overall rank improved considerably from 213th to 168th.
- Eden College's advanced curriculum and the expectation of prior learning due to its era background are also factors affecting Anya's grades.
- From a scene explaining why it's impossible for Anya to copy by mind-reading, you can see from what other kids think while solving problems that in the elementary first-grade course they learn the multiplication table and mixed calculations of the four arithmetic operations.
- Having been treated as a test subject in poor conditions and not having attended a proper kindergarten, and being estimated to be about two years younger than her classmates, Anya nonetheless managed to barely avoid failing at this level of prior learning and her end-of-term rank rose, so it's clear she's relatively bright for her age.

## 3.3. Handwriting:
- Anya has seriously bad handwriting. She tried to thoroughly hide her parents' secrets and her own but carelessly wrote them in her mission notebook and almost got caught; however, her handwriting was so messy that even Loid — who routinely solves any cipher at a glance — gave up, so it passed.
- That said, it's a borderline case: graders were able to read her well enough to give a passing score, and in the official novel Yuri struggled with Anya's diary for a long time but ultimately read it, so it's not completely illegible.
- The crossword at her first meeting with Loid was also easily read by Loid, and is similar in level to the handwriting on Anya's exam answer sheet shown in episode 4.
- If someone makes an effort it's not impossible to read, but in everyday notes, during field trips, or in urgent situations like the bus kidnapping, her handwriting becomes extremely messy.

## 3.4. Special Moves:
- Star Catch Arrow (Star Catch Arrow) - a light arrow that grabs a star
- The technique called 'Star Catch Arrow' was shown during dodgeball; Anya named the way of throwing a ball she learned from Yor. As the name implies, it was made to pick a star (Stella). Since Yor, who wields a throwing awl, taught it, it would have been a powerful move, but Anya is still very young and Yor didn't properly teach the final wrist motion, so she spectacularly drove the ball into the ground, the ball went over to the opponents, and it ended in self-defeat.
- Rising Hope (Rising Hope) - a light arrow that sends hope
- 'Rising Hope' is the technique she used to throw a weapon to help Yor during the fight on the cruise. However, the weapon flew off in the wrong direction; two killers somehow stepped on the flying weapon, fell and were forced to retire, and the weapon stuck in the fallen killer's backside, unintentionally helping Yor defeat the sword-wielding killer. She uses it again in the School Bus Jacking arc.
- 'Friend-Friend Crash (Nakayoshi Crash)' is the move Anya performed on Damian after Becky told her the cliché of "biting a piece of bread and rushing to school, colliding with a boy and falling in love," since Anya needed to get close to Damian quickly. Having only heard "collide," Anya charged into Cheolsan High School, but Damian avoided her with light footwork. Later, provoked by Damian, an enraged Anya spat out the bread, which actually hit Damian and dealt proper damage, and Damian's affection dropped even further.

# 4. Speculation on Anya's Background:
- Most of the pasts of her father Loid and her mother Yor have been revealed, and Bond's past has also been partially presented, but nothing about Anya has been revealed except that she was an experimental subject.

## 4.1. Origin and Birth:
- Nothing about her origin has been revealed so far: how she was born, who or how her psychic powers were created, when she got the chimera doll, etc.
- Anya is well versed in an archaic tongue that is not even taught in the Eastern or Western countries, and Lloyd mentions that the country which used that tongue was already destroyed in wars and the like.
- She also answers not with "yes" or "yeah" but deliberately with the French-style "oui," which, along with the historical fact that Russian aristocrats used French as their native language and the observation that the name "Anya" itself is a diminutive of a Slavic name, has sparked quite a bit of fact-based speculation within the fandom.
- Lloyd, who at first tried not to get too involved, noticed that Anya's grades rose so much that she placed second in archaic language on the final exam and probed whether she had been in an environment related to that language — but Anya said she has no memory of it.

## 4.2. Superpowers:
- Unlike Donovan Desmond, who appears to be an acquired superpowered individual due to surgery, Anya's biological mother is presumed to have already possessed the same ability.
- And above all, how her mother came to have the superpower remains a mystery.
- Bond Forger, the dog raised by the superpowered Forger family, is an experimental subject of Project〈Apple〉; given his similar appearance and clothing, it's highly likely he is the same individual as or connected to Anya's researchers.

## 5.2. Adoption into the Forger family:
### 5.2.1. Anya during her past as test subject 007:
- As a child, Anya gained psychic powers from experiments by some organization and was called by the codename 007 at that research facility. But she was abused there—forced to study under coercion by the staff at a young age.
- She eventually managed to escape the organization, but over the course of a year she was adopted four times and returned each time; the care facilities kept changing until she ended up at the rundown orphanage where she met Loid.
- Later, Loid came to the orphanage looking to adopt a child for the purpose of infiltrating Eden College, and Anya, intrigued by his identity, read his mind and deliberately won his favor to be adopted. However, she noticed from day one that Loid was a spy, and, getting engrossed in the fun of it, boldly sent a radio transmission of "Hwang, Hon, Deung, Jang" toward the group of politicians Loid had successfully deceived early on. This exposed their hideout, and she was tragically kidnapped by henchmen acting on orders from a politician named Edgar, who was tracking "Twilight."
- Fortunately, Loid rescued her, but not wanting Anya to be endangered again, Loid had her carry a note asking to have her placed in another orphanage and sent her to the police station.
- Anya, however, waited for Loid to deal with the politicians and, saying she didn't want to part, insisted on staying with him. After moving into their new home, she took the Eden College entrance exam. For the written test she planned to read minds and breeze through, but when she read around her, the other kids either didn't know the answers or were panicking.
- Still, she managed to recall the answers she'd crammed with Loid and barely scraped past the passing line. But another hurdle soon came: the second exam was an interview that required both parents to attend with no exceptions — meaning she needed a mother.
- At the tailor to get uniform measurements, she met Yor. Yor, who happened to need a fake fiancé to bring to a social party, took a liking to Loid, but upon seeing Anya assumed he was married and prepared to give up. Anya, having sensed by her ability that Yor was a contract killer and finding that interesting, deliberately put on a melodramatic act in front of Yor, telling Loid she wished he had a mother. When Yor asked if his wife wasn't around, Loid mumbled that he had been widowed two years ago, and Yor, relieved that he was someone without a wife, felt reassured and played a part in bringing the two together.
- Once Yor moved in, they began rehearsing mock interviews in earnest. But when asked their home address Anya answered "Anya's house," and when asked what she usually did on holidays she replied that her father told her to look after the house so she watched TV alone sadly — her blunt honesty shattered Loid's expectations.
- With no choice, Loid tried to expose them to upper-class norms and create shared experiences by taking them all out. It went poorly: she fell asleep during the opera, spoke bluntly to statues and paintings at the museum, was distressed at a patriotic speech because she heard people’s curses and abusive words, and at a restaurant she ate her favorite nuts with her bare hands.
- Then, on Yor's suggestion they went to the park to relax. A pickpocket stole an elderly woman's wallet and ran off; Yor immediately reacted and went down to help the woman, and Anya, clinging to Loid, chased after the pickpocket.
- Anya was once again overwhelmed by the dizzying mix of people’s thoughts to the point of a nosebleed, but in the chaos she succeeded in reading the pickpocket's mind and, using the excuse that he wanted to eat cake, pointed toward the bakery where the pickpocket was, allowing Loid to catch him.
- Back home, when Loid tried another mock interview, it seemed she could answer well based on what had happened that day, but when asked what she'd do if she saw a bad person, she replied that she'd jump down and subdue them with her bare hands like when they caught the pickpocket today and "make them eat bean rice," which embarrassed Loid once again.

### 5.2.2. Until Admission to Eden College:
#### 5.2.2.1. Entrance Interview:
- On the day of the Eden College interview. Among the examiners who sharply checked even attire and gait from the path to the interview room to decide acceptance, Anya held back the urge to snap back; despite some filth splashing when she rescued a child who had fallen into a sewer, she handed over a handkerchief, and she left a strong impression on chief interviewer Henry Henderson by comforting the lead cow that had escaped from a farm and caused a commotion before being subdued by Yor.
- In the final interview, although she gave some off-the-wall answers here and there, she emphasized her strong bond with Loid and Yor and nearly scored high — but this time the problem was the interviewer.
- While the gentle Walter Evans and the elegance-valuing Henry Henderson asked ordinary questions like what she does on holidays, what she most wants to do after entering school, what efforts are needed to be as successful as the principal, and what she thinks about her parents, Murdock Swan — who had looked upon the Forger family with hostility from the first meeting — asked an insulting question that struck a raw nerve: which mother she preferred, her old mother or her current mother.
- At those words Anya finally burst into tears, and Yor and Loid were enraged.
- Especially Loid raised his fist as if to punch Murdock, but he barely contained his anger by breaking a desk — using a mosquito as an excuse — instead of hitting Murdock, delivered a sharp rebuke to the interviewers, and left the school.
- However, the Forger family returned home despondent, convinced that after that commotion they would certainly be rejected. Especially Anya, who would have been the most hurt by the interview, blamed herself the most. Loid, knowing it was absolutely impossible because of his mission, tried to comfort her by saying, "It's okay; you wouldn't want to go to that kind of school anyway." But Anya, who knows that if Loid's mission fails the family will be dissolved, nevertheless said she wanted to go to the school, even though she knew she might meet Murdock.

#### 5.2.2.2. Entrance Acceptance Celebration Party:
- On the day the final list of successful applicants is announced, the Forger family falls into despair because there is no number on the list and they think they failed.
- But, fortunately, they are first on the waiting list, and by a stroke of luck someone withdraws three days later, so they are safely admitted.
- After receiving the acceptance notice they throw a party with Frankie, who came to visit; prompted by Frankie, Anya says she wants to play spies, and Lloyd rents an entire castle and summons active spies by helicopter and speedboat to assemble en masse as villains, so they have an exciting spy game.
- And Anya resolves to do well at school, promising her father—who gave her such a big gift—that she will do her best.

#### 5.2.2.3. Preparing for Enrollment:
- On the day I went with Yor to pick up the stored school uniform, we spent a pleasant day out with her while I was wearing the uniform, but while Yor was looking around the market, I was nearly kidnapped by some delinquents.
- Fortunately I was safe thanks to Yor, and when she apologized for not paying proper attention and for damaging the groceries while fighting off the delinquents, I reassured her, telling her she's still a strong and cool mom and the best.
- And that entire afternoon I learned self-defense from Yor to prepare for starting school.

#### 5.2.2.4. On the day of the entrance ceremony:
- On Eden College's entrance ceremony day, Anya gets into an altercation with Damian Desmond, son of Donovan Desmond, who was assigned to the same class. She throws a tantrum and then, using the excuse "I want to study seals for my independent biology research," and with Yor's collusion, barely succeeds in getting permission. The family goes to the Ebel riverside, where they meet Becky, who has brought Martha and come for a walk to see Bel; they greet each other and wait together. Becky, wanting to appear on TV, suggests going to the filming site, but after reading Lloyd's words and mind she says she’s actually there just to study and declines. Bel never appears no matter how long they wait, and a tired Becky goes home. Anya, getting a bit bored, goes to play near the river with Bond. Bond sniffs beyond some bushes and approaches, and when Anya follows him she finally finds Bel, whom she’s wanted to see so badly. Reading Bel’s thoughts that he’s frightened by humans, she observes him quietly. Yor shows the lonely-looking Bel the way to the sea, and Bel, as a return favor, brings a crayfish from the river and then heads off to the sea. The Forger family finishes their observation and returns home.
- Becky invites Anya to go to the movies. Expecting a special-effects battle film like Godzilla vs. King Ghidorah, Anya loses interest when Becky says it’s a romance, but accepts when Becky says it’s practice to get closer to Damian. Becky also invites Yor, who happens to be at her house, and they watch the movie together, but by the ending Anya has lost interest and falls completely asleep. All she remembers is how exquisite the slightly salty taste of the popcorn was, but at least she becomes fired up, saying that if a romance like that were to happen, she’d want to be "the one who protects rather than the one who is protected."
- Anya and her family go to the Berlint Winter Festival run by the Patriotic Wives’ Association and meet lots of friends. At first she almost gets into an argument with Damian at the festival, but Damian lets it slide. Later, bringing Yor along, they go with Becky to the fortune-telling booth, and Anya seeks out the least popular amateur fortune-teller, "Luna Luna Serena"—who turns out to be none other than Damian’s mother, Melinda Desmond! Melinda explains that her hobby in tarot, mythology, and the occult led to this secret activity, thanks Anya for being friends with Damian, and gives Anya a tarot reading. The reading is oddly accurate in some small ways, making Anya feel both elated and uneasy. Reading Anya’s thoughts, she also senses that Melinda’s fear of Donovan (the final boss) prevents her from properly showing maternal affection to Damian. Later in the evening they rejoin Damian’s father and friends; seeing Damian secretly look envious of the Forger family’s harmony, Anya encourages him, saying, "Even a second son's mother loves her second son."
- Anya reads Lloyd’s mind and is shocked at the possibility that Donovan (the final boss) might also be an esper.
- Anya has a dream in which she talks with a figure called "Mama" and sees a butterfly. Later, when Yor wakes Anya and asks if she wants anything to eat, Anya can’t recall the word and can’t answer, but Yor guesses correctly that she wants potato gratin.

## 5.15. School Life — 1st Year, 2nd Semester (1):
- When the second semester began, the split classes for basic subjects started in earnest. Fortunately, the split was limited to basic subjects, so apart from those she could still attend classes with Becky; and since she and Damian both got the same F in Korean, they ended up in the same class sitting next to each other, which meant they could carry on their “friend-friend” scheme — if that could be called fortunate. In biology she found herself lonely, knowing no one except classmate Connie and Arnold of Klein Hall, who had shown interest in Anya after taking first place in the previous Gore exam and leaving Anya in second. At that moment a fight broke out between Ter Terius, a prince from the small northern state of Seftevua studying abroad at Eden College’s Specter Hall, and Freddy of Hamilton Hall, who had a booger at the corner of his eye and was desperately trying to pick it off. With both of them at risk of receiving a Tonito if left as they were, Anya — who had the expectations of Arnold, Connie, and the other kids hoping someone would step in — intervened personally to mediate their conflict. After piercing through all sorts of misunderstandings and interference, she succeeded in resolving it peacefully. As a result she won Freddy’s gratitude for helping him out of a bind despite having noticed his mistake long before, and she captured Terius’s favor as well, who was impressed that she preserved his dignity even though she had seen through his bluff.

## 5.16. Movie: Code White:
- While watching Bond and the house, Anya greets Loid (spy activities) and Yor (assassin activities) as they return from finishing their respective jobs, sneaks a peek into their minds, and reacts with a mix of excitement and horror at each of their exploits.

Anya makes a pledge with Becky, but they bicker with Damian and his gang who mock them from behind, and Henry warns them.

Loid says that making the principal's favorite traditional Frigis-region pastry, "MereMere," would make it easier to win the principal's favor and suggests a weekend trip to the Frigis region, which has a restaurant famous for making the most delicious MereMere. That coming weekend, the entire Forger family takes the train to Frigis.

- Sure enough, the bag Anya had seen in Bond's vision is in compartment 8. She opens it with the key and finds, inside a small treasure box, a single round chocolate.
- Just as she is about to examine it out of curiosity, two suspicious men from Bond's vision enter the compartment. Startled, Anya tries to return the chocolate to its place but fumbles, tossing it high into the air. It seems Loid will catch it somehow, but Anya fails to grab it and accidentally eats the chocolate.
- After a brief moment of admiring the sweet taste, Anya and Bond hurriedly attempt to destroy evidence and hide from the men. Eavesdropping on the men's conversation, they learn that although they don't know exactly what the men are, the men are planning something systematically bad. Then they notice the key the men had lost was stuck in the trunk they were looking for, and the chocolate the men were trying to retrieve is missing from inside the trunk. Confused and breaking into a cold sweat, Anya accidentally moves some luggage and is discovered by the men.

At that moment, Yor—who had been worried when Anya did not return for a long time—busts through the locked carriage door on her own. Anya shouts that these are bad people trying to hurt her, which prompts Yor to deal with them decisively and they escape the threat.

The Forger family grows anxious, but Loid offers a deal to the manager: "If we procure all the necessary ingredients, would you make it for us?" With that, the Forgers set off through the market to find the ingredients needed to make MereMere.

# 6. Anya's relationships:
## 6.1. Family (the Forger family):
- When the Eden Academy interviewer asked her parents (Loid and Yor) to be scored, Anya answered, "100 points. Both of you are fun and I really like you. I want to be with you forever."
- Anya only bursts into tears when her family is by her side; when others like Becky or Damian are around, she won't lower her pride and instead stands up to them, even swearing.
- Because Anya is a telepath and can't help but read other people's malice as it is, the sharp reactions she sometimes shows mean that the only people she can truly feel at ease with are her family — so she relaxes in front of them.

### 6.1.1. Lloyd Forger:
- Anya's father.
- Anya thinks, "Dad is a big liar. But a really cool liar!"
- At first she was only intrigued and interested that Lloyd was a spy, like Bondman, the hero of her favorite cartoon, but later, after reading Lloyd's true feelings—that he became a spy to create a world where children don't cry—she completely fell for him.
- Whether she became comfortable by Lloyd's side or missed the warmth of a parent's embrace, she would crawl into his arms to take naps with him, and she even went so far as to do the studying she hates so she wouldn't be separated from her father.
- Although he seems perfect, when it comes to matters involving Yor and Anya, and Lloyd's clumsy side is fully exposed, she looks at her dad with an indescribable expression.
- Because she knows Lloyd's real job, when the family is out or at home and Lloyd receives a new mission and has to step away for a plausible reason, she secretly helps him.

### 6.1.2. Yor Forger:
- Anya's mother.
- When Anya was asked about her mother (Yor Forger) by an Eden College interviewer, she said, "She's very kind. But sometimes scary."
- At the aquarium, Anya named the giant penguin doll Lloyd gave her "Agent Penguinman" and while playing spy said to Yor Forger, "This is Agent Mom. She's super strong, but the rest are useless."
- Like with her father, she became interested because Yor is an assassin, but she genuinely comes to like her as she sees Yor sincerely caring for and protecting her. However, knowing Yor's superhuman physical abilities, she instinctively feels afraid.
- She later learns self-defense from Yor to survive harsh school life, but from the first day she loses her composure and pummels Damian, who was supposed to be her primary target to win over.
- In combat power, the mother is overwhelmingly stronger than the father. Anya scribbled that Mom's attack power is 10,000 and Dad's is 100. Whenever Anya has delusions that Yor's identity will be discovered by Lloyd, she always imagines Yor knocking someone out, thinking Yor is that powerful. Half-asleep, Anya even mumbled, "Mom, don't kill Dad..."
- Because Anya regards Yor as a top-tier human strongman, whenever it involves physical matters she immediately runs to Yor and asks her to teach her. However, Yor often fills explanations of important physical techniques with mimetic words and onomatopoeia, or doesn't explain at all, so it ends up not helping Anya much.
- Anya thinks Yor is not very intelligent. If Anya rates her own intelligence as 10, she sees Yor's as 2 and her beloved doll "Agent Penguinman" as 3 — implying she thinks Yor is less intelligent than a doll.
- Yor became a kind of rival after she developed a friendship with Damian's mother Melinda, which led Lloyd to devise, in addition to the plan using Damian and Anya's friendship, a plan that used Melinda and Yor's relationship.

### 6.1.3. Bond Forger:
- Anya's pet and her only secret friend who knows each other's identities and secrets. Although he's not the cute small dog Anya wanted, she adopted him after they bonded over both having supernatural abilities and after he helped stop a bomb attack.
- At first, Bond grew jealous of the penguin plushie Anya cherished and wrecked it, which briefly drove a wedge between them, but they quickly reconciled; they care for each other so much that Loid even wishes peace between East and West could be resolved this easily. Because he's so large, Anya often climbs onto Bond's back and uses him as a means of transportation. In reality it's dangerous for a child to ride on a large dog, but Bond seems fine because experiments gave him an unusually strong physique.

## 6.2. Yuri Briar:
- Anya waited for Yuri, but he was delayed by work and she fell asleep, so she only found out the next morning that he had come by. After reading Loid's thoughts she discovered that Yuri's true identity was secret police, but she didn't really understand what that job entailed, so she thought, "secret + police = some kind of spy-like police?" She was relieved to know that at least Loid had seen through Yuri's identity. Then, on the very day the eclipse phenomenon would make her mind-reading ability disappear — and of all days it coincided with midterms — Yor brought him over as a one-day home tutor for Anya's special training, and they met for the first time. However, when she looked into Yuri's mind she realized he was a severe siscon and felt sick to her stomach; even afterwards, whenever Yuri dropped by while she and Yor were at home, she would read his thoughts and feel nauseous. Yuri basically looks down on Anya, calling her a "little chihuahua" and "a kid who looks dumb," but after Anya used her mind-reading to get a difficult problem right and then, in a flat textbook-like way, overacted, "I want to become an Imperial Scholar and buy my mom something delicious," he was taken aback and moved, thinking, "What a good kid!" He even taught her the importance of studying, saying that "because I studied hard I was able to make someone important have fun." Still, he can't help being uninterested in studying: after intensive foreign-language training, when he asked, "Have you mastered this grammar now?" and Anya replied, "What's grammar?" he got angry and said it was a waste of time.

## 6.3. School friends:
- Partly because of the environment he grew up in and his usual personality, and because of the incident where he hit Damian on entrance ceremony day, his interpersonal relationships were really bad up until the early-to-mid first semester — he was treated like a complete outcast by his classmates, except for Damian's gang, who picked fights with his close friend Becky almost every day. However, thanks to his actions in the bus-jacking incident, his classmates' attitudes changed from the latter half of the first semester.

### 6.3.1. Becky Blackbell:
- At first Becky looked down on Anya as "a kid she had to take care of," but after seeing her endure Damian's teasing with her characteristic smirk as if it didn't bother her, Becky reevaluated her — she realized Anya wasn't just a child but had a composed, adult-like calm.
- Later, Becky was moved and opened her heart to Anya after Anya risked receiving demerits and landed a punch on Damian's face for stepping on her foot, and Anya and Becky became best friends.
- As a bonus, after seeing Anya's family photo Becky fell head over heels for Loid and declared she would become Anya's mother.
- Although Anya acts oddly at times, Becky is definitely on Anya's list of people she likes, and Anya even worries that if she does poorly in school she'll be separated from Becky and end up lonely — showing that Anya also regards Becky as a precious friend.
- Anya's vocabulary is growing quite quickly and her pronunciation is becoming much more accurate; this is largely due to Becky, who talks with her the most.
- Besides family outings, Anya is broadening her horizons through new experiences with Becky, like going shopping together or visiting the zoo.
- Conversely, Anya also helps Becky recover a childlike side she had lost from being too mature; they became friends who are mutually helpful.
- When dealing with others, Anya often hides her true feelings and acts.
- Since Anya frequently reads the minds of those around her, she tends to behave — regardless of her real feelings — in ways that prevent trouble and make things run smoothly. Even when she's with her family, she's the only one who knows both Loid's truth and Yor's truth, but she hides that and plays the part of an "ordinary daughter."
- However, Becky accepts Anya purely as a normal peer friend, and for Anya the time spent with Becky is the only time she can be an entirely ordinary girl.
- Because of this, when she's with Becky she seems not to bother reading minds.

### 6.3.2. Damian Desmond:
- At first, Anya thought of Damian as "second son. The son of Father's mission target. There are many benefits if we get along. But he pesters people so I don't like him," and "second son, trash kid."
- He is Donovan Desmond's second son and the target of Operation Strix's Plan B, the <Friend-Friend Operation>, the person Anya must become closest to, but from their first meeting a quarrel broke out and an angry Anya landed a full-power "sincere punch" taught by Yor to his face.
- However, from Loid's point of view, fortunately Damian developed rather subtle feelings; seeing Anya sobbing and apologizing left him completely stunned and he became totally aware of Anya.
- Anya thinks that if the world becomes peaceful her family can live together in safety, so she actively tries to help Loid's mission.
- She reads Loid's Plan B <Friend-Friend Operation> and tries to get close to Damian.
- Because Anya chased after Damian a little obsessively, her friend Becky misunderstood and thought she liked him.
- From their first encounter, Damian picked a fight over differences in wealth and status and even openly vowed to bully her, which made Anya so mad she retaliated; not only was she the only one to receive the so‑called "Tonito" punishment, she was branded by classmates as a violent girl who hits innocent people for no reason and became ostracized.
- Anya herself was given up for adoption four times before meeting her current family and now desperately tries to maintain the relationship, so she respects her father and empathizes with Damian, who worries about failing to live up to expectations; she also seriously helps Damian in his efforts to gain his father's approval.
- After reading Damian's mind, she accidentally saw how she looked through his love‑struck eyes and got goosebumps, so she actually tried to put more distance between them.
- In the first-year end-of-term prom party episode, Anya either guessed Damian's feelings without using her powers or, while dancing together, for the first time revealed her ability to another person.
- Of course Damian dismissed her as watching too much anime and they quibbled again, but the fact that she planned to reveal her powers to Damian before Loid and Yor shows how much she had opened up to him.
- Damian likes Anya, but being tsundere he couldn't honestly convey his feelings.
- Anya saw Damian's family members (except Donovan) once and read their thoughts: from Damian's mother Melinda she read twisted love and hate toward Damian and a deep fear of his father Donovan, and from Damian's older brother Demetrius she read a mindset that seemed to have given up on understanding people, which made her think the second son's family was strange.

# 8. Miscellaneous:
- Anya refers to herself in the third person, and when someone else calls her by a title instead of her name she sometimes emphasizes it by shouting, "Anya!"
- Because of her telepathy and extremely curious nature, early on people suspected all sorts of incidents happened because of her. However, the setting was already a mess on the brink of disaster, so incidents happened on their own and those suspicions turned out to be unfounded. Instead, her telepathy synergizes with her knack for anticipating crises, helping her notice and resolve events before they occur. Also, although very curious, she knows she's just a child despite her abilities, so she skillfully gets Dad and Mom to act and thereby contributes greatly to solving cases — in that sense she often shines.
- No matter how much telepathy or crisis-preparedness she has, she's still a very young child and lacks the physical strength to confront adults, so she's frequently taken hostage or put in danger and thus becomes the focus of many incidents. Outside the story, this is also because her parents are practically world-class powerhouse monsters, making it hard to create a sense of danger with them, so the creators rely on the weak child Anya — which is why many villains in this comic are particularly garbage who try to kill even a child without remorse.
- Anya sleeps a lot; when she's sleepy her eyes become uneven.
- Other students at Eden often try to boast, only to be teased and end up dejected. That said, the Forger household is only upper-middle-class at best, and the vast majority of students come from wealthy families or prominent political and business clans, so it's an unavoidable situation.
- Anya isn't strong with language, but she sometimes speaks in ways beyond her age. When Loid thinks to himself, "Should we switch her for another child even now...," she cries and says, "Anya — great value for money!" When getting measured for clothes she asks, "Are you selling Anya off?" and after being measured declares, "Father, Anya's measurements have been determined!" Before Yor was part of the Forger family she once said, "Mother, you do not exist," and when Yor moved in she said, "A mother was born for Anya — what a celebration." Looking down from a high spot in the park at people walking below she says, "Humans are like trash," and when Loid and Yor go on a date and don't come home until late she says, "Father, Mother, you're late. Where are you playing around?" In the cruise episode she says, "If you don't finish the exploration, the ship will sink." Although she usually speaks informally, when she uses polite speech she overuses masu (ます), even in places where you'd normally use desu (です), an astonishing speech quirk.
- Regarding Dad's missions, she often daydreams a sequence like: "I do something amazing → Damian sees it, praises me, and becomes friendly → I go to Damian's house with Dad → Dad says to Donovan 'let's end the war' and Donovan agrees → world peace!" She then tries to carry it out and is crushed when Damian reacts realistically (differently from her imagination). From episode 11 of the anime, when she imagines such scenes the voices of the other characters are Anya imitating those voice actors rather than the actual voices, which further emphasizes that these are childish, self-centered fantasies.
- The name Anya is Slavic (Ання or Аня). This comes from the fact that the characters' places of origin differ, with the motifs for their origins being the western frontier of Western Europe and the eastern frontier of Eastern Europe.
