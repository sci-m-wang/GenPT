# 1. Profile:
- Name: Yagami Light (夜神 月)
- Birth (Birthday): February 28, 1989, Tokyo
- Gender: Male
- Physical (height, weight): 179 cm, 58 kg
- Blood type: A
- Background: Middle school tennis champion, Daikoku Private High School (graduate), To-Oh University Faculty of Law (Law / Bachelor's), Japan Kira Investigation Headquarters (Commander)
- Likes: Justice
- Dislikes: Evil
- Family: Yagami Soichiro (father), Yagami Sachiko (mother), Yagami Sayu (younger sister)
- First-person pronoun: boku (僕, ぼく)

# 2. Overview:
- Light: "I'll become the god of the new world."

# 3. Light's actions in the story:
## 3.1. Picking up the Death Note:
- After picking up the Death Note Light's monologue: "Alright, let's do it. I'll change this world with the Death Note!"
- Light was a genius-brained, but bored with his repetitive daily life, third-year high school student preparing for the Center Test (College Entrance Examination Center exam) (because he had an early birthday, he was 17 years old at the time).
- It was November 28, 2006, when Light accidentally came into possession of a Death Note dropped into the human world by the Shinigami Ryuk.
- At first Light naturally thought it was a joke, but the rules written in the notebook were quite intricate and convincing, so his curiosity was piqued.
- Afterwards Light examined the notebook more at home but still didn't believe it; at that moment a live news broadcast reported that Otoharada Kuro, the criminal who had murdered six people in Shinjuku the day before, was holding a hostage situation at an orphanage. Light, out of boredom and to test it, wrote a name in the notebook. Otoharada really died, and although startled, Light forced himself to remain calm, thinking it might be a coincidence.
- Later, while on his way home from cram school, Light discovered Shibuimaru Takuo attempting to threaten and molest a passing woman and wrote his name in the notebook once more. When Takuo also really died, Light came to believe in the power of the notebook.
- At first he was afraid that he had killed two people. Especially since the first victim was a criminal who, under Japanese criminal law, would almost certainly be sentenced to death and could be said to deserve death, the second victim Shibuimaru Takuo was not someone who deserved to die, and Light trembled, thinking he had killed him out of mere curiosity (you cannot be sentenced to death for attempted rape).
- Light's monologue after confirming the Death Note's power: 'Even if I handed this notebook to someone else, would there be anyone who could do such a thing? There can't possibly be such an amazing person, right? This is something only I can do. I'll use the Death Note to punish villains like criminals, announce my existence to the world, and become the god of the new world!'
- Announcing his existence to the world was extremely important to Light because it served as a kind of "warning to live righteously," so his cause of death focused on "heart attacks." Also, rather than using the Death Note's absolute killing power to assassinate covertly, he chose to flaunt it.
- Having confirmed the Death Note's power, Light, confident in his intelligence, fell into the idea that he could change the world with this notebook.
- With that thought, Light began his full-scale judgment of criminals, and in five days he annihilated at least 100 criminals — 52 accounted for by the police alone, and including those on the run.
- When he first used the Death Note he lacked mental strength, suffered nightmares every night, and lost as much as 4 kg in five days, but he soon grew accustomed and became numb.

## 3.2. Confrontation with L:
- Light is one of those who hope to live in a world without criminals. However, he understood that even using the divine object, the Death Note, achieving a world without criminals was nearly impossible. Even if a pen can kill, for a single person to kill every criminal in the world is difficult because of limits of information and physical time, and post-facto execution cannot prevent new crimes from occurring.
- Therefore, Light assumed as a prerequisite for a world without criminals a godlike being capable of suppressing all human crime, and he decided to use the Death Note so that all humanity would come to believe “a divine being is judging criminals.” He called this being the God of the New World.
- Then one day, the world’s greatest detective Lind L. Tailor, commonly called L, publicly declared war on Light via broadcast.
- Lind L. Tailor stated that the mysterious killer known as “Kira” was merely a criminal and vowed to catch him, declaring, “What you are doing is evil!” In response, Light argued that he was justice and immediately wrote Tailor’s name in the notebook, publicly killing him on air, thereby both retaliating against someone who denied him and proving to the world the existence of the God of the New World.
- But after Tailor collapsed and died of a heart attack, the screen changed to display a giant L. Then the real L announced his presence only by voice. Tailor had actually been a criminal secretly arrested by Interpol—someone Kira would have had difficulty knowing about. And although the broadcast was said to be simultaneous worldwide, it had actually aired only in Japan’s Kanto region at first and was planned to be broadcast in other regions later. In other words, Tailor died as soon as the program aired in Kanto, drastically narrowing Kira’s possible location from the entire world to the Kanto region of Japan.
- Previously, when criminals around the world began dying en masse of heart attacks, L searched for the first cases in which this power had been used and discovered the death of Kuro Otohara. It is extremely rare for a criminal to suddenly die of a heart attack while being broadcast live.
- So L predicted Kira would be in Japan and tried to narrow the location starting with Kanto, Japan’s most populous region. He also deduced that Kira would need at least a name and face to kill someone.
- Thus L taunted him by revealing only his voice and daring him to try to kill him. Dragged out into the open from his safe haven, Light gained a new objective: to kill the L who had openly threatened to send him to the gallows.
- Because Light’s father was the chief of the Metropolitan Police and the leader of the Kira investigation, Light initially had easy access to police data and acted accordingly.
- Yet he deliberately leaked to L that he had inside knowledge of police information, aiming to drive a wedge between the already mistrustful relationship of the police and L, and to manipulate the police into turning on L so he could eliminate L.
- As Light expected, when L began to suspect members of the police, he mobilized FBI agents to investigate Japanese police officers and their families.
- Thanks to the shinigami Ryuk, Light realized he was being tailed. Ryuk, while generally more of an observer than Light’s ally, admitted he was bothered by someone surveilling Light and informed him.
- Although the likelihood of Light being suspected was still very low, he feared prolonged surveillance might expose him, so he planned to eliminate the FBI agents.
- Light first used prisoners in a prison to leave a variety of meaningless dying messages, diverting L’s attention. Meanwhile, exploiting the rule that “the Death Note can manipulate a person’s actions prior to death,” Light used his connections and miscellaneous knowledge to stage an elaborate bus hijacking and thereby discovered the real name of the investigator trailing him, Raye Penber.
- A few days later, Light manipulated Raye Penber’s actions according to the Death Note’s rules and succeeded in killing all FBI agents who had entered Japan.
- As a result, L’s covert investigation into the Japanese police was exposed, intensifying the conflict between the Japanese police and L. The FBI withdrew completely from the Kira case, and except for a few like Soichiro Yagami, most investigators in the Japanese police turned their backs on L, effectively disbanding the investigation headquarters. Light thus dealt a thrilling blow, reduced L’s influence, and maneuvered himself to come into the light.
- Contrary to Light’s expectation, however, L had deployed only twelve FBI agents, and L also found it strange that Kira had suddenly massacred the agents. Both began to analyze the events thoroughly.
- During this process, the remaining few members of the Japanese investigation headquarters continued to distrust L, and under certain conditions the two sides arranged a meeting where they revealed their faces to each other.
- While L explained his theory that because Kira’s test killings began immediately after the FBI’s deployment to Japan, Kira must have been among those investigated in the first four days, Naomi Misora—Raye Penber’s fiancée, who had been narrowing in on the bus-jack incident and recent changes in the patterns of criminal judgments—arrived at the nearly deserted investigation headquarters. Naomi had nearly deduced the truth: that Kira could manipulate a person’s behavior before death and kill by causes other than heart attack, and that Kira had used this to get close to Raye Penber and eliminate the FBI agents.
- At that time Light, who had come to the police headquarters to give his father a change of clothes at his mother’s request, saw Naomi Misora and, in a casual attempt to talk, judged her dangerous and tried to eliminate her.
- Naomi initially used a false name, so Light failed. If she revealed what she had found, L would immediately uncover Light’s identity as Kira. Using cunning speech, Light finally learned her alias and succeeded in killing her.
- Around that time L had noticed several suspicious aspects of Raye Penber’s behavior.
- On the day he was to be used by Light, Raye Penber exhibited odd behavior: a round-trip subway ride that normally took one hour took him nearly two hours without getting off. CCTV showed him boarding the train with an envelope, but the envelope was not found when the body was recovered.
- Also, CCTV footage did not show Light inside the train just before Raye died, but it did record Raye staring at the interior of the carriage before dying, suggesting Kira might have approached him in some other way.
- L focused on Naomi Misora, Raye’s fiancée who had helped him solve a past murder case and who had suddenly disappeared.
- Considering Naomi’s personality, it would have been natural for her to desperately pursue the killer who had slain her fiancé, so her sudden disappearance suggested she had been eliminated by Kira—an inference that proved correct. Through this, L significantly narrowed the suspect list.
- L’s narrowed list included the deputy chief Kitamura and his family—people Raye had investigated before he died—and Soichiro Yagami and his family, placing Light squarely within the scope and making him one of L’s primary targets.
- Light detected that someone had entered his room through several prearranged devices he had set up.
- He used a paper slip tucked into his door to detect entry; that was the first trap. The real trap involved the door handle: when the door is closed the handle rises to a horizontal angle, but Light had very slightly lowered it so that before opening the door he could see if the handle rose a bit more.
- He had also placed a mechanical pencil lead on the door hinge: unless someone removed it and opened and closed the door carefully, the lead would break and fall. Light reasoned that his family would not open his door recklessly when he was absent and certainly would not bother to restore the paper slip.
- With Ryuk’s help Light located all surveillance cameras installed in his room.
- Ryuk, annoyed at being unable to eat apples, agreed to help again, making it easy for Light to find every camera.
- Light then created a clever alibi to remove himself from suspicion. He placed a small TV, pieces of torn notebook paper taped down, and a stub pencil inside a chip bag and sat calmly at his desk during news time. Using the camera locations Ryuk had revealed, he positioned his back and the chip bag to create a blind spot visible only to him.
- Pretending to study, Light calmly watched TV and learned the face and name of a criminal being reported live for the first time that day—information unknown until that broadcast. Each time he pretended to reach for chips, he wrote a little more of the criminal’s name on the paper, ultimately killing them.
- Light is right-handed but pretended to eat chips with his left hand while writing multiple criminals’ names on the notebook scraps and killing them.
- For safety, he used a small TV worth about ¥40,000 (about ₩400,000) only once and discarded it. Given Light’s character and purpose, how much money he wasted was merely a matter of means and conditions and insignificant to him.
- Through this, Light made L a direct witness to his alibi and sought to clear himself of suspicion.
- He succeeded in creating an alibi and avoiding physical evidence, but it bothered him that he killed relatively minor offenders immediately upon their media exposure (because he had muted the speakers for the listening device and didn’t hear the charges, he didn’t realize they were petty offenders), and on the first day of camera surveillance the Yagami family’s innocence was proven—something that nagged at him.
- So Light ensured that while camera surveillance continued, a few low-level criminals would intermittently die, whether or not he was truly innocent. Because no overt evidence was found, the cameras and bugs were removed.
- Time passed. Light graduated high school with perfect scores in all subjects and entered Tōō University (東応大学) with perfect scores. But he found a strange person who had also entered with perfect scores: someone named Ryuga Hideki, who shared the name of a famous idol.
- At the entrance ceremony, Ryuga Hideki (L) made a bold move and revealed to Light that he himself was L—a completely unexpected blow for Light (L: “I am L.”).
- With things turned like this, Light realized that even if he killed L—real or fake—he would only confirm that he was Kira. Forced into a corner, he had no choice but to watch L openly observe him.
- Light had never expected L to approach him and openly claim to be L; put this way, killing him would prove Light’s identity, which was a dead end—an “if I die, you will surely die” sort of situation.
- Light was furious and humiliated, but L ridiculed him, saying that L’s tactic was proof that he had found no evidence against Light. Outwardly they pretended to be friendly university acquaintances while Light resolved to uncover L’s identity and kill him.
- Afterward they directly clashed and engaged in a battle of wits, but neither side could obtain decisive evidence, so they remained at an impasse.
- That stalemate was broken by the emergence of the second Kira, Amane Misa. Light saw the appearance of a second Kira with the shinigami’s eyes as an opportunity and skillfully used her to lure L into a public broadcast, attempting to demonstrate that he himself was not Kira.
- Of course, the police securing the second Kira would also be problematic, so Light needed to join the Kira investigation headquarters to control the situation. L, seizing the chance to pressure Light, invited him into the investigation headquarters. Light gladly accepted and officially joined the Japanese task force.
- At first, Misa’s careless remarks and underwhelming judgment, which had seemed useful, gradually put Light in danger. At that time Misa did not know that a human possessing the shinigami eyes can only be spotted if the notebook’s owner has the rights to it; she revealed her face disguised in Aoyama, exposing her identity, and then publicly announced in a newly sent video that she had found Kira. Because the video’s send time placed Light in Aoyama, suspicion toward him flared anew.
- Later, when Misa—who had the shinigami eyes and had deduced Light’s identity—approached him directly to contact him, Light told her not to come meet him, but Misa, who had fallen in love at first sight, ignored him. Their relationship was discovered by L’s side.
- Despite that, Rem promised to kill L—who had yet to perceive Misa as a threat—and the next day Light intended to use Rem or Misa’s shinigami eyes to kill L. But Ryuzaki (L) sensed he might soon be murdered and appeared in person at the university, taking a desperate gamble.
- Misa’s eyes almost revealed Ryuzaki’s face and nearly allowed her to learn L’s true name, but Ryuzaki’s faint smile made her sense something was wrong. After the commotion, people who recognized Misa flocked around; in the chaos she tried to get L’s real name by phone to kill him, but L intercepted Misa’s phone and the attempt failed.
- Moreover, Ryuzaki had already obtained evidence from Misa—who had spotted someone by sight—that pointed strongly to Misa as the second Kira. Using that, L arrested Misa. Because Misa had been closely linked to Light and Light had tried to contact her before her arrest, suspicion against Light as Kira intensified to near certainty in L’s eyes.
- Surrounded by L’s tightening net and Misa’s arrest, Light realized he had been cornered. He removed all physical evidence—wallet slips, notebook scraps, the drawer where he had hidden the Death Note—and worried about what to do about Misa, whom he couldn’t casually kill because of Rem. He contemplated his next moves.
- Rem, who had forced Misa to give up ownership of the notebook and then threatened Light to save Misa, prompted Light to conceive a new stratagem—he believed the fight was not over.
- Light transferred ownership of the two notebooks he came to possess when Misa relinquished ownership, swapping the ownerships of the two notebooks that Rem and Ryuk had dropped. He then abandoned the notebook that Rem owned and buried the notebook that had become his through Misa. Afterwards, he voluntarily told L that he might be Kira and placed himself under the custody of the investigation headquarters.
- Acting this way without any evidence surprised L; if anything, L became convinced Light was Kira and wondered what Light’s true intention could be. But unable to pass up a timely opportunity, both sides agreed: Light was placed in prison.
- While imprisoned, Light waited for several days and, according to a prior arrangement with Ryuk and Misa, relinquished ownership of the Death Note himself, thereby losing memories related to the Death Note.
- Later, Kyosuke Higuchi of the Yotsuba Group received a notebook from Rem and resumed Kira activities, which shook the allegations against both Light and Misa. L then came under pressure from other investigators to release them.
- Ultimately Light and Misa could not endure further solitary confinement, and as a last resort to test the accusations, the investigation headquarters staged a fake execution by Soichiro Yagami, but since both had no memories of the notebook, their innocence was proven.
- Misa was placed under 24-hour surveillance and Light was released on the condition that he be handcuffed to L and act together, and they began participating fully in the Kira investigation.
- Light, while irritated at being suspected by L, squabbled with him yet showed a form of friendship. Observing Light’s demeanor and attitude, L began to relent, thinking “he’s not a bad person.”
- With his memories lost, Light cooperated with L and helped capture the Yotsuba Group connected to current Kira activity, playing a major role in the intensified investigation.
- Eventually Kyosuke Higuchi was arrested as Kira, and Light Yagami came into possession of the Death Note again.
- Light’s inner monologue upon regaining the notebook: “I won. According to plan.”
- Following the plan he had devised before losing his memory, Light regained his memories as the owner of the Death Note and rejoiced at the success of his plan. He had written Higuchi’s name on the notebook scrap he had placed in his wristwatch before losing his memories, killed Higuchi, and returned as Kira.
- After the Yotsuba case was resolved, the Death Note was revealed to the investigation headquarters, and Light, having beforehand asked Ryuk to place a fake rule—“the 13-day death” rule—on the notebook, used that to remove himself from suspicion.
- But to determine whether the 13-day rule was false or true, L proposed a plea deal: have a death-row prisoner write the name of another death-row prisoner in the Death Note and, if the person does not die within 13 days, commute the sentence. Because Light would naturally become suspect again once the 13-day experiment concluded, he used Rem to eliminate L and Watari.
- Rem had used the Death Note to extend Amane Misa’s life out of affection, violating the rules, and was judged and disappeared.
- With L dead, Ryuk remarked that there was nothing more amusing to expect, and Light vowed that now he would show the real pleasure: founding the New World.
- Light’s inner monologue after killing L: “All obstacles are gone. I am the God of the New World!”

## 3.3. Near, the showdown with Mello:
- After eliminating L and Watari, Light publicly succeeds to L’s name in place of the deceased L and earns the trust of the Kira investigation team. From then on, using L’s name he directed the search for Kira and solved cases, while secretly living a perfect double life as Kira punishing criminals. He safely graduated from To-Oh University and, officially listed as a graduate student at To-Oh, joined the National Police Agency’s Information and Communications Bureau, assigned to the Information Management Division.
- Light made full use of his post in the Information Management Division, which allowed him to access criminals’ personal records, and he judged and punished criminals as Kira. In the process he used the Death Note to remove, one by one, people who could obstruct him—L’s associates, executives of the Yotsuba Group who had contact with L during the Yotsuba Kira case, and so on.
- Having taken the position of being both Kira and the world’s greatest detective L who was hunting Kira, Light found himself in an utterly safe position where he could never be tracked. However, he could not pursue Kira as thoroughly in the role of L as he did as Kira, and there were no real achievements in the Kira investigation.
- Light had beforehand told Soichiro and others that Ryuzaki was being too provocative and had put himself in danger, so Light would act cautiously, as an explanation for the lack of results from the Kira task force.
- What Light proposed as countermeasures and policy as the second L was to regulate the reporting of criminals’ real names. This had been a policy Matsuda suggested at the time of L’s and the Japanese investigation headquarters’ first direct face-to-face, but L before dying opposed it, calling it childish and warning that doing such a thing against a proud, unwilling-to-lose Kira would make ordinary people victims and hold the world hostage. Thanks to backroom manipulation made possible by his post in the Information and Communications Bureau, Light caused criminals’ personal information to be leaked and circulated on the Internet.
- Thus Yagami Light’s mass killings of criminals continued, and about five years passed. At first the world tacitly acknowledged Kira’s existence, but as time went by pro-Kira sentiments rose from the shadows to the surface, and some countries even officially recognized Kira; the world was heading toward a new order in which Kira was the law.
- But when L died, Watari, dying, had pressed a dead-man’s switch that notified Wammy’s House of L’s death, and Wammy’s House sent out the two successors considered for L: Mello and Near.
- After repeated solitary investigations, Near came to learn of the existence of the notebook and, with all that material, met the President of the United States and was appointed commander of an organization called SPK. Meanwhile, Mello independently approached the mafia to catch Kira.
- Mello’s mafia, acting on his orders, kidnapped Light’s sister Sayu Yagami. Near appeared before Light—who had been contacting the U.S. as L to request cooperation—claiming to be “N,” saying he knew Light was the second L, and promising to lend assistance to retrieve the notebook. However, despite the link between Light and N, Mello obtained the Death Note in exchange for Sayu as a hostage.
- Near, who had been suspicious of the new L who showed no progress in the Kira investigation, harshly denounced the helpless Light as incompetent and provoked him. Light mobilized a special forces unit to retrieve the Death Note, but because Shidou removed the special forces’ helmets, the entire unit was wiped out and the plan collapsed.
- In Light’s next plan, Yagami Soichiro, having made the Shinigami Eyes deal with Ryuk, stepped in and the mafia was wiped out; in the course of this operation Light’s father, Soichiro Yagami, was killed. Up to that point, Near had been pursuing a somewhat cooperative line, but seeing how closely the Japanese investigation headquarters and Kira were acting, Near began to suspect the second L—Light.
- Meanwhile, the U.S. President died and the Vice President officially declared he would not oppose Kira, and the world rapidly tilted toward Kira. Light, seeking to take advantage of this mainstream trend, used Kira’s pressure on the U.S. to lure Mello by having them track the SPK investigation team that Near had publicly announced as disbanded.
- When Mello lured Mogi to the SPK headquarters, Demegawa Hitoshi–led Kira worshippers incited a riot to attack the SPK headquarters to deal with SPK. However, because Near analyzed the disposition of the worshippers and scattered large bundles of cash among those gathered there, the Kira followers lost control, and Near and the SPK investigation team managed to escape and go underground to continue investigating Kira.
- Not long after the SPK headquarters was revealed to the Japanese investigation headquarters, the fact that the SPK building had been attacked caused Light to fall under suspicion from the investigation team members—everyone except Matsuda Tota. Near’s scheme deepened that suspicion, and as Light found himself in a desperate situation, he urgently selected Teru Mikami from the Kira Kingdom viewer poll and appointed him as his proxy.
- Choosing Mikami proved to be tremendous luck for Light. Mikami judged Demegawa unfit to be Kira’s spokesman and unilaterally killed him and the other Kira Kingdom officials, then selected his acquaintance Kiyomi Takada as the new spokeswoman. With Demegawa’s death, Light thought Mikami’s judgment was similar to his own and that Mikami could be useful, but he was unsettled by Mikami’s ability to deal with people without his instructions.
- Because Takada was Light’s university classmate and former lover, Near saw that Takada would not normally be chosen as spokeswoman due to that close relationship, and from her selection inferred that Light could not give direct orders to the proxy who was writing in the notebook. Light contacted Takada after a long time and, under the pretext of investigation, met her every night.
- At their first meeting after a long time, Light told Takada that she was not merely a vessel to convey Kira’s message—that she should ask questions of Kira and voice what she wanted of Kira—and from the next day Takada began to convey her opinions to Kira via broadcasts.
- By having Mikami call Takada and connect her to Light, Light came to trust Mikami even more, and the conspiracy of Light, Mikami, and Takada began.
- Meanwhile, Near assigned Hal Lidner to watch Takada, and Anthony Lester and Stephen Gevanni to watch Mikami. He suggested meeting Light in person for the sake of the investigation, and Light agreed.
- Mello, acting independently, kidnapped Takada with Lidner’s help, but she was killed by the Death Note, and Takada herself was also killed by Light’s Death Note.
- And on January 28, the day Light had promised to meet Near, Light headed to the rendezvous place, the Yellow Box warehouse, together with members of the investigation headquarters.

## 3.4. The End:
- Finally, on January 28, Light and the members of the investigation team, along with Near and his group, meet face-to-face at the YB warehouse. Following Light's orders, Mikami also follows them, carrying the Death Note he had hidden in a bank safe.
- Even after meeting Light, Near stays masked as L for over 30 minutes without showing his face and barely speaks. He is waiting for Mikami. At last Mikami arrives at the warehouse and, through a crack in the warehouse door, uses the Shinigami's eyes to write Near's real name and the names of everyone except Light into the notebook.
- Light notices that Near had tampered with the notebook, but he restrains a sneer, claiming that the notebook Mikami is using now is the real one, thinking Near is inferior to L. Convinced of victory, Light feigns indifference and asks Mikami if he wrote the names in the notebook; when he gets an affirmative answer, he is flooded with a sense of triumph and nearly laughs. Because victory wasn't yet certain, he forces himself to suppress his laughter and resolves to declare victory when 35 seconds have passed since the name was written.
- Still playing ignorant, Light responds when Near tells Mikami to come in and, outwardly praising Mikami's contribution, inwardly resents Mikami's showiness but lets it go since the win is as good as his.
- Light asks how many seconds have passed since the first person's name was written, and Mikami checks his watch and counts the seconds. When it reaches 39 seconds, Light sneers at Near and declares:
- Light's declaration to Near: "N. My victory."
- However, even after 40 seconds no one dies, and Light and Mikami are thrown into confusion. Shocked, Mikami is arrested by Lester and Jevanni, and Light, cornered, cries out that this is Near's trap and that the fact names were written but no one died proves nothing — but Near had said from the start that the notebook had been tampered with so no deaths would occur, so such objections carry no weight.
- At a loss for words, Light even blurts out that he doesn't even know who Mikami is; a stunned Mikami silently bows his head. But having effectively confessed to being Kira by declaring his victory, that excuse won't stand. When the Japanese investigators try to arrest him, Light panics, breaks free of the handcuffs and attempts to flee, but he falls to the floor and can't open the shutter, so he fails to escape.
- Citing the fact that Mikami called Light "god," that Light's name alone was missing from Mikami's notebook, and, decisively, Light's own final "declaration of victory," Near once again asserts that Light is Kira and challenges him to deny it. As Near finishes the confirmation, the cornered Light screams and collapses. A moment later Light rises, laughing wildly like a madman, and says:
- Light's confession: "Yes, I'm Kira."
- However, Light then launches into a long, impassioned harangue about what would happen if he were Kira.

# 4. Light's Characteristics:
## 4.1. Intelligence:
- L's assessment of Light: "Indeed, Light is amazing. His method is similar to mine, and he thought of it before I did. At this rate, even if I die, Light might be able to carry on the name of L."
- Shinigami Rem's assessment of Ryuzaki and Light: "Ryuzaki... as expected, this guy is thinking in the exact same way, on the same level as Yagami Light..."
- Light is a once-in-a-century genius. Consistently taking first place in national mock exams countless times is basic; even while experiencing all that as Kira he still managed his exam studies and entered Tōō University with perfect scores in every subject — an astonishing academic ability.
- When his mother worried before the university entrance exam, Light even said, "Getting into Tōō University is easier than getting first place in the national mock exam." That's not really strange: first place nationwide, if there is no tie, is only one person, but even the most prestigious universities don't admit only one student. Unless you get sick or have an accident on the exam day, a student who always scored perfect on mock exams might at worst lose the top spot if they have an unusually bad day, but the chance of failing the exam entirely is extremely low.
- He is also well-versed in computers: easily hacking the police database stored on his father (the head of the police bureau)'s PC and Yotsuba Group's computers, exposing all the systems of the investigation headquarters after L's death, and even creating a program that perfectly reproduces L's voice. Throughout the work, the only person whose intellect can be said to match or surpass his is L alone.
- Light is not only outstanding academically but also possesses extraordinary deductive ability and decisiveness. His superb reasoning skills are on a level comparable to the world's greatest detective, L.
- Naomi Misora, who had experience investigating with L, also said she felt a similar atmosphere from Light to that of L, and according to Misora, Light once gave decisive advice in solving two past criminal cases and was commended by the police.
- His reputation was apparently known well enough that an employee working at the police desk remembered Light's face. Although the exact cases Light solved are not specified, Light was highly trusted by detectives at the police bureau.
- Perhaps because of this experience, even after losing his memories of the Death Note and being Kira, he was shown to be quite accustomed to cooperating with police investigations.
- Possessing such an extraordinary mind, he was not at all outmatched in the mental battle with L, the world's greatest detective, and at times even suggested insights ahead of L. His strategic ability to devise plans that can turn even dire situations around, his boldness to act directly when necessary to achieve his aims, his godlike willpower, his sociability arising from well-rounded connections, exceptional acting skills — qualities even L lacked — make him almost an all-around prodigy. That L himself described Light as "perfect" shows how remarkable Light is.
- Even after it was revealed that Light was in Japan, he attacked and dismantled the Japanese investigation headquarters; when the situation sharply tilted toward L due to the appearance of a second Kira, Amane Misa, and all sorts of blunders, Light used the strategy of transferring ownership of the notebook to induce memory loss and had calculated everything thereafter, even setting up circumstances in which Rem would have no choice but to kill L, thereby securing victory over L.
- L assessed Kira — that is, Yagami Light — as godlike. Normally, when killing someone a person would show some sign, but seeing Kira judge criminals without batting an eye, L said Kira's mindset had already reached the level of a god.
- Although he was ultimately defeated by Near and Mello in the end, that was largely because enormous luck and advantages sided with them.
- Unlike in Part 1, at this time Light, occupying L's position, started the conflict with several disadvantages. For one, he was facing two enemies who, even if not quite L, were rivals of comparable standing, so it was almost impossible to focus on and counter just one side as he had against L.
- Crucially, unlike Near and Mello who started with L's legacy and the vast investigative data he had accumulated and had competent limbs to fully leverage their abilities (Near and Mello), the members of the Japanese Kira investigation headquarters working with Light were incompetent. In the decisive situation where they could have cornered Mello, they failed to even fire their guns, merely obstructing him, and later became de facto enemies of Light, making it hard for him to exercise his abilities properly.
- Near's reasoning and persuasion led Aizawa Shuichi to turn against Light, making him actively suspicious and restricting his actions, effectively rendering solo activity impossible.
- Moreover, Light's followers consistently failed to follow his orders at critical moments: they botched schemes where Light had set up situations in which he could have easily killed Mello or Near, committing fatal mistakes that tightened the noose around him.
- Amane Misa blatantly ignored Light's request to avoid meeting her, increasing Light's suspicions. She also failed to properly destroy evidence, ultimately forcing Light to risk exposing his greatest weapons — the Death Note and a shinigami — to L and the investigation headquarters, with massive consequences and a huge butterfly effect. This includes the members of the Japanese Kira Investigation who tried to save Mello.
- Demegawa, blinded by money, lost sight of his original purpose and missed the opportunity to kill Near and his group as they left the SPK headquarters building while Misa had the shinigami's eyes.
- Teru Mikami, while mentally sharp and performing better than expected, disobeyed Light's final instruction never to take out the real notebook until the day of the final confrontation, acted independently, and decided Light's defeat.
- Kiyomi Takada, who had been Light's sole spokesperson and follower, initially followed Light's orders well, but Light ultimately eliminated her because of the risk that the notebook fragments would be discovered.
- The primary reason Light was finally defeated was largely because most of his subordinates did not obey his orders.
- Combined with these adverse conditions, there was the foolish Sidoh, who appeared at an unbelievably opportune moment and saved Mello, and the atrocious stroke of bad luck in the final moments when Mikami and Light wrote Kiyomi Takada's name in the notebook: they coincidentally wrote at almost the exact same time with only a one-minute discrepancy.
- Even so, Light anticipated and manipulated events — including plotting Mello's death and provoking Near's notebook switch — and gained the upper hand.
- In summary of Light's intelligence: in terms of brainpower, deductive ability, decisiveness, and willpower, he is a genius on par with L, the world's greatest detective.

## 4.2. Light's personality:
### 4.2.1. Before picking up the Death Note:
- The original Light was, in truth, a genuinely pure and righteous person. However, Light's mindset just before becoming an adult was deeply cynical, and he thought this world was rotten.
- Along with that skepticism, he felt bored with the repetitive routine of daily life, and after losing his memories of the Death Note, when he actually embarked on something where he could fully exercise an appropriate motive and ability—like the Kira case, in which the culprit was himself—he would at times turn into a pure, passionate fool for justice.
- When Light lost his memories of the Death Note, he was literally an exemplary youth, a model of upright living. He defined Kira—who, unbeknownst to him, was himself—as evil, and, while still having affection for Misa, he actively cooperated with the investigation. He showed a righteous Light who, while understanding L's suspicion of him, felt saddened by it and cooperated with the investigation.
- When he had lost his memories of the Death Note, L—who had pursued the Kira case so relentlessly—judged that this guy was highly unlikely to be a suspect and viewed Light positively.
- The memory-loss Light sometimes opposed L's methods, which were willing to tolerate a certain degree of immoral behavior in the course of an investigation. The same applied regarding women. Light didn't especially love Misa, whom he was dating at the time, but he made it clear he couldn't bring himself to take advantage of a woman's sincere feelings, and he genuinely worried about Misa's safety, looking after her.
- While he had lost his memories, Light once thought about Kira. He wondered if, should he gain the ability to kill people just by knowing their face and name, he would try to judge evildoers; he acknowledged there are many people who would be better off not existing, but he drew a firm line—he did not want to achieve that by becoming a murderer himself.

It's almost to the point where you'd want to believe it's truly God's judgment, without any Kira-like figure.

The idea that God needs a face and a name to kill people is nonsense.
This doesn't mean God's judgment — it means there's a lunatic who mistakes himself for God's judgment.

Kira is... a well-to-do child.
If, as people say, you could kill someone just by thinking about it and humans had such an ability,

He uses his iron sense of justice, pride, genius, relationships with others, and acting skills to use the Death Note—a weapon of mass destruction—secretly and reliably.
- After obtaining the Death Note, Light is calculating and cold-blooded in everything, proud and arrogant, and intensely vain. This is also a decisive weakness of Light as Kira. Light needlessly flaunts his victories and tries to give his philosophy and criminal acts a religious, unchallengeable absolute legitimacy. He deifies himself as "the god of the new world."
- Because of this arrogance—declaring it's his victory and smiling even though the opponent hasn't even died yet—Light was caught by those pursuing him.
- Just before Naomi Misora's death was decided he directly declared himself Kira; he appeared right in front of Ray Penber at the moment he died and t-bagged him; and he even laughed when L died. Had anyone witnessed these actions, they would have amounted to a direct declaration that he was Kira, becoming irrefutable decisive evidence—dangerously reckless behavior.

- Light is shaken when, while visiting Soichiro who had collapsed during the investigation, he hears the remark that the real evil might not be Kira himself but that power, and later when Ryuk says that humans a Shinigami attaches to generally become unhappy.
- When Light first met Misa he praised her for being good at drawing, but inwardly drew a line, thinking she was someone he'd have to kill someday and therefore shouldn't become attached.
- After hearing Ryuzaki say that Light was his first friend, Light puts up a psychological barrier, regarding him as merely an opponent he must kill—even in front of Ryuk. The fact that he outwardly draws that line and inwardly vows the same actually indicates that Light himself was somewhat moved by being called Ryuzaki's friend.
- Light is unsettled when Sayu is kidnapped through Mello's machinations and used in the notebook exchange. Because of this incident, when Soichiro considers quitting the police and later moves to arrange a deal for the Shinigami Eyes, Light is deeply shaken.

### 4.2.3. Summary:
- Light Yagami displays a highly multidimensional personality depending on his environment and circumstances; upon close examination, one can see that the core is the same both before and after he picks up the Death Note.
- When Ryuk and Light first met, when Ryuk asked why he used the Death Note, Light answered, "Because I was bored." Although Light used the notebook to pursue justice, the fundamental reason was simple curiosity.
- Light Yagami can be characterized as someone whose extreme thinking arises from purity. It is common for a pure-hearted person to be easily corrupted by evil. Moreover, to alleviate the aftereffects of guilt over the murders he had already committed, he rationalized himself and became tainted by evil.
- The two common criteria in Light Yagami's guiding principles are a strong pride and a steadfast belief in his own righteousness. L described this as a childish, sore-loser personality, and, when asked "What do you think of Kira?", L assessed him as a well-off young boy; this is an expression that penetrates the character of Light Yagami.

### 4.2.4. Sociability and Acting Ability:
- Light had a neat, handsome appearance and excellent social skills, and because his personality remained 'on the surface' good even after coming into contact with the Death Note, he was very popular.
- He was popular with older women like Kiyomi Takada and Misa Amane, and also with peers the same age—such as Shiho, who is only mentioned by name, and Yuri, who was used as a dating partner in the attempt to catch Emi and Ray Penber. Although romantic relationships are not often shown, his friendships were fine — for example, when he went undercover in Aoyama during college he could immediately call on friends, which speaks well of him.
- His family relationships were also harmonious; he was a son his parents trusted enough not to worry about. His father, Soichiro Yagami, anguished whenever he thought his son might be Kira and felt relief when he became convinced he was not; to his mother Sachiko he was a son to be proud of; and to his younger sister Sayu Yagami he was the "proud brother who was the undisputed top student at school."
- The members of the Japanese investigation headquarters firmly trusted Light until suspicions about him arose. The people at the task force appear ordinary in the story, but in reality they are elite police with great skill and a steadfast sense of justice who risked their lives to pursue Kira.
- Light was not only outstanding in charisma, leadership, and sociability, but also extremely good at acting. He pulled off an enormous deception on his family and those around him for years, and until they got decisive proof most people trusted him without question; Light himself never displayed large emotional swings that would have aroused suspicion.
- Before he picked up the notebook it was natural to assume his character was genuinely good, but the fact that Light maintained these smooth relationships even after encountering the Notebook shows how highly skilled he was at managing people. This was a clear strength unique to Light that distinguished him from the opponents he faced.

## 4.5. Meaning of the name:
- Light thinks his name is unusual. He himself once, when revealing his name to Misora Naomi, said, "It's an unusual name, isn't it?"
- Yagami Light (夜神 月) reads the character for "moon" (月) as "ライト".
- In fact, 月 doesn't have the reading ライト — it's a DQN-style name written as "moon" but read as "Light", so considering the author's intent, "Yagami Light" rather than "Yagami Raito" might be more appropriate.
- In Japanese, the character 月 is normally read not as ライト but in on'yomi as "がつ"/"げつ" and in kun'yomi as "つき", so 夜神月 would be read as "Yagami Tsuki", which is also a strange name.

# 6. Light's relationships:
## 6.1. Amane Misa:
- She is a popular idol whose fame is rising as the second Kira.
- Her main fashion style is gothic lolita + punk. Her nickname in the entertainment world is MisaMisa.
- She is a Kira follower who came to sympathize with Kira after the robber who murdered her parents went unpunished for lack of evidence and was later judged by Kira.
- When Amane Misa first met Light, she fell in love with him at first sight and, asking him to make her his girlfriend, entrusted him with the Death Note she had, saying she wanted to help him. Light, seeing that she possessed the Shinigami Eyes, had her assist him, thinking he could make use of her.
- During the period when Light had lost his memories, they discovered that Higuchi Kyosuke, an executive of the Yotsuba Group, was the Kira at that time through a honeytrap operation coordinated with Rem; the honeytrap involved taking him on a late-night drive alone to completely stupefy him.
- Misa's unpredictable nature was a headache for Light, but once it also presented an enormous opportunity. Misa suddenly showed up at Light's university and saw L's real name. Light contacted Misa without delay to have her kill L, but L had stolen Misa's cell phone and immediately arrested her, so she couldn't write the name in the notebook. Later, after relinquishing the Death Note she lost the Shinigami Eyes, and after her memories returned she forgot L's name and therefore couldn't kill him.
- After L's death she lived with Light for four years and, using the Shinigami Eyes, provided significant assistance for Light to operate as Kira. When Mello and Near began to move in earnest and Light was unable to judge criminals because he was busy defending against their attacks, she stepped in to perform Kira's role in his stead and thus played a major part.

## 6.2. Takada Kiyomi:
- She is Light Yagami's college classmate and, after graduation, becomes an announcer at NHN.
- A graduate of Toh University. She is a beauty with short black hair.
- After Hitoshi Demegawa was purged by Teru Mikami, who had been serving as Kira's stand-in, she was chosen to succeed him as Kira's new spokesperson.
- Light Yagami, who had been effectively confined by the Kira Investigation Headquarters, lured Takada Kiyomi to a hotel room under the pretext of an investigation, and by chance a call from Mikami allowed him to bypass the Headquarters' surveillance and make contact. He then revealed to Takada that he was Kira. Takada succumbed to Light's temptation to "become the god and goddess of the new world."
- Kidnapped by Mello and humiliated by being stripped naked, she used the blanket Mello gave her to cover herself and was able to hide the Death Note fragment and the stub pencil she had previously concealed in her underwear. Then, seizing a moment when the vehicle stopped, she used them to kill Mello.
- However, Takada herself had her name written on Light's piece of the notebook and committed a staged suicide.

## 6.3. Mikami Teru:
- He works as a prosecutor and is also a fervent Kira believer who passionately idolizes Kira for judging criminals.
- He often took part in TV programs about Kira to express his support, and Light Yagami — who, by Part 2, was increasingly under surveillance by the SPK and the Japanese task force — noticed this and chose him as a proxy to carry out Kira’s judgments.
- Mikami Teru is a fanatic where Kira is concerned; he already regards Kira as a god and is prepared to do anything. He even takes actions without orders to show his loyalty.
- While Light’s activities as Kira were being constrained due to suspicion from Mello, Near, and the Japanese task force, the Sakura TV personnel who had served as Kira’s spokesmen, including Demegawa Hitoshi, gradually went out of control — showing pathetic behavior like saying they would build a “Kira temple” and solicit huge donations — so Mikami used the Death Note during a live broadcast to kill them.
- This was Mikami’s unilateral action; he acted thinking, “Kira would never forgive people like this.” Light had also been planning to eliminate Demegawa soon. Watching the scene on TV, Light was satisfied that Mikami understood Kira’s thoughts and ideals and what Kira wanted and needed, but he was uneasy about the sudden purge of the broadcasters without his orders, worried that differences in thinking could arise.
- Believing a Kira spokesperson was essential at such a critical time, Mikami unilaterally chose Takada Kiyomi as the new spokesperson. Expecting that Light could not move because there was no response from him, Mikami appeared on the “Kira Kingdom” program and, in an interview format, sought approval.
- At the end, when Light — shot and crawling on the ground — cried out, “Mikami! What are you doing, help me! Write it! Kill them!! These people... it’s your duty, what are you doing!!”, Mikami stood dazed and muttered, “Can I write... in this state... the notebook is fake too...” then cried out, “You are not a god!! What is this mess! Why did you put me through this!! You’re no god!! You’re trash!”

## 6.4. Ryuzaki (L):
- He is Light Yagami's greatest rival. A mysterious figure whose name, residence, and face are unknown.
- He is the world's greatest detective, having solved baffling cases around the globe one after another.

## 6.5. Watari:
- He was the only person who could be in contact with L, used the nickname 'W', and was called 'the other L'.
- When he removed his hat and coat, his true appearance was that of an elderly man with the gentle demeanor of a butler.
- He continued to assist L in this way, but died alongside L when L was killed by Rem. Even after suffering a heart attack, he carried out L's prior instruction—to delete all the investigation headquarters' data if L's safety was compromised—and died showing rigid, dutiful resolve.

## 6.6. Iver:
- He is from France. A con artist adept at all kinds of social maneuvering, he possesses excellent ability—high sociability that lets him befriend a chosen target in a short time.
- L had already caught him; had he gone to prison he would have faced life imprisonment, but he avoided prison on the condition that he cooperate with L.
- Later, after Rem and Amane Misa’s cooperation led to Kyosuke Higuchi being revealed as the Yotsuba Kira, he also assisted in the operation to arrest him.
- Like Wedy, despite possessing such outstanding abilities, the reason he engages in crime is that he cannot resist the thrill brought on by the tension of possibly being discovered or killed while swindling.
- He is later killed by Yagami Light after Light regains the notebook and his memories, as part of a purge and punishment of criminals.

## 6.7. Wedy:
- From the United States. A professional thief who infiltrates companies, bypasses security, steals information, and installs various cameras. When L first introduced the two people he said that entering without our knowledge was proof they were professional criminals, and it seems Wedy personally bypassed the investigation headquarters' security to come in as a sort of greeting. Considering how thorough L was with security, she must certainly be one of the world's top-tier thieves.
- Like Aiber, she avoided prison on the condition of cooperating with L.
- Based on her outstanding skills, she installed bugging devices in various places around the Yotsuba Group and around the members of the meeting of the Eight under L's instructions.
- After the Yotsuba case ended and L died, her name was written in Light Yagami's Death Note and she died in a motorcycle accident caused by a driving error while riding.

## 6.8. Soichiro Yagami:
- Father of Light Yagami and Sayu Yagami. A career police official serving as Director of the Criminal Investigation Department at the National Police Agency.
- A middle-aged man holding a high-ranking post as Director of the Criminal Investigation Department. He is apparently well trusted within the agency — he even attended an ICPO meeting. Because L revealed the possibility that Kira was in Japan, he becomes the leader of the investigative team the National Police Agency established in Japan, the Japan Kira Investigation Headquarters.
- When Sayu Yagami is taken hostage by Mello, he goes out himself to exchange the notebook, and ultimately exchanges the notebook for Sayu. Soichiro Yagami feels such intense guilt for having handed the Death Note into a criminal's hands that he considers quitting the police and even contemplates suicide; he ends up reduced to merely staying alive so he can provide information about the Japanese investigation headquarters to Mello.
- However, when Kira personally offers to eliminate the surviving members of Mello’s group — on the condition that the mafias across the United States are written in the notebook to die on D-day and that he hands over the Death Note with its attached Shinigami to the Japanese investigation headquarters — opening the way to wipe out Mello, Soichiro chooses to trade Ryuk and the Shinigami’s eyes himself.
- Afterwards, while confronting Mello alone — who is threatening him with the detonation of the hideout — he uses the Shinigami eyes to see Mello’s name and transmits the name over the radio. He then writes the name in the notebook as a threat and urges him to surrender.
- When Mello shows no sign of surrender, he threatens him by writing part of his name in the notebook as a warning, but during the standoff he is critically wounded by a barrage of submachine-gun fire from the mafia member Jose, who had been feigning death and lying on the floor.
- Those wounds prove fatal, and he ultimately dies.

## 6.9. Matsuda Tota:
- As a member of Japan's Kira Investigation Headquarters, he doesn't think deeply, and his actions and youthful enthusiasm aren't backed up by experience. He's often looked down on by people at the Kira Investigation Headquarters; he's ordinary himself, but because those around him are exceptionally intelligent, he's openly treated like a fool.

## 6.10. Ukita Hirokazu:
- One of the members of the Japanese Kira investigation headquarters investigating the Kira case.
- He personally tried to enter the Sakura TV building to stop the airing of a videotape alleged to have been sent by Kira, but was killed—dying of a heart attack—after his name was written in the notebook by Amane Misa, who had been watching nearby with the Shinigami's eyes, becoming the first casualty of the Japanese Kira investigation headquarters.

## 6.11. Mogi Kanzo:
- One of the last figures to remain at Japan's Kira Investigation Headquarters. 
- Normally taciturn but quietly gets his work done; in contrast, when he played the role of Amane Misa's manager he used his solid build and looks to portray a bright, positive, and energetic manager as if he came from the athletic world. 
- After Matsuda Tota, who had been posing as Amane Misa's manager, was made to appear to have died in an accident, he became her manager when Misa became the Yotsuba Group's exclusive model as part of an internal investigation supporting the case. Afterwards, when Yagami Light and Misa came under suspicion again, he was assigned to monitor Misa, and when she joined NHN to support the investigation he again acted as her manager — in short, he ended up being involved with Misa many times.
- Misa calls Mogi "Mojjing."
