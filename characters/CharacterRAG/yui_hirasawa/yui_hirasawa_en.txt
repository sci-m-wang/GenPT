# 3. Features of Yui:
- She handles guitar and vocals for After School Tea Time, and the instrument she uses is a Gibson Les Paul Standard, a pre-2008 model. It's an expensive guitar that would be hard for a high school student to buy outright, but thanks to Mugi-chan she was able to get it for 50,000 yen. The color is Heritage Sunburst.
- Yui's education: Sakuragaoka High School — 1st year Class 3 → 2nd year Class 2 → 3rd year Class 2 → N Women's University, Faculty of Education, 1st year
- She often participates in lyric writing. Songs she wrote lyrics for thinking of her younger sister Ui include "U&I", "Rice Is a Side Dish", and for Azusa the song "I Met an Angel", among others.
- Yui alternates between genius and fool, showing a variety of sides. And in that process, even if she seems slow, she steadily grows and occasionally shows signs of maturing.

## 3.1. Appearance:
- Yui has a brown bob and wears a yellow hairpin on the right side of her hair. She wore a sky-blue hairpin in middle school, but afterwards she wears a yellow one. This hairpin is something Yui is attached to, so she feels incomplete without it.
- When Yui was in kindergarten she had her hair tied up upward like an onion; in early elementary she wore it tied to the side; in upper elementary it changed to twin side-tails; then from middle school onward she settled into a stable short-cut hairstyle. She changed her hairpin when she entered high school.
- Although her hairstyle changes often, Yui never wears a style that exposes her forehead. Because her forehead is rather wide, she really hates it being exposed. Even when she had a major accident right before graduation photos—botching her bangs—Ritsu suggested smoothing it over with a headband, but Yui refused, saying she couldn't do that.
- When wearing her uniform, she doesn't wear pantyhose with the summer uniform, but she does wear pantyhose with the winter uniform.
- For casual clothes she likes bright colors and items with lots of cute decorations, and she evenly wears shirts & pants, shirts & skirts, and dresses; Ui is similar too.

## 3.2. Personality:
- Yui isn't shy around strangers and has a very sociable personality. She would even casually strike up conversations with members of punk-style bands she first met at live houses. She also chatted freely with classmates she had just met.
- When Yui first joined the light music club, she casually ate the refreshments Tsumugi put out, and today she told Nodoka she probably couldn't go home together because she had to go to club activities, saying, "Today Mugi said she'd bring lots of snacks." Mugi is gentle and playful, so she became close to Yui.
- Judging by how quietly popular Yui is with her classmates in the classroom, it's probably because she's kind and easily gets along with others. However, she can also be excessively insensitive and exasperating, not worrying even when she inconveniences others.
- Despite her pronounced airheadedness, the friends around her get along with her well. Basically she's good-natured and bright and kind to everyone.
- Nodoka said of Yui, "She does things that are beyond words, but seeing that harmless smile puts me at ease," and the other light music club members agreed.
- Yui has a my‑pace temperament and isn't easily embarrassed; she can wear clothes that would be embarrassing to others and remain unfazed. For that reason she readily goes along with Yamanaka Sawako's cosplay hobby.

## 3.3. Airhead (Dojikko):

- According to Nodoka, when Yui concentrates on one thing she can’t even see anything else. For example, Nodoka once said that when they were in kindergarten Yui spent the whole afternoon catching crayfish by a nearby stream and filled Nodoka’s bathtub at home, she once played with a turtle and ended up being late for kindergarten roll-call, and she once drew an apple with a crayon and tried to eat the red crayon because it looked tasty.
- Yui brought a solid powdered curry (only powder) to a group camping trip instead of a retort pouch curry with meat and vegetables. She also tried to make takoyaki but forgot to bring the octopus.
- As a child, Yui announced in front of everyone with a smile that her future dream was “I want to become a kindergarten teacher so I can play a lot with children.”
- According to Grandma Tomi Ichimonji, when Yui remembers one thing she forgets everything else, and Yui has taken that as a compliment all this time.
- Mio said Yui memorizes one chord and forgets three. Yui jokingly replied, “What? You pretended not to notice when I fell and my underwear showed…”
- Yui’s mother once said, “Yui sleeps well anywhere.” Yui also took that as a compliment. She’s a positive person.
- Maybe because she can’t read the mood, Yui casually fires off blunt remarks, but she herself seems unaware when those remarks hit the target.
- Once on a school trip in Kyoto Yui got lost and called her underclassman Azunyan at school to ask what she should do.
- While traveling with the light music club members, Yui saw Mt. Fuji, took a photo with her phone, and sent it to her younger sister Ui with the message, “Inside Mt. Fuji right now, I can see the Shinkansen.”
- Yui usually oversleeps. Sometimes, when she decides she has to do something, she gets up absurdly early. Over her high school years she gradually started oversleeping less.
- Yui is lazy and enjoys loafing around at home. After joining the light music club she became absorbed in the guitar, but before that playing dead was her daily routine. On holidays she can be glued to the kotatsu and not come out.
- Yui joined the light music club without knowing how to play any instrument. They didn’t have proper instruments, so at Ritsu’s suggestion the members got part-time jobs to save up with Yui, but they still came up short; later, with Mugi’s help they were able to buy a guitar at a much cheaper price. Even though a friend helped buy the guitar, it was still a nuisance for the shop.
- In her third year of high school, not wanting to run the marathon she made a bunch of teru teru bozu to bring rain and hung them all outside her house. When she fell during the marathon and scraped her knee a little, she went to neighbor Grandma Tomi Ichimonji’s house to be treated and leisurely drank tea with yokan. The light music club members, Ui, and Sawako searched for Yui for quite a while without knowing this. When Ui heard Yui had disappeared just before the checkpoint, she immediately knew where Yui was. Because Sawako and the club members split up to search, Sawako couldn’t find Yui and ended up going to a mountain where wild boars come out.
- When Yui entered university she fell asleep from the very entrance ceremony, drooled on Akira Wada’s suit — whom she was seeing for the first time — and confused the president and Nodoka in her dream. She tried to ask him for a light music club flyer but shouted right into Akira’s ear, and in the dormitory, when Akira came to complain about her making noise, she clung to him calling him a friend. Akira’s friends Yoshida Ayame and Hayashi Sachi gave up trying to intervene. Akira didn’t like Yui at first, but they gradually became close.
- After becoming a university student, Yui moved out of her home and lived in the dorm with Ritsu, Mio, and Tsumugi. Thanks to that, Ui had more time and was able to join the light music club with Sakurako. Of course, even as a college student Yui still loafs around her dorm when she has no classes. Once she didn’t oversleep and spent practice focused, throwing tsukkomi at others, but it turned out she had a fever and those actions were done unconsciously.

### 3.3.1. But actually a genius?:
- Yui is usually lazy, but when she becomes interested in something she shows frightening concentration, ignoring everything else and producing incredible results.
- Early in her activities in the light music club she was a musical novice who couldn't even read sheet music, yet during midterms she memorized all the guitar chords, and even turned a failed midterm into a perfect 100 on the retake by cramming. However, when she remembers one thing she tends to forget three, so when she focused on studying she ended up forgetting the guitar chords she had once mastered and had to relearn them.
- Before the first-year training camp she had supposedly forgotten all the guitar chords, but at the camp she listened to Death Devil's killer performance recording tape and, while the other members were discouraged, she nearly exactly replicated the guitar parts she heard on the tape, shocking Ritsu and the others.
- Later, in her third year Yui arrived at school an hour early on the first day like she did in first year and practiced guitar alone in the clubroom. To her right was a piece of bread with jam on it. Then, after going with the light music club members to check class assignments, she flopped over by herself and cried, "Waa~ I fell..."
- After performing live she would collapse exhausted, tongue sticking out, face-down on the desk. Although her lazy habits never completely disappeared, Yui gradually improved.
- After returning from the graduation trip to London, she wrote lyrics for a song to play for Azusa with the other light music club members and also handled the finishing touches.
- In first year Mio didn't want to be the vocalist, so after special training from Sawako Yui was able to sing while playing guitar, but she worked too hard and strained her voice, so Mio took over as vocalist. In second year she forgot the lyrics and Mio continued singing in her place, but since then Yui hasn't forgotten lyrics during live shows.
- Yui has perfect pitch. She's bad with gadgets, so until she saw Azusa's tuner she didn't even know what a tuner was and just played by feel. During the rainy season someone said the guitar strings had loosened, and when she checked the octave tuning she said it was lowered by a half of a half of a half (1/8) tone — Mio and Azusa actually measured it with a tuner and it was exactly that. Seeing Yui making little effort, Azusa muttered, "Her talent is going to waste..."
- Yui's academic grades are wildly inconsistent. In first year she failed math with 12 points but got a perfect 100 on the retake; in the second semester finals and the first-semester finals of second year she barely passed to avoid failing, then in the second semester of second year she scored higher than Mio in every subject. Nodoka once told Mio that Yui, once she gets started, can reach an amazing level.
- In her third year Yui didn't give much thought to college entrance exams, but after being accepted to her third-choice school she was also accepted to her first-choice private N Women's University where she had taken the exam. Her test scores are extremely up and down, but sometimes she outperforms Mio.

## 3.5. MC Ability:
- Yui is fairly decent at MCing during lives. At the freshman live she told her story about joining — she’d thought the percussion section was where you play castanets — and spun it in a somewhat funny way to encourage people to join without pressure. Seeing this, Ritsu was impressed, but Yui couldn’t find the right moment to wrap up and went off on tangents, saying things like “Sawa-chan said castanets are hard,” or “Sawa-chan is the music teacher’s nickname.”
- At the final live, Yui shows herself to be relatively proficient at keeping up her lines and introducing the members. Still, she also gets sidetracked as usual—while talking about the play she dragged on about her role as a tree. At the end she even had the line, “After-school tea time is always after-school.”
- Yui does all this ad-lib in front of large crowds without preparing scripts or the like. She doesn’t get nervous in front of many people, and thanks to her natural affability she can smoothly keep the banter going on stage. She even turns mistakes into comedy. However, because she’s underprepared she sometimes repeats herself, forgets the next song, goes off on tangents and rambles, uses words unfamiliar to people outside the members (e.g., Sawa-chan, Azu-nyan, Ton-chan), or speaks in ways others can’t understand.

### 6.1.1. Episode 1 「廃部!」- Disbanded!:
- On an early April morning when the entrance ceremony at Sakuragaoka Girls' High School is being held, Hirasawa Yui is woken by her younger sister Hirasawa Ui. She gets up, says she'll be late, hurriedly puts on her school uniform, but slips on her butt, grabs a piece of toast with jam in her mouth, and rushes off to school.
- Running, she returns a greeting to an aunt who says hello, pets a stray cat, and when she arrives at school she finds it's only 8:10, before the entrance ceremony starts. Having arrived not late, Yui is excited thinking, "I'm a high school student now."
- But two weeks after entering school, Yui hasn't decided to join any club. Her best friend Manabe Nodoka shocks her by saying she'll end up unemployed if she does nothing, which startles Yui into wondering if there's something she could do. She can't decide right away, saying she's bad at sports and useless at cultural activities so she doesn't know what to do.
- That same day, Tainaka Ritsu suggests joining the Light Music Club to her close friend Akiyama Mio, but Mio is already on her way to submit an application to the Literary Club. Ritsu rips up Mio's application, and dragging the indignant Mio to the staff room, tells music teacher Yamanaka she wants to join the Light Music Club.
- But according to Teacher Yamanaka, the Light Music Club has lost all its existing members to graduation, and if they don't gather four members by the end of April, the club will be disbanded.
- After finishing their talk, Teacher Yamanaka leaves the staff room to prepare for the next class and happens to meet Yui, who has come to pick up some handouts. Yui, Mio, and Ritsu make eye contact. Yui, looking at Mio and Ritsu, takes the handouts when Yamanaka calls, but gets nervous from the exchanged glances and drops the handouts on the floor; while picking them up with Yamanaka, she bangs her head under the desk. Seeing that, Ritsu decides Yui is a scatterbrain.
- After Yui and Teacher Yamanaka leave the staff room, Mio, having finished her business, says she'll go join the Literary Club, but Ritsu, seeing that the Light Music Club has no members left, thinks that if they join now she could become the club president and drags Mio, who had planned to go to the Literary Club, into the Light Music Club.
- Ritsu and Mio decide to wait for new members in the music room, which serves as their clubroom. In the afternoon a girl with thick eyebrows and blond hair comes for a tour.
- Ritsu immediately invites the girl to join, and Mio, saying it's rude to tell a stranger to join out of the blue, tries to leave the music room. Ritsu retorts that they watched a performance together in middle school and formed a band — Ritsu on drums and Mio on bass — and accuses Mio of being stingy. Mio protests she didn't actually go to a performance and might be misremembering what she saw on TV. Ritsu insists anyway that Mio will join, and when Ritsu says, "When we go pro later, we'll split the pay 70:30," Mio replies "Don't lie" and gives Ritsu a light flick on the forehead.
- The girl, amused by the two, laughs and says she only knows how to play keyboard but asks to be treated well, then introduces herself as Kotobuki Tsumugi. Ritsu is pleased and says they only need a guitarist now, but apart from Mio, who Ritsu brought, and newly enrolled Tsumugi, no one else comes that day.
- That afternoon, walking home with Nodoka, Yui is still worried because she wants to join a club but doesn't know what to do. When Nodoka says, "Just join one, right?" Yui clings to Nodoka and whines, "But I don't know what I should do!", and Nodoka sighs while stroking Yui's head.
- Meanwhile, Ritsu and the others hold a meeting at a hamburger shop about how to attract new members. Mugi, who has come to a fast-food place for the first time, mixes her fries into Ritsu's fries while Ritsu and Mio talk and watches with fascination. She then suggests, "What if we told recruits we'd give them a car or a vacation home?" Ritsu suggests, "Or buy them ice cream, or help them with homework?" but Mio worries people wouldn't join for reasons like that.
- As time passes and Ritsu gets bored, Mio snaps, "What if you get tired of it first?" Following Mugi's suggestion, they decide to make recruitment flyers. The next day, among the three flyers they brought, Ritsu's messy handwriting and Mio's writing that is too small are rejected, and Mugi's flyer is chosen. Still, by the end of April no one has joined, and Mio worries they won't be able to form a band and the club will be disbanded. Ritsu shouts that she doesn't want that.
- At that moment, Yui sees the Light Music Club's recruitment flyer and, thinking the club might play simple music like castanets, remembers being praised for playing castanets well in kindergarten and submits an application. At lunchtime, Nodoka, hearing that Yui applied to a club, asks if such a club exists and takes Yui to where the flyer is. Nodoka asks if it's a place that plays things like guitar. Yui panics, "What? I can't play things like guitar..." When Nodoka asks if she knows any instrument, Yui says "castanets," and Nodoka sighs.
- Ritsu and the others are delighted to learn from Teacher Yamanaka that Yui submitted a membership application and that they have four members now, wondering, "I wonder what Hirasawa Yui is like?" After school, Yui walks down a spooky corridor where the Horror Club meets and heads to the Light Music Clubroom. As she passes the Horror Research Club, the Occult Circle, and the Yokai Club, a mysterious girl peeks her head out from a door gap behind a frightened Yui. Standing at the clubroom door, Yui imagines opening the door and Krauser II appearing, shouting, "What?! You want to quit?! Do you think you'll be safe after quitting?! KILL!!" and is scared.
- Yui, who didn't really know about the Light Music Club, is frightened in advance... Standing alone in front of the clubroom, she cries and pleads when Ritsu speaks to her, insisting she's not like that. Once inside, when it becomes clear she can't handle instruments at all, she embarrassingly says she'll quit, crying that she joined on a whim, got her hopes up, and doesn't know how to apologize. But the members, worried that losing the fourth member would leave them short-handed, offer Yui cake because she likes snacks and then play "Please Give Me Wings." After the performance, Yui exclaims, "You're not very good! But somehow you looked like you were having fun," and officially joins the Light Music Club that day, deciding to learn how to play guitar. Ritsu borrows Mio's camera to take a commemorative photo, but because of the angle only Ritsu's forehead is in the picture.
- The next day at lunch, Nodoka, told by Yui that she formally joined the Light Music Club, says since Yui doesn't have a guitar she'll need to buy a new one and worries if Yui can do well. After the meal, while Mio, Ritsu, and Mugi prepare for practice, Yui hums a tune in the classroom with a big smile.

### 6.1.2. Episode 2 「楽器!」 - Instruments!:
- A few days after Yui joined the light music club, Nodoka asked her to go home together after school, but Yui insisted she absolutely had to go to the clubroom today and told her to go ahead. Nodoka thought that at last Yui had found something she wanted to do — she wasn’t sure whether that was a happy or sad thing — and when she heard from Yui that Mugi had brought a bunch of delicious snacks, she sighed and said, “Weren’t you going to practice guitar in the clubroom?”
- When Yui went up to the clubroom she asked Mio, Mugi, and Ritsu why they had joined the light music club.
- Yui felt that Mio gave off a grown-up vibe. Mio told Yui that she plays bass instead of guitar because the guitar is the center of the band and she’d be embarrassed to stand in front of everyone; just imagining that made her blush and feel frustrated.
- About Tsumugi, Yui thought she seemed calm and laid-back, so cute — her nickname is Mugi (ムギ). When Yui asked when she learned keyboards, Mugi said she started piano at four and had even won prizes at competitions. When Yui learned that Mugi would bring tea, cake, and tableware, she was surprised.
- Yui thought Ritsu was cheerful and energetic. When Yui told her the drums suited her well, Ritsu said other instruments didn’t fit her personality but the drums did.
- After hearing the three of them, Yui thought, “You can tell someone’s personality just by why they chose their instrument.”
- While drinking tea, Yui asked Mugi why she’d wanted to join the light music club instead of the choir, and Mugi smiled and said she wanted friends who were fun and cheerful — people she wouldn’t normally meet. Seeing that, Mio and Ritsu muttered together that they were the odd ones out, and then Mio asked Yui, “Hirasawa, did you buy a guitar yet?” Yui asked Mio to just call her “Yui” since she’d already called Mio “Mio.” Mio was embarrassed but agreed. However, Yui hadn’t bought a guitar yet and was shocked to learn she would need at least 10,000–50,000 yen. The club members decided to go to the music store together on the weekend.
- Back home, with Ui’s help, Yui got a 50,000-yen advance from her mother and met the club members who had arrived early and were waiting. They petted a dog by a crosswalk, browsed a clothing store, a toy shop, and a snack shop, and at the music store they eyed a 250,000-yen Les Paul guitar but couldn’t buy it because of the price. Watching that, Mio told a story about when she had a similar experience buying her bass, and Ritsu said she shouted at the shopkeeper to make it cheaper. While Tsumugi admired their stories, Mio’s attention was drawn to a left-handed guitar.
- To raise money for Yui’s guitar, the light music club members all got part-time jobs. Shy at first about what to do, Mio decided she couldn’t sit out when everyone else was trying to help Yui and said she’d join. Ritsu found a traffic survey job counting passing vehicles.
- On the first day of the traffic-survey job they worked in pairs: Yui & Mugi, Mio & Ritsu; on the second day Yui & Ritsu, Mio & Mugi. They took turns measuring traffic volume and ate the bentos they’d brought and the bento Ui had made. Mugi wore a wide-brimmed hat and Ritsu a narrow-brimmed hat; the members working outside passed the hats around, so Mio wore Mugi’s hat and Yui wore Ritsu’s.
- The members who worked for two days earned 8,000 yen per day, totaling 16,000 yen, and when all amounts were added it came to 64,000 yen; even combined with the 50,000-yen advance Yui had received, the total was only 114,000 yen, still short. Yui decided she’d buy a guitar that fit her means, returned the part-time pay to her light music club friends, and planned to buy a cheap guitar, but when she came to the music store again she fell for the guitar’s charm once more.
- Watching that, Mugi asked the clerk for a discount, and the clerk, seeing Mugi’s eyebrows, realized she was the owner’s daughter and was surprised. Thanks to Mugi shouting, “Make it cheaper~!” Yui bought the Les Paul guitar for just 50,000 yen.
- Back home, Yui sat on a chair like a hobbyhorse in her room and rocked back and forth with delight, ad-libbed while holding the guitar, and seeing herself in the mirror exclaimed, “ミュージシャンっっぽい!!” She muttered to herself, “I should practice my autograph!” and Ui scolded her for being noisy.
- The next morning when Ui came to wake her sister, Yui was sleeping with the guitar tucked beside her. In the clubroom the members praised how well she fit in, but she still didn’t know how to play guitar.
- When Ritsu peeled the protective film off the guitar, Yui was momentarily startled, but Mugi gave her a cookie and she calmed down, thanking Ritsu and saying a guitar should definitely be played. Following Mio’s instructions, Yui plugged the cable into the amp and the guitar and played, and Ritsu shouted they should hold a Budokan-style live before graduation, but first Yui and the members needed to practice. Yui decided to practice and unplugged the cable, but she hadn’t turned off the amp, so the amp’s sound rang out.

### 6.1.3. Episode 3 「特訓!」- Special Training!:
- One day before the midterms, Yui was practicing in the clubroom and asked Ritsu if it wouldn't be scary to get cut by a guitar string. Ritsu said that if you're not careful your finger could be cut and blood would gush out. But Mio, who's afraid of scary stories and tales about wounds, heard Ritsu and crouched in a corner, covered her ears and screamed. When Yui said there wouldn't be any blood, Mio cleared her throat once and said it would be fine once calluses formed. Then, when Yui touched Mio's fingers and said they were soft, Mio blushed and got embarrassed.
- That afternoon, on the way home Yui ran into Nodoka, learned the midterms were coming up and got flustered, but she muttered she'd be okay. In the end Yui got 12 points on the midterm and failed, and she was the only member of the light music club and of her class who had to take a make-up exam.
- Yui was comforted by Ritsu and Mugi about failing, but Ritsu got annoyed when Yui admitted she hadn't studied. When Ritsu asked why she hadn't studied, Yui said she was so absorbed practicing guitar she couldn't study, but she had properly learned the chords. When asked about grades, Ritsu pulled out a math test scored 89, and Yui muttered that it wasn't like Ritsu. It turned out Ritsu had also crammed the night before at Mio's request, and Mio and Mugi had gotten better scores.
- A few days later, a week before the make-up exam, Yui was in the clubroom after school as usual, but at Sakuragaoka school rules forbid students who failed from taking part in club activities, so strictly speaking Yui shouldn't have been there. When Ritsu pointed that out, Yui playfully said she just came briefly to grab some snacks, but Ritsu said that made no sense and put Yui in a headlock.
- If Yui dropped out the light music club might again fall below the required number and be disbanded. So Yui tried to stay home and study instead of coming to the clubroom, but she couldn't concentrate and ended up smiling and playing guitar or just killing time goofing off.
- Meanwhile Mio, Ritsu and Mugi texted Yui to encourage her but weren't sure she'd actually study. Mio and Mugi sent ordinary encouragement messages, but Ritsu tried to record a real-time video of herself tossing a potato chip high and catching it in her mouth to send to Yui — and ended up spilling the opened cola on the desk.
- In the end, unable to focus, Yui asked Mio to teach her the day before the make-up exam, and the group gathered at Yui's house. That day Yui's parents were out for work, so only Yui and Ui were home; the group, who didn't know Ui, had vaguely assumed Yui's sister would be like Yui, but they were surprised by Ui's polite, kind manner. Ritsu teased Yui, asking if her sister had absorbed all of Yui's good qualities.
- While eating the homemade snacks Ui had brought, the group learned Ui was a third-year middle schooler who wanted to come to Sakuragaoka, and they suggested she could just learn from her sister — but Ui said she could do it herself. Meanwhile Yui was putting on an act in front of Ui.
- Anyway, the light music club decided to focus on Yui's studying and Mio taught her. Yui was bored, but when Mugi said they'd have cake during the break she paid attention. Ritsu, unable to endure the boredom, laughed at a book on the shelf and got flicked on the head by Mio for being noisy, ending up with a bump; she then tickled Yui's feet, got another bump, and was kicked out of the room. When she tried to come back in she burst through the door noisily, flopped while getting up, and Mio smacked her again for being loud.
- Meanwhile Nodoka came with a packed lunch to see Yui, and told them that when she had a cold in middle school Yui had brought her the handouts for class — and that Yui's score back then had been 10 points.
- Mio also brought up that Ritsu had had a similar thing happen before and they chattered for a while, then after Nodoka left they refocused on studying. Around 9 p.m. Yui dozed off and dreamed of going on a polar expedition with the club. In the dream, Mio as expedition leader woke Yui along with Mugi, warning they'd freeze to death if they slept, and Ritsu was outside the log-cabin lodge, in an igloo, warming herself by a fire with a snowman labeled “Yui (唯).” When Mio, depressed and saying she couldn't do it by her own strength, and even tearful Mugi appeared, the real-world Yui, talking in her sleep, cried as she wished Ritsu luck.
- Fortunately Yui was able to solve the problems well, and since it was late the other members left first; at that time Ritsu was playing video games in front of the TV with Ui.
- The next day the club members anxiously waited for Yui, who was taking the make-up exam. Mio paced by the desk, Ritsu munched snacks and echoed Mio's worries but only got nagged, and Mugi, with a blank look, poured tea into a cup while the green tea overflowed. After receiving her answer sheet, Yui left the classroom, ran into Teacher Yamanaka who encouraged her, and returned to the clubroom. Yui had been agonizing over factoring and reworking factored expressions, but luckily she passed with a perfect score of 100 and they took a commemorative photo. However this time she had forgotten the guitar chords and had to relearn them from the beginning.

### 6.1.4. Episode 4 「合宿!」- Training Camp!:
- While cleaning the classroom, Mio picks up a camera and, taking pictures, happens upon an old tape of the light music club's band (Death Devil). After hearing Death Devil's live tape, Mio suggests they go on a training camp to prepare for the school festival after summer break. Ritsu, who was arguing with Yui about what they should do at the festival and then got a flick on the head from Mio, complains "Why are you hitting only me?", and Mio says since they're the light music club they should do a live performance. Just then Mugi comes to the clubroom, and when Ritsu asks where they'll hold it, Mio asks Mugi if she has a villa or something. Mugi readily answers she does, so the training camp location is decided to be Mugi's beach villa.
- The next day Yui, who had been sleeping in Uui's pajamas, is late but manages to join them safely. At Mugi's beach villa the group unpacks, and Mio plays the radio with the old light music club tape and says she was inspired by that tape. Yui and Ritsu, who had already changed into swimsuits, beg to start with playing in the water, and when even Mugi follows, Mio puts down her bags and shouts, "I'll come too!"
- Arriving first on the beach, Yui and Ritsu role-play being stranded on a deserted island, then run into Mio, who has come out in a black bikini. Jealous at Mio's chest, Ritsu shouts "Take that! (喰らえ!)" and throws a ball, then goes to swim with Yui. Mio, hit by the ball, mutters "Ouch..." but still admires the beautiful scenery.
- Mugi builds a sandcastle like a Western-style castle, Yui buries herself in the sand leaving only her head showing drawn into a bird picture on the sand, and Mio gets scared and crouches down when she sees a rock covered in barnacles, then is further startled when Ritsu tells a scary story. The club members have fun until sunset, and Mio plays hard too, then suddenly realizes with a start that they haven't practiced.
- Tired from playing and drowsy after dinner, Yui and Ritsu sit on the practice room floor chatting, say good night to each other, and try to fall asleep. Seeing them, Mio brings her bass and amp, sets them in front of the two, and plays to wake them up, teasing Ritsu about needing to diet so she'll play the drums. When Yui complains the guitar is heavy, Mio says, "Then you should've bought a lighter guitar..." and when Yui playfully asks who suggested buying this guitar, Mio points out, "You did."
- After a while lying down again, the two imagine how well Mio would look in a maid outfit. In their imagination, Mio wears a maid outfit and goes "Moe moe~~ kyun❤️". Ritsu then shouts "Moe moe kyun" and, trying to pass it off as a joke, fumbles — only to get bopped by Mio.
- At night Yui plays the old light music club's song in front of the fountain, and fireworks set off by Mugi explode behind her; Mio is so moved by the sight that she cries. Ritsu admires it and says she wishes things would be like this when they hold a Budokan live later, and Mio remembers when she decided to form a band with Ritsu and when they set Budokan as their goal.
- Mio then plays the old light music club's recorded tape, but of all things the voice from the radio shouts, "We've been waiting for youuuu!" which startles everyone, and especially Mio, who crouches in a corner muttering, "I can't see, I can't hear..." Feeling mischievous, Ritsu tells the tale of the "knee-hollow ghost" to scare Mio, which of course makes Mio even more frightened and she sobs. Seeing this, Yui and Mugi point out that Ritsu went too far, and feeling guilty Ritsu apologizes to Mio and, together with the others, tries to comfort her. When Mio, told it's not real, asks if that's true and turns her head, Yui and Ritsu, seeing Mio's tear-streaked, flushed face, both do a "moe moe kyun" on her.
- Back in the practice room Mio is still crying, and Ritsu apologizes again. Meanwhile Yui plays the song she had performed earlier, though she hasn't quite mastered the guitar technique yet. In the open-air bath, Yui thanks Mio, saying that today's performance and everyone having fun happened because Mio was the one who first brought up the idea of a training camp; when Mio gets shy, Ritsu teases her.

### 6.1.5. Episode 5 「顧問!」- Advisor!:
- One day after the training camp, Ritsu shows Yui, who has come into the clubroom, an album full of photos of the old light music club (Death Devil), and Yui's gaze is drawn to the long-haired girl in the pictures.
- While Yui sits on a long bench practicing guitar, Mio and Ritsu are discussing songs when Yui accidentally nicks a fingertip on a string; Mio, startled, crouches in the corner screaming. While Ritsu teases Mio, Yui asks for a bandage and happens to meet Yamanaka-sensei, who has come to borrow a music stand; Yamanaka-sensei disinfects Yui's finger and puts on a bandage.
- Mugi, passing by the hallway, sees this and watches from across as Yamanaka-sensei tells Yui that since calluses haven't formed yet she might cut it again, and that once calluses form she'll play the guitar better — Mugi's eyes sparkle. Yui asks, "Sensei, did you play guitar by any chance?" but Yamanaka-sensei says that it wasn't her but a friend who played, and when Yui thinks the teacher is a nice person her eyes meet Mugi's, who was hiding behind a door.
- Actually, Mugi had gone to the faculty room earlier to ask whether the light music club could use the stage at the school festival, but because the light music club hadn't been formally registered as an extracurricular club she couldn't get permission to use it. Ritsu, Yui, and Mugi worry about whether they can use the music room freely and eventually go to ask Nodoka, a student council officer. Ritsu says she received the club registration form last spring and will submit it herself, but they learn the light music club isn't an official club because she forgot to submit it that day while eating cake. Meanwhile, Mio has been crouched trembling in the corner the whole time.
- Fortunately, Nodoka fills out the paperwork for them and the light music club begins the formal registration process, but the members learn they don't have an advisor and all go to find Yamanaka-sensei. When Ritsu holds a textbook up to her face like a telescope, a startled Sawako is asked to be the light music club's advisor, but she refuses, saying she is the advisor for the brass band and can't take on a second post. Then Yui asks, "Sensei, you're also a graduate of this school, right?" and when Yui says she saw a photo in the old light music club album, Sawako stiffens, hurriedly runs to clear the photos from the clubroom, but by the time she gets there Ritsu has already taken the photos.
- When Yui turns on a radio with a tape in it, a voice shouts, "I've been waiting for you guys to come!" Mio is terrified, Sawako embarrassed, and they crouch together in the corner. When Yui hands Sawako the guitar, Sawako takes off her glasses and plays as if possessed, then shouts not to use the music room without permission, collapses to the floor and laments, "When I became a teacher after graduation I pushed a refined image..." and proceeds to tell them old stories.
- Sawako had graduated several years before the members entered Sakurago and had been a member of the old light music club. At the time, she had a crush on a male student and confessed, but he told her, "I like wild girls," so she got into heavy metal. Contrary to Sawako's expectations, the boy didn't like her heavy-metal look — the clothes, makeup, and demon mask — and eventually dumped her. A few years after graduating she returned to Sakurago as a teacher.
- After hearing Sawako's story, Ritsu promises not to tell the others and asks her to be their advisor; while Yui praises Ritsu as reliable, Mugi blushes and stares at Sawako and Ritsu holding the textbook like a telescope. Puzzled, Mio and Yui ask if she might have a cold, but Mugi says that's not it.
- In any case, Sawako accepts the role of light music club advisor, but she criticizes the members' practice and skill as still lacking, especially scolding them for not having a vocalist and asking how they expect to perform live — this is not a place to drink tea. However, she likes the cake Mugi brought, and from then on when Sawako is in the music room with the members they enjoy tea time together.
- That night, while Mio is thinking about lyrics for the song they'll use at the school festival live, she receives a text Mugi sent from her phone: "Sawako-sensei became the advisor — my heart's fluttering after all."
- The next day, Mio has brought lyrics titled ふわふわ時間 and hesitates whether to show them to the others, when Ritsu, Yui, and Mugi all argue that they should see them first — because Ritsu is the club president, because Yui and Mugi are friends who understand Mio, and because Ritsu is Mio's emotional home. Then Sawako reads the lyrics written on the paper, and curious Ritsu looks at them from beside Sawako.
- ふわふわ時間 lyrics:

キミを見てるといつもハ一トDOKI☆DOKI (When I look at you, my heart always goes thump☆thump)
ゆれる思いはマシュマロみたいにふわ☆ふわ (My fluttering heart is like a marshmallow, fluffy☆fluffy)

- Sawako and Ritsu, having read up to that point, twisted in their seats saying it gave them goosebumps, and Ritsu scratched her back. When Mio worried, “Are these lyrics really too much?” Ritsu said it wasn’t that, just unexpected for her image. But Yui and Mugi admired that it was really well written, and Mugi looked at Yui—who had been praising Mio—with sparkling eyes. Ritsu asked Sawako for her opinion too, and Sawako, thinking that if it was a cute, adorable song written by Mio it would help her own image, decided on Mio’s song. 
- The problem was who would take the vocals: Mio refused, asking how she could sing a song like that, so Yui took the vocals and received special training from Sawako. As Mugi blushed while staring at Sawako taking Yui away, Mio, who’d been sitting next to Ritsu, whispered to Ritsu, “Could it be that Mugi likes Teacher Sawako?” When Ritsu asked if it was true, Mio covered Ritsu’s mouth, and Mugi said it wasn’t that, she just thought it looked nice seeing two women together. While Mio felt relieved, when Ritsu asked Mugi if that was okay, Mugi replied, “If they’re happy with it, wouldn’t that be fine?” which embarrassed Mio and Ritsu. 
- Three days before the school festival, Nodoka announced that the light music club had been approved as an official club and would be able to hold a live show, but this time, because Yui’s voice had gone hoarse from practicing too hard, Mio, who had written the lyrics, took the vocals. Mio couldn’t bear the embarrassment, blushed, and fainted.

### 6.1.6. Episode 6 「学園祭!」- School Festival!:
- On the morning of the school festival three days later, Mio went to the clubroom and found it empty. She looked for everyone, but they were all busy and couldn't come practice right away. Yui's class, First Year Class 3, had set up a yakisoba stall called "YakiYaki," and hoarse-voiced Yui, being on her first shift, had to fry the yakisoba.
- Ritsu, Mio & Mugi's class set up a haunted house called "Love-Love Nightmare House." Ritsu had strongly pushed for a maid café as a festival plan, but Mio refused out of embarrassment, so they came up with the haunted house idea instead.
- Ritsu proposed the plan and sold tickets at the entrance, and since Mugi was inside playing a ghost, she couldn't come practice either. Ritsu urged Mio to go in, saying Mugi was inside, and watched Mio enter with a lecherous grin, pleased that things were going according to plan. Mio, who hated scary things and hadn’t known Mugi was the ghost, screamed when she saw her.
- Meanwhile, Nodoka, wearing a police cap and an armband with the kanji 集行委 (executive committee), was patrolling the school and found Yui. She told her the show started at 3:00 and they should practice as much as possible while they had time. Nodoka had arranged a substitute to cover Yui’s yakisoba shift, so Yui dashed to the music room to practice, and Nodoka smiled watching her go. After their shifts, Ritsu and Mugi followed Yui to the clubroom and practiced with Mio. Meanwhile, Sawako had brought goth-loli outfits, and Mio worried, "Do we really have to perform wearing those?"
- After lunch the group moved instruments and equipment to the live room; while Yui struggled and groaned, Mugi hummed and carried things without difficulty. Seeing Nodoka handling student affairs, Yui felt excited that the Light Music Club’s first performance was drawing near.
- When Yui arrived, Mio hadn't shown up yet—only Ritsu and Mugi were there—so Yui asked when they'd become friends with Mio and when Mio had become so shy. Ritsu said they'd been friends since elementary school and that even back then Mio would get embarrassed when people said things like "Your hair is pretty!" or "You're left-handed!" Yui pointed out that it was because of Ritsu.
- Just then Mio arrived and, unusually bright, said she couldn't tremble forever, but she still couldn't completely shake it off—her hand holding a teacup trembled—and she whined to Ritsu that she didn't want to be the vocalist. Ritsu brought up doing the MC and demonstrated introducing the members. She jokingly called Mio the Light Music Club's boss who hates scary things, the "Dangerous Queen," and Mio smacked her, asking who she was calling dangerous. This model has such an appealing tone that it became one of the industry standards among electric guitars, but it's notorious for its thick neck (50s) and considerable weight, so guitarists who focus on technique don't often choose it.
