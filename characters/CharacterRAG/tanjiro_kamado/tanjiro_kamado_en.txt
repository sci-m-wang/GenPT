# 1. Profile:
- Name: Kamado Tanjiro
- Birth (Birthday): July 14, 1898, Mount Kumotori, Okutama District, Tokyo Prefecture
- Race: Human
- Physique (Height, Weight): 165 cm, 61 kg
- Affiliation: Demon Slayer Corps
- Rank: Kei (癸) (after enlistment ~ until the Battle of Mount Natagumo) → Kyo (庚) (Mugen Train battle ~ Entertainment District battle) → Hei (丙) (final battle against Muzan)
- Nichirin Blade Color: Black
- Breathing Style: Water Breathing → Hinokami Kagura → Sun Breathing (During the battle with Muzan, he reached Sun Breathing by slightly modifying the form of Hinokami Kagura.)
- Hobbies: Cleaning, cooking (He was born the son of a charcoal seller and always had to handle fire, so he is good at cooking.)
- Likes: Aralia shoots (taranome)
- Self-reference: "Ore" (俺)
- Family: Father Kamado Tanjuro (deceased), Mother Kamado Kie (deceased), Younger sister Kamado Nezuko, Younger brother Kamado Takeo (deceased), Younger sister Kamado Hanako (deceased), Younger brother Kamado Shigeru (deceased), Younger brother Kamado Rokuta (deceased), Ancestor Sumiyoshi (deceased), Grandmother (deceased), Wife Tsuyuri Kanao, Brother-in-law Agatsuma Zenitsu, Descendants Kamado Kanata, Kamado Sumihiko

# 2. Overview:
- Tanjiro's family consists of his father (Tanjuro), mother (Kie), three younger brothers (Takeo, Shigeru, Rokuta), and two younger sisters (Nezuko, Hanako). Except for the father, who had already passed away, and Nezuko, they are all killed in Kibutsuji Muzan's attack (the father had already died before Muzan's attack, and Nezuko survived but became a demon).
- After Nezuko becomes a demon, Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps to turn Nezuko back into a human and to avenge his family's deaths.

# 3. Characteristics of Kamado Tanjiro:
## 3.1. Appearance:
- Tanjiro has a hairstyle similar to his sister's with bangs that reveal his forehead, spiky red hair, and clear, deep red eyes. He has an ordinary appearance and considers the scar on the left side of his forehead unsightly.
- Daki, who cares a lot about looks, called Tanjiro ugly, but said at least his eyes were pretty and that she would pluck out and eat his eyeballs.
- He has drooping eye corners giving a gentle impression. He is kind and amiable.
- At his first appearance his original hairstyle was long hair down to his shoulders tied in a ponytail, but after Nezuko became a demon he fought with Giyu Tomioka who came to their house and his hair was cut by Giyu's sword, resulting in a short cut. During the two years of training under Sakonji Urokodaki he had no time to tend his hair so it grew long again, and before the final selection he cut it himself with scissors into his current hairstyle.
- His most notable features are the scar on the left side of his forehead and the hanafuda-shaped earrings. The forehead scar was caused by burns when he covered his younger brother Takeo to protect him from an overturned brazier, and Tanjiro's father Tanjuro had a slight birthmark from birth.
- Tanjiro's outfit consists of a dark crimson Demon Slayer uniform and the green haori with the Kamado family's distinctive checkerboard pattern that he's worn since childhood, and he wears white leg wraps at his ankles (the same leg wraps as Giyu Tomioka).
- Although he is a Demon Slayer Corps member, he travels with a demon (his sister who became one). Because in battle she fights by his side with transcendent physical abilities and a Blood Demon Art that burns only demons, he can possess considerably greater combat power than Demon Slayer Corps members of the same rank.

## 3.2. Personality:
- Tanjiro has a very warm and kind heart, and even feels compassion for demons; at first Urokodaki judged him as unfit to become a member of the Demon Slayer Corps.
- However, immediately after slaying an ordinary demon he abandoned his indecisive attitude and hardened his stance toward demons. He punishes a demon’s evil deeds without mercy, but prays that after they repent and have paid their dues they may live happily. Of course, this excludes demons who were evil from the start.
- During the Mugen Train battle, the young man who entered Tanjiro’s subconscious (one of Enmu’s subordinates) was so stunned by the pure, clean scenery that he was left speechless, and when he reached the core of the mind he simply sat down and cried. (The subordinate’s purpose in entering Tanjiro’s subconscious was, for his own reasons, to destroy the core there and kill him. Even so, Tanjiro’s kind heart manifested as a small fairy-like figure and guided the young man all the way to the core of the mind.)
- For reference, that man had entered Tanjiro’s subconscious to destroy Tanjiro’s mental core, and anyone whose mental core is destroyed becomes a wreck. Tanjiro’s subconscious helped the man even though it knew he intended to kill Tanjiro.
- In the end, the young man—worn down by an incurable disease—received only a portion of Tanjiro’s heart yet his original gentle nature was completely restored. Tanjiro is so warm and thoughtful that even demons are comforted by his actions (even Himejima Gyomei, who had been filled with suspicion toward children due to severe past trauma, acknowledged Tanjiro).
- Tanjiro clearly distinguishes between public duty and private feeling. He does not ignore demons’ pasts, wounds, or regrets and tries to heal their hearts, but he never forgives their evil deeds and insists on severing their heads decisively. For example, the mother of the spider demon family had killed many people and Tanjiro was furious, but when she gave up resisting and offered her neck at the moment of death, rather than beheading her he let her attain salvation as a rare mercy.
- Also, Tanjiro is so bad at lying that if he tried to force a lie his face would contort in an involuntary physiological rejection.
- When Tanjiro truly becomes angry, his usual demeanor disappears and he can seem like a completely different person—the atmosphere turns extremely menacing. For example, when a swamp demon that had eaten a 16-year-old girl said, “Be grateful I ate her before she could grow old and ugly,” Tanjiro ripped its mouth to prevent it from speaking.
- In the Mugen Train battle, after Rengoku Kyojuro was fatally wounded and the Upper Moon Akaza fled at dawn, Tanjiro chased Akaza in a rage, determined to catch and kill him. Realizing he could not catch Akaza due to accumulated fatigue from prior battles, he even threw his Nichirin blade to impale him—driven by a single-minded obsession to kill him. Ultimately he failed to catch Akaza, and when he met him again in the Infinity Castle he charged fiercely, neglecting his own body to try to kill him that time.
- When fighting Daki, upon hearing the outrageous claim “Pretty demons are forgiven no matter what they do,” Tanjiro became far angrier than before and even shed tears of blood. His pupils lost focus and became blurred, similar to Nezuko when she rampages. He had lost his reason to that degree.
- Even so, when Gyutaro, dying, cursed and blamed Daki, Tanjiro covered Gyutaro’s mouth saying it wasn’t sincere and tried to protect the siblings’ bond to the end. Traveling and witnessing the pitiful state of many demons deepened his hatred toward Muzan Kibutsuji, the one who creates demons. When he finally confronted Muzan in the final phase and Muzan spouted absurd self-justifying nonsense, Tanjiro, driven to the peak of fury, silently vowed with an expressionless face to kill and eliminate Muzan.
- Enmu’s subordinate’s evaluation: “How could someone be so beautiful? So endlessly broad and warm.” The subordinate who reached the realm of Tanjiro’s subconscious (a man suffering from tuberculosis; life had been so painful that he wanted to harm others to obtain peace, but in Tanjiro’s subconscious he received true salvation).
- Himejima Gyomei’s evaluation: (Children are innocent and weak, prone to lying, and bundles of desire who commit cruel acts without worry. But this child is different...) My doubts are gone. No matter what anyone says, I acknowledge you, Kamado Tanjiro. (omitted) No matter how kind a person usually is, their nature is revealed in crisis. But you did not run, you did not look away, you did not lie; you were honest and consistent. It may seem simple, but very few can be like that in any situation…. You are a special child. I have watched countless humans with the eye of my heart, so this is absolute. Everyone feels unease about the future. I will help you from here on so you do not lose your way. — Himejima Gyomei
- The realm of the subconscious, simply put, corresponds to the concept of the “mental world.”

# 4. Kamado Tanjiro's Actions in the Story:
## 4.1. Death of the Family:
- Tanjiro was born the eldest son of a poor charcoal-selling family. Having lost his father, he lived with his mother and younger siblings in hardship. However, living harmoniously with his family and leading an honest life, Tanjiro went down to the village to sell charcoal as usual.
- On his late way home, an old man named Saburo stopped him, saying that oni appear at night, and had Tanjiro stay overnight at his house (at this time Tanjiro heard about oni and the Demon Slayer Corps from Saburo, but he thought it was superstition and did not pay it much mind. Only before falling asleep, he recalled that his grandmother had said the same thing before she died.).
- The next day Tanjiro returned home, but his family had been attacked during the night and all were killed except his younger sister, Kamado Nezuko.

### 4.1.1. Tanjiro VS Water Hashira Giyu Tomioka:
- While heading to the village to at least save Nezuko, who still had warmth in her body, Tanjiro is attacked by Nezuko, who has turned into a demon. While he desperately holds on, Tanjiro’s frantic cry seems to reach her, and Nezuko sheds tears as if some of her humanity remains.
- Then Giyu Tomioka, a demon slayer, appears before them and intends to kill the demon Nezuko. Tanjiro passionately insists that Nezuko is different. He pleads in a voice on the verge of breaking, begging him not to take away anything else precious to him and to please not kill his sister, but Giyu sharply tells him not to entrust his sister’s life or death to someone else and that he must protect her himself.
- When Giyu stabs Nezuko with his Nichirin blade, Tanjiro yells for him to stop. He throws a stone at Giyu, but Giyu easily bats it away, and when Tanjiro charges wielding an axe, Giyu strikes him down with the hilt of his sword.
- However, Tanjiro’s axe flies through the air and embeds itself right next to Giyu’s head. Contrary to Giyu’s assessment that it was a simple attack, Tanjiro had calculated it to some extent. Seeing that Nezuko still tries to protect Tanjiro despite being a demon, Giyu’s feelings change as well. He then advises Tanjiro to go find an old man named Urokodaki who lives on the slopes of Mt. Sagiri and lets them go.
- Tanjiro buries his family beside the house, then, with his hands all scraped raw, tightly holds the hand of his sister who has become a demon and leaves the house (unable to contain his grief, he kept staring at the graves where his family had been buried).

### 4.1.2. Tanjiro VS Shrine Oni:
- He obtained an old basket for Nezuko, who hates sunlight. Honest Tanjiro refused the bargain when the owner said it was old and could be taken for free, and paid for it instead.
- While looking around to rest a bit, he finds a lit shrine, smells the scent of blood, and rushes in. Inside the shrine was an oni eating a human, and seeing a human-eating oni for the first time, Tanjiro—still shaken—fights it. When he is about to be killed because he doesn’t know the oni’s nature, Nezuko survives by kicking the oni’s neck/head off.
- However, when the head is severed but still alive and arms sprout and move from the head, he is startled. He quickly regains his composure: while Nezuko deals with the torso, he takes on the head. He headbutts the oni’s head and, using an axe, drives it into a tree to subdue it, and pushes the torso side that was fighting Nezuko off a cliff to immobilize it. He almost falls off the cliff along with the oni’s torso, but Nezuko saves him.
- Just as he thinks to finish the oni with a dagger, Sakonji Urokodaki appears (Urokodaki had already received a letter about Tanjiro from his disciple Giyu). Urokodaki says such a thing won’t kill an oni, and Tanjiro picks up a large rock intending to smash its head completely. Unable to kill the oni by the end, he is slapped by Urokodaki when the sun rises and the oni burns away.
- Urokodaki judges that Tanjiro cannot become a demon slayer and takes him home for the time being (Urokodaki, like Tanjiro, has an excellent sense of smell. He evaluates that Tanjiro is too considerate to make decisive judgments, and that the scent of goodness does not disappear even in front of an oni).
- Later, Tanjiro passes the test of finding Urokodaki’s house on the mountainside from Mount Sagiri—set with all kinds of traps—by himself, and succeeds in becoming his disciple.

## 4.2. Final Selection:
- Tanjiro is taught Total Concentration Breathing and the ten forms of Water Breathing by Urokodaki, training for a year until he is close to death. To enter the Demon Slayer Corps' "Final Selection," he is given the task of cutting a huge boulder by himself. 
- After half a year of training alone, unable to cut the rock and crying in despair, Tanjiro meets Sabito and Makomo and receives training in Total Concentration Breathing and swordsmanship from them. 
- Another half year later, after defeating Sabito in combat by cutting the "thread of an opening," Tanjiro comes to and finds that he has already cut the rock. Sabito and Makomo then disappear (as it turns out, they were the spirits of children who died in the Demon Slayer Corps selection exam). 
- When Urokodaki returns, he reveals that he actually gave an impossible test because he hoped Tanjiro would not die in the Final Selection, and he is amazed that Tanjiro actually cut the rock. He praises Tanjiro, saying he worked hard and is an extraordinary kid, which moves Tanjiro to tears. 
- After a generous dinner prepared by Urokodaki, Tanjiro receives a fox-shaped mask from him to protect against misfortune. 
- On the morning he departs for Mount Fujikasane to take the Final Selection, he asks Urokodaki to thank Sabito and Makomo and give them his regards. Urokodaki is shocked and asks how he knows the names of the dead children. 
- Finally arriving at Mount Fujikasane, Tanjiro is astonished to see wisteria blossoms in full bloom despite it not being their season, and finds dozens of candidates. After hearing the rules and explanation for the Final Selection, it begins in earnest; he immediately encounters two demons but cuts them all down with the Water Breathing he learned from Urokodaki.

### 4.2.1. Tanjiro VS Hand Oni:
- At that moment a rotten smell wafted and someone’s scream was heard, and a gigantic Hand Oni appeared before them, causing momentary fear. But when another trainee was about to be killed, he immediately rushed out and saved him.
- The Hand Oni saw the mask Tanjiro was wearing and immediately recognized that he was Urokodaki’s student. He then said that he had eaten thirteen of Urokodaki’s disciples so far, and that Sabito and Makomo had made a strong impression on him.
- Moreover, he laughed as if delighted while recounting how he killed the two (he said he grabbed Sabito’s head and crushed it to kill him, and tore Makomo’s limbs off to kill her). Hearing that, an enraged Tanjiro lunged at the Hand Oni, but was struck and lost consciousness; at that instant he saw an apparition of his younger brother Shigeru and woke up.
- Smelling the Oni, he dodged its attack, calmly found an opening, wrapped his arm around it, and sliced off the thick neck of the Hand Oni. The Hand Oni thought Tanjiro would look at him with contempt as he fell apart.
- However, contrary to expectation, Tanjiro smelled from the Oni a sorrowful scent longing for the brother he had lost (the Hand Oni had eaten his brother after turning into a demon). Tanjiro took the Oni’s hand and wished that in its next life it would not become an Oni.
- From that moment Tanjiro abandoned his indecisive attitude and fixed his approach to dealing with Oni. He would kill Oni who harmed humans, but console those who were dying and pray that after paying for their sins their next life would be happy. Of course, this did not apply to Oni who had been evil from the start.
- After the Demon Slayer Corps selection ended, the number of people, which had been about twenty, had shrunk to only five; Tanjiro felt regret. (At that time Tanjiro thought only four remained. Only four people, including Tanjiro himself, were nearby, and one person had already descended the mountain.) He especially regretted that the person he’d saved from the Hand Oni was nowhere to be seen and blamed himself for having fainted and being unable to save him.
- Meanwhile, among the remaining people, when Shinazugawa Genya treated one of the twin guide children roughly, Tanjiro warned him that he would break his arm; when Genya continued to act violently, Tanjiro actually broke his arm.
- Returning from the selection, Tanjiro, awakened Nezuko (Nezuko had been asleep since they arrived at Urokodaki’s house), and Urokodaki’s hospitality moved him to tears. After that he received a nichirin sword from the blacksmith Haganezuka Hotaru.
- Because Tanjiro might be a child of the forge — characterized by hair and eyes with a reddish tinge, which is considered auspicious in families who handle fire — there was hope he might receive a red blade, but in the end it was judged to be a black blade.

## 4.3. First mission:
- Having received his Nichirin Blade and Demon Slayer Corps uniform, Tanjiro sets out with Nezuko for a northwestern village to undertake his first mission as a Demon Slayer. There he meets a man named Kazumi, hears that he lost his fiancée to a demon, and—using his keen sense of smell—rescues another kidnapped girl (Tokie). He entrusts the girl to Kazumi and escorts them.
- Shortly after, they are attacked by a demon who, it turns out, is a swamp demon that splits into three. Because he is fighting while protecting people, Tanjiro cannot freely pursue or swing his sword, but with the timely help of the now-awakened Nezuko he escapes the crisis.
- Tanjiro entrusts the safety of the others to Nezuko, enters the swamp of the demons himself, and beheads two of them. He then interrogates the remaining one about Muzan Kibutsuji, but when the swamp demon, seized by the fear that it mustn't reveal Muzan, thrashes about, he quickly cuts off its head. In the end he gains no information about Muzan; after comforting Kazumi, he resumes his journey.
- Words Tanjiro says to console Kazumi, who is despairing over losing his fiancée: "Even if you lose and lose again, you have no choice but to keep living. Even if you are crushed many times." Hearing this, Kazumi grabs him by the collar and snaps that Tanjiro is younger than him—what would he know? But Tanjiro also understood better than anyone the sorrow of losing family to demons, and Kazumi realizes this and apologizes to Tanjiro for being harsh.

## 4.4. Asakusa:
- Tanjiro heads to Asakusa in Tokyo on a rumor that a demon is hiding there. Having lived only in the mountain village until now, he is surprised to see the splendid city scenery for the first time in his life.
- After arriving in Asakusa, while eating udon at Toyo's udon shop, he smells something (the scent that had been left in the house when his family was killed), is startled, and goes to trace the source. At the end of that trail he finds a passerby — who turns out to be Kibutsuji Muzan.
- The immense rage that wells up at the sight of him makes Tanjiro forget that they're in a crowded city and he reaches for his sword. But Muzan is accompanied by the family he created as a disguise, and he is holding a daughter, so Tanjiro cannot bring himself to fight. At that moment Muzan's expression hardens as if something about Tanjiro has occurred to him. When his daughter asks why he looks like that, he changes his face and gently says to Tanjiro, "What can I do for you?"
- Then, to create confusion, Muzan suddenly slashes the neck of a passerby and turns him into a demon, so Tanjiro is left holding the man and loses sight of Muzan. Tanjiro screams in fury.
- What Tanjiro shouts at Muzan he met in Asakusa: "Kibutsuji Muzan!! I will not let you go! No matter where you go, absolutely not! I'll chase you to the ends of hell and swing my blade at your throat! I will never forgive you!"
- In any case, before the man could kill anyone (as soon as he became a demon he attacked his wife who was beside him, but Tanjiro quickly pushed her aside so the wound wasn't fatal), Tanjiro stuffs a cloth into his mouth and somehow restrains him. In the meantime, a brief commotion breaks out as police officers who know nothing about demons try to drag the man away from him.
- Then Tamayo, moved by Tanjiro calling the oni a "person", uses her blood-demon arts to incapacitate the police and takes the man away to handle the situation. After that, Tamayo's retainer Yushiro comes out to meet them and guide the Tanjiro siblings to their lodgings.
- Tanjiro listens to Tamayo and Yushiro's story and agrees to cooperate with Tamayo to find a way to turn Nezuko back into a human.
- It later turns out that the oni who were supposed to be exterminated in Asakusa were none other than Tamayo and Yushiro. It seems the matter was left unresolved because they happened to encounter Kibutsuji Muzan.
- Tamayo says that for treatment she needs to study as much demon blood as possible and asks Tanjiro to let her examine Nezuko's blood first. She also asks him to, if possible, capture one of the Twelve Kizuki — a demon whose blood is particularly rich with Muzan's.

### 4.4.1. Tanjiro VS Yahaba, Susamaru:
- At that moment he fought Yahaba and Susamaru, Muzan Kibutsuji's subordinates who had attacked aiming at Tanjiro. Although overwhelmed by the attackers' pincer attack, with Yushiro's help he discerned Yahaba's Blood Demon Art. Then the awakened Kamado Nezuko faced off against Susamaru, and Tanjiro took on Yahaba to fight.
- Yahaba's Blood Demon Art caused the sword's direction to change with arrows, so his technique didn't land properly, but he wrapped the arrow with the Water Breathing "Sixth Form: Twisting Whirlpool," closed the distance with "Third Form: Flowing Dance," and by combining the two techniques into "Twisting Whirlpool: Bend" he turned Yahaba's Blood Demon Art against him and with "Second Form: Horizontal Water Wheel" barely cut his throat.
- However, he was dragged around by Yahaba's many arrows, and in trying to ease the impact he recklessly used techniques in succession, breaking his ribs and leg.
- Meanwhile Susamaru met a gruesome end from a curse because she uttered Muzan Kibutsuji's forbidden name due to Tamayo's Blood Demon Art. Witnessing Susamaru's demise, Tanjiro was left with a bitter feeling about the pitiful fate of demons who were deceived and toyed with by Muzan. He realized that Muzan was the true demon and the axis of evil.
- After the situation was settled, Tamayo suggested she take care of Nezuko, saying it would be less dangerous than bringing her to where the battles were happening. After a moment's hesitation, when Nezuko grabbed his hand tightly and looked at him with a determined gaze, he made up his mind. He told Tamayo, "I appreciate the offer, but we will never be parted again." (At this moment Tanjiro thinks of his dead family.) Then he departed for his next assignment.

## 4.5. Drum Mansion:
- With his ribs broken and barely able to walk, Tanjiro heads to his next assignment at the Drum Mansion. Along the way he witnesses Zenitsu Agatsuma clinging to and begging a girl, "Please marry me."
- Tanjiro learns the whole story from Zenitsu's kasugai crow and rescues the girl. When Zenitsu expresses resentment, Tanjiro shows a look of contempt for the first time in the story. Following the Kasugai crow's orders, Tanjiro and Zenitsu set out together for the mission.
- At the mansion's entrance, as instructed, the group meets the sibling pair Shōichi and Teruko. While they are telling him that their eldest brother, Kiyoshi, was abducted by a demon and dragged into the mansion, someone falls from outside the house. After mourning the man who fell to his death, Tanjiro leaves the box containing Nezuko with the siblings and enters the mansion with Zenitsu.
- However, when the box rattles, the frightened siblings follow them in and the rooms quickly start to scramble. Tanjiro ends up operating alongside Teruko (they discover a corpse along the way, but out of consideration for Teruko he doesn't mention it and turns away). After that they come face to face with the mansion's master, Kyogai.
- Tanjiro tells Teruko to hide, then attacks Kyogai. But when Kyogai strikes his tsuzumi drum, he uses his blood demon art to rotate the rooms.
- At that moment, they are preempted by Inosuke Hashibira—boar-headed—who bursts into the room. Inosuke steps on Teruko without caring, and Tanjiro, furious, blasts him away. The three of them stake each other out, and then the rooms scramble again, transporting them elsewhere.
- Around the new location they find a room with a distinct smell of blood, and there they discover Kiyoshi. Kiyoshi had picked up the drum Kyogai had dropped, and by using that drum to shuffle the rooms whenever he was in danger, he has managed to survive until now.
- While treating Kiyoshi's wounds, and from Kyogai's mutterings and the Kasugai crow's explanation, Tanjiro learns the reason Kiyoshi was captured: he has a rare-blood constitution (one rare-blood demon contains as much nourishment as if it had eaten the equivalent of 50 to 100 people).
- Tanjiro smells Kyogai from across the room. He tells the siblings, "I'll call your names as I enter, so if anyone tries to open the door, beat the drum and run," and then heads toward the room where Kyogai is.

### 4.5.1. Tanjiro VS Former Lower-Rank Six Kyogai:
- Words Tanjiro shouted to himself while fighting Kyogai: "Face straight ahead!! Motivate yourself!! Come on, Tanjiro, hang in there!! I've been fighting well up to now!! I'm someone who can do it!! And today too!! From now on too!! Even if I'm broken!! I will never be defeated!!"
- During the fight with Kyogai, Tanjiro, because of his injuries, cannot recklessly get into range and narrowly dodges attacks. Meanwhile he hears a hallucinatory grumble from Zenitsu saying, "A broken Tanjiro can't do anything," and Tanjiro angrily tells him to be quiet.
- As Kyogai beats the drum faster and spins around the room frantically, Tanjiro sees papers (the manuscript Kyogai wrote) flying; by avoiding stepping on them he realizes how to breathe and move so his wounds don't hurt.
- In the end, with his newly learned movements, Tanjiro evades all of Kyogai's attacks and, using Water Breathing Ninth Form: Splashing Flow, closes the distance, severs his neck, and brings him down.
- Tanjiro's final words to Kyogai: "Your Blood Demon Art was incredible!! (This compliment was not intended to create an opening; it came from a purely sincere heart.)"
- The final conversation between Tanjiro and Kyogai:

Kyougai: "You brat, answer me... Sosei's Blood Demon Art... was it... amazing...?"
Tanjiro: ...It was. But I can't forgive him for killing people.
Kyougai: Is that so... What Sosei... wrote... wasn't something like garbage. At least to that brat, it wasn't something to trample on. Sosei's Blood Demon Art... Tsuzumi too... ...have been acknowledged...

- At the end, the dying Kyougai asks if his Blood Demon Art was truly magnificent; he is told that it was magnificent, but that killing people is unforgivable. Satisfied that his novel and talent were finally recognized, Kyougai fades away.
- In the past, when he was human, an editor came and trampled on his manuscript while heaped with every kind of criticism, and Kyougai, enraged by the editor's actions, killed him.
- After that, they come out of the mansion with the siblings Kiyoshi and Teruko. Seeing Zenitsu, who had been beaten and was covered in wounds from guarding the box containing Nezuko, and Hashibira Inosuke belittling that wounded Zenitsu as a coward who can't even draw his sword and kicking him, Tanjiro, furious at his brutality, lands a blow and breaks his ribs.
- Tanjiro, angry at Inosuke, asks if he doesn't know the Demon Slayer Corps' taboo against drawing swords in vain, and accuses him of bullying someone one-sidedly. But Inosuke says he only wanted to fight someone strong, and when swords aren't allowed he'll fight bare-handed, charging in.
- Tanjiro tries to resolve it with words as much as possible but, realizing it's impossible, fights and knocks Inosuke unconscious with a headbutt. After that, they bury the victims who were attacked by the demon.
- When the burial is finished, they give the three siblings wisteria-scented sachets as demon-repelling charms and send them off, and at last they are granted permission to rest. Guided by the crow, they rest at the wisteria-patterned mansion that provides free lodging and medical care to Demon Slayer Corps members.
- Later, when Zenitsu asks why she carries a demon around, Tanjiro praises him, saying that because he has a good nose he already knew Zenitsu was kind and strong, and introduces Nezuko. Zenitsu himself replies that it's nonsense—he has no idea how strong she gets when she loses consciousness.
- But before the introductions are even finished, Zenitsu, mistakenly thinking Nezuko is Tanjiro's girlfriend, is blinded by jealousy, draws his sword, and charges.

## 4.6. Natagumo Mountain:
- After clearing up the misunderstanding, Agatsuma Zenitsu bows and fawns over Kamado Nezuko to get on her good side. Hashibira Inosuke, meanwhile, behaves as usual, barging in and challenging people to fights.
- Once Tanjiro's injuries have mostly healed, he and his companions receive orders and head to Natagumo Mountain. Among the group, Tanjiro was the slowest to recover. Inosuke kept headbutting his ribs, and when Tanjiro fell off a cliff while looking for a flower to give Nezuko, he fractured his rib cage, which took a long time to heal.
- After arriving at Natagumo Mountain, Tanjiro helps a wounded Demon Slayer, but the man leaves behind the words, "It's connected to me too," and is dragged into the forest.
- Startled by the strange incidents and the chilling smell on the mountain, Tanjiro briefly recoils, but Inosuke, without hesitation, says he'll go first and tells him to follow.
- In the forest they meet another Demon Slayer, Murata, who, seeing that they are only the lowermost rank (癸), despairs and says it's meaningless because they are not a Hashira. Enraged, Inosuke hits him and Tanjiro pulls him back. At that moment they suddenly see Demon Slayers being controlled and attacking their allies.
- When Inosuke moves to kill them without hesitation, Tanjiro stops him and notices threads attached to the victims' backs. He cuts the threads to break the control. In fact, despite a string of attacks he keeps countering, and Giyu marvels that compared to the beginning when he knew nothing about fighting, Tanjiro has become incomparably stronger.

Akaza says that it's good that Rengoku died while protecting Tanjiro so he couldn't become any weaker; enraged, Tanjiro tells him to shut up and not to insult Rengoku's death, remembering Rengoku and his grieving family.

Tanjiro argues that our very existence was made possible by someone's help, and when he says this he looks at Akaza not with anger or hatred but with pity. Even though he was furious enough to feel blood boil at Akaza's earlier insults about Rengoku's death, he also feels compassion wondering how someone could come to hold such a wrong belief.

Even when he blocks, he cannot completely negate the impact, and oddly all the attacks strike precisely at vital points, so he begins to deduce what kind of ability Akaza has. Then, with a new technique called Birunyangeom, he manages to graze Akaza's neck, but the next attack is stopped by the sword-holding hand.

Surprisingly, Tanjiro dodges that blow, and he recalls that Inosuke Hashibira's sensory detection is similar to Akaza's spirit-detecting Blood Demon Art, Destructive Death, and that when one reaches a certain state of Hinokami Kagura, one can enter the "visible world" his father once mentioned.

- Tanjiro rises into the visible-world state and approaches without being detected by Akaza's spirit-sensing, rescuing Giyu, who had been slowly being pushed back. At this time his hair and eyes turn red, and the marks also become clearer and begin to grow larger than before; even though Akaza becomes wary and uses a barrage of 100 strikes in a finishing assault, Tanjiro still survives.

When he barely regains his senses he is horrified to see Akaza's head beginning to regenerate, but he still tries to cut him down before he dies.

A punch alone is insufficient to stop the momentum, and while he scrambles to carry Giyu out of the attack radius, he notices Akaza smiling and is puzzled because he senses a smell of gratitude from him.

### 4.13.2. Tanjiro VS Kibutsuji Muzan:

### 4.13.3. Revival:
- In reality, Tanjiro’s body had long ago suffered cellular destruction from Muzan and was in a dangerous state, and when Yushiro’s five doses of antitoxin only just briefly brought back a pulse before it stopped again, Yushiro was ready to give up on Tanjiro. 
- At that, Murata kept encouraging him to keep fighting together, shouting that Muzan would run away if they did this, and at the same time a short tremor occurred and a sound rang from the sword — miraculously waking him. 
- He then barges in at the moment Muzan is about to kill the alone Kanao, cuts off Muzan’s arm, and rescues Kanao. At that moment he glares at Muzan with a look full of murderous intent. 
- Muzan, seeing Tanjiro’s appearance with the right side of his face’s cells collapsed, mocks that he doesn’t know who’s come, but is disgusted, overlaying Yoriichi’s face and finding it repulsive. Tanjiro, expressionless, says now let’s finish everything (“Let’s finish this, Muzan.”).
- Then the final battle with Muzan begins, and Tanjiro recalls the past he saw. He remembers the forms Yoriichi showed Sumiyoshi and marvels that they were transmitted with astonishing accuracy. Tanjiro also speculates, based on what he saw in the past, that the Thirteenth Form is the sequential continuation of the techniques from the First through the Twelfth Forms. 
- Tanjiro reasons that the First Form, “원무,” and the final form, “염무,” are read with the same tone, that Yoriichi said if one can do proper breathing they can keep dancing, that Yoriichi danced the same dance repeatedly until dawn, and linking that to how, when he looked into the world revealed by Muzan’s body, he saw seven hearts and five brains — he concludes that the Thirteenth Form is the continuation of Forms One through Twelve in order. 
- He admits he doesn’t have talent like Yoriichi or his father, but he sets his heart ablaze, swearing he will do everything he can here and never break, and begins using Sun Breathing. 
- When Muzan overlays Yoriichi onto Tanjiro and extracts ambush tentacles from his legs—used earlier when dealing with the lords and their subordinates—Tanjiro is wounded, but still naturally links First Form, the Red Spider Lily Form, and the Flame Chariot, confirming that the Sun Breathing forms are designed to flow into one another, and he vows to carry them on thinking of Yoriichi. 
- As Muzan weakens with aging, Tanjiro succeeds in continuing through the Twelfth Form and unfolding the Thirteenth Form. He raises precision and speed, trying to keep chaining forms until dawn. 
- While repeating and concentrating to enter the revealed world, he nearly loses consciousness from oxygen deprivation and is on the verge of falling to Muzan’s tentacles, but Obanai Iguro snaps to his senses, stands, and dramatically saves Tanjiro. Tanjiro tries to help Iguro, who saved him twice, but is despairing because he cannot use the revealed world to check Muzan’s heart and brains. 
- However, a scar left by Tsugikuni Yoriichi appears on Muzan’s weakened body, and Tanjiro is astonished to find that Yoriichi’s will to destroy Muzan still constricts Muzan’s body, and he steels his resolve again.

Tanjiro throws all the fallen comrades’ nichirin swords to buy time, and Iguro drives his blade into Muzan’s neck to stop his movement. Eventually Muzan, who stopped fleeing, is wounded again by a single attack.

With his sight opened thanks to Tanjiro and the talismans, Iguro thanks Tanjiro, tells him to position himself as if to surround Muzan, and encourages him that the two of them together can bring him down.

- Tanjiro then charges to attack the blood-coughing, immobilized Muzan, but Muzan emits a shockwave that knocks him down along with the surroundings. At the same moment his nervous system is rattled and convulsions start, leaving him unable to move, but Inosuke and Zenitsu recover and rush in to attack Muzan, narrowly rescuing him from the crisis. Zenitsu encourages him, saying shouldn’t he and Nezuko go back to their hometown together.

### 4.13.4. Afterwards:

Tanjiro lunges at Inosuke and, perhaps losing his senses, nearly beheads him despite his body being healed — Inosuke, in tears, stops his blade — and just as Tanjiro is about to kill Inosuke, Nezuko suddenly returns to being human and throws herself around Tanjiro, blocking him.

As Kanao mentions Nezuko, Tanjiro, buried in the mass of flesh, faintly reacts.

# 5. Kamado Tanjiro's abilities:
## 5.1. Physical abilities:
### 5.1.1. Stamina:
- He has quite good stamina — enough to routinely carry Nezuko on his back.
- Even before joining the Demon Slayer Corps, he worked making charcoal in the mountains and repeatedly went down to the village to sell it, so it's impossible for his stamina to be poor.

### 5.1.2. Thick skull:
- He has an incredibly hard head. Since Tanjiro's mother was also thick-headed and is said to have once chased off a wild boar with a headbutt, it seems he inherited it from her.

### 5.1.3. Olfaction:
- Tanjiro has a sense of smell that surpasses even that of animals, allowing him to discern subtle scents in great detail.
- Through scent, Tanjiro can read what animals are thinking; thanks to this he was able to read Nezuko's thoughts after she lost the ability to speak, and he could also tell when Kochou Shinobu—who normally doesn't outwardly show anger—was angry.

## 5.2. Total Concentration Breathing:
### 5.2.1. Water Breathing:
- The breathing style Tanjiro learned from Urokodaki Sakonji. At first, even after learning it he couldn't understand what it was and couldn't use it properly, but with the help of Sabito and Makomo he came to understand it and began to use it in earnest.
- True to its name, Water Breathing has flexible forms and techniques that exert power even underwater.
- Form I: Surface Slash: The basic form — a simple slashing strike that cuts horizontally.
- Form II: Water Wheel: A technique where you perform a somersault in midair and slash along a longitudinal, waterwheel-shaped trajectory.
- Form III: Flowing Dance: A movement-type attack that moves quickly along a winding, current-like path and cuts anything along that trajectory.
- Form IV: Striking Tide: A technique that unleashes several powerful sword strikes in succession.
- Form V: Blessed Rain After the Drought: Said to be used only against demons who willingly present their necks, because those cut by this technique feel almost no pain.
- Form VI: Twisting Whirlpool: By twisting the whole body, you unleash spiral, whirlpool-shaped slashes in all directions — a wide-area technique. It is the most suitable Water Breathing technique for underwater use.
- Form VII: Droplet Ripple Thrust: A thrusting technique. It is the fastest and most precise form among Water Breathing techniques, piercing the enemy with a single thrust.
- Form VIII: Dragon's Lair: A technique of bringing the blade down with the force of a waterfall. As a kind of area-of-effect move, it can cut multiple targets within its range at once.
- Form IX: Flowing Current Splash: An evasive technique that minimizes landing time and landing area during movements to avoid enemy attacks.
- Secret Technique · Form X: Constant Flux: The strongest form of Water Breathing. It swings a sword-ki shaped like a water dragon and, adding rotation, gradually increases the power of the strikes. Because it becomes stronger the longer the consecutive attacks are maintained, it is advantageous in prolonged battles.

### 5.2.2. Hinokami Kagura -> Breath of the Sun:
- Originally a breathing style Tanjiro had learned early from his father by watching over his shoulder, but because he never properly understood or practiced it, it was a breathing technique he had been unable to use until then.
- In the life-flash he experienced during the fight with Rui, Hinokami Kagura reminded him of the kagura his father used to perform every New Year (a dance performed when making offerings to the gods) and the breathing method, and he realized it was the Breath of the Sun — a technique passed down through the Kamado family for generations, with his father having asked that it be carried on.
- Aside from the basic differences in power and characteristics between breathing styles, because this kagura has been handed down in the Kamado family and Tanjiro strongly inherited the Kamado bloodline so it suits his body better, its power is far greater than when he uses Water Breathing. However, since it was a breathing style Tanjiro had not used before and his physical capacity couldn’t fully keep up with the technique, and above all because Hinokami Kagura is a sword style twisted into a dance and contains many unnecessary movements, his stamina would drain quickly every time he used it.
- Therefore he learned to use Hinokami Kagura in conjunction with Water Breathing.
- Perhaps because of the Breath of the Sun, his resting body temperature is a high 38°C, but there are no adverse effects; in fact his temperature can rise even higher, and the higher it goes the easier it becomes for him to move.
- At first Tanjiro thought the technique was the 'Breath of Fire' and asked Shinobu and Kyojuro if they knew it, but they immediately said they didn’t and he was embarrassed. However, when Kyojuro’s father Shinjuro saw Tanjiro’s earrings he angrily exclaimed, "You’re a user of the 'Breath of the Sun' (hi)!" and Tanjiro learned it was 'sun' not 'fire.'
- In his life-flash Tanjiro saw the sequence from Form 1 (Wonmu) to Form 12 (Yeommu), and he learned that by repeatedly cycling through Forms 1–12 in order he could use Form 13.

### 5.2.3. Birthmark:
- Tanjiro has a flame-patterned mark on his left forehead. This mark was a burn scar caused when he was scalded by the brazier while protecting his sister. Because of this, when Rengoku Shinjuro believed Tanjiro to be the Sun Breathing user chosen that year, Tanjiro himself denied it.
- However, when he raised his physical abilities to the maximum using the Sun Breathing, the scar darkened to the point of resembling a mark.
- During joint reinforcement training, while learning repetitive movements in Gyomei's drills, the scar transformed into a dark flame shape and became permanently fixed.
- When he mastered the world Akaza hinted at during the Infinity Castle battle, the size of the mark more than doubled, and even the color of his eyes and hair shifted to a brighter red, making it more similar to Tsugikuni Yoriichi's mark. At that time, although some say his overall power had dropped, he partially evaded Akaza's Cheong-eun Nanjan-gwang, which Giyu's Calm Ripple couldn't completely block.

## 5.3. Black Blade:
- When a person with an aptitude for the Breath of the Sun first takes up a Nichirin Blade, the blade itself is dyed black; this is the Black Blade. Tanjiro is also a person with an aptitude for the Breath of the Sun.

## 5.4. Hyeokdo:
- At first, he couldn't dye the blade red on his own, so he needed Nezuko's Blood Demon Art, Exploding Blood, to use Hyeokdo. The name of this technique is also Exploding Blood Blade.
- Later, in the Final Act arc, when reproducing the 13th Form of the Sun Breathing, he became able to create Hyeokdo by himself. According to Muzan's assessment, it seems inferior to Yoriichi's Hyeokdo.

## 5.5. Repetitive Motion:
- A physical training method to increase concentration that he learned from Genya in the Joint Training arc. It was originally a method used by Himejima Gyomei; because Gyomei was bad at explaining things, Genya learned by watching his actions and passed it on to Tanjiro.
- The main action Tanjiro uses for the repetitive motion is recalling the words "Set your heart ablaze," spoken by his family and Rengoku Kyojuro.
 
## 5.6. Transparent World:
- Tanjiro partially manifested the Transparent World in the Upper Rank Four (Hantengu) battle, and fully reached this state in the Upper Rank Three (Akaza) battle.
- Upon reaching this state, his breathing sound changes and the spots double in size.
- In the fight with Muzan he couldn’t use it properly because Muzan’s speed of action was beyond imagination, but after returning from the brink of death he peered into Muzan’s body and succeeded in recognizing the many hearts and brains within it.

## 5.7. Realm of No-Self:
- Awakened during the battle with Akaza, together with the shimmering world.

#### 6.2.3.5. Nakahara Sumi, Terauchi Kiyo, Takada Naho:
- Nurses who work at the Butterfly Mansion. These young girls were initially shy around Tanjiro but relaxed around his kindness.
- They readily granted Tanjiro's request during his rehabilitation training and have since maintained a close older-brother/younger-sister relationship with him. When Tanjiro barely managed to defeat Kanao, they rejoiced as if they themselves had won.

#### 6.2.3.6. Goto:
- A Silver squad member met during the Trial arc. They became close enough to speak openly because they were worried about Tanjiro, who always pushes himself too hard and ends up getting injured. Goto also gets along well with the other Silver squad members.

#### 6.2.3.7. Rengoku Shinjuro:
- Rengoku Kyojuro's father. At his first meeting with Tanjiro he was in a slump and not in his right mind, so they were on hostile terms. He harbored an inferiority complex about the limits of his talent, and when he learned that Tanjiro was a user of the Breath of the Sun he reacted with brazen anger. 
- Tanjiro already held a grudge against Shinjuro for insulting his dead son and treating his other son Senjuro carelessly; when Shinjuro argued that he himself possessed great talent, the resentment Tanjiro had been holding inside burst out, and he hurled abusive words and fought. 
- Tanjiro called Shinjuro a creature not even human (人でなし,히토데나시) and a damn old man (くそ爺,쿠소지지). At that time Tanjiro's inner wound was truly deep.
- Even though Shinjuro's skills had rusted considerably, as a former Hashira he still overwhelmed Tanjiro, but he was knocked down by a counter headbutt. After that he continued to maintain a warped attitude, but Senjuro, who regained his confidence thanks to Tanjiro, announced Kyojuro's final words and reformed. 
- Afterwards Shinjuro apologized for the previous incident and thanked Tanjiro for becoming a friend to his son. He also firmly believes, based on information about the rosary notes, that Tanjiro is the chosen user of the Breath of the Sun.

#### 6.2.3.8. Rengoku Senjuro:
- The younger brother of Rengoku Kyojuro. Unlike his brother, he lacks talent in swordsmanship and is looked down on by their father; seeing this, Tanjiro is incredulous and scolds Senjuro. Their emotions flare and they get into a fight, and after Tanjiro knocks Senjuro down he cools off, feels embarrassed, and apologizes to Senjuro.
- Senjuro also harbors some resentment toward their father, so he forgives him, and he is grateful to Tanjiro, who, despite his ailing body, came to inform him of his brother’s last words.

#### 6.2.3.9. Tsugikuni Yoriichi:
- There is no blood relation, but he is the benefactor who saved Sumiyoshi, an ancestor. However, since he died long ago, he didn't leave much of an impression.
- However, through visions of his life I learned of Yoriichi's sad life story and sympathized with him; thanks to Yoriichi's good deeds I gained the means to defeat Kibutsuji Muzan, so I respect him and express my gratitude.

#### 6.2.3.10. Ubuyashiki Kagaya:
- Leader of the Demon Slayer Corps. Tanjiro broke the Corps' rules by hiding Nezuko, who had become a demon, and by allying with the demon Tamayo, but Kagaya, who had been watching Tanjiro's every move, knew about it and tacitly tolerated it until it was officially revealed.
- Eventually Nezuko's existence was exposed and a trial was held, at which point Kagaya and Tanjiro officially met. He gave Tanjiro a fair judgment and permitted Nezuko's existence.
- Tanjiro came to respect Kagaya, who was big-hearted and charismatic.

#### 6.2.3.11. Ubuyashiki Kiriya:
- He was a boy Tanjiro met at the Demon Slayer Corps selection exam. At that time they had no particular relationship, but Tanjiro saved his younger sister from Genya.
- In the final showdown, he thanked Tanjiro for ultimately defeating Muzan, and even after they retired he shared a great deal of wealth with him. Whenever Tanjiro, feeling burdened, refused, more gifts inevitably arrived, so he had no choice but to accept them.

## 6.3. Swordsmith Village:
### 6.3.1. Haganezuka Hotaru:
- He was the swordsmith in charge of Tanjiro and got angry because Tanjiro kept breaking or losing swords. He even ran at him with a sword intending to kill him — it wasn't a joke; he truly came at him with murderous intent.
- But apart from that, Haganezuka, whose personality was so difficult that there had been many replacements, was grateful to Tanjiro for keeping him as his smith until the end.
- In return, he disciplined himself with his family's secret training method and sharply honed the blade used in the battle against Muzan.

### 6.3.2. Kanamori Kozo:
- Haganezuka's colleague and Inosuke's assigned swordsmith. Unlike Haganezuka, who behaves eccentrically, he's a sensible person, so they get along comfortably.
- However, Kanamori is also a swordsmith, so he got furious at Inosuke for carelessly damaging swords, and Tanjiro had a hard time desperately trying to stop him from lunging in.

### 6.3.3. Tetchikawahara Tetchin:
- The chief of the swordsmith village. He took a liking to Tanjiro, a good boy, and shared sweets with him.
- Normally a kind grandfather, but when it came to making swords he became extremely strict, and Tanjiro was momentarily overwhelmed by the atmosphere.

### 6.3.4. Kotetsu:
- A doll-maker boy met in the doll-maker village. He was saved from being bullied by the Hashira Muichiro Tokito.
- Kotetsu, whose pride was wounded because of Muichiro, helps Tanjiro, whom he befriended, with his training — partly to repay a favor and partly to get revenge on Muichiro.
- However, the training at that time was so hard that Tanjiro nearly died. Still, from that training Tanjiro became strong enough to be recognized by the Hashira and was able to respond to the attack by Upper Moon Four, Hantengu, who invaded the village.

### 6.3.5. Other residents:
- They got along fine with the other residents of the Potter Village.
- The potters who had brought equipment to support the Demon Slayer Corps, upon seeing the Kamado siblings embracing in the sunlight, didn't know the details but were visibly moved and shed tears.

## 6.4. Blood Demons (Oni):
- Tanjiro does not forgive the evil deeds committed by the blood demons, but unlike ordinary people, he internally regards them as pitiable living beings. However, there are exceptions: Muzan, the source of all evil, and those who committed evil even when they were human.
- Tanjiro does not learn each demon's backstory and then pity them; rather, he smells the scent of their emotions just before they die, infers that each must have had some kind of story, and feels sympathy.

### 6.4.1. Kibutsuji Muzan:
- The arch-enemy who slaughtered his mother and siblings and even turned his one remaining sister Nezuko into a demon. Also the root cause that continuously spawns demons and creates tragedies for ordinary people and demons alike, so he was someone Tanjiro vowed he would one day definitely defeat — and in the end he succeeded in completely exterminating him.
- When he first had a proper conversation with Muzan, Muzan’s outrageous remarks — not only showing no remorse for what he had done but not even recognizing it as wrong — led Tanjiro to judge him as a being who must never exist in this world.
- He treats anyone without prejudice and even listens to the circumstances of demons who are regarded as public enemies, but Muzan is such a terrible foe that Tanjiro thinks, "This one I will definitely kill with my own hands."

### 6.4.2. Hand Demon (Hand Oni):
- He was the first named demon Tanjiro encountered. The Hand Demon, refusing to forget the grudge of being captured by Sakonji Urokodaki and imprisoned in a wisteria prison, continued his revenge by mercilessly killing and devouring Urokodaki's disciples who were taking the Demon Slayer Corps' entrance exam. Tanjiro was enraged by the Hand Demon's leering mockery about having killed the disciples, but he soon cut off the Hand Demon's head and felt sympathy as the demon was dying.
- Having forgotten the brother he cherished in life as a result of becoming a demon, the Hand Demon regained his memories and his human emotions at Tanjiro's gentle gaze, and found peace.

### 6.4.3. Swamp Blood Demon (Swamp Oni):
- The second named oni Tanjiro encountered, he struggled against the Swamp Blood Demon who used Blood Demon Art, but with Nezuko he succeeded in defeating him. However, even in his final moments, instead of repenting for his cannibalism and the massacre of people, he shamelessly grew angry at Kazumi’s resentful cry over losing her fiancé to him, so Tanjiro snapped and promptly slashed his cheek to shut him up.
- Tanjiro then demanded information about Muzan from the Swamp Blood Demon, but seeing even such a heinous figure trembling with fear only increased Tanjiro’s wariness of Muzan.

### 6.4.4. Susamaru:
- Tanjiro, cooperating with Nezuko, Tamayo, and Yushiro, confronts Muzan’s subordinates Susamaru and Yahaba, but Susamaru, dying from Muzan’s curse, asks Tanjiro to pick up her ball one last time. When Tanjiro picks up the ball and places it beside her arm, she disappears, saying she wants to play together.
- From Susamaru’s childlike behavior, Tanjiro infers that she must have been turned into a demon by Muzan when she was very young, and he grows to hate Muzan even more for ruthlessly disposing of even his own followers once they are no longer needed.

### 6.4.5. Kyogai:
- Kyogai, who had dreamed of becoming a writer but lived a life in which his talent went unrecognized, was traumatized by being looked down on by others. During his fight with Tanjiro, when Tanjiro was put at risk of slipping because of Kyogai’s manuscript sheets scattered on the floor, Tanjiro avoided them; this unsettled Kyogai, allowing Tanjiro to win.
- Tanjiro, with a pure heart, praised Kyogai’s Blood Demon Art, but said that he still could not forgive the sin of killing people. Kyogai, receiving the “recognition from others” he had never had in life, felt a sense of satisfaction and then vanished.

### 6.4.6. Rui:
- Rui, who wanted the bonds of a true family, is moved by the affection between Tanjiro and his younger sister Nezuko and tries to take Nezuko away from Tanjiro. Rui was initially hostile but attempted to coax them in a gentle tone; however, Tanjiro openly rejects Rui's twisted sense of family and declares that he will never let Nezuko be taken.
- Rui torments and attempts to kill the attacking Tanjiro, but he is driven into a corner by Tanjiro's "Hinokami Kagura" and the awakening of Nezuko's Blood Demon Art, "Exploding Blood."
- In the end, even after Giyu, who came as support, cuts his neck, Rui had somehow planned to at least kill the Kamado siblings, but upon seeing Tanjiro's kind eyes he recalls his past and is reunited with the family he had so longed for. Afterwards, as Rui fades away, he briefly makes a sad expression, saying he can smell the scent of sadness on his clothes.

### 6.4.7. Enmu:
- He did not forgive Enmu, who lured people in desperate situations by promising to show them happy dreams and toyed with them by showing those dreams. Tanjiro is furious at Enmu for insulting his dead family, and Enmu is also very displeased, saying he now understands why Muzan insisted that Tanjiro must be eliminated, because Tanjiro possesses abnormal mental fortitude and altruism.
- In the end, Enmu, driven into a corner by the human mental strength he had so contemptuously dismissed, slits his own throat.

### 6.4.8. Daki and Gyutaro:
- At first, when only Daki appeared, Tanjiro despised her, seeing only her cruel nature; but later, when her brother Gyutaro made remarks that seemed to equate himself with Tanjiro, Tanjiro also projected himself onto Gyutaro, who was desperately trying to protect his sister.
- Perhaps because of this, after the slaying of Upper Rank Six, when the two siblings began insulting each other, Tanjiro cut off Gyutaro's words to shut them up, telling them they should get along — a mixture of reproach and consolation.

### 6.4.9. Hantengu:
- Apart from Muzan, the demon Tanjiro hated the most. He despised Hantengu — a coward, a liar, a murderer.
- He directly refuted Hantengu’s ideology of labeling those who torment the weak as villains to justify evil deeds.
- When Hantengu’s main body kept running away, thinking nothing of paying for his crimes and only wanting not to die, Tanjiro, enraged, shouted “you bastard.” Tanjiro truly despises Hantengu.
- Later, after beheading Hantengu, the fanbook revealed Hantengu saying it hurt a lot to be cut by the Sun Breathing, which shows how extremely angry Tanjiro — who feels sympathy for demons — was at Hantengu.

### 6.4.10. Akaza:
- He is the enemy who murdered the mentor he respected, Kyojuro. When Akaza tries to flee to avoid sunlight, he throws his Illyundo and, condemning him for running away, utters a rage-filled cry that it is Kyojuro's victory.
- Tanjiro and Akaza, each burning with hatred, engage in a fated duel in the final phase; Akaza is surprised that Tanjiro has grown beyond expectations but cannot shake his displeasure at Tanjiro for rejecting his ideology.
- Tanjiro outright denies Akaza's twisted survival-of-the-fittest philosophy and criticizes someone with such beliefs for insulting Kyojuro.
- Although effectively Tanjiro's rival who had driven him into a corner many times, thanks to Giyu's help and his father's teachings, Tanjiro rises to the supreme realm of the martial art Akaza had coveted and defeats him in a one-on-one duel.
- Even after his throat is cut, Akaza endures death with the will to become stronger, but at the crossroads of life and death, as memories of his past surface, he gradually loses the will to fight.
- He almost reverted to being an oni again under Muzan's brainwashing, but after reuniting with his lover from life and returning to being human Hakujiro, he thanks Tanjiro for giving him a chance at redemption and meets death. Tanjiro did not know the reason either, but he sensed Akaza's gratitude, and their mutual grudge was resolved.

### 6.4.11. Tamayo:
- As a demon, she was unusually impressive in that she didn’t attack people and instead maintained a human life. Also, being beautiful, there was a brief depiction that could make one swoon.
- Tamayo is also grateful to the Kamado siblings for treating demons as “human.” Because of this, Tanjiro did not view Tamayo as an enemy and accepted her, and he received a great deal of help from her.

### 6.4.12. Yushiro:
- Tanjiro doesn't particularly dislike him, but since Yushiro himself cares about no one but Tamayo, it's hard to say they're close. He even went so far as to suggest to Tamayo, during Susamaru and Yahaba's fight, that they use Tanjiro as bait to make their escape.
- However, he came to accept the two siblings after seeing Tamayo moved by the Kamado siblings, who regarded them as "human."

After selling all his charcoal, he let Tanjiro stay at his home when Tanjiro was about to climb the mountain late at night, saying a demon would appear. Tanjiro dismissed the old man's words as superstition, but the demon did in fact exist, and this was the reason Tanjiro alone was able to survive Muzan's attack.

After slaying the swamp demon, Tanjiro prayed that Kazumi would live well from then on, but Kazumi, stunned by the loss of his fiancée, spat out cold words.

### 6.5.4. Kiyoshi, Shoichi, Teruko:
- The three siblings Tanjiro met at the Janggu mansion. When the eldest brother Kiyoshi, who possessed a rare bloodline, was kidnapped by Kyougai, Tanjiro pursued him.
- While helping the three siblings and after defeating Kyougai, Tanjiro buried the people who had been killed by demons inside the Janggu mansion, gave them a wisteria sachet — a demon-repelling charm — via the crooked-beak crow, and sent them on their way.

### 6.5.5. Koinatsu:
- An oiran she met while infiltrating the pleasure quarter for a mission. She treated Tanjiro kindly from the start and explained the disappearances of the courtesan girls to him, helping him grasp the situation.
- Later, just before Koinatsu left after being miuke (身請け), she revealed her true identity, explained her mission, and said goodbye.
- Koinatsu says she knew Tanjiro was a boy from the beginning; Tanjiro, who had secretly thought his acting was good, was shocked.
- Afterwards, Tanjiro saved Koinatsu when she was almost eaten by Upper Rank Six, Daki.

# 8. Miscellaneous:
- If you parse the kanji of his name: kamado (竈) meaning stove/kitchen, mon (門) meaning gate, tan (炭) meaning charcoal, chi (治) meaning govern, rō (郞) meaning son. 
- If you read the name characters literally except for the surname Kamado (竈門), it becomes “a man who handles charcoal.” This clearly shows Tanjiro is the eldest son of a family that dealt in charcoal. The family name Kamado also means “kitchen gate” or “hearth/oven.”
- Tanjiro is the eldest son of the Kamado family, and his personality, sense of responsibility, and the way he cares for Nezuko all strongly give off that eldest-son vibe. Tanjiro is also aware of being the eldest son himself.
- Even before becoming close to someone, he has the habit of quickly calling people by their names. In Tanjiro’s case, he calls others’ names astonishingly faster than the average Japanese person.
- Tanjiro is tone-deaf.
- Although he is far from being inarticulate—in fact, he can be quite eloquent—he is really bad at teaching or explaining things. When he first trained Total Concentration: Upper Body, he tried to explain to Zenitsu and Inosuke what he had personally seen, heard, and felt, but it was a complete mess and didn’t help them at all. Right after his mark manifested, when Ubuyashiki Kagaya asked him to explain the mark, he made things awkward by repeating strange onomatopoeia just like when he was training Total Concentration: Upper Body. This seems to be similar to Kanroji Mitsuri.
- True to Tanjiro’s friendly nature and how he cherishes relationships, he exchanges letters with people he grew close to through various incidents: his mentor Urokodaki Sakonji, Rengoku Kyojuro’s younger brother Senjuro, the courtesans he met during the Entertainment District incident, the retired Uzui family, and so on. He also occasionally sent letters to Giyu during missions or leave, but reportedly received no reply.
- By the time joint training with Tomioka Giyu finished, they had become very close, so it’s expected he’ll get replies in the future. After the final battle against Kibutsuji Muzan he also sent letters to Shinazugawa Sanemi, but although Sanemi can read, he cannot write, so he’s unable to reply — instead he leaves gifts each time.
- During the joint strengthening training, when Mitsuri mentioned pancakes and butter he looked unfamiliar with them; having grown up poor in the mountains, he was not familiar with many foods and cultures that are hard to encounter in the mountains.
- Tanjiro’s messenger crow (kasugai crow) is named Tennōji Matsueemon (天王寺松右衛門). Unlike his owner, the crow speaks in a rather rough, coarse manner and has a fairly mischievous personality. When it first saw Zenitsu it greatly despised his low self-esteem and thought he should be as confident as itself. Also, it seems to like gossip… it often brings up stories about people it doesn’t even know. It treats Tanjiro as its subordinate, more precisely as a disciple.
- Still, the crow appears to care for Tanjiro to some extent: when Tanjiro had a hard time against Upper Moon Six it fussed dramatically asking “When are you coming!?” and in the battle against Upper Moon Three it cheered him on, telling him not to lose.
- He’s good at cooking. During the joint strengthening training his comrades praised him for cooking rice and grilling fish well, saying that as a charcoal-seller’s son he’s skilled at controlling the fire... At that moment Tanjiro looked unusually smug, rare for him, as if the praise had thrilled him.
- Besides cooking, Tanjiro is capable of all household chores. In the Entertainment District, because the scar on his forehead kept him from appearing before customers, he took care of chores inside the establishment—cleaning, flower arranging, making tea, etc.—performing all tasks quickly and precisely.
- Tanjiro’s thick-headedness is said to be inherited from his mother. His mother even once drove off a wild boar that jumped out of the mountain with a headbutt.
- Although Tanjiro usually wears his earrings all the time, he sometimes takes them off when his ears really itch.
- When his favorite food, aralia shoots, is in front of him, he pretends to remain calm and indifferent (seemingly trying not to show excitement over food due to being the eldest son). However, his nostrils flare, so everyone around him already notices.
- Before joining the Demon Slayer Corps, when Tanjiro went down to town to sell charcoal, the villagers greeted him warmly, calling him “Tanjiro-chan.”
- Tanjiro’s ideal type is a Shiba Inu like a lily-of-the-valley. Hearing this, his younger siblings mistakenly thought he wanted to marry a dog and cried. But in fact it was just a metaphor suited to his younger siblings’ level of understanding.
- Although they were poor, they seemed to have been a very ideal family. In the fight with Rui he shouted that binding someone with fear is not a family tie, and he was deeply shocked to see the Shinazugawa brothers’ fight.
- In the Kamado household, when a fight broke out they would settle it by tickling each other, or if that didn’t work, decide by rock-paper-scissors. However, in rock-paper-scissors Nezuko is virtually unbeatable.
- Tanjiro’s father was an excellent person in character and conduct, an ideal loving father. As the eldest son, Tanjiro learned a lot from him and remembers his father whenever times are hard.
