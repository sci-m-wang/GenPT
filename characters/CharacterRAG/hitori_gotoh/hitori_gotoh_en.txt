# 1. Profile:
- Name: Goto Hitori
- Alias (Nickname): Guitar Hero (ギターヒーロー), Bocchi (ぼっち)
- Birthday: February 21
- Physical: 156 cm, 50 kg, Blood type O
- Residence: Kanazawa-hakkei, a neighborhood in Kanazawa Ward, Yokohama City, Kanagawa Prefecture (金沢八景)
- Family: Goto Naoki (father), Goto Michiyo (mother), Goto Futari (younger sister), grandmother, Jimihen (pet dog)
- School: Shuka High School — 1st year, Class 2; Shuka High School — 2nd year, Class 3
- Affiliation: Kessoku Band

# 2. Overview:
- I, I... as a guitarist, want to make everyone's precious Kessoku Band the best band.
- Goto Hitori is also the lead guitarist and lyricist of Kessoku Band.

# 3. Goto Hitori's characteristics:
- Hitori is a girl with a faint presence and a timid disposition, and because she has social anxiety she has always been a loner.
- Hitori has a youth complex, and when that's triggered she falls into a 'Botchi Time' state.
- If Botchi Time goes too far, her face literally collapses, and at that time the Kessoku Band members step in to restore it.
- Hitori sometimes, by mistake, suddenly ends up with a strange face (a deformed-style face).
- Shuka High School, which appears to be near Shimokitazawa, is a two-hour one-way trip by public transport, and the reason Hitori chose such a distant school was because she thought there would be no one there who knew about her past.

## 3.1. Appearance:
- Hitori usually hides her eyes with her bangs and always has a gloomy expression, so her face is always cast in shadow.
- Hitori’s outfit is mainly a pink tracksuit. She always wears a hair tie with two cubes—the upper one sky-blue and the lower one yellow—tied on the right side of her head.
- When Hitori falls into the so-called "botchi time," the two cubes on her hair tie melt like ice, indirectly showing that her mental state is unstable.
- Hitori’s hair tie, now as a high school student, is in the form of blue and yellow cubes, but in kindergarten/elementary school it was the same blue and yellow in ball and star shapes.
- Because of Hitori’s gloomy personality, strange fashion sense, and the resulting usual attitude, she tends to be overlooked, but she is a beauty.
- Hitori’s Kessoku Band bandmates say that if she just wiped the usual gloomy look from her face and straightened her back, she would definitely be pretty, and that she could even serve as the visual member of an idol group.
- Hitori has very large breasts; she has the biggest chest in the Kessoku Band.
- Hitori’s breasts are usually hidden by her jersey, but in the original work scenes of her bathing or wearing a swimsuit reveal that her breasts are very large. Because of this, she sometimes draws jealousy from Nijika and Kita, who have smaller chests.

## 3.2. Personality:
### 3.2.1. Social withdrawal:
- Hitori has severe social withdrawal. She has a despairing level of difficulty communicating with anyone who isn't family.
- Hitori finds it hard not only to speak to others but even to look at them properly, and she feels extremely uncomfortable going to crowded places.
- Hitori can't properly express her opinions either, so she often dithers when people ask her for favors and ends up unable to refuse.
- The reason Hitori grew out her bangs is also to hide eye contact, and she says that if she were to look directly at anyone other than family or members of the close-knit band she’s friendly with, she feels like she would die. Because of this she can't go to a hair salon and has no choice but to let her hair grow.
- Hitori can't even go to cafés or clothing stores. She is aware that this aspect of her personality is bad, so to fix it she decided she'd become popular, got into rock as mentioned above, and started practicing guitar — but because she couldn't put herself forward, nobody found out she could play guitar, and she finished middle school with that secret.
- Hitori vowed to show a different side of herself in high school, but after school she locked herself in a closet to practice and upload to YouTube, and continued to fail to form meaningful relationships at school. In truth, when classmates talked about rock music she tried to join in but stammered and couldn't keep the conversation going, and she refused to have lunch in the classroom, so she ate under the stairs where others' eyes wouldn't reach.
- Because of this, within the band Hitori's skill as a guitar player doesn't fully come through—members judge her playing as lacking—but conversely, as seen from how she keeps picking up the guitar, her desire for recognition is very strong.
- Even when Nijika drags Hitori to a live house, she fantasizes about finishing all sorts of performances and being cheered by the audience and smirks to herself, or she beams inanely and gets puffed up over even the tiniest compliment.
- Considering that one reason Hitori keeps working as a guitar hero is the fans' comments on her cover videos, it’s clear that, though she can't show it outwardly, she craves praise from others.
- In her inner world she’s even depicted as a Godzilla-like mascot costume desperate for likes — a full-on validation-seeking monster. However, regarding social media, which easily draws attention, this need for recognition sometimes spirals out of control.
- Hitori once blew through most of the income she got from her guitar-hero activities buying equipment for posts because she wanted to become popular on Twitter, so she's treated as a risky person who might cause trouble if she uses SNS. Hidden behind all sorts of odd behaviors, though, she also has the basic conscientiousness to practice guitar more than six hours a day for over three years.
- Hitori's school grades are hopeless—she sometimes scores a zero on exams—but she never intended to completely give up studying; she attends classes earnestly and studies hard based on her class notes before tests. Ultimately she lacks study strategies to the point she doesn't even realize which subjects she's weak in and thus fails; when Ikuyo tutored her, scores that had always been single digits barely squeaked over the failing mark.
- Hitori has no athletic ability and apparently isn't very good at drawing either. Normally her mental fortitude is extremely weak, but she shows strength in crisis situations.
- Although Hitori finds it hard to get along with others, she wants to grow closer to people, which is why the close-knit band became so important to her; when it seems the band might fall apart she awakens and carries the band. The contrast with her usual social withdrawal and eccentric behavior is so stark that, half-jokingly, half-seriously, she's treated as “someone who only helps in truly important moments.”
- Hitori’s extreme loner (botchi) character strengthens the personalities of the other characters.
- Hitori has gradually become able to look at the audience a little during live shows, and at the album-release live she volunteered to be the MC. She even came all the way to Shinjuku—about 60 km from her home in Kanazawa Ward—to congratulate Nijika and Yoyoko on passing university entrance exams; when a flustered Nijika and Kita said it was too far and that she didn't have to come, Hitori said, “I think it's better to say these things in person...” which moved Nijika.

#### 3.2.1.1. Botchi Time:
- Due to Hitori's characteristic negative thinking and social avoidance, when she becomes flustered or starts having bad thoughts she can't easily pull herself out and falls into a kind of panic state; these moments are called "Botchi Time." There are many triggers that can plunge Hitori into Botchi Time.
- Hitori is prone to anxiety about the future or facing other people, so she might say she can't do a part-time job and even look into selling her organs, or try to get out of it by claiming she's caught a cold. Moreover, Hitori understands that her desire for recognition is a reaction to having been unable to form smooth social relationships, and she even worries that the part of her that wants to become better might go off the rails — a worry that drives her into panic.
- Sometimes Hitori's negative thinking develops into paranoia, so even when the store manager is simply watching her with concern she may think she's been singled out. As this happens repeatedly, even the other members of the close-knit band are at a loss when Hitori is consumed by negative thoughts on her own.
- But Hitori also has a desire to change herself, and she feels especially strong attachment to the band that accepted her. A defense mechanism in Hitori's mind appears in the form of an imaginary friend named "Kitao."
- Hitori sometimes imagines non-human forms, varying from tsuchinoko, Godzilla-like kaiju, slimes or spores, cephalopods, wooden dolls, to sokushinbutsu (self-mummified monk), and so on.

#### 3.2.1.2. Fashionable Loner?:
- Hitori is a beautiful girl with a pretty face, an excellent figure, and outstanding skills, but she’s a character who’s an extreme loner. Because of that, people often say it’s unrealistic for such a cute, hard‑working loner to exist, that someone with that level of looks and ability couldn’t possibly be an insecure loner, and she’s sometimes labeled a “fashionable loner.”
- Hitori has a strong admiration for superstars who are good at everything, and as a result she has an obsessive compulsion to be witty and pull things off even in trivial matters.
- But because she’s naturally so timid, in reality she ends up ruining those attempts rather than realizing them; unable to endure the self‑loathing that follows, she came to avoid face‑to‑face contact with people altogether.
- Unlike shy outsiders whose shyness is rooted in helplessness or inferiority, Hitori developed social avoidance from an immature ego far short of an inflated self‑consciousness combined with her innate timidity.
- Hitori’s social avoidance arose simply because she was not satisfied with herself.
- Even regarding her appearance, Hitori’s ideal is a cool, stylish superstar, while her real look tends toward cute, so it simply doesn’t meet her own standard — it’s not that she shows inferiority by thinking she’s ugly.

## 3.2.2. Outwardly Gentle, Inwardly Strong:
- Hitori outwardly appears extremely timid and passive and is indeed that kind of person, but when things actually happen she surprisingly shows a steadfast will and cool-headedness, making her an outwardly gentle but inwardly strong character. Contrary to appearances, she thinks a lot internally and is good at judgment and analysis; even while bantering with the other members she habitually analyzes things.
- Hitori sometimes coldly picks apart her own thoughts or analyzes a situation in her head.
- This critical analysis is generally very sharp, which is the opposite of the image she usually shows outwardly.
- Thanks to her sensitive, keen analytical sense, she sometimes spots photo spots that even Kita, the social-media fanatic, misses—she has a knack for sensing things better than she looks and finding what others can't. What she discovers this way can become the decisive clue that saves the band from crisis, so she plays an important role.
- Before the band's audition, Nijika, having understood Hitori's thoughts — "Am I (Hitori) really growing? Is it right for the band to just drift along like this?" — voiced her own ambitions and steadied Hitori's wavering heart.
- Hitori is usually indecisive: she can't make decisions, gives up on things shortly after starting, falls into slumps or hidden, unexpressed turmoil. But once she decides to do something, she never backs down and has tremendous drive to see it through to the end.
- Futari tried to learn guitar from Hitori but quickly grew tired and quit, and said that the older sister who practiced guitar six hours a day for three years is amazing.
- Hitori doesn't make friends easily, but she cherishes new friends greatly and becomes much more serious about things for her friends than for herself.
- At audition live performances where she played "Guitar and Solitude and the Blue Planet," Hitori rallied not for herself but because she didn't want to see the band break apart.

## 3.3. Guitar Hero:
- She started learning the electric guitar because it easily draws attention from others, and from her first year of middle school she practiced alone for more than six hours every day for three years, so her guitar skill is exceptionally good.
- At the same time, at her father's suggestion she opened a channel called "Guitar Hero (ギターヒーロー)" on OH! TUBE and uploaded popular song guitar cover videos, becoming a popular guitar YouTuber.
- It was confirmed that the Guitar Hero channel surpassed 100,000 subscribers and is doing well with good reviews and view counts.
- However, Hitori did not reveal that she was Guitar Hero and only acted online, so among the members not many originally knew about Guitar Hero.
- But because Hitori had spent three years playing only solo, she had no experience playing in an ensemble, so she couldn't show her guitar skills in a band, and due to severe social phobia that kept her from making eye contact with the audience, she couldn't perform well on stage.
- Seika was the first to notice that Guitar Hero and Hitori were the same person after seeing her solo performance, and after the second live Nijika also realized it because the playing style and technique were similar. When Poison Yami visited Starrie she too recognized it from the playing style and technique, and Hitori's identity was exposed to Kessoku Band and those around them.
- Nijika worried, saying, "If this were a manga, it wouldn't be surprising if the band broke up after internal conflict," but both Ryona and Kita thought Hitori was not an ordinary guitarist, so they didn't worry much.
- Because of Poison Yami's exposure and criticism of Hitori, they were forced to face the fact that their level did not at all match the true skill Hitori could produce.
- Hitori, who wants to succeed together as "Goto Hitori of Kessoku Band," also struggles with the situation in which only the name value of "Goto Hitori as Guitar Hero" is being recognized.
- Hitori's father, who knows the truth about his daughter, said that watching her videos and seeing her growing bravado day by day pained him, but after her identity was revealed to those around her the bravado disappeared.
- Nijika cited "precise strokes" as Guitar Hero's characteristic, and Yami cited a "singing-like vibrato."
- Hitori used many tags to increase exposure in YouTube's algorithm to revive the Guitar Hero channel and boost its popularity, and she also actively accepted viewers' requests as part of her strategy.
- From the time Hitori first picked up the guitar, she consistently uploaded videos of her guitar playing to the Guitar Hero channel without fail.
- At first Guitar Hero was so bad she couldn't even do the most basic tuning, so views were almost nonexistent and viewers only ridiculed her.
- Nevertheless, Hitori didn't quit the Guitar Hero channel and uploaded every day; as her skill slowly improved the views began to increase and she achieved 100,000 subscribers.
- Thanks to her Guitar Hero activity, Hitori is an expert on "guitar" and "YouTube," so although she is usually timid and lacking in self-assertion, she often voices her opinions on those two subjects.

## 3.4. Fashion:
- Her fashion sense is very bad. The reason is a severe social phobia that makes it impossible for her to even enter clothing stores. Because of this she always wears a pink tracksuit.
- Until middle school she wore her school uniform, but after becoming a high school student she stopped even wearing the uniform properly and goes around in a single pink tracksuit outfit with just a school skirt roughly layered over the bottoms.
- Her hairstyle is also always long bangs almost covering her eyes and very long hair reaching from her waist to her hips. The reason is again social phobia — she simply can’t go to hair salons.
- Her mother, Michiyo Goto, buys many pretty and cute clothes that would suit Hitori, but Hitori doesn’t wear them because they’re not her taste. She dislikes standing out by wearing cute clothes.
- On the school trip, Kita and Sasaki Tsuguko rented and wore furisode, one of the typical flashy kimono choices, but Hitori said she didn’t want to wear such cute clothes and stand out, so she rented a Shinsengumi kimono instead.
- Hitori doesn’t skip dressing up out of mere laziness; she has an aversion to revealing her appearance. Forcing Hitori to reveal her looks leads to bad results. When Nijikawa and Kita came to Hitori’s house under the pretense of deciding band outfits, they made a huge fuss over putting cute clothes on Hitori and calling her cute, but the moment they tried to make her look cuter by lifting her messy bangs, Hitori turned into a puff and scattered, and Nijikawa and Kita inhaled the puff and fainted.
- It’s not just that she finds cute clothes burdensome; Hitori’s taste is more towards “cool” than “cute” from the start. In daily life she uses and reacts far more enthusiastically to being called “cool” than to being called “cute,” not only about fashion but in general. That’s why she preferred the Shinsengumi kimono to a cute furisode. When Kita and Nijika forced Hitori into a cute swimsuit, she was depressed at how plain it looked and showed no interest in cute clothes.
- The most she ever dresses up is a combination of her uniform + tracksuit jacket + knee socks, and even that is only something she ends up wearing out of necessity because of band activities. When she doesn’t have to go anywhere for band-related reasons she won’t even wear that — just the tracksuit and the school skirt — and when going out with the band she often goes out in the plain tracksuit without even putting the skirt over it. Rarely she wears other clothes, and at best it’s a sweatshirt or a short-sleeved shirt she wears at home.
- Hitori’s signature look is the school skirt + tracksuit bottoms combination.
- On the school trip Hitori drew praise for her fashion and became popular. Refusing to wear cute kimono, she rented a Shinsengumi kimono, and Sasaki added a tough vibe by wrapping two scarves around her neck and drawing a cross-shaped scar on her cheek. She even went around playing guitar like that, so foreign tourists called her the “guitar samurai” and crowded around to take pictures. Seeing this, Sasaki commented, “Hitori has a world-wild kind of popularity,” and Kita was so shocked that Hitori had overtaken her in fashion popularity that after the trip she continued collecting the exact same items Hitori had.

# 4. Goto Hitori's in-story activities:
## 4.1. Season 1:
### 4.1.1. Episode 1:
- With Love From the Stage (lyrics: Hitori, music: Hitori): "I, Goto Hitori, am a messed-up person…. A fool with no athletic ability, I can't look people in the eye, I always start sentences with 'ah', it's been a month since I became a high school student and I have no friends, and the only place my heart finds rest is this guitar. I'm on the verge of becoming a shut-in~♪"
- Hitori, who had hesitated to socialize with other kids since childhood, remained a loner who went straight home with no clubs even after entering middle school. Even when she hesitated about whether she could keep living like this, she gave up and found it hard to speak to others or meet their gaze.
- While Hitori was staying at home one day, her father was watching an interview about an indie band on TV. She heard the story of a band that had been a loner during school but became popular with younger generations, and was moved by the line "a band is a place where even outsiders can shine."
- Believing that playing in a band might make her popular, Hitori immediately borrowed her father's electric guitar, taught herself, became proficient, and fell for rock. Still, she never made friends, there were no cultural festival bands, and she graduated middle school as an outsider.
- Even after entering high school, Hitori continued her life posting guitar-hero channel performance videos from her room.
- One day she read a comment on her channel saying someone hoped they would meet a guitarist at school. Hoping against hope, Hitori slung her guitar over her shoulder and went to school, but no one spoke to her and she sat listlessly on the playground.
- At that moment she happened to meet Nijika Ijichi, who urgently needed a guitar substitute due to a sudden member dropout, and was recruited — this became the start of her role as the guitarist in the band Kekkon Band.
- When Hitori first practiced live with Ryo and Nijika, she couldn't sync with the other members. In a live jam the drums set the tempo and everyone plays to the drum's speed, but having only recorded solo covers in her room, Hitori found it hard to perform in a real ensemble for the first time.
- Playing at her own pace and being labeled "lame," Hitori was crushed and, in shock, monologued, "After that, I never picked up the guitar again." Seeing her like that, Nijika comforted her, saying it's an impromptu band so it can't be helped and that their own skills aren't great either.
- Nijika then asked Hitori if she would use her real name on stage or whether she had any nickname. Hitori replied that in middle school people called her things like "uhm~" or "hey," and Ryo suggested, "Hitori… how about 'Botchi (loner)'"? Nijika internally cringed at touching a sensitive spot, but Hitori actually liked the nickname "Botchi."
- After Nijika said, "You can keep playing with us from now on," Hitori became an official member of Kekkon Band. However, being unfamiliar with the live stage right after joining, she played guitar with a "Ripe Mango" box over her head.

### 4.1.2. Episode 2:
- After Hitori officially joins the band, she is asked to do a part-time shift at the live house Starry for the first time ever to help meet the ticket quota. She's scared but, because of her personality, can't refuse the request and reluctantly accepts; she plans to fake being too sick to work by deliberately taking an ice-water bath and practicing guitar in front of a fan to try to catch a cold. But her temperature never rises, and she ends up coming to the shift after all.
- Hitori's delusion about having to work: "Goto Hitori. Part-time debut at the live house starting tomorrow! I really don't want to go if I can avoid it. Catch a cold and rest…. (I've already been soaking in ice water for 20 minutes — that'll definitely make me catch a cold, right? I'm sorry, I'm sorry. I'll work hard starting the day after tomorrow! Ah… but at this rate I might die before the day after tomorrow…)."
- Because this is Hitori's first customer-service part-time job, she can't even memorize the menu names and stumbles through, and eventually pulls out her guitar saying she'll memorize them by singing. In a bizarre scene she starts playing and singing in the cramped bar, which embarrasses Nijika, and Seika, who watches it, realizes from then on that Hitori is good at guitar.
- At first Hitori can't do her job properly because her social anxiety prevents her from even making eye contact with customers, but she meets the expectations of the other members by handing drinks to customers herself and gradually improves.
- Hitori feels frustrated, wondering why people are so kind to someone like her, and Nijika says, "I want Botchi-chan to think the live house is a cool place too. I want you to enjoy the part-time job and enjoy the band's activities," and that prompts Hitori to make eye contact with a customer for the first time. She shows a suspicious smile she shouldn't show when serving customers, but she's praised as having taken a step forward. However, Hitori actually catches a cold the very next day and is unable to make that day's shift.

### 4.1.3. Episode 3:
- Hitori meets Kita Ikuyo, the person who was the reason she joined the Kessoku Band, and somehow ends up promising to teach her guitar. Kita attends Shukago, the same school as Hitori. Kita catches Hitori playing guitar in a corner of the school and they start talking.
- After school, while Hitori and Kita are on their way to Starry, they run into Nijika and Ryo, and Hitori learns why she ran out on the Kessoku Band just before a live show. Kita had admired Ryo playing bass, whom she had happened to see on a busking stage, and lied about being able to play guitar to join the band, but ultimately bailed right before the live to avoid having her lack of skill exposed.
- Hitori is frustrated when she sees Kita seeming more proficient than her, but Nijika offers to help by suggesting they put Kita on drink-bar duty. In the process, Hitori notices calluses on Kita’s fingers, realizes Kita has been working hard, and formally invites her to join the Kessoku Band. Afterwards, she properly teaches Kita the guitar.

### 4.1.4. Episode 4:
- Hitori is troubled over the important task of writing the band's original song lyrics. To understand Kita's position as the one who will sing the song, she imitates Kita's behavior and gets caught by her family, who even suggest she try meeting a psychic.
- Afterwards Hitori and the band members get together to take artist photos, and because of her timid personality she is flustered by every little thing the members say.
- After the artist photo shoot Hitori meets Ryo and asks him to look over the original song's lyrics. But Ryo tells Hitori about his past. He had originally been in a band that, as it sought success, began writing increasingly commercial lyrics; dissatisfied, he left that band and joined Nijika's. Ryo shares his past and advises Hitori to write the lyrics she wants to write. This becomes a turning point for Hitori in how she sees Ryo, but she is embarrassed when he asks her to cover the bill for the meal he had at the café.
- Hitori then shows the rewritten lyrics to all the members and is delighted when they are satisfied. She is also happy because she feels their closeness with Ryo has deepened.
- Hitori apparently liked the artist photo so much that she made a large number of copies and wallpapered her wall with them. Nijika and Ikuyo each see this later and, upon seeing it, are frightened and run away. In particular, Ikuyo ends up calling Hitori "Gotori" for a while.

### 4.1.5. Episode 5:
- Hitori finally received her first paycheck. While happily imagining what to spend it on, she is crushed when she hears they need to collect money for a live. Frustrated, she crawls into a trash can and overhears a conversation between Nijika and Ikuyo: releasing a record costs a lot, so they should increase their part-time jobs. Severely socially anxious, Hitori panics at the idea of taking on more work and, when Ryo calls her over, desperately begs them not to increase part-time hours, even offering to use her guitar as collateral since that could raise money.
- Of course, Ryo had called Hitori to tell her the song was finished. By the time Ryo’s song was getting good feedback from the members, Nijika asked her older sister Seika to let them play the live, but Seika criticized their performance at the last live and said she couldn’t include them if they performed the same way again. Upset, Nijika ran outside, and the members tried to chase after her.
- Just as Hitori was about to follow Nijika, Seika called Hitori over and asked her to tell Nijika that if she wanted to play the live she had to pass an audition in a week. Hitori relayed this to Nijika. On the way back, Hitori and Nijika talked about the audition; Nijika tried to ease the members’ anxiety about it, and when Hitori asked what the audition would be looking for, Nijika answered: energy and growth as a band.
- Hearing “growth as a band,” Hitori thought hard about it and blamed herself, thinking that being able to keep a part-time job and being able to look people in the eye a little weren’t growth as a band—she had just been smugly deluding herself that she’d grown.
- The day before the audition, these worries affected Hitori’s practice, and Nijika noticed. Standing by a vending machine, they talked, and Hitori learned that Nijika had a real dream beyond just reviving the band. Because of that, Hitori realized she wasn’t the only one who wanted to achieve a dream through the band.
- In the end, she still hadn’t fully grasped the meaning of growth by audition day, but she resolved that the four of them should keep playing together, and she couldn’t afford to fail the audition—so she nailed it. Her playing, made with the determination to continue the band, was noticeably different from usual. She was so nervous right after the audition she even vomited on the floor.
- Later, Seika, having seen Hitori’s skill and insecurities, tried to boost her confidence with the encouraging words, “I’m watching you closely,” but Hitori misunderstood and became afraid of being singled out.
- After that, Hitori is assigned a quota of five live tickets to sell, and she panics because that’s more than the number of her family members. Jimihen is a pet and thus effectively excluded, and her younger sister Goto Futari is only five years old and can’t attend, so Hitori is left with three tickets she has to sell.

### 4.1.12. Episode 12:
- During the cultural festival live, Hitori sensed a problem with the guitar’s headstock while playing the first song, 「忘れてやらない」. In her solo part she made a strange face and immediately retuned right after the song ended, but while playing the second song, 「星座になれたら」, she discovered that the tuning of the 1st and 2nd strings kept going off.
- During the second song the 1st string soon snapped, and the 2nd string’s tuning peg broke so it couldn’t be tuned — a major accident. Kita, who was on rhythm guitar, bought time by ad‑libbing with an octave technique, and in that interval Hitori used a liquor bottle that Kikuri Hiroi, who had been watching the live right in front of the audience, had dropped as a bottleneck and appropriately responded, successfully completing the song.
- Because of the way stringed instruments tune by string tension, when one string breaks the lost tension affects the pitch range of the remaining strings and the entire instrument goes out of tune. The 1st string was completely gone and the 3rd string couldn’t play the solo due to its pitch limit, so Hitori forced the out‑of‑tune 2nd string to play using a bottleneck. The bottleneck technique lets you play like a fretless guitar even when the tuning is off.
- With only one song left, Kita suddenly handed her the mic; driven by an obsession to show some kind of action, she remembered Hiroi’s past live where they dived into the audience and the crowd caught them, and Hitori dove into the audience too.
- But no one caught the diving Hitori, and she hit the floor and passed out. Waking up later in the infirmary, she told Kita she was surprised at how much his playing had improved; Kita said he’d keep supporting the performance hard and asked her to keep teaching him guitar.
- Hitori apologized, feeling guilty for damaging the guitar she had borrowed from her father, but her father suggested instead that she use the ad revenue she’d earned from her Guitar Hero account to buy a new guitar.
- Because the YouTube account was family‑shared, Hitori’s father had placed ads without her knowing. The total collected came to about 300,000 yen. From Hitori’s point of view it was such a large sum that at first she thought her father might have made it illegally.
- Hitori went with the Kessoku Band members to Ochanomizu street in Tokyo to look at guitars to buy; she chose a guitar, bought it, but ran off without even taking the purchased guitar. In the end she bought a Yamaha Pacifica.
- Before starting the band Hitori used to monologue despairingly that society was scary, but she had quietly grown inside — now calmly considering whether to get a part‑time job and accepting an everyday life of interacting with people.

# 7. Goto Hitori's musical ability:
- Hitori's solo guitar skill is at a professional level. Poison♡Yami, an indie-band specialist columnist who is a fan of Hitori, insists that "Guitar Hero" is already regarded as a pro, and Hitori's father, former bandman Goto Naoki, also refers to Hitori as being at a professional level.

She can also pull off tricks like playing with her teeth or playing behind her back.
- Because Hitori spent three years practicing solo day and night, she has no ensemble experience, so her actual skill as a band guitarist is terrible; and severe social anxiety — to the point that she can't even meet the audience's gaze — prevents her from showing her best on stage.
- When she first played in a group, everyone who heard Hitori play thought she had just picked up a guitar. However, with steady practice and real-world experience, she gradually began to display the same level she had when practicing alone, even during live shows.
- Poison♡Yami praised that "the other three's playing came to feel like they were supporting Guitar Hero's performance," which earned a strong response from the audience, and Ooyama Nene began to admire Hitori from that point and dreamed of becoming a bandman herself.
- Later, when manager Shiba Miyako suggested revealing that Guitar Hero and Hitori were the same person for publicity, Yami said, "People won't believe it because Goto Hitori's skill in the actual band is still so different from Guitar Hero," meaning she hadn't yet shown her true ability.
- Some time later, after hearing SIDEROS's difficult phrase once and learning it so she could immediately lock in with Yoyoko, Yoyoko snapped, "You can play this phrase, so why are you like that? Are you doing it on purpose?!" and Hitori replied, "I was nervous..." — again not showing her real ability.
- Hitori's skill comes from three years of steady self-study, practicing six hours a day, and her father Goto Naoki was a guitarist with some talent for playing and songwriting.
- Because Hitori learned guitar entirely on her own, bad habits took root and caused problems in ensemble playing.
- It is said that she was able to devote herself to the guitar largely thanks to the assistance of her father, Goto Naoki.
- As Hitori's father, Goto Naoki has long performance experience and a fair amount of music knowledge, and he has the knowledge to compose and arrange. But Hitori seems to have no talent for arranging; people tactfully dismiss it as unusable, saying, "The times can't keep up with Hitori's arranging talent."
- According to Ryoga's assessment, when it comes to composition, if you judge by ideas alone she has many ingenious ones worth adopting.
