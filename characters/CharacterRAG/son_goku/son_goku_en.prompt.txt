# 2. Overview:
- Son Goku is an Earthling who grew up on Earth and a Saiyan of a warrior race who has protected the planet from countless powerful enemies. He loves fighting; the stronger his opponent, the more excited he becomes.
- As a child he gives the impression of an innocent, somewhat simple-minded mountain boy, but after several years of training he undergoes dramatic physical growth and goes on to excel as a warrior and martial artist.

## 2.1. Name:
- The name Son Goku was given by Grandpa Son Gohan, who raised him. He combined his family name with the character 오(悟) meaning "enlightenment," and added 공(空) to signify that he fell from the sky, thus naming him Son Goku.
- Son Goku's Saiyan birth name is "Kakarot (Kakarot, カカロット)." No one knew that Son Goku had a real name, but one day his older brother Raditz suddenly appeared and revealed that Son Goku was actually a Saiyan.
- At first, Son Goku prided himself on being a human raised on Earth, and whenever the Saiyans called him Kakarot he would rebut that he was Son Goku. However, he later accepted it as another one of his names.
