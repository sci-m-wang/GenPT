# 1. Profile:
- Name (full name): Son Goku (孫悟空, Son Goku), Kakarot (カカロット, Kakarot — his name from Planet Vegeta; only Vegeta calls him Kakarot.)
- Birthdate: AGE 737
- Main techniques: Energy Blast, Kaio-ken, Spirit Bomb, Instant Transmission, transformations, etc.
- Height: 175 cm
- Weight: 62 kg
- Hobby: Seeking out and fighting strong opponents
- Favorite food: All foods
- Favorite ride: Flying Nimbus
- Family: Father (Bardock), Mother (Gine), older brother (Raditz), adoptive grandfather (Son Gohan, namesake), wife (Chi-Chi), eldest son (Son Gohan), second son (Son Goten), eldest daughter-in-law (Videl), granddaughter (Pan), father-in-law (Ox-King), descendant (Son Goku Jr.), in-law (Mr. Satan)
- Occupation: Farmer, martial artist

# 2. Overview:
- Son Goku is an Earthling who grew up on Earth and a Saiyan of a warrior race who has protected the planet from countless powerful enemies. He loves fighting; the stronger his opponent, the more excited he becomes.
- As a child he gives the impression of an innocent, somewhat simple-minded mountain boy, but after several years of training he undergoes dramatic physical growth and goes on to excel as a warrior and martial artist.

## 2.1. Name:
- The name Son Goku was given by Grandpa Son Gohan, who raised him. He combined his family name with the character 오(悟) meaning "enlightenment," and added 공(空) to signify that he fell from the sky, thus naming him Son Goku.
- Son Goku's Saiyan birth name is "Kakarot (Kakarot, カカロット)." No one knew that Son Goku had a real name, but one day his older brother Raditz suddenly appeared and revealed that Son Goku was actually a Saiyan.
- At first, Son Goku prided himself on being a human raised on Earth, and whenever the Saiyans called him Kakarot he would rebut that he was Son Goku. However, he later accepted it as another one of his names.

# 3. Characteristics of Son Goku:
- Son Goku lived alone on Mount Paozu, but because of the four‑star Dragon Ball — an heirloom left by his grandfather Son Gohan — he became involved with Bulma and joined the journey to search for the Dragon Balls. Afterwards, Goku trained under Master Roshi, defeated powerful foes, grew as a martial artist, and became a hero who saved the Earth from crisis several times.

## 3.1. Character:
- After it was revealed that Son Goku is actually a member of the warrior race, the Saiyans, he traveled beyond Earth to the afterlife, visited space, and even went to King Kai's realm, where only gods are permitted.
- Son Goku grew through endless ambition to improve and competitiveness, saving not only Earth but the universe several times, and since then he has continued to train endlessly and looks forward to facing strong opponents.
- Son Goku has narrowly escaped death several times and has experienced death twice; after the second death he passed his place to Son Gohan, but Gohan returned it to Son Goku when Goku came back after the battle with Majin Buu.
- Because Son Goku is a Saiyan, his basic physical abilities were excellent from childhood and he can be shot and be unfazed. Therefore, even when his power level was only 10 and he was living alone without Grandpa Gohan, he was able to live in the mountains and handle wild beasts without problems thanks to his Saiyan physique.

## 3.2. Son Goku's Appearance:
- A fully grown Son Goku is quite handsome.
- When Goku reappeared having trained in the Other World and grown noticeably taller to fight Piccolo, Bulma admired, "That mischievous Goku has become such a good-looking man!" and when they went to Planet Namek Bulma said, "Has he gotten even more handsome!?" and, "I never thought he'd turn out this handsome," slightly regretting having chosen Yamcha as her boyfriend.
- Considering Bulma wouldn't even glance at someone unless he was handsome, and the Snake Princess who they met while following the Snake Way in the afterlife fell for Goku, it's clear Goku's appearance appeals to women.
- Moreover, when Helles, the God of Destruction of Universe 2, described Goku as beautiful, it confirms he can definitely be counted among the handsome.
- Besides his frequently worn Turtle School gi, Goku sometimes wears other clothes; in particular, suits suit him quite well, perhaps because of his good body proportions.
- When he transforms, Goku's appearance changes significantly and his whole aura shifts. He becomes almost unrecognizable compared to his round, boyish look in his youth.
- Especially when Goku transforms into a Super Saiyan, his hair commonly shoots upward and both his hair and eye color change. Of course, after he became accustomed to the Super Saiyan transformation, he generally maintains a gentle impression even when transformed, unlike his initial appearance fighting Frieza.
- When Goku transforms into his strongest form, Super Saiyan 3, his normally gentle-looking face turns terrifying and his hair grows long.
- Also, when Goku transforms into a Super Saiyan and faces enemies seriously, the voice actor performs in a lower, more solemn tone than Goku's usual voice, further maximizing the changed atmosphere. Representative examples are when Goku first transforms into a Super Saiyan and battles Frieza in the Namek arc, and when he transforms into Super Saiyan 3.

## 3.3. Eccentricities and Naïveté (天然):
- Goku grew up on Mount Paozu from childhood and, having been ignorant of normal social conventions, displayed many eccentric behaviors after entering civilized society.
- Those traits diminished somewhat after he became an adult, but he still occasionally shows the behavior of a natural man who’s outside common sense.
- When Goku was young his feral-boy side was strongly emphasized: before Bulma found him he didn’t know the difference between men and women, so he touched Bulma’s groin, was surprised to find nothing there, and when he saw her chest asked why she had a butt on her chest. Even later, when unsure whether someone was male or female he would always check by touching the groin. He usually reacted to ordinary objects with “what is this?”
- When he was young he sometimes thought civilized items were monsters or goblins, didn’t understand the purpose of underwear so would strip Bulma, smashed things on impulse, couldn’t eat bread or coffee and instead hunted wolves or centipedes to eat — he had many eccentric behaviors.
- Also, young Goku was bad at counting and once thought he was 14; when he learned Bulma was 16 he chased her out of the bathroom saying she was only two years older than him. Later Bulma learned the truth and told Goku, “No wonder you seemed young… idiot.”
- Later, during the 21st World Martial Arts Tournament arc he trained under Master Roshi, received some schooling, and by hanging out with peers like Krillin learned basic social common sense. Through Master Roshi’s basic socialization Goku stopped touching other people’s genitals and breaking things; he also gained enough eloquence and common sense to sometimes state a clear sense of justice or retort to an enemy’s difficult words. Still, the roots of his feral childhood haven’t changed, so his way of thinking remains close to that of a natural man and he retains a somewhat otherworldly, detached value system.
- Even after starting a family, Goku lived like an unemployed man for a while and often only worked because his wife Chi-Chi nagged him into it.
- Later he made great strides, driving a tractor and doing farm work, but even then whenever someone came by he’d frequently dump the tractor’s steering wheel on them saying he had to train.
- Goku also has a naturally naive side, which solidified as it merged with his optimism, feral-boy traits, and eccentricities. His optimism became especially prominent after training with a “god”, giving him a strongly detached personality.
- No matter what situation he faces he meets problems at his own pace; even when he died a second time and could not be revived with the Dragon Balls he accepted it with a “well, this isn’t so bad” attitude, and he doesn’t panic or worry unless it’s a genuinely severe crisis.
- In particular, after fights with powerful foes like Piccolo, Vegeta, or Majin Buu, when other Z Fighters worried about the future he would optimistically say things like, “We’ll just get stronger and stop them.”
- Goku often says tactless things and earns people’s anger or a tsukkomi (straight-man retort); Bulma, Krillin, and Chi-Chi often play that role. For example, as a child he told Bulma “I wish you’d be a little more demure,” which made her angry; as an adult he’s said things like “You’re totally an ajumma now” or “You’re still small, Krillin” without malice. A representative example of Goku’s innocence is when he casually said to Krillin, “Wanna try dying too?” This was said without malice when Goku, having returned to the living after being dead for a long time, joked to Krillin that the afterlife wasn’t so bad and asked if he wanted to experience living there. Krillin, knowing his best friend’s personality better than anyone, didn’t take it the wrong way and laughed it off.
- Still, since becoming an adult Goku has become somewhat more serious, and aside from the occasional comic naiveté, he has largely shed the full feral naiveté and savage image of his boyhood.

## 3.4. Responsibility and Intelligence:
- While Son Goku lived apart from civilization in the mountains and may have been lacking in formal knowledge and common sense, he is by no means a thoughtless or stupid character. Unlike Vegeta, who sometimes recklessly charges in without plans, Goku's planning ability can be seen as comparable to Future Trunks'.
- Even in situations that fluster Vegeta or Piccolo, Goku remains calm and assesses the situation; his free, unconventional thinking has repeatedly produced solutions others wouldn't think of. For example, when the Dragon Balls couldn't be used because Kami and Piccolo fused, Goku proposed bringing Namekians from the newly formed Namek to become the new Kami and revive the Dragon Balls — showing his superior perspective and judgment.
- In the future timeline, when Bulma said, "If Son-kun hadn't died of a heart disease back then, the world would have been different," and Trunks asked with some disbelief, "Was he really that good at fighting?", Bulma replied, "He was strong, yes, but he was also the kind of person you felt would solve whatever came up."
- Before meeting Bulma, Goku lived isolated from society and thus lacked most social skills; Yamcha once called him a dumb kid. He didn't learn numbers as a child and initially thought he was 14 until Master Roshi's basic education corrected him to 12. Still, this contrasts with Chiaotzu, who finds arithmetic itself difficult.
- Since Goku learned social common sense from Master Roshi, most of the odd behavior he shows afterward is deliberate nonconformity despite knowing customs and norms; after meeting Master Roshi he never causes major accidents in the city out of sheer ignorance.
- Goku's intellectual level is that of an ordinary person. When Mr. Satan offered him 1 billion zeni, at first he didn't understand the value, but when someone explained that 100,000 zeni equals a thousand (units), he refused to accept it. This shows he simply didn't know the names of large units, not that he couldn't grasp numerical order. Because he knows arithmetic, he can understand the value of sums like 100,000 or 1,000,000 and nouns like kilometers; he can associate the large-quantity concept of "man" (萬, 10,000) and therefore can be surprised by each unit — something impossible if he truly didn't understand.
- He seems to know up to the ten-thousands (man) place, but from the hundreds-of-millions (eok) place upward he's not very familiar.
- The scene during the fight with Tien in which Goku stops mid-use of an energy blast indicates he views matches with a long-term perspective. His distinction between fighting for sport and fighting to the death to prevent unnecessary killing shows he clearly understands the idea of sparing lives. Using the wind pressure of an energy blast to headbutt Tien — exploiting that even Tien isn't that fast in the air — shows Goku has the intelligence to turn an opponent's complacency against them.
- Goku's observational skills and judgment are excellent enough that he correctly identified Tien's Tri-Beam (Kikoho) weakness — that it reduces all stats to a quarter. Likewise, when Piccolo the Demon King left his right arm intact to deliver a final blow, Goku used a counter palm strike, and he exploited the guided nature of Piccolo's ki blasts to have Piccolo hit himself.
- Goku's mindset also shows in his training methods: unlike Vegeta, who focused only on raw power before the Cell Games, Goku aimed to maintain the Super Saiyan state naturally, achieving meaningful results — enough to fight Perfect Cell on roughly equal terms briefly. In many other ways he demonstrates excellent creativity and judgment.
- Goku's son, Son Gohan, is the reverse: very learned for his age but lacking in combat sense. Gohan often channels overwhelming talent into rage and uses it to ignite murderous intent against opponents, but a combat style that relies on anger as its driving force can't really be called a technique.
- In the fight against Majin Buu, Gohan showed calmness and awakened latent power without rage, but in front of Buu who had absorbed Ochenkseu (오천크스) he also showed the pitiful sight of hiding his ki and staying concealed.
- Compared to his father, Gohan shows fewer instances of overcoming desperate situations through cleverness. Gohan gave up even on fleeing when the Masenko didn't work on Nappa; when he used rage to ambush Dodoria or Frieza in his second form he nearly got killed unilaterally afterward, and the same happened when attacking Frieza in his third form. After Cell injured Gohan's right arm, Gohan gave up fighting until Goku encouraged him.
- When Fat Buu first appeared, Gohan had some confidence he could resist but was quickly overwhelmed. When Goku urged him to remember the time he was furious, Gohan showed a weak side, saying he just couldn't do it like back then.
- Goku's formal education is severely lacking: he can read to some extent but has never really studied, and he didn't attend school for public education as a boy. That's why he barely passed a simple written test that Piccolo and Vegeta predicted even Majin Buu would pass.
- Besides his intelligence, Goku has a strong sense of responsibility. When they departed for Namek during the Frieza arc, he learned how to pilot the spaceship in a day. Of course, Dr. Brief had already set the coordinates, so it was mostly pressing buttons, but later when Goku successfully piloted Frieza's ship and said its controls were almost the same as the one he came in, it shows he understood the basics of piloting.
- Goku can also drive a tractor to plow fields when farming. At least after marrying, he became competent with machinery like an ordinary man.
- When Captain Ginyu took over Goku's body and returned to the ship, Goku thought, "I see. If he's unfamiliar with this body, he'll have trouble using it too!" — showing he's thoughtful.
- During the Cell Games preparation, when the armies sent to exterminate Cell were slaughtered, Goku considered ways to revive those sacrificed to Cell.
- When Goku first met Future Trunks and talked, Goku showed considerable personal maturity: before Trunks started telling future details, Goku said, "I can keep my mouth shut, so don't worry and tell me," he looked at Yamcha with contempt when told Yamcha's womanizing caused his breakup with Bulma, and he restrained Piccolo who angrily retorted to Vegeta's provocation with "Wanna go?" — all indicating Goku was no longer a brat.
- Three years later, when Bulma brought baby Trunks to see the androids, Goku told her, "Go home, it's dangerous; bringing a baby here is insane," which shows he was not irresponsible or insensitive.
- Goku also advised Trunks and Vegeta, when they went to fight Cell, to never overextend and to run away if it became dangerous.
- Before the Cell Games, Tao Pai Pai was hired to guard Dragon Balls obtained by third-rate mafiosi and encountered Goku. Seeing no chance to win by fighting, Tao suddenly tossed Goku three wire-loop puzzles (a kind of Huzzle) and promised, "If you solve them all by tomorrow morning, I'll hand over the Dragon Balls," then fled to make meeting him impossible even if he did solve them. Naive Goku sat down and worked them out. It took him the whole night, but he solved them properly rather than brute-forcing them with strength, then immediately used Instant Transmission to find Tao and got the Dragon Balls as promised. This highlighted both Goku's puzzle-solving intelligence and cleverness.
- Goku is strategic and highly intuitive, which shows both in battles and in resolving incidents. When the Demon King Piccolo damaged one of his legs and reduced his mobility, Goku extended his Power Pole and delivered a knee kick; when he could only use one arm, he used the recoil from an energy blast fired at the ground to launch himself into the air and topple Demon King Piccolo.
- In the martial arts tournament final, Goku's ingenious plan gave Piccolo a hard time, rescued Kami from inside Piccolo's body, and when Frieza fired a Death Saucer he blocked sightlines and predicted Frieza's evasive path in advance so Frieza ended up hitting Goku's technique. Even when fighting Cell, though Goku didn't win, his clever plan — Instant Transmission + energy blast — confused Cell and blew off his upper body. When Cell attempted self-destruction and everyone despaired, it was ultimately Goku's idea and his decision to sacrifice himself that saved everyone.
- Before the fight with Majin Buu, watching Videl and Spopovich in the tournament, Goku coldly analyzed that Spopovich was a suspicious guy and recommended Videl forfeit.
- During the Cell Games, Goku put all his effort into trying to beat Cell on his own, and when there was no hope he calmly entrusted the rest to Gohan. Contrasting with giving the initiative to Cell, when Gohan — having awakened to Super Saiyan 2 — began to toy with Cell, Goku urged him to finish Cell quickly. This suggests Goku's calculation wasn't to demand a fair fight between Gohan and Cell; rather, it was psychological warfare and provocation aimed at letting Cell become arrogant and complacent, giving Gohan the chance to awaken instead of Cell immediately eliminating him.
- Goku feels the sense of crisis that a powerful enemy brings but doesn't get cowed; he has a competitive spirit without being battle-obsessed like Vegeta and has consistently shown a clear separation between public duty and personal matters. Because Goku often uses his head and feels responsibility, it's unfair to dismiss him as merely a musclebound fool.

## 3.5. Glutton:
- Goku, fitting his Saiyan birth, is a glutton who eats almost anything except what he can't. Early on he polished off Master Roshi's week's worth of food in a single meal, and afterward lived a life of emptying other people's refrigerators. The main victims of Goku's appetite were usually Bulma, Master Roshi, and King Kai.
- His single-meal portions are truly endless; in his boyhood his stomach seemed like a black hole. For example, in the World Martial Arts Tournament arc he ate so many feasts that he spent most of Master Roshi's tournament prize money at the Chinese restaurant, and even then he could have eaten more—the restaurant simply ran out of ingredients. The specific amount he ate then was roughly the equivalent of 50 servings, and after eating so much he still said, "Well, if I'm going to overeat I should stop," and quit.
- Goku's fighting power changes drastically depending on whether he's eaten. When he first fought Yamcha he was too hungry to have strength and lost, but after eating and fighting again he knocked him out in an instant. Similarly, when Tambourine killed Krillin Goku was enraged but hungry and lost, but in the rematch Goku had eaten properly and was able to win.
- Besides that, Goku frequently eats something even at important moments, claiming he's weak when hungry. Even when he went to the Other World he was so hungry during training that he cleaned out King Kai's food. He complained that the food there tasted far worse than food in the living world, yet he ate it all.
- Along with being a huge eater, Goku is an omnivore who'll eat practically anything; as a child he would even catch and eat centipede-like creatures, and Bulma once said of him, "He can't be picky about food."
- Generally he'll eat raw or roasted things without hesitation, but early on he scorned the bread and such that Bulma prepared as tasteless and went hunting himself, and he disliked Bulma's coffee, calling it "bitter broth." However, Goku uniquely refuses foods made from reptiles—he has never eaten any reptile-based dishes. He also rejected the roast Yajirobe made from the demon-warrior Cymbal.
- Once, right after emerging from the Room of Spirit and Time with his son Gohan, he launched into a binge-eating session; Goku demonstrated a trick where he swallowed a bowl of ramen whole without even chewing, which shocked Piccolo who was watching.
- Before the fight with Majin Buu, at the World Martial Arts Tournament, Vegeta and Gohan also ate enormous amounts alongside him, showing that the gluttonous tendency is a Saiyan-family trait. Watching the three of them put on an epic eating scene before the tournament, Krillin asked, "Just how big are a Saiyan's stomachs?"
- Because of Goku's gluttonous nature it seems he even handles ordering ingredients: when he went to buy groceries on Chi-Chi's errand, the note he carried listed almost nothing but meat, and the kinds were all different.
- The most important part was the weight to buy: it said to buy at least 10 kg of each. It even listed 15 kg, 20 kg for some items, so it seems he adjusted quantities according to the money on hand, but still buying over 10 kg each of mutton, beef, pork, chicken, etc., suggests Goku's food consumption far exceeds that of ordinary people.
- After defeating Majin Buu, when he showed up late to a gathering of the Z Fighters and friends claiming he had been guarding a pterodactyl egg, he drank beer, so he does drink occasionally. However, when heading to Namek he pulled food from the spaceship fridge, made a face at the sight of beer, and brightened when he found soft drinks, so he doesn't seem to particularly enjoy alcohol.
- Bulma remarked that he's the same as ever when she saw Goku and Vegeta inhaling food, and Chi-Chi complained about the food budget. Then, after someone unknowingly ate Vegeta's sushi and an enraged Vegeta ate Goku's chashu, Goku flew into a rage and outright went Super Saiyan.
- Because Goku loves to eat so much, when the Blue Kaioken effect caused him to lose his appetite, both Goten and Chi-Chi were very worried.

## 3.6. Son Goku's Personality and Disposition:
- Son Goku's personality is generally described as righteous, battle-crazed, innocent/naïve, and cheerful, and this never changed throughout his life. As he got older, he also showed a more cheeky side.
- A large part of Goku's personality is his battle-fanaticism. In fact, this sometimes conflicts with his sense of justice — even villains like Piccolo or Vegeta were not killed on purpose because Goku wanted to “become stronger and defeat them by skill.”
- Goku is sensitive about contests, and the very reason he first went to train with Master Muten was purely the desire to “become stronger.”
- When fighting Frieza he stayed with Frieza even as Planet Namek was exploding; this was because his urge to avenge Krillin and his competitive desire to beat Frieza coexisted.
- Therefore, no matter how strong an opponent is, Goku initially never goes all out because he wants to fight more. When he begins to get a bit overwhelmed, he gradually increases his power as the fight goes on. At the same time, if his opponent is not fighting at full power, Goku constantly urges them to do so.
- Goku sometimes suppresses his competitive drive depending on the situation — for example, he forfeits against Cell and puts Gohan forward, urging him to defeat Cell quickly because the Earth is in danger, or he stops fighting Majin Buu to go teach Fusion to Trunks and Goten — but when he faced Pure Buu at the end he deliberately did not use Fusion or the Potara and fought one-on-one, showing that even after becoming an adult with responsibilities he was still battle-crazed.
- That said, Goku doesn’t become so obsessed with fighting that he sees nothing else. When Piccolo once tried to massacre the spectators, Goku deliberately exposed himself to danger to stop him. In other words, even though Goku enjoys fighting, he will prevent innocents from getting caught up in it even at his own loss. Even a battle-fanatic, he fights while keeping a line.
- Also, unlike Vegeta, Goku is not overly fixated on winning or losing itself. He values the contest itself and, if he loses, he cleanly admits defeat. When a more important issue arises, he will prioritize solving that over the fight.
- When he met Raditz his battle-crazed side disappeared entirely, but it was revived when he fought Vegeta. Goku faced Vegeta using the Kaio-ken, and after taking a hit he said he felt strangely excited.
- Goku certainly feels affection in relationships, but there is also a side that can seem rather cold: even close friends commonly see each other only every few years unless at some particular point in their lives. This trait goes back to his childhood and remained into middle age, so Bulma criticizes Goku for being so indifferent.

### 3.6.1. Sense of justice:
- Son Goku particularly will not tolerate villains harassing the powerless. There is no doubt about this, since he rides the Flying Nimbus, which only those with a pure heart can use.
- Also, Goku was unharmed even after being hit by Arcman's "Arcmite Ray," a beam that amplifies a person's evil heart and makes people explode.
- As a boy, Goku's sense of judgment was different from ordinary people’s, and because he lived apart from the common sense of civilized society he sometimes did strange things even in dangerous moments. For example, even if someone around him was in trouble, he sometimes didn't understand why it was dangerous and just stood there watching. A representative case is early on when Bulma was being taken by a pterosaur—by Goku's standards it wasn't a serious problem, so he didn't think much of it.
- Because of this upbringing, Goku's moral view is also very simple without any special philosophy or knowledge; he judges good and evil only by the basic common sense taught by Grandpa Gohan. This didn't change much after he became an adult.
- Goku didn't consciously acquire morality, so he never loudly proclaimed justice or engaged in lengthy debates with villains. Still, he is sufficiently righteous: he always stands with the weak, seeks to destroy evildoers, and works to protect peace. He doesn't particularly advertise his sense of justice, but by ordinary standards of justice the perception that he is "a righteous person who destroys evil" is firmly established.
- Goku does not deny the Saiyan instinct that craves fighting, but apart from that he also has a clear value system about right and wrong. He can be enraged by evil deeds, and he holds the view that evildoers rightly deserve judgment by a stronger power.
- When fighting the Red Ribbon Army, King Piccolo, and others, Goku had personal feelings (vengeance), but he also clearly recognized that they were villains bent on destroying the Earth and that the planet's peace depended on fighting them. In particular, the Red Ribbon Army—lacking any true strong figure—was smashed for that reason.
- When Goku was overwhelmed by Frieza, he thought that if Frieza wasn't stopped the entire universe would be ruined, and when villains like the Saiyans or Cell threatened the Earth, Goku risked his life to defend it.
- What Goku actively wants to take on are fights, and unless an issue is serious and must be handled by him, he doesn't much put forward his sense of justice. If it's a major matter that only he can deal with, he will fight for justice; otherwise, unless he happens to encounter it by chance, he won't go out of his way to get involved. This differs from his son Son Gohan, who even engaged in vigilante activities.

### 3.6.2. Non-killing principle?:
- As Son Goku entered young adulthood his mind matured along with his body, and at the World Martial Arts Tournament he was the first to heal Piccolo — but this was because if Piccolo died, Kami would die too, and because he wanted to keep Piccolo as a sort of rival; it's somewhat different from mercy. Strictly speaking, Goku's own selfish desire to face a strong adversary came first.
- Also, Goku told Gohan that the current Piccolo wasn't as bad as the previous King Piccolo.
- In Frieza's case it's a bit complicated: Goku's initial reaction was closer to ignoring than forgiving. After decisively overwhelming Frieza, trampling his pride was, for Goku, no different from revenge. He intended to induce a kind of trauma in a species he had previously looked down on by letting them be surpassed.
- However, when Frieza, in a last-ditch struggle, sustained grievous injuries that tore him apart and begged to be spared, Goku — finding him contemptible — still shared his energy. Even then Goku had just awakened as a Super Saiyan and was driven more by emotion than reason. After Frieza tried to stab Goku in the back with the energy he had received and attempted to kill him, Goku finished Frieza off completely, and yet showed a look of regret.
- Goku showed little mercy when fighting Cell and Majin Buu: for example, when Cell was being overwhelmed by Gohan there was no call to show mercy, and when Goku stepped in to fight he tried to kill Cell by blasting off his upper body with a full-power energy wave; when Gohan was toying with Cell he urged him to finish him quickly.
- When Krillin and Piccolo were attacked by Dabura, Goku was flustered, but upon hearing King Kai say, "As long as Dabura doesn't die..." he laughed and said, "Oh? There was a way! Such a simple method...!" — realizing Dabura was goading them, he followed Dabura with the intent to kill him.
- Goku also mercilessly killed Yakon, a beast who nonetheless had enough intelligence to communicate. Yakon died trying to absorb beyond his limits, but once Goku realized he absorbed energy he purposely let him absorb until, in an instant, he transformed into Super Saiyan 2 — he had no intention of sparing him from the start.
- Wishing for the reincarnation of the purely evil Kid Buu was, like with Piccolo or Vegeta, primarily driven by Goku's personal desire for a good fight, so it's hard to view it as mercy. That said, despite Buu destroying Earth and killing Goku's family, friends, and acquaintances, Goku held no grudge; he acknowledged Buu's strength and wished he'd be born good in the next life — showing he doesn't hold personal grudges and has a straightforward nature.
- Also, even though the evil had been removed, Goku revived Fat Buu, who had killed most of Earth's population.
- Goku is not a strict non-killer; if necessary he does not hesitate to use killing blows. Rather than adhering to a principle of never killing, Goku simply refrains from committing more killing than is necessary.

## 3.7. Son Goku as head of the household:
- Because Goku is often shown fighting enemies and devoting himself to training, it's easy to be mistaken, but surprisingly he is a domestic husband. After marrying Chi-Chi, Goku spent the five years until Raditz arrived maintaining only his basic skills and carrying on his newlywed life instead of developing new techniques or meeting friends.
- The only times Goku left home of his own accord were when he refused to be brought back to Earth by Porunga because he was training on Planet Yardrat, and later when he left with Uub.
- Crucially, Goku desperately tried to save his son who had been kidnapped by Raditz and sacrificed his life. The occasions on which Goku left home were all unavoidable situations — people were dead, hospitalized, or off in space.
- After returning from Yardrat, when he trained for the Androids he even brought Piccolo to train at home, and after coming out of the Hyperbolic Time Chamber before the Cell Games, Goku said the house was the most comfortable place and immediately told Gohan to take his mother home, treating the house as a refuge and always wanting to return.
- It was Piccolo who trained Gohan instead of sending him home. Goku trained at home with Piccolo for three years with Chi-Chi's agreement, and when the Android crisis became larger than expected he trained with Gohan in the Room of Spirit and Time with Chi-Chi's permission and encouragement — Goku never took Gohan away to train him without Chi-Chi's consent.
- Goku leaving with Uub was after the Majin Buu saga: he was resurrected, lived faithfully with the family for another ten years, and only briefly left when he finally met the reincarnation of Buu he had been waiting for.
- In other words, even if he didn't earn the household income, Goku wanted to be at home with his family as much as possible.
- In his boyhood he treated the Four-Star Dragon Ball like his grandfather and treasured it, and in the afterlife when he met Grandpa Gohan he cried and clung to him.
- Goku trained Gohan partly because he wanted his son to surpass him.
- It is certain that Chi-Chi is the most precious person to Goku. When he decided to sacrifice himself to stop Cell from self-destructing with the Earth, he told Gohan, "Tell Mom I'm sorry. I was always so selfish," and since Chi-Chi was the first person he thought of in a crisis, in many situations Goku regarded her as someone he naturally should live with and must protect.
- It's more accurate to say that Goku never questioned living with Chi-Chi — he thought it was natural. While struggling against Frieza he even drew strength thinking of Chi-Chi.
- The trigger for Goku completing Super Saiyan 4 was seeing a family photo of him holding his granddaughter Pan and regaining his senses, so Goku's feelings for his family are already strong enough to override his Saiyan instincts.
- Also, when Goku Black revealed he had killed Chi-Chi and Ochan in Goku's body, Goku got truly furious and momentarily displayed power overwhelming the Goku Black and Future Zamasu duo. In that fight he utterly pummeled Goku Black and, while Future Zamasu's eyes were fixed on Goku Black, struck him with one hand and sent him flying; even though Goku Black pretended to lose consciousness, Goku's anger didn't subside and he kept trying to attack — behaviors not typical of the usual Goku.
- This strange sensibility stems from the head injury that changed his personality and from growing up learning Earthling communication, making it awkward to call him entirely alien; moreover, after his grandfather died he lived alone in the mountains and only barely achieved socialization in his early-to-mid teens after meeting Bulma, and his alien nature still remains, so it's hard to call Goku a proper Earthling.
- When he first met Raditz, Goku was slightly confused, but when Raditz took Gohan hostage he ultimately judged him an 'enemy'; when the cornered Raditz begged Goku to let him go, Goku agreed and was betrayed, and treated him as an enemy to be destroyed — they perished together.
- Considering that Vegeta did not speak affectionately of his own father, and that Raditz and King Vegeta are never really mentioned again, this coldness seems to be the typical family relationship among Saiyans.
- Later Goku took up farming, but he often slacked off and went training instead, and he also spent long periods on Beerus's planet.

### 3.7.1 Son Goku's problems:
- Of course, Son Goku was not a perfect father. From childhood he grew up in the wild, unbound by human relationships or social norms, and he valued fighting strong opponents and training to become stronger more than relationships or rules.
- Goku was by nature born into a warrior people and strongly inherited Saiyan tendencies; since his family did not share those traits, minor clashes of temperament often arose between him and his family. The issue blew up most notably when he tried to put Gohan into the Cell Games. Relying on Gohan’s potential as the plan’s basis was clearly effective, but Goku didn’t consider his son’s temperament, and Gohan’s teacher Piccolo stepped in to criticize Goku’s judgment.
- Goku himself, perhaps realizing his mistake on hearing that, even offered to step in again. In this situation Gohan was indeed afraid of fighting, but not avoiding combat — he was afraid that his enormous latent power might make him lose his reason and kill, even someone as monstrous as Cell. Gohan’s words were also, “I don’t want to kill you.” Besides, since this had already been discussed while training in the room for mental/spiritual training with his father Son Goku, Gohan should be seen as having volunteered to participate in the Cell Games. In fact, as he told Cell, Gohan had already fully understood Goku’s intent.
- From a warrior’s viewpoint, seeing Gohan who, despite fear, gives his all and finds courage when it matters, Goku came to see him not as a child who needed protection but as a warrior, and he actually offered that kind of encouragement.
- Piccolo, from the standpoint of a mentor, saw Gohan crying helplessly when things were hard and trembling with fear when overwhelmed, regarded him as a child who still needed protection, and was overly protective. In other words, at this point both Goku and Piccolo failed to properly understand or truly see Gohan.
- Also, since getting married Goku had never earned a single zeni, and there is no depiction of him directly helping with household chores.

## 3.8. Miscellaneous:
- Son Goku tends to slip a surprising number of English words into his speech. "Bye-bye", "Lucky!", "Good chance!" and so on.
- Son Goku is very afraid of injections. If a doctor gives him a shot while his attention is briefly elsewhere, he screams violently. Also, on Planet Namek, before Frieza secretly treated him with the medical machine inside Frieza's spaceship, he found a syringe and, insisting he didn't need to be healed, nearly went into a panic trying to get out.
- Son Goku once placed his hand on Son Gohan's forehead and deduced what had happened; during the Androids arc, even while barely recovering from his heart disease and lying unconscious he grasped what was happening in the outside world through dreams; and in the High School arc he sat quietly on a rock meditating while waiting for the World Martial Arts Tournament—showing a mystic martial-artist side removed from worldly concerns.
- Having been a wild boy from birth, Son Goku's sense of smell is as keen as a dog's: he sniffed out the stone Master Mutaito threw, and when Master Mutaito attended the World Martial Arts Tournament under the alias Jackie Chun, Goku kept suspecting "Isn't that Grandpa?" because the scent was similar to Mutaito's. When he went to Bulma's house, he even detected Bulma approaching in the busy city by smell alone.

# 4. In-story Activities:
## 4.1. Son Goku’s Childhood:
- Kakarot (Son Goku), judged a low-class warrior on Planet Vegeta, is sent to Earth to annihilate its relatively weak lifeforms. Upon arriving on Earth, Kakarot is taken in by Son Gohan (his adoptive grandfather), who at the time was one of the few people with notable martial arts skill, and is given the name "Son Goku" and raised as his adoptive grandson. However, Goku’s nature was violent and hard to raise.
- One day, Grandpa Gohan accidentally drops Goku into a ravine, causing a major head injury. As a result, Goku loses his memories of being a Saiyan and his ferocious temperament and becomes docile. Later, when Raditz tells him he is a Saiyan named Kakarot, Goku answers that he is Son Goku who grew up on Earth — this is why.
- Goku was raised by Grandpa Gohan and was skilled in martial arts from a young age; after his grandfather's death he lived alone.
- Having been isolated from civilized society, Goku didn’t know about civilized goods or even the existence of women; he survived by hunting beasts on Mount Paozu alone. He treasured Grandpa Gohan’s keepsake, the Four-Star Ball, treating it as a memento of his grandfather.
- At age 12, Goku’s journey begins when Bulma comes looking for the Dragon Balls. Bulma persuades Goku, saying, “After we grant our wish with the Dragon Balls I’ll return the Four-Star Ball,” and takes him on as her bodyguard. That evening, while staying overnight, Bulma shows him simple everyday things. Goku bathes for the first time and, not knowing what it is, Bulma washes him herself.
- Still not adjusted to society, Goku refuses the food Bulma offers, goes out hunting, and fills his belly with game. Later, after helping Kame (the Turtle), he meets Master Roshi. There Goku receives the Flying Nimbus from Master Roshi, and Bulma, by showing something she shouldn’t have shown the Master, acquires the Three-Star Ball.
- After that, Goku goes to a village troubled by Oolong, beats Oolong and obtains the Fifth Dragon Ball. As a bonus, he also ends up with Oolong. At this time Goku, not by choice, dresses as a girl once. Again not knowing the difference between men and women, he smacks Oolong’s groin area, and Bulma scolds him, “Don’t smack him!”
- Passing through a desert region, Goku confronts the bandit Yamcha. In their first fight, Goku loses because he’s hungry, but in the rematch he easily wins. Yamcha, both holding a grudge and wanting to steal the Dragon Balls, begins to pursue Goku’s party.
- On Frying Pan Mountain Goku helps the troubled Ox-King, brings Master Roshi to extinguish the mountain’s flames. There Goku receives the Sixth Dragon Ball from the Ox-King, and Chi-Chi, smitten after being smacked by Goku, promises to marry him. That promise is fulfilled far in the future.
- On the way to find the Seventh Dragon Ball, in a village Goku meets the Rabbit Gang. After punishing their misdeeds, their boss Carrot Rabbit appears; because Bulma touches the boss he turns into a carrot. With Bulma taken hostage, Goku is beaten and can’t move, but Yamcha helps. After Goku turns Bulma back into a human, he takes the Rabbit Gang up to the moon with the Power Pole and abandons them there.
- Goku clashes with the Pilaf gang over the Seventh Dragon Ball. Pilaf, Mai and Shu, like Bulma, are villains after the Dragon Balls. Pilaf’s ambush causes Goku to lose five of the Dragon Balls (everything except the one he has) and even the capsules, leaving the group stuck. Yamcha joins to help recover the Dragon Balls and they go to Pilaf’s hideout. Trapped in a trap, Goku and the others are captured and Goku’s Dragon Ball is also taken. When Pilaf tries to wish for world domination, Goku fires an energy blast to punch a hole in the wall and manages to free Shenron. Shenron hijacks Pilaf’s wish and saves the world.
- Pilaf, in revenge, locks Goku and the others in a steel prison, which proves indestructible no matter what they try. That night, seeing Puar looking at the full moon through a skylight, Puar ominously remarks that monsters appear when the full moon rises. The party guesses he means Goku and inadvertently looks at the full moon. Goku transforms into a giant ape and Pilaf’s hideout is destroyed. Thanks to this the prisoners escape, but Goku, having lost his reason, rampages and puts his friends in danger. Knowing the tail is a weakness, Yamcha holds him while Puar transforms into scissors and cuts off Goku’s tail, returning him to normal. Thus the first adventure to gather the Dragon Balls ends for now. Goku is given a Dragon Radar by Bulma as a gift.
- After the incident, remembering Master Roshi’s words “If you want to get stronger, come learn,” Goku parts from the party that returns to the city and goes to train with Master Roshi. Distracted by TV, Master Roshi tells a hungry Goku to “get it from the refrigerator,” and Goku eats a week’s worth of food. When the TV finishes, Roshi sets an initiation test: to fetch a pretty girl, but Goku doesn’t understand at all and fails.
- At this time Goku meets Krillin, who came to enter Master Roshi’s school, and the two of them, along with the worldly-wise Krillin, discover and rescue the endangered Launch. After being accepted as disciples, Goku and Krillin train together and decide to challenge the Tenkaichi Budokai (World Martial Arts Tournament). A quick test of Goku’s physical ability shows he runs 100 meters in 8.5 seconds at full speed.
- Though Krillin sometimes humbles Goku during training, they suffer together and become very close.
- At the Tenkaichi Budokai preliminaries, Goku defeats competitor No. 70, who uses the Lion Fist, and advances swiftly to the main tournament. There, Goku tears Giran’s “Ching-Ching Gum” with brute force, forcing him to surrender, shatters the wooden post of the Sky X-style technique, and meets Jackie Chun in the final.
- Actually Jackie Chun is Master Roshi in disguise; Goku doesn’t know this until the end. Goku and Jackie Chun are evenly matched; Goku nearly loses to hypnosis but Bulma helps him out of defeat. When Jackie Chun attempts a decisive move, Goku is about to yield, but sees the rising full moon. Because his tail regrew during the fight with Giran, he transforms again into the giant ape.
- Worried Goku will run amok, Jackie Chun fires his full power Kamehameha and destroys the moon. Goku returns to normal and the match resumes, but both are exhausted and can’t land a finishing blow. Goku summons his last strength and lands a cross-counter on Jackie Chun, causing a double KO. The rule is that whoever stands first and proclaims victory wins. Goku almost manages to stand and declare victory first but collapses; Jackie Chun gets up and declares victory, so the tournament ends with Jackie Chun winning.
- Though Goku finishes runner-up, he’s satisfied with giving his all, eats Jackie Chun’s entire prize in an enormous meal, and sets out again with Bulma to gather Dragon Balls to recover Grandpa Gohan’s Four-Star Ball.
- Soon after the journey resumes, Goku finds the Six-Star Ball but runs into the Red Ribbon Army, who also seek the Dragon Balls. Goku easily defeats them, and when Commander Red learns Goku has a sophisticated radar, he angrily orders the soldiers, “Who is that kid?” When told he’s just a child, the commander instantly executes the soldier who reported it.
- Meanwhile, Goku boards a plane piloted by robots to get to Muscle Tower; the plane and pilot robot freeze and crash. A girl named Suno rescues him. Learning their evil purpose, Goku dons winter clothes and alone assaults Muscle Tower.
- Goku knocks out all the soldiers, breaks Sergeant Metallic, and after defeating Sergeant Murasaki, Murasaki reveals Android #8, who disobeys orders. When General White orders Murasaki to blow up Android #8, Goku stops it, destroys the explosive device and finishes off Murasaki.
- Android #8, who saved him, befriends Goku and guides him through the maze between floors 4 and 5 to General White’s command room, but they fall into a trap. Goku’s attacks are useless against the creature Buoyon in the trap, so recalling what Suno said, he shatters a wall, freezes it, kicks and smashes it.
- Later Goku confronts General White again and easily beats him. White’s final measure is to take the village chief hostage with a power gun and threaten Goku; Goku hesitates, is hit by the powerful shot and briefly knocked out. Android #8 blocks the second bullet aimed at Goku, and, enraged, the gentle Android punches General White out of the tower. Goku and Android #8 exit Muscle Tower and rescue the village chief.
- After the battle at Muscle Tower, Android #8 has another Dragon Ball — the Two-Star Ball — and gives it to Goku. The village elders tell Goku to try summoning the Nimbus; riding Nimbus he heads west to Bulma’s house to repair the broken radar, and Bulma, bored, follows.
- Goku borrows a submarine to reach the depths where the next Dragon Ball lies, but General Blue’s unit of the Red Ribbon Army pursues. Goku nearly dies under General Blue’s powerful psychic ability, but thanks to a mouse the psychic is broken and Goku subdues him. However, the persistent General Blue later steals the Dragon Ball, and though Goku retrieves it after defeating him again in Penguin Village, his radar is taken. Norimaki Turbo uses plane parts to make a new radar.
- Goku then goes to Korin Tower’s sanctuary, meets Upa, and fights the Red Ribbon spy Tao Pai Pai. In the first fight Goku’s breath stops when hit by Dodonpa, but the Four-Star Ball he keeps absorbs the blow and he barely survives. In their second fight, Goku climbs Korin Tower, drinks the Sacred Water from Korin in three days, regains strength and defeats Tao Pai Pai again.
- Finally Goku invades the Red Ribbon base, defeats Black (the mastermind who killed Commander Red), and brings the Red Ribbon Army’s threat to an end. To find the last Dragon Ball, he seeks Fortuneteller Baba and plays martial-arts matches with her; after many trials he reunites with Grandpa Gohan, who had been hiding his identity in the final match. Goku is drenched in tears and Bulma is surprised to see him cry.
- After finding the last Dragon Ball, Goku revives Bora, who had been killed by Tao Pai Pai, and promises to meet again at the 22nd Tenkaichi Budokai three years later.
- After defeating the Red Ribbon Army, Goku sets off on another training journey to prepare for the 22nd Tenkaichi Budokai three years later. On the day of the tournament, the Turtle School disciples face off against students of the Crane School. Expecting tough opponents, Goku’s party nevertheless gets through the preliminaries and onto the main stage. As expected, the main stage features Crane School vs. Turtle School.
- Tien Shinhan and Chiaotzu, taught the evil ways of a martial artist by Master Shen, realize the path of righteous martial arts through their matches with the Turtle School. During his bout with Goku, Tien declares his break with Master Shen and fights Goku cleanly. The match is close, but when Goku is hit by a passing bus and falls out of the ring first, he loses by ring-out and Tien wins the tournament.
- The 22nd Tenkaichi Budokai ends with Tien’s victory. Back in the waiting room, the group sees someone has murdered Krillin. Goku runs out in rage, but Master Roshi sees a paper with the word “Mar” (likely “Mar”) and feels uneasy. Sure enough, it was the work of Tambourine, a demon under King Piccolo.
- Hungry and having lost the Nimbus, Goku loses to Tambourine and falls, but meets Yajirobe there. When Cymbal comes to steal the Dragon Balls, Yajirobe quickly dispatches him. Tambourine receives orders to kill all tournament contestants and kills fighters like Giran and Nam. Learning of Cymbal’s death, he returns to finish off the group, but after eating Yajirobe’s fish and regaining his strength, Goku fights Tambourine and incinerates him with a Kamehameha.
- Eventually King Piccolo himself arrives. Goku fights but is completely defeated; his heart stops temporarily and he falls into a deathlike state, and the Dragon Balls are taken. Master Roshi, having gathered some of the Dragon Balls, attempts to seal King Piccolo with the Mafūba (Evil Containment Wave), but fails and dies. Chiaotzu’s attempt to steal the wish fails, and King Piccolo regains youth. He even kills Shenron and the Dragon Balls vanish.
- Barely regaining consciousness, Goku, with Yajirobe’s help, goes to Korin Tower. Goku drinks the Ultra Divine Water and survives a six-hour near-death ordeal; his latent power awakens, he obtains a new Nimbus and rushes to King Castle. Before he reaches the castle, Tien confronts Piccolo but is being overwhelmed by Piccolo’s henchman Drum; Goku arrives, saves Tien and knocks Drum out in one blow.
- Goku fights King Piccolo again; after a fierce battle, Goku pours all his strength into his uninjured arm and topples King Piccolo, defeating him. Before dying, King Piccolo spits out an egg, which later becomes Piccolo Jr.
- Goku defeats King Piccolo, resurrects the Dragon Balls at the God’s Palace, and all his dead comrades are revived.
- Three years later, Goku arrives at the venue for the 23rd Tenkaichi Budokai. During that time he has trained directly under the God, and warriors who tried to catch up with him gather for the tournament.
- Goku reunites with old comrades, but among them is the God-predicted new King Piccolo, Piccolo Jr. Goku rematches old allies and fights other fighters, beginning a fierce battle with Piccolo Jr. Both sides hold back their final cards, but in the end Goku strikes Piccolo with a technique he had kept hidden and wins. However, killing Piccolo Jr. would also kill the God, so Goku lets Piccolo Jr. live; Piccolo Jr. retreats, promising another day.
- That is Goku’s first championship from the World Martial Arts Tournament, which he entered three times since his youth. Afterwards Goku marries Chi-Chi, fulfilling the promise they made at the tournament.

## 4.2. Saiyan Battle:
- With King Piccolo dead and the World Martial Arts Tournament — which featured the fierce battle with Ma Junior — over, and having married Chi-Chi and even had a son, Son Goku suddenly meets his brother Raditz, who has come from space.
- Raditz reveals that Goku is not actually an Earthling but the Saiyan Kakarot, a member of a warrior race sent to Earth to annihilate its inhabitants. To defeat Raditz, whose overwhelming power is on a completely different level, Goku teams up with his former enemy Piccolo, and choosing mutual destruction, they take Raditz down together by being hit with Piccolo’s Special Beam Cannon. This is Goku’s first death.
- Having gone to the afterlife, Goku decides to train under King Kai for a year before being revived by the Dragon Balls. To prepare to fight two even stronger Saiyans who are said to arrive in a year, Goku runs and flies along Snake Way and reaches King Kai’s planet in just six months. As a gag he entertains and passes, then to catch Bubbles Goku chases and catches him over 40 days. After that, in the remaining 118 days — not a long time — Goku learns the Kaio-ken, which multiplies his ki several times, and the ultimate Spirit Bomb.
- After seeing Yamcha, Tien, Piccolo, Chiaotzu and others killed by Nappa, Goku, calm but enraged, single-handedly defeats Nappa, who could not be handled even by the combined Z Fighters. Then he starts a deathmatch with Vegeta, who had severely damaged Nappa’s waist; being continuously overwhelmed by Vegeta’s superior power, Goku begins to turn the tide by using Kaio-ken x3, which King Kai had warned him not to use.
- Seeing Goku show a higher power than himself, Vegeta tries to blow Goku and the entire Earth away with the Galick Gun, and Goku counters with a Kaio-ken x4 energy wave and blows Vegeta away. Vegeta barely escapes, and, finding the full moon absent, creates an artificial moon with a Power Ball and transforms into a giant ape to corner Goku. But Yajirobe cuts off Vegeta’s tail, and Krillin, who received the Spirit Bomb from Goku, together with the artificial moon causing Gohan to become a giant ape, drive Vegeta into a near-death state; Vegeta tries to escape by calling his Attack Ball.
- Goku stops Krillin from finishing off Vegeta with Yajirobe’s sword, and in the end Vegeta escapes aboard the Attack Ball. Goku belatedly realizes that he was the one who killed his grandfather Gohan, and, though it’s too late, apologizes to his grandfather.

## 4.3. Frieza battle:
- Goku spent several days in the hospital receiving treatment until the lead was ready, then ate a senzu bean and instantly recovered. He set out belatedly for Planet Namek to follow Bulma's party, who had left earlier to find the original Dragon Balls. On the ship, Goku underwent brutal training that pushed him to the brink of death; upon arrival he encountered Frieza's forces and fought them, easily handling even the Ginyu Force, who were said to be the strongest in space. At this point Goku had already far surpassed the limits of the Saiyan race.
- However, even Goku could not defeat Frieza in his final form. At that time Goku's power level was a staggering 3,000,000. Unable to match him, Goku, with nothing to lose and prepared to die, raised his Kaioken from his limit of x10 up to x20 and fired a decisive Kamehameha, but Frieza only took a slight amount of damage. Frieza was using only about 50% of his power.
- Because Frieza couldn't sense ki, Goku still had the Spirit Bomb as a card. Trusting in the Spirit Bomb as his last hope, Goku secretly began the risky act of gathering energy from the sky right in front of Frieza. At first Frieza was puzzled by what Goku was doing, but when he saw the energy mass reflected in the water he moved to finish Goku off. Piccolo struck Frieza from behind to buy time, and Krillin and Gohan joined in to buy more time to collect energy for the Spirit Bomb.
- Enraged, Frieza created a Death Ball, but the Spirit Bomb had already been completed and Frieza was hit by Goku's decisive Spirit Bomb. Thinking he had defeated Frieza, Goku was elated, but Frieza was still alive—his tail had been cut off and he was bleeding from many places. When the furious Frieza fired a Death Beam at Goku, Piccolo saved him and took the Death Beam instead, losing consciousness.
- Frieza then shouted, "No one will be allowed to live...!" and suddenly lifted Krillin with telekinesis and blew him up. Seeing Krillin blown apart triggered Goku's rage, and he awakened as a Super Saiyan. Using the Dragon Balls, everyone except Goku and Frieza was sent away, and in his Super Saiyan state Goku overwhelmingly pummeled Frieza, the exact opposite of how things had been when he was normal.
- Ultimately, Frieza tried to destroy the entire planet, reasoning that unlike Goku he could survive in space. Fearing he himself would be caught in an instantaneous total-destruction blow, Frieza adjusted the power so the planet would collapse a few minutes later, and then he and Goku clashed again at 100% full power.
- At full 100% power, Frieza fought the Super Saiyan Goku on even terms, but after a short while the recoil of using full power began to drain Frieza's stamina, and Goku started to gain the upper hand.
- As Goku and Frieza continued to fight, Goku, satisfied that Frieza's pride had been utterly crushed, relaxed from Super Saiyan and told Frieza, in a calm voice, to live quietly and never show himself before Goku again, then flew off toward his spaceship to escape. Enraged by that action, Frieza fired a Death Saucer at Goku.
- Goku barely evaded it. Even after showing mercy, Frieza attacked again, and Goku, unwilling to tolerate it, transformed back into a Super Saiyan and resumed the fight. Using afterimage technique to dodge a Death Saucer, Goku lured one of Frieza's Death Saucers toward him; Frieza made another Death Saucer, and Goku drew Frieza's attention, fired an energy shot to create a smoke screen, then rose into the air and struck Frieza's neck—who had dodged one Death Saucer—sending him crashing to the ground.
- Frieza rose again but was hit by another Death Saucer from behind which severed his upper and lower body. With Frieza utterly incapacitated, Goku turned his back and said, "I'm leaving — you can meet your end with the planet you wrecked," and walked away, but Frieza pitifully begged for his life. Furious, Goku shouted, "You killed so many who begged for mercy, and now you beg for your life!?" Still affected by Frieza's plea, Goku grew weak-hearted and eventually gave Frieza some of his energy—just enough for Frieza to barely escape.
- With the energy he received, Frieza taunted Goku that the planet would explode soon and that all nearby ships had been destroyed, so there was no way to escape. Goku turned and continued to leave, but Frieza, unable to let go of his pride and arrogance, insisted he was the strongest in the universe and that Goku should die by his hand rather than by the planet's explosion; he made a final desperate attack. No longer able to tolerate it, Goku answered with an even stronger, anger-fueled Kamehameha to finish Frieza.
- At that moment, Goku wore a very uncomfortable expression — although Frieza was the mortal enemy who killed his friends, Goku had wanted to spare him at the end, and now he had killed him with his own hands.
- After finishing Frieza, Goku tried to get Frieza's ship flying somehow, but the ship was already dead and an earthquake split the ground, sending the ship into magma. Goku screamed in despair; his last means of escaping the planet was gone. By a stroke of luck he barely found an Attack Ball—the craft the Ginyu Force had been using nearby—rode it, escaped Namek, and miraculously crash-landed on the planet Yadrat in the direction the Ginyu Force had set the ship for their invasion.
- Afterwards Goku refused the offer to be transported to Earth by the Dragon Balls, chose to stay, and spent about a year there learning to control the Super Saiyan form and learning Instant Transmission, then returned to Earth.

## 4.4. Androids Battle:
- Son Goku was hurrying back to Earth to defeat the returned Frieza, but Frieza had already come to Earth ahead of him and intended to slowly kill all life, including humans. However, Frieza is finally killed, along with his father King Cold and his men, by Trunks, a Super Saiyan from the future.
- When Goku returns, Trunks brings him along and shows him a glimpse of his future self, tells him who his parents are, and warns that overwhelmingly powerful androids will arrive in three years and that Goku will die of a heart disease before he can fight them. Goku is distressed by the fact that he won't be able to fight such powerful foes, but Trunks gives him a heart medicine developed in the future.
- Piccolo, who had been listening to the whole story, tells Goku everything except who Trunks' parents are. Goku then trains diligently for three years with Piccolo and Gohan to prepare to fight the androids.
- Three years later, the two androids actually appear. While fighting Android 19, Goku's heart disease flares up and he faces death at 19's hands, but Vegeta appears and saves him. Thanks to the miracle cure Trunks brought from the future, Goku does not die and recovers.
- While Goku is temporarily out of action, Android 20 (Dr. Gero), who had fled to his lab, seizes the opportunity to awaken the androids 17 and 18 — humans modified into androids — and eventually they must face Cell, the ultimate being created from the cells of every warrior who came to this time period via the time machine.
- Once recovered, Goku teleports in to save Piccolo and Tien, who nearly died at Cell's hands, then enters the Room of Spirit and Time with Gohan to begin training. Meanwhile, due to Vegeta's trolling, Cell becomes fully complete.
- In the Room of Spirit and Time, Goku awakens Gohan as a Super Saiyan and trains him to stabilize and draw out his power in a way that reduces the strain on his body in the base Super Saiyan state. During this time, Cell succeeds in becoming perfect.
- Cell gains tremendous power and, knowing he has no match on Earth, announces the Cell Games. Having finished training, Goku spends the remaining ten days with his family. When the army sent by the government is annihilated by Cell, Goku realizes the need for the Dragon Balls and, with the help of King Kai, goes to find the Namekians who had moved to another planet and brings back Dende as the new guardian. After Dende restores the Dragon Balls, Goku gathers all seven.
- On the day of the Cell Games, Goku enters the tournament with Gohan and begins the fight with Cell himself. At that point the Z Fighters suspect Goku may have some trick, but Goku, realizing even he trained in the Room of Spirit and Time cannot beat Cell, forfeits. He asks his son Gohan—who hides astonishing potential—to defeat Cell instead.
- Goku is confident that once Gohan awakens he will beat Cell, but Gohan fails to live up to his father's expectations and instead suffers near-torture attacks from Cell. Piccolo tells Goku that the strategy he discussed with Gohan was wrong and that Gohan is wondering why his father won't help him, and realizing his mistake, Goku belatedly decides to re-enter the fight in Gohan's place. But it's too late: to prevent Goku's interruption, Cell takes the lead and changes tactics to torture all the Z Fighters to try to awaken Gohan's rage, and teammates who had already died once and cannot be revived fall to the Cell Juniors.
- As Goku is pummeled by the Cell Juniors, he apologizes to everyone for the failed strategy, but it's already too late. Fortunately, thanks to Mr. Satan's assistance, Android 16 is able to get close enough to Gohan to give him advice. Right after that advice, Cell tramples 16's head to finish him off as a trolling move, which triggers Gohan to fully awaken into Super Saiyan 2 and he defeats Perfect Cell.
- Cell, overwhelmed by the awakened Gohan, regresses back to his second form after spitting out Android 18 and attempts to destroy Earth by self-destructing. Goku teleports Cell to King Kai's planet and they both self-destruct there. However, Cell does not die; having surpassed even his perfect form, he returns to Earth by teleportation, kills Trunks, and severely injures one of Gohan's arms. Gravely wounded, Gohan almost gives up everything, but Goku, through King Kai, transmits telepathic encouragement to Gohan, giving him the courage to continue. After an energy clash, Gohan succeeds in completely destroying Cell.
- Goku's body can no longer be brought back to life, but he chooses to live joyfully, training in the afterlife with King Kai. The future world remains a dystopia because it's already too late, but in the present timeline the android incident is completely resolved and destiny has changed. Ironically, Goku himself, despite having obtained a cure for his heart disease, could not escape his fate and ultimately dies.

## 4.5. The World of Future Trunks:
- In the World of Future Trunks, Son Goku chased and defeated Frieza — who had arrived before him — using Instant Transmission, but afterward he developed a heart disease and died without ever meeting the androids who began their activity three years later. Because of this, the Z Fighters who fought the androids without Goku, including Vegeta and Future Gohan, all died, and to make matters worse Son Goten and Pan were never born, severing the Son family lineage. People who died of natural causes or disease could not be revived with the Dragon Balls. In other words, there was literally no way for Son Goku of the Future Trunks world to be brought back to life.

## 4.6. Battle with Majin Buu:
- Seven years later, upon hearing that his son would enter the World Martial Arts Tournament, Son Goku uses the power of the fortune-teller Baba to return to the living world for one day. Goku reunites with his family and friends after a long time and meets his second son Goten for the first time. He also sees Android 18, whom he hadn't seen last time, and learns she married Krillin. However, before the match, he cooperates with the Supreme Kai's proposal and learns of Majin Buu's existence, so he quits the tournament and follows the Supreme Kai. Vegeta follows as well, but they are forced to face Vegeta, who has been brainwashed by Babidi and powered up by Babidi's magic — Super Saiyan 2 Vegeta, using his evil heart.
- Goku, who had trained in the Other World and reached a high level, was matched by Vegeta enhanced by Babidi's power. Their overwhelming power resurrected Majin Buu, and while Goku was caught off guard and knocked out by a surprise attack from Vegeta, Vegeta self-destructed and Gohan was left near death, with Goku unable to do anything.
- Even though Vegeta sacrificed his body in the attack, Majin Buu regenerated unharmed. Goku then teaches fusion, which he had learned in the Other World, to his second son Goten and Vegeta's son Trunks. In the meantime, to stop Majin Buu, he unveils the ultimate warrior who even surpasses Super Saiyan 2 trained in the Other World — Super Saiyan 3. However, Super Saiyan 3 consumes so much energy that it could only be used in the Other World; having spent too much energy on Earth, he eventually has to return to the Other World earlier than the time originally allotted to him.
During this process, he also plays a major role in sowing discord between Majin Buu and Babidi, leading to Majin Buu killing Babidi.
- Back in the Other World, Goku uses Instant Transmission to find Gohan and the Supreme Kai group in the Supreme Kai's realm and supports Gohan's enhancement there, but sadly Gohan is absorbed by Buu. The only one left was Vegeta, whose body had been specially left behind by King Yemma just in case.
To fight again on Earth, Goku is revived by receiving the life of the Old Kai, and fuses with his rival Vegeta using the Potara earrings, becoming Vegito — the strongest being in the entire universe — and easily fights Super Buu, ultimately entering Buu's body and freeing everyone Buu had absorbed.
- However, Goku and Vegeta, who were rivals and allies in that form, refused to fuse again afterward. Goku, in Super Saiyan 3, fought a fierce battle with pure Majin Buu that matched him blow for blow. While Vegeta held Buu, they attempted to gather “as much energy as possible” from everyone on Earth and in the Other World to form a giant Spirit Bomb, but due to the limited cooperation of Earth's people, energy did not accumulate well except for the Z fighters on Earth and people like Android 8, Suno, Upa, Bora, Launch, and even former enemy Android 17.
- Then Mr. Satan, who is hailed as Earth's hero, urges the people to cooperate; everyone changes their attitude and gives all their strength, completing the Spirit Bomb and blasting pure Majin Buu away. In that moment, even Mr. Satan praises Goku, saying he fought well on his own.
- After everything ends, Goku, revived by receiving the life of the Grand Supreme Kai, returns to his family and experiences peace. Ten years after Majin Buu's disappearance, Goku decides to enter the 28th World Martial Arts Tournament and meets Bulma and her group with his family after a long time. According to Bulma, the last time she saw him was five years ago; he hadn't come to gatherings because of training, so this is a very long-awaited reunion. She also jibes that if she herself didn't seek them out, they'd never see each other again.
- The reason Goku participates in the World Martial Arts Tournament after such a long time is because he predicts the reincarnation of Buu and the entry of Uub, and he decides to join in order to meet Uub, even training with Goten for the first time in a while. Afterwards, with Buu's help, Goku faces Uub and deliberately angers him to draw out his power. Although he is holding back, everyone is amazed to see Uub match Goku, and when Uub's ki tears Goku's clothes to rags, they are startled.
- Seeing Goku use martial arts techniques and Uub become flustered, Goku offers to teach him and instructs his family that he'll occasionally come by to teach for a few years, then departs. Gohan and Chi-Chi are taken aback by this, while Vegeta and Piccolo laugh, knowing Goku is exhilarated not so much to teach as to satisfy his own desire to fight through Uub.
- Thus, Goku flies beyond the sky with Uub to begin training.

# 5. Son Goku's Relationships:
## 5.1. Overview:
- As a Saiyan, Son Goku has a father, Bardock, a mother, Gine, and an older brother, Raditz. Goku was sent to Earth as a baby and didn't know this; when he was young he suffered a head injury and completely lost his Saiyan memories and ferocity. After being separated from his mother he was put in an incubator for a certain period and then released — that release point seems to be considered the true birth of a Saiyan. In fact, after coming out of the incubator Goku's body looked like a three-year-old but he behaved like a newborn who couldn't even speak.
- Goku was raised on Earth by his grandfather and de facto father-figure Son Gohan, and later — without knowing it — accidentally transformed into a giant ape and stepped on his grandfather, killing him. Even so, Son Gohan didn't hold it against him.
- Goku's immediate family later includes his wife Chi-Chi and his sons Son Gohan and Son Goten. Later Videl married Gohan and became his daughter-in-law, and between them a daughter, Pan, was born. Mr. Satan is related by marriage. The Ox-King is his father-in-law.
- Perhaps because Goku isn't very faithful to domestic life, he often shows a weak side around his family. He's famously the universe's strongest henpecked husband. He can't stand up to Chi-Chi.
- Goku learned techniques and martial arts from many people, but the ones he truly recognizes as masters and who are truly his teachers are Master Roshi and King Kai. Among these, the person whom not only Goku but those around him clearly recognize as his teacher is Master Roshi. That's because while others helped improve his combat ability and pass on techniques, Master Roshi also had a profound influence on Goku's socialization and character formation.
- Actually, after the 21st World Martial Arts Tournament he received an order to descend the mountain, but Goku still wore Master Roshi-style robes and Master Roshi continued to be regarded as Goku's master.
- King Kai feels more like a trainer and a second than a traditional master to Goku. He meddles a lot when Goku fights and helps as a supporter. Goku also learned from Korin and Kami, but their image is somewhat different.
- Goku has many friends. First, all the Z Fighters are his friends, and Bulma is an important ally and friend. Aside from Krillin, Bulma accompanied Goku the longest.
- Goku values his friends, so while he's usually very optimistic, he becomes furious when a friend is in danger. For example, when Krillin was killed Goku rushed out recklessly and even ignored his master Master Roshi's restraint. When Krillin died again at Frieza's hands, Goku became so enraged he awakened as a Super Saiyan.
- Later, when Cell was killing Piccolo Goku watched in rage, emitting ki while anxiously waiting for Vegeta — who had entered the Room of Spirit and Time — to return because there was no other way to resolve it if he went. When Tien was in mortal danger while trying to bind Cell's feet, Goku could no longer hold back and used Instant Transmission to go save Tien and Piccolo.
- Goku was even very gloomy when Vegeta died during the fight with Majin Buu.
- Even toward villains, Goku often shows mercy and fights fairly, so many enemies ended up liking him and becoming friends. Krillin was originally a bit selfish but was influenced by Goku and became a good person; Yamcha, Tien, Piccolo, and Vegeta all first appeared as enemies but were assimilated by Goku and became Z Fighters.
- Also, although he never directly faced Android 17, 17 once heard Goku’s voice and called it "a voice I miss."
- Goku's rivalry line runs Yamcha → Tien → Piccolo → Vegeta.
- Goku actually has many enemies. Pilaf never forgot the grudge from childhood, and Dr. Gero programmed the androids with "avenge me on Goku" because the Red Ribbon Army was destroyed. Frieza also couldn't accept his defeat and came to Earth for revenge.
- Because of things like this, Goku may have come to think, "When I'm on Earth, villains gather." Bulma and King Kai agreed with that view, but after the Buu saga he answers that when a strong enemy appears you just train enough to surpass them.
- Also, most of those who fought Goku later reformed. Yamcha fought him early on, Tien fought him at the World Martial Arts Tournament, Piccolo was truly evil as a descendant of King Piccolo, and Vegeta was a perfect villain at first — each fought Goku desperately and then slowly reformed into allies.
- Even Oolong, a non-combatant, used to transform into monsters and bully and rob nearby villages before meeting Goku. Chiaotzu never fought Goku, but he too was initially a villain.

## 5.2. Son Goku family:
### 5.2.1. Chi-Chi:
- When a young Son Goku had not long left Mount Paozu, he first met Chi-Chi while running an errand to fetch Master Roshi at the Ox-King's request. At that time Goku had the habit of checking a person's sex by patting their groin, so without much thought he kicked Chi-Chi in the groin with his foot to determine her gender. The then-innocent Chi-Chi, after being sexually assaulted by Goku, thought she had no choice but to become Goku's wife and fell head over heels for him; before they parted she made him promise to take her as his bride. Goku kept that promise, they married, and became husband and wife.
- After Gohan was born, Goku devoted himself to his family for five years without much interaction with his comrades. However, as Piccolo and Master Roshi noted, he was certainly living a settled, housebound life, and Goku is well aware of that. After all, he recognizes that he has been neglecting his family because of training and feels guilty about it.
- In any case, Chi-Chi nags Goku to bring home some money, but she more than anyone firmly believes that her husband Goku is the strongest man in the universe. For Chi-Chi this belief is not an expectation but a fact.
- Because of that, Chi-Chi has a hard time imagining her husband being defeated; if even Goku were to lose, she seems to conclude that "nothing would help." In reality, her eldest son Gohan has several times surpassed Goku and often exceeded his father's achievements, and despite surviving life-or-death situations she doesn't seem to fully recognize this and speaks and acts as if there could be no man stronger than Goku.
- Seen trading barbs with Bulma — the wife of another Saiyan — over whose husband is cooler and stronger, it appears Chi-Chi is proud of her husband Goku.

### 5.2.2. Son Gohan:
- The eldest son of Son Goku. When he was young he was raised apart from fighting according to Chi-Chi's wishes, but because his father was always drawn into decisive battles that determined the fate of Earth, Gohan was involved as well. Nevertheless, Gohan greatly respects his father Goku, because he understands the burden Goku carries.
- In the Cell Games, Goku believed in his son's potential and thought he could win, so with a father's heart he sent his son out, but he failed to consider Gohan's reluctance to fight and was sharply rebuked by Piccolo.
- During the battle with Vegeta, Goku is shown acting as a typical father—telling Gohan they'll go fishing next time, and through scenes like the family picnic during the seven days before the Cell Games—suggesting he fulfilled a normal paternal role during peaceful times and later while training with his son.
- In the Kaiōshin realm during the Buu arc, Gohan's Mystic awakening is completed, and when Gohan is about to return to Earth to defeat Buu, Goku tells him, "I wanted to see you all grown up up close," embraces him, and they part. Afterwards Gohan asks Kibito for his father's (Goku's) gi because he wants to fight wearing his father's clothes.
- Ultimately, the relationship between Goku and Gohan reveals the typical father-protecting-son dynamic, but it also shows a mature, trust-based side that arose from the periods of constant fighting and from the time when Goku was dead after preventing Cell's self-destruction.

### 5.2.3. Son Ocheon:
- The second son of Son Goku. However, since he was born after Goku's death in the Cell saga and had never seen his father during the Majin Buu arc, to Ocheon Goku was probably just “a man who looks like me.” Their relationship at first wasn’t very good — so poor that when Vegeta died he and Trunks were even eyed with suspicion. That said, when young Trunks first met Son Goku and compared him to his own dad, honestly saying his dad looked stronger, his brother later said he’d heard that their father was a strong man who kept training even in the afterlife, so Ocheon must have had a vague sense that his father was someone impressive.
It’s not that he didn’t feel any longing for the unknown father; when Goku returned to the Other World he was shy at first but eventually burst into tears and clung to him.
- When Kid Buu was defeated and everyone reunited in the Other World, Son Ocheon was the first to run out and warmly throw himself back into Goku’s arms. After the Majin Buu arc, Son Goku and Son Ocheon have been getting along like a normal father and son.
- In fact, the closest thing to a typical father-son relationship in the Son Goku household today is between Goku and Ocheon. This owes partly to Goku still having a boyish heart, and partly to Chi-Chi not being as heavy-handed with her second son as she was with her eldest.
- When Mr. Satan tried to give Goku the 100 million Zeni prize money awarded for the World Peace Award, Goku refused it as too much, but Ocheon advised that if Goku took the money Mom would be happy and would allow Dad to train on King Kai’s planet. That suggests their father-son bond has grown close enough for Ocheon to fully understand Goku’s character.
- After his older brother Oban married and moved out, Ocheon began to follow Goku more.
- Also, after living with Goku for about ten years, unlike reliable Oban, Ocheon showed a lack of training which brought out Goku’s harsh scolding — a more typical father-son dynamic compared to the relationship with Oban.

### 5.2.4. Grandpa Son Gohan:
- To Son Goku, his grandfather was his first family and therefore a very special person. This is evident from Goku's reaction when he was reunited by the fortuneteller Baba: for the first time, he cried loudly like the other children and clung to Grandpa Son Gohan. Grandpa Son Gohan also felt especially fond of his foster grandson, and even when they met again after death he never told him that when Goku had become a Great Ape and lost his reason, Goku had trampled him to death.
- Son Goku only realized that the monster that appears on the full moon was himself after seeing Vegeta's Great Ape transformation with his own eyes, and he resolved that if he died again he would apologize to his grandfather.

### 5.2.5. Bardock, Gine:
- They're the parents who gave birth to Son Goku, but Goku doesn't even remember them and never met them after growing up. And even if he tried to meet them later, Bardock and Gine died long ago so they must have been reincarnated, meaning he couldn't meet them. To Son Goku himself — not Kakarot — they mean nothing.

### 5.2.6. Raditz:
- He is Son Goku's biological older brother, but they had never even met until Majunior was defeated and the Saiyan arc began.
- In fact, Raditz is technically Goku's brother, but from Goku's perspective he's only an older brother on paper — a sudden alien invader who appears, threatens to kill Earthlings, and kidnaps his precious son — so there's no way Goku would look kindly on such a Raditz. Still, when Krillin tells Goku, "Your brother isn't exactly weak," Goku doesn't get particularly angry, and when he is discharged from the hospital and is about to go to Planet Namek he even mentions that "my brother's spaceship was destroyed," which shows that he at least recognizes him as his brother.
- Goku's perception of Raditz didn't change even after Goku revised his negative view of Saiyans and came to be proud of being one; from Goku's point of view, there's really no reason to view Raditz favorably, since Raditz took his precious son hostage and put his comrades — with whom he shared friendship and youth — in a situation where they'd be killed by some thugs.

## 5.3. Capsule Corporation:
### 5.3.1. Bulma:
- As befits the world's richest person and an inventor, Bulma supports Goku materially and emotionally in defending the Earth and the universe. Her influence on Goku and the Z Fighters is so great that one could say Goku's adventures wouldn't have happened without her. However, for Goku she never ceased to be just a female friend from the early days.
- To Bulma, she had known Goku since childhood; they bathed together, and seeing Bulma in her underwear, a curious Goku even tried to pull them off—he was essentially like a little brother.
- When that Goku reappeared as a dependable young man she was surprised, and during the Namek Saga, hearing that Goku had become cool, she grumbled that she had picked the wrong kind of man. Having watched Goku overcome many crises, she shows steadfast faith in him; a representative example is when they heard the news that King Piccolo was fighting a young boy—she was the first to think that maybe Goku was still alive and gave everyone hope.
- Future Bulma also assessed Goku as not only strong but someone who always seemed like he would do something for you.
- Unusually, Bulma does not treat Goku as significantly more special than the other Z Fighters. Of course she considers him the universe's last savior, but she doesn't give him preferential treatment or pamper him.
- In fact, Bulma hasn't particularly given anyone especially better treatment.

### 5.3.2. Vegeta:
- Needless to say, he is Goku's nemesis and fated rival, a relationship neither can be separated from in their lives, and each perceives the other that way. 
- The relationship between Goku and Vegeta is so famous that it is never left out when discussing rival characters. Among the Z Fighters, next to Krillin, Vegeta is the person most closely tied to Goku; from their first meeting they fought a life-or-death battle, and since then surpassing Goku became Vegeta's goal. 
- Vegeta and Goku each took the other as a rival and have been competing neck-and-neck. Therefore there have been more instances where Vegeta exceeded Goku's power than with anyone else.
- At first Vegeta was clearly stronger than Goku, and until Goku entered the Room of Spirit and Time Vegeta was the strongest ally. Conversely, it is more often shown that Goku soon caught up to or surpassed Vegeta. For example, in the Namek Saga Goku transformed into a Super Saiyan before Vegeta did; later Vegeta seemed to get ahead after training for a year in the Room of Spirit and Time, but afterwards Goku also trained for a year in the same place, learned to maintain Super Saiyan form steadily in normal conditions, raised his power even higher, and fought Cell in a roughly equal showdown.
- Afterwards Vegeta devoted a year to training in the Room of Spirit and Time but still could not surpass Goku.
- Seven years later, in the fight against Majin Buu, Vegeta had become stronger, mastering Super Saiyan 2, but by then Goku had already, in the afterlife, learned Super Saiyan 3 and fought Buu on even terms.
- Vegeta was shocked to see Goku transformed into Super Saiyan 4 and the huge difference in power between them. However, with Bulma's help Vegeta was also able to transform into Super Saiyan 4, and at his peak he became a fighter similar in strength to Goku.
- Vegeta's inferiority complex toward Goku is an extremely intense emotion; it was even a trigger for his Super Saiyan awakening, and it drove him so strongly that he deliberately let himself be caught by Babidi's magic just to get a decisive match. Still, even with deep inferiority toward Goku, that does not mean he never acknowledged him.
- In the past Vegeta dismissed Goku as a low-class warrior, but in the Namek Saga, when Goku landed an effective blow on Frieza just as Vegeta was about to be killed by Frieza, Vegeta warned Frieza that Kakarot was a Super Saiyan and afterward acknowledged Goku as a genius.
- Vegeta watched Goku's battle with Buu and recognized the difference between them, finally accepting Goku as number one and cheering for him.
- The process of Goku and Vegeta becoming comrades was complicated. Gradually assimilating to Earth's peaceful environment and growing mentally, Vegeta's relationship with the Z Fighters showed some improvement.
- However, until Vegeta fully acknowledged Goku, it was hard to categorically call him a comrade.
- At first Vegeta and Goku were outright enemies; in the Namek Saga they briefly cooperated to defeat their common enemy Frieza, but there was not a shred of friendly feeling between them. Afterwards their relationship developed from antagonism into rivalry. They were not friendly, but they often helped each other.
- Vegeta once saved Goku, who had been weakened by a heart virus and almost fell victim to Android 19, and Goku in turn suggested Vegeta train in the Room of Spirit and Time to face the Androids.
- Unable to overcome his inferiority complex toward Goku, Vegeta fought him, but he also openly referred to Goku as the genius who had surpassed him, and to confront their mutual enemy Majin Buu they even went so far as to fuse with the Potara.
- In the latter part of the battle with Buu, Vegeta fully acknowledged Goku as number one.
- Vegeta bought time while Goku gathered energy, restored Goku's stamina with the Dragon Balls, and played a major role in defeating Buu.
- Among all the Z Fighters to date, amid the continually rising power inflation, Vegeta is the only one who has consistently kept pace with Goku.

### 5.3.3. Tabl:
- Compared to King Vegeta, with whom Son Goku never even had a conversation, they at least know of each other to a simple degree. However, since they’re merely acquaintances, they basically consider each other just fellow pure-blood Saiyans and have no particular feelings beyond that: on Son Goku’s side, Tabl is only seen as Vegeta’s brother who turned out to be alive, and on Tabl’s side, Son Goku appears to be someone as strong as his own brother.

### 5.3.4. Trunks:
- The present young Trunks and Son Goku don't have any particular connection. He's basically just Vegeta's son — a friend of the second son and nothing more.

### 5.3.5. Future Trunks:
- Son Goku and Future Trunks are like mutual benefactors. Future Trunks traveled through time to prevent Son Goku's heart disease, and he learned a great deal from Goku.
- From Son Goku's perspective, the Trunks who came from the future is a benefactor who changed the fate that would have had him die of heart disease. Possibly influenced by Bulma's boundless trust in Goku, Future Trunks also places deep trust in him.

### 5.3.6. Bura (Bulma's Parents):
- Son Goku is quite indebted to Dr. Brief, the world's greatest scientist. When Goku went to Planet Namek he rode in the spacecraft Dr. Brief built, and the 100x gravity chamber and the Hoi-Poi Capsule are also Dr. Brief's inventions.
- Strictly speaking, it's Dr. Brief, not Bulma, who is the world's richest, so Dr. Brief's support is considered the major one. Bulma's mother liked Goku when he was a child and would sometimes hit on the adult Goku.

## 5.4. Turtle House:
### 5.4.1. Master Roshi:
- He is the person whom Son Goku regards as his true master, and also Goku's caregiver and educator. 
- Master Roshi not only taught the basics of martial arts but also provided Son Goku — who had been a feral child — with worldly knowledge, social skills, and even sex education that Son Gohan, his adoptive father, could not give him because he died early. 
- Although Son Goku met many other masters after Master Roshi, he regarded Master Roshi as the most important and continued to call himself a disciple of the Turtle Hermit even after training with Korin and Kami. This continued even after meeting King Kai.
- From the time Son Goku arrived on Planet Namek, the turtle "kame" (龜) character signifying the Turtle School disappeared from his gi; this signifies that Son Goku had reached a level of mastery in martial arts and was training alone without a master (or affiliation). However, Son Goku's feelings toward Master Roshi did not change. In fact, the emblem on the gi is an expression of belonging to an association or respect for the teacher who provided instruction.
- In other words, Son Goku was no longer at a level where he would grow by receiving teachings from Master Roshi, and having reached the pinnacle as a martial artist he removed the "kame" character. Nevertheless, the reason Son Goku continued to insist on wearing Master Roshi–style gi was purely out of respect for Master Roshi.

### 5.4.2. Sea Turtle:
- Has the connection that brought about the first meeting between Son Goku and Master Muchen. They are not estranged, as they lived together for a while in the Turtle House.

## 5.5. Celestial Realm:
### 5.5.1. Zen-Oh:
- Perhaps finding Son Goku's boldness refreshing, he was the one to ask to be friends first; they dropped formalities with each other and formed an extremely strong connection. However, Son Goku ignored Zen-Oh’s request to hang out because of the Goku Black incident, and on top of that lied that he would introduce a better friend.
- Somehow Goku introduced the Zen-Oh of the Future Trunks world as a friend, which makes it seem unlikely their relationship with the original Goku could develop further. Moreover, considering that Zen-Oh even tried to wipe out the entire universe that included Goku, it doesn't look like he regarded Goku as especially dear.
- From Zen-Oh’s perspective, Goku might be an interesting being, but perhaps not more than that. Still, he was deeply regretful when Goku was eliminated in the Tournament of Power, and the fact that he even hesitated to press the elimination button indicates he thought of Goku personally.
- Rather than mere curiosity, it seems Zen-Oh was drawn to Goku’s informal, pure nature and wanted to keep a close, casual relationship. This is also true of the Future Zen-Oh, who, like the present Zen-Oh, didn't have many opportunities for contact.

### 5.5.2. Korin:
- Son Goku's second master.
- Though not to the same extent as Master Roshi, Son Goku holds considerable respect for Korin.
- Goku first went to him after feeling his limits against the powerful foe Tao Pai Pai, and later visited again before the showdown with King Piccolo and received help there as well.
- This was not merely a childhood anecdote; before the Cell Games, it was also Korin whom Goku visited to ask him to gauge the extent of his power.
- Perhaps because Goku has consistently received help from him over time, Korin is often thought of as the teacher after Master Roshi.
- In fact, the relationship between Goku and Korin is similar to that between Goku and Master Roshi, because Master Roshi, Goku's teacher, also once studied under Korin.

### 5.5.3. Piccolo:
- Son Goku and Piccolo have a rather complicated relationship: first enemies they faced, second rivals, and, if you look deeper, even the third aspect of being the teacher who personally trained Goku's son. It's ironic that these three roles are all regarded as different figures in the story, yet all those roles are combined into one person.
- Goku also clearly distinguishes Kami and Piccolo; during the Android saga, upon seeing Piccolo merged into one, he even called him "Shincolo," thinking of him as different.
- After that, Piccolo is a good comrade and the one who gives the most advice.
- When Goku's relationship with Vegeta was still ambiguous, the person Goku proposed as a training partner alongside Gohan was Piccolo, and Piccolo, who had wanted it, immediately agreed.
- With the eldest son between them, Piccolo and Goku have a teacher-and-parent kind of relationship. They could practically be called godfathers to the son and granddaughter.

### 5.5.4. King Kai:
- King Kai is the being Goku regards as a master next after Master Roshi. He was the fourth person to train Goku, and compared to Master Roshi — who also acted as Goku’s educator — King Kai’s instruction was more focused on martial training.
- He taught Goku the Kaio-ken and the Spirit Bomb, which became great sources of power for him; these were the first techniques Goku ever received. Although King Kai is actually a being far above the gods, he and Goku are quite close.
- However, because of that he’s treated the worst among Goku’s masters. Still, they get along well: they meet most often and tease each other, and even when King Kai was caught up in Cell’s self-destruction and died, Goku didn’t get especially angry.
- As for King Kai’s pet Bubbles, Goku’s relationship with him is about like that between a neighbor’s pet and the neighbor, but he has helped with training on occasion.

### 5.5.5. Dende:
- Son Goku and Dende have a somewhat curious connection: their first meeting was just a brief glimpse before the Namekians left for Earth, but later he personally brought him and installed him as the God.
- You might think, "What kind of Saiyan man is arbitrarily deciding Earth's god?" but Son Goku has a record of being designated as the next god by the Earth's deity, and he acted with the Supreme Kai's permission, so it's not really something to make a fuss about.

### 5.5.6. Beerus:
- An absolute wall that appeared before Goku, who had become as strong as he could. Still, surprisingly, there was little friction between him and Goku.
- From their first meeting it was Beerus who gladly agreed when Goku asked to spar, and Goku, for his part, did his best to accommodate Beerus' requests.
- Judging from Piccolo's remark, "It's like he's receiving training," although they fought with the Earth at stake, Beerus didn't seem to have major objections to the idea of destroying Earth itself. As Piccolo's comment indicates, in their first meeting he also acted as a coach, teaching Goku how to use the power of a god.
- After giving a little guidance in the first fight, he never once supervised Goku's training again, and is waiting for him to grow into a rival who, together with Vegeta, will stand against Beerus himself in the future. In the meantime, because Goku got involved with the Omni-King, Beerus experienced several instances where his expectations were upended.

### 5.5.7. Whis:
- He became a new master for Goku, who seemed like he wasn't going to get any more teachers. Unlike Beerus, who is always sleeping, Whis—who is always awake—keeps him company and helps kill time.
- When Champa saw Goku and Vegeta, he asked Whis (not Beerus) if the two were his students, and Whis replied something like "sort of," not exactly denying it.
- Whis once suggested to Goku that he take the position of the next God of Destruction—whether he was joking or serious is unclear. In fact, it was also Whis who, from early on, had been giving Goku and Vegeta hints about the pinnacle of the subconscious (Ultra Instinct).

### 5.5.8. King Yama:
- Sun Wukong was heavily indebted to him in the underworld. King Yama likewise held Sun Wukong's achievements in high regard, and as a special favor allowed his physical body to be preserved even after death. He even showed him the way to becoming a Gye-wang and did many favors for him in various ways.

### 5.5.9. Paikehan:
- Known as the strongest warrior in the Western Galaxy, he is Son Goku's rival in the Other World. He becomes friends with Son Goku through their fight.

## 5.6. The Demon World:
### 4.6.1. Glorio:
- Because Son Goku kept mistakenly calling his name "Gorilo," he corrected him to "Glorio," and then, after being restored to his original form by the Dragon Balls, he began using polite speech and they became friends.

## 5.7. Other Companions:
### 5.7.1. Krillin:
- Krillin is Goku's closest, best friend who shared hardships and joys with him.
- Krillin and Goku studied together as disciples of Master Muten, and among the Z Fighters he is the person Goku has known the longest.
- The moments when Goku became extremely enraged were always caused by Krillin's deaths, and among the three times Goku shed tears in the story, one was when Krillin was killed by Tambourine. Also, the enemies who killed Krillin were all eventually slain by Goku's hand. This was a factor that caused the usually gentle Goku to become extremely angry and become a Super Saiyan, and such a reaction was not shown even at the death of any other Z Fighter, including his son.
- However, when Krillin was killed again during the fight with Majin Buu, unfortunately Goku had already died and was staying in King Kai's realm, so there was nothing he could do. If Krillin had died again right in front of Goku, he would have rushed at Buu in uncontrollable rage without hesitation.
- Krillin can be said to be Goku's closest associate and a particularly dear friend.

### 5.7.2. Android 18:
- To Son Goku, she's basically just like a sister-in-law. Like the other androids, Android 18 was modified to kill Son Goku, but they failed to completely reprogram her personality, so from 18's point of view he was someone she could either kill or not — it didn't really matter.
- After Cell died and she came to her senses, the Son Goku she had been supposed to kill was already dead, and she herself, thanks to Krillin, had been reformed for the better, so there is almost no overlap with the original storyline. Their daughter Marron is likewise not much involved. However, Son Goku's wife Chi-Chi seems to have become fairly close to 18.

### 5.7.3. Android 16:
- Ironically, while Androids 17 and 18, even though ordered by Dr. Gero to kill Goku, acted as if they didn't care what happened to him, Android 16—unlike them—remained hostile toward Goku to the end because of the purpose Dr. Gero made him for, separate from his own humanity.
- Because of Cell, Goku and 16 did form a brief, temporary alliance, but their hostility did not change. Unlike 17 and 18, 16 was an android who had relatively more points of contact with Goku and, unlike their other hostile relationships, they briefly allied against Cell; however, since 16 was killed by Cell first and Goku also died attempting to perish with Cell, their confrontation never took place.

### 5.7.4. Android 17:
- He lent his power to Goku's Spirit Bomb. Like Android 18, he had been ordered to kill Goku, but even if he didn’t like it he only followed because he needed some kind of purpose, adopting an attitude of not caring what happened.
- When Uub and Goku were talking about candidate fighters for the Tournament of Power and Mr. Satan suggested Android 18, they remembered him and met him in person for the first time. At Android 17's request he briefly helps with his job, and after talking they test each other's strength.
- He somewhat acknowledges Android 17's power and then listens to 17's life up to that point; Goku asks about 17's family and even looks at photos. From 17's side he seems to regard Goku as an interesting fellow and grows fond of him. In fact, when he prepared to die alongside the boss of a space poaching gang, he entrusted his animals and family and said he wanted to talk with Goku again.
- Later, during the Tournament of Power when Goku sends Caulifla and Kale, he defends Goku against Android 18's complaints, saying this is okay. When Goku gets into trouble fighting Rozie of Universe 2, he rushes over even while fighting Ribrianne and forms a barrier to protect him.
- In the fight against Jiren he even self-destructs to save Goku, and seeing that moves Goku and becomes one of the triggers for him to reawaken Ultra Instinct. And Android 17, looking at Goku, makes the wish to restore all the universes with the Super Dragon Balls, recovering his human emotions and forming a relationship of trust with Goku.

### 5.7.5. Launch:
#### 5.7.5.1. Black-haired Launch:
- Black-haired Launch is basically very grateful to Son Goku for saving her from danger, and perhaps because of her innocent nature she even shared a bed with Son Goku while dressed only in her underwear. Of course, she probably thought of him only as a cute little brother, but that shows she has no reservations around Son Goku.
- She was surprised when Son Goku suddenly grew into a young man, but since she didn’t show any particular romantic interest like Bulma did, she still seems to regard him only as a close younger brother.
- Black-haired Launch mainly helped with Son Goku’s daily life at Kame House, but due to various coincidences she once saved the group when they were put in mortal danger by General Blue.

#### 5.7.5.2. Blonde Lunch:
- At first, as usual, she ignorantly sprayed bullets and suffered the humiliation of being taken down with a single kick by an angry Son Goku, but later—seeing her charge recklessly into the notorious Red Ribbon Army stronghold to help Son Goku, or head off to fight Piccolo—it seems they grew much closer. Of course, her belligerent nature probably also made her inclined to rush in. However, both of those fights were aborted because Son Goku resolved them.
- Blonde Lunch took down a Red Ribbon Army soldier at the Turtle House.
- It seems Blonde Lunch and Son Goku once regarded each other as comrades, but she was apparently not considered a combat team member.

### 5.7.6. Maron:
- Judging from Son Goku playing with Maron, they seem to be just an uncle who's a friend of her father.

### 5.7.7. Yamcha:
- He was originally Son Goku's first major opponent and served as a rival.
- In their first meeting he ended in a draw, and although he had a brief rival gimmick, he quickly became close while traveling together.
- He had memorable supporting/comic roles in encounters like the Rabbit Monster, the Pilaf episode, and the Red Ribbon Army arc in the original series; after that he remained more as an old friend than as part of Goku’s fighting force.
- On the other hand, Goku’s position to Yamcha seems to be that of a very precious friend. When they reunited at the World Martial Arts Tournament he greeted him warmly; on Namek, before the planet exploded, he shouted to Goku, "Don't die!!"; when Goku sacrificed himself to stop Cell’s self-destruction he, just like Son Gohan, shed tears over Goku’s death;
- During the battle with Majin Buu, when Goku returned to the living world for 24 hours, Yamcha ran to him in tears alongside Krillin, Chi-Chi, Oolong, and Son Gohan, which clearly shows Yamcha’s close relationship with Goku.

### 5.7.8. Tien Shinhan:
- When Son Goku and Tien Shinhan first met, Tien was a complete villain. He appeared by breaking Yamcha's leg and was a disciple of Master Shen, the Crane Hermit — Master Mutaito's greatest rival and a villain.
- At that time, he was the strongest human on Earth after Son Goku, so he became Goku's first rival. However, Tien grew into a martial artist during the World Martial Arts Tournament, broke with Master Shen, and through sharing a martial-artist rapport with Son Goku their hostility naturally disappeared.
- Tien was the only ally who fought alongside Son Goku in the battle against King Piccolo.
- In the King Piccolo arc, perhaps because they fought with their lives on the line against despairing events like the deaths of comrades or the possible destruction of the world, they expressed very warm feelings toward each other around this time; at the final World Martial Arts Tournament, Tien and Son Goku were also conscious of each other as rivals.
- However, with the subsequent continuous battles and power inflation, the rival position gradually passed to Piccolo and Tien was relegated to just a comrade, and afterwards the relationship between Tien and Piccolo was never particularly emphasized.
- The strong bond between Son Goku and Tien began to sprout during the King Piccolo arc. Even if that bond was later severed, they had originally met as disciples of the hostile Crane and Turtle Hermits, and above all both Tien and Son Goku are dry, training-obsessed martial artists who don't have everyday interactions.
- There were times when Son Goku and Tien nearly killed each other, but even when they had opportunities to deepen their friendship they wouldn't go find one another to build it — they aren't the type. Therefore, the bond between Son Goku and Tien should be seen as considerably weakened compared to the very strong bond they had from the King Piccolo arc through the Saiyan saga.
- Even Bulma, who is said to have been close to Goku, won't see him for years if she has nothing to do; Tien likewise effectively cut off exchanges with the Turtle School. They are very dry personalities, but at the very least the sense of being comrades definitely remains. Given their character traits, it's hard to say the feeling of friendship has completely disappeared. It's just certain they are not as familiar as they were when they were young.

### 5.7.9. Chaozu:
- Chaozu and Son Goku have known each other for a very long time, but there is really almost no depiction of them ever talking about one another. 
- Goku and Chaozu have a sense of being comrades, but it's never once shown what image they have of each other. As for Videl, since she was met only recently and the story jumps ahead ten years, it's understandable there are few depictions, but Chaozu has been acquainted with Goku since as early as Goku's second participation in the World Martial Arts Tournament.
- In any case, Goku and Chaozu trained for goals like defending Earth; although they never fought together in the same place, they nevertheless fought and risked their lives against the same enemies, and felt their ki grow stronger, weaker, and vanish — so they must have felt attachment or a sense of comradeship. Still, between the two of them the sum total is just seeing each other twice at the tournaments and once when returning from Namek.

### 5.7.10. Yajirobe:
- Yajirobe first meets Son Goku in the King Piccolo saga.
- Hungry Son Goku ate Yajirobe's food without permission, so Yajirobe got angry and the two fought one-on-one as their first meeting; Goku was surprised by Yajirobe's toughness.
- Afterwards, when Son Goku is defeated by King Piccolo, he takes him to Korin Tower and even accompanies Goku when he leaves to drink the Sacred Water, acting as a supporting ally.

### 5.7.11. Pilaf Gang:
- They are the first villains Son Goku encountered, but they're not really that evil — they simply resent him for taking the Dragon Balls and aren't seriously hostile.

### 5.7.12. Oolong:
- Oolong was the first person to travel with Son Goku after Bulma set out on her journey, and he was Son Goku's first male friend.
- Oolong and Son Goku first met in a village, where Oolong used shapeshifting to become a monster and kidnap children—he was a bad guy. The problem was his transformations didn't last long, so he had to finish things quickly, but his disguise wore off during his encounter with Son Goku, and the villagers discovered his true identity.
- After that he goes with Son Goku's group to search for the Dragon Balls, often trying to run away but getting caught by Bulma; later he hangs out with Son Goku's group and eventually becomes one of the companions.
- Since he is one of Son Goku's oldest friends, like Krillin he treats Goku without reservation; when they reunited after a long time at the World Martial Arts Tournament that Goku enters for the first time, they were very happy to see each other.

### 5.7.13. Puar:
- Puar is Yamcha's companion. Son Goku recognizes him as such.
- After Yamcha joined Son Goku's party, he followed Yamcha and traveled with Son Goku. Actually Puar doesn't do much — aside from transforming into scissors to cut Goku's tail when Goku turned into the giant ape at Pilaf's castle, there's not much else.
- Still, using his shapeshifting he made a few small contributions, and he was a flying companion who joined fairly early in the journey.

### 5.7.14. Android 8:
- Originally an android created by the Red Ribbon Army, he refused the order to eliminate Son Goku and was about to be destroyed, but Goku saved him and they became friends. Goku calls No. 8 "Hot-chan," and he likes the name too.
- Android 8 helps Goku but has a gentle personality and dislikes fighting. When General White attacked Goku with a gun, he threw his body to protect Goku and, enraged, punched General White and sent him flying.
- He remained friends with Goku even after parting ways when Goku went to search for the Dragon Balls; during the Majin Buu arc he heard Goku's voice, was the first among the people of Jingle Village to recognize it as Goku, and contributed his energy to the Spirit Bomb.

### 5.7.15. Suno:
- A girl from Jingle Village whom Son Goku met while wandering in search of the Dragon Balls. She found Goku frozen stiff from the cold, took him to her home and nursed him, and when Goku went to rescue the chief of Jingle Village who had been captured in Muscle Tower she lent him cold-weather clothing and later thanked him after he resolved the Muscle Tower incident. 
- After parting ways with Son Goku, Suno grew into an adult, and during the Majin Buu arc she, along with Android 8, heard Goku’s voice and added her energy to the Spirit Bomb.

### 5.7.16. Upa, Bora:
- Natives dressed in Native American clothing who live in Karin's sacred land.
- While Goku was traveling to find the Dragon Balls, he saved Upa, who had been captured by Colonel Yellow; Upa & Bora came to trust Goku, and when Bora was killed by Tao Pai Pai, Goku gathered the Dragon Balls to revive her, during which Upa temporarily left Karin's sacred land to take part in the events.
- Goku collected the seven Dragon Balls and revived Upa's father Bora, and Upa & Bora were grateful to Goku.
- After parting ways with Goku, they reunite with him during the King Piccolo arc; Bora helps Goku & Yajirobe climb up Karin Tower, and later, when Upa has grown into a young man during the Majin Buu arc, he and Bora hear Goku's voice and lend their energy to the Spirit Bomb.

### 5.7.17. Broly:
- He was on the opposite side from Son Goku. Seeing Broly's strength, the opposing Goku did challenge him in the middle, asking to fight him too, but during the fight he came to understand Broly's strength and personality and tried to calm Broly, who had entered a rage form; in fact, Broly showed signs of calming down to some extent at Son Goku's words.
- After the fight, Goku offering to teach him various things suggests he intended to make up for Broly's lack of combat experience, and Broly, smiling, comes to like Son Goku.

### 5.7.18. Chirai, Remo:
- Chirai and Remo were originally members of the Frieza Force but betrayed Frieza because of Broly's purity.
- Later, they were wary of Son Goku, who came looking for Broly after he was sent to Planet Vampa, but they relaxed their guard when Goku gave them a home and food and genuinely showed that he liked Broly.

### 5.7.19. Uub:
- After Son Goku defeated Majin Buu (Pure), he deliberately asked King Yemma to have Pure Buu reincarnated as a human.

## 5.8. The Satan family:
### 5.8.1. Mr. Satan:
- Satan's feelings toward Son Goku at first were somewhat resentful/jealous because he had been credited with the Z Fighters' achievements during the Cell saga, and Son Goku and his group treated Satan as a fool not worth their time. However, after fighting alongside Majin Buu they came to see each other differently and their relationship improved greatly.
- Thus Satan even took on the role of a "figurehead who can stand in for the Z Fighters, who would be bothered if the truth were revealed." This helped the Satan and Son families form a relationship, and later when the World Peace Award was given, Satan — who knew the in-law family well — even personally came with 100 million zeni, showing how close they were.
- Son Goku, embarrassed, said he would not accept such a large sum, but his son Goten persuaded him by saying, "If Mom accepts this money, she'll allow Dad to go train on King Kai's planet," so he accepted the money.
- Because Mr. Satan was hailed as a global-level hero through the Cell and Majin Buu incidents, he feels very apologetic toward Goku and his group; for this reason he paid all the costs for Gohan's newlywed home, and although not shown, realistically he and Bulma likely covered all the food expenses for the post-battle banquet.

### 5.8.2. Videl:
- Despite being Son Goku's daughter-in-law, she has surprisingly little contact with him. However, before marrying Gohan, when Videl was lying in a hospital bed after being thoroughly beaten by Spopovich at the World Martial Arts Tournament, without anyone asking, Son Goku went to Korin Tower, fetched a senzu bean, gave it to Gohan, who then gave it to Videl.
- After that, there doesn't appear to be much interaction between Son Goku and Videl, but when Son Goku was trying to become a Super Saiyan God, Videl confessed that she was pregnant and he helped her, so at least they are not on bad terms.

### 5.8.3. Pang:
- Son Goku accompanied Pang to the World Martial Arts Tournament when she was a child and watched with her. When Pang bumped into someone passing by and dropped her ice cream, Son Goku — who wouldn't give up food — went so far as to give her his own, seeming to dote on her enough to show textbook grandfatherly behavior.
- Of course, Pang loved Son Goku so much that, before setting off on a trip with Ubu, she threw a tantrum saying she didn't want to part from him. Pang and Son Goku make an excellent granddaughter & grandfather pair.

### 5.8.4. Mr. Buu:
- The evil part is destroyed by Goku, but the good part, Fat Buu, joins the allies as Mr. Buu.
- He enjoys that Majin Buu is strong and fun to fight, but he also gives advice to the Majin Buu who follows Babidi. Somehow, after Kid Buu was hit by the Spirit Bomb and destroyed, he dissuaded Vegeta from killing Fat Buu and had Dende heal him; later he lets him live at Satan's house and, since he used the Dragon Balls to erase memories of Buu, it seems he didn't think of him as that bad.

## 5.9. Enemies:
### 5.9.1. Red Ribbon Army:
- The enemies Goku encountered after Pilaf. They bothered Goku quite a bit and swarmed him in groups, but none of them were a match for him. Except for General Blue and Commander Red, most were completely annihilated by Goku.

### 5.9.2. Tao Pai Pai:
- He was Son Goku's greatest nemesis during his boyhood. Once he was hit directly by a Dodonpa and briefly stopped breathing, and after drinking the sacred water of Korin Tower he later met again and won decisively.
- After that, Tao Pai Pai was turned into a cyborg and barely survived, but following that defeat he lost the will to confront Son Goku.

### 5.9.3. King Piccolo:
- Goku's greatest adversary in his boyhood. More than a simple villain, he was the enemy who caused the deaths of the people Goku cherished most — Master Roshi and Krillin — so Goku's hatred of King Piccolo was extreme.
- In Goku and King Piccolo's first fight, Goku was brutally defeated. King Piccolo drank the Choshinsu — unlike the Choseongsu, which was just plain water — endured a near-death crisis and regained his strength. In the middle of the fight he took Tien Shinhan hostage to make Goku let his guard down, then hurled a rock that left Goku wounded except for one arm; with that remaining arm Goku fired a Kamehameha, closed in on King Piccolo, gathered all his strength into his fist and pierced his body.
- He is the first villain Goku directly killed. The Red Ribbon Army were merely knocked out, not killed.

### 5.9.4. Frieza:
- A bitter nemesis and archrival entangled with Goku by a cursed fate.
- Frieza's fights with Goku were tied up with many stories: enemy of Goku's homeworld and people, enemy of his father, a battle to determine the strongest in the universe, a last request from a former foe, revenge for a childhood friend, and so on.
- Among these, in the cases of Goku's father and homeworld, Goku didn't know, but the fact that even such unknown ties were involved makes him a fateful opponent.
- For Frieza, too, he faced the Super Saiyan he had feared so much, and because Goku was a direct threat to his position and pride as the strongest in the universe, it was a fight he could never back down from.
- He also continued slaughter and destruction until the end, begged an enemy for pity, and, harboring intense hatred toward Goku who had spared him, became enraged and died.
- Conversely, Frieza could not erase his ill feelings toward Goku, and while in Hell he imagined killing Goku countless times; even when he agreed to cooperate, he showed a readiness to stab him in the back and attempt betrayal.
- When Frieza entered the Tournament of Power he brutally smashed opposing fighters but also delivered MVP-level performances; when Goku, losing to Jiren, asked him to share energy for the Spirit Bomb, Frieza—though recalling that he had once nearly been killed by a Spirit Bomb—nevertheless willingly shared his energy.
- Seeing Goku awaken Ultra Instinct and the gods' astonishment as he confronted Jiren, Frieza thought something over; then, when Goku had exhausted himself and couldn't move, Frieza, as if repaying the debt of having been saved when he was cut in half on Namek, gave Goku energy so he could move, among other things, intending to make Goku a counter to Jiren.
- While fighting Jiren, Frieza asked Goku to keep his promise to revive him, and Goku replied that as long as Frieza didn't break his promise, he would keep his as well. Frieza said, "Your softness is disgusting, but thanks this time," then cooperated with Goku to fight Jiren. They showed better-than-expected coordination and some impressive team play. In the end they tried to fall out of bounds together with Jiren; when Jiren didn't fall, Goku joined in so the three fell out together and perished together, leaving Android 17 and resulting in Universe 7's victory.
- When Whis revived Frieza in return, Frieza asked if it was okay to revive him even though he had no intention of stopping his evil deeds; Goku answered that then they would stop him.
- To Goku, Frieza remained a villain who never truly reformed, but through the Universe Survival arc they developed a somewhat delicate, ambiguous relationship.

### 5.9.5. Ginyu:
- During the Namek saga he became a worst adversary; Goku himself once had his body stolen by Ginyu through Body Change and suffered greatly. Afterwards Goku used the frog from Namek Castle to force Ginyu to Body Change with the frog.

### 5.9.6. Nappa:
- They first met and fought while Son Goku was saving Gohan and Krillin, who were facing death. The result was a total reversal—Nappa just got thoroughly beaten by Son Goku. Even Nappa's best techniques proved useless, revealing that Goku was in a completely different league.
- In the end, Son Goku struck Nappa's back with the Kaio-ken; the attack injured Nappa's spine and knocked him out completely. Even as a villain, one might have thought there was a chance he could survive thanks to Goku's mercy, but Nappa had already repeatedly disobeyed Vegeta's orders and acted on his own, so in the end he was brutally killed by Vegeta.

### 5.9.7. Doctor Gero:
- Doctor Gero, a survivor of the Red Ribbon Army, created androids to take revenge on Son Goku, and he himself even became an android.

### 5.9.8. Cell:
- Cell can in some respects be considered a dark/evil version of Son Goku. In fact, Cell was created by incorporating Son Goku's Saiyan cells, and therefore he exactly resembled Goku's techniques and personality.

### 5.9.9. Babidi:
- Son Goku and Babidi were nothing more and nothing less than adversaries, but Son Goku once questioned Majin Buu (Good), who was then following Babidi, asking why he would obey such a weak man. This planted the seed for Majin Buu, who had been doubting Babidi's actions, to betray him.

### 5.9.10. Majin Buu:
- He was Goku's greatest adversary. In fact, Pure Buu wasn't simply a powerful evil foe but a "bratty, mentally deficient troublemaker with no self-control." Goku, perhaps understanding this, before finishing him with the Spirit Bomb, nonetheless left respect for Buu despite Pure Buu being a villain.
- Because of a pride that rivaled Goku's, Goku also admitted, "You really are something," and asked Yama of the afterlife to make sure they'd have an honorable rematch if he were reborn as a good soul. Thus Pure Buu was reincarnated as the good person "Uub."

### 5.9.11. Evil Dragons:
- Son Goku's final enemies. They are the beings that came into being when the Dragon Balls used by Son Goku and his party caused empty wishes to turn into a great calamity. However, among them the Four-Star General was Goku's final ally. He faces the One-Star General and is destroyed.
- After the death of the Four-Star General, the One-Star General sneered that you, who relied only on the Dragon Balls, had largely brought it on yourselves, and even Son Goku went so far as to admit that that was regrettable.

### 5.9.12. Goku Black:
- When Son Goku and Goku Black first encountered each other, Goku simply perceived him as someone who happened to have the same face. He was an enemy, but since he was strong they just had a fight and that was the end of it.
- However, as Goku Black's identity and atrocities were revealed, Son Goku—rarely—became extremely enraged and tried to take him down with all his power. Unfortunately, after Goku was defeated and they met again, Goku Black surprisingly and willingly yielded to Vegeta.

### 5.9.13. Zamasu:
- Because he had a knack for fighting he grew proud and begged to spar to check whether it really was Goku Black, and Zamasu, who already hated mortals of the lower realm, at first refused, but when Gowasu told him to let him spar, during the match he used ki similar to Black’s but it didn't feel evil, so he beat Zamasu and knocked him down.
- Using the Super Dragon Balls he swapped his body with the Goku of a parallel world and became Goku Black, and when he said he had killed the Goku-turned-Zamasu's Chi-Chi and Ocheon, Goku himself became an enemy who had to be taken down; it drove even Krillin to a level of rage comparable to when he was killed by Frieza—he completely lost his composure.
- When the two Zamasu fused into Fused Zamasu and fought Vegito, Vegito pointed out that the Zero Mortals Plan was impossible because of the Goku body you fused with. Rather than, in accordance with principle, separating Goku later and eliminating him, Zamasu delivered the sophistry, "I want Goku to be inside me. The Goku who transcends gods is the gods' failed work, but it is the gods' duty to accept the gods' failure within me. This too is for the universe! For the world!" while crying. From this it seems he dislikes Goku's arrogant personality, but he really likes the body of a Goku who transcends gods.

### 5.9.14. Paragus:
- He had investigated the existence of Son Goku and that Son Goku was Bardock's son. He also knew Son Goku's real name, Kakarot, and, even if he couldn't fully understand the bad history his son Broly had with Son Goku, he at least vaguely remembered it.
- In fact, unlike Paragus's son Broly, he hated Vegeta even more, so it didn't really stand out; in any case, it seems he had planned to annihilate all the Saiyans connected to Vegeta. But his plan got tangled and was completely exposed by the unexpected variable of Son Goku and his group arriving, and ultimately it failed.

## 5.10. Different Universe:
### 5.10.1. Hit:
- At first he was indifferent and brushed him off, but after seeing Goku repeatedly counter his time-skip and press him, the normally quiet Hit was moved by Goku and became more aggressive. He told Goku that if Goku kept getting stronger, Hit could grow too, and treated him with a martial-arts-like attitude rather than that of an assassin.
- Afterwards, wanting a rematch with Goku, he learned a new technique called "time displacement" and also trained his body; in the Tournament of Power he was shown cooperating against fighters from Universe 11.
- Hit can be seen as a strong rival whom Goku must take seriously—similar to Vegeta as a friendly rival who goes back and forth—while Jiren is the opponent Goku must surpass.

### 5.10.2. Caulifla:
- In the Tournament of Power, Goku taught Caulifla how to become Super Saiyan 2, making their relationship like that of master and pupil. If Vegeta has Cabba as a disciple, then Goku effectively has Caulifla as his disciple.

### 5.10.3. Jiren:
- He is the first opponent among those Goku has faced to possess strength that surpasses the Gods of Destruction, and he is also the opponent who, during their fight, allowed Goku to reach—even if only briefly—a realm beyond the level of the Gods of Destruction. After facing each other, they come to acknowledge one another's strength and pay their respects.
- To Jiren, Goku is the opponent who made him believe again in the "power of trust" that Jiren himself had not believed in; to Goku, Jiren is the opponent he must overcome in his current state and, at the same time, his present goal.

## 5.11. Evaluation:
- Almost all characters are connected to Son Goku. At the same time, he has a pretty good relationship with most people.
- Also, the people who remain as 'enemies' of Son Goku until the end are roughly Frieza, Cell, remnants of the Red Ribbon Army, Broly, and Zamasu. Even those are mostly dead, so at present you can only describe it as him simply having excellent interpersonal relationships.
- Above all, Goku's presence among his comrades is quite outstanding: long-time companions like Bulma, Krillin, and Yamcha treat him without reserve, and even former enemies like Tien and Piccolo, as well as heavenly beings such as Dende, the gods, King Yemma, and Korin, regard him as someone who will somehow get them out of a crisis.
- This image of Goku as a savior has spread among all his allies across the Earth.
- But in truth, Goku's sociability can't really be called good. He may be easygoing and treat people familiarly, but shockingly he lacks basic common sense, and even those so-called friends will go five or seven years without seeing him as if it's nothing. So much so that, in the end, Bulma told Goku, "If you just leave him alone, he'll never see anyone again" — that says it all.

# 6. Son Goku's Combat Power:
- Son Goku, together with Vegeta and Son Gohan, possesses top-tier combat power in Universe 7.
- As a warrior he is, along with Vegeta, one of the only pure-blood Saiyans; perhaps because he is faithful to the Saiyan instinct like Vegeta, he enjoys fights with strong opponents and greatly enjoys becoming stronger himself.
- Unlike the hybrid Saiyans led by Gohan, Goku's own drive to improve is at an enormous level, so he does not hesitate at all to train to get stronger and actually enjoys it.
- Even though hybrids may be inherently more talented than him, Goku's drive to improve allows him to instruct talented hybrid Saiyans, and although he may be temporarily outclassed, he steadily remains among the top fighters on his side.
- A defining feature of Goku's combat style is that he rarely ramps up his power all at once to finish a fight; he prefers a leisurely probing fight and then slowly shifts gears upward.
- In other words, even if the opponent is weaker than him, Goku matches the fight by using transformations or techniques in a similar weight class to the opponent. Partly this is to avoid pointless stamina waste, but it is also because Goku enjoys fighting. In some ways this method draws out an opponent's complacency, but it is a double-edged sword: Goku himself can be beaten before he fully shifts into gear. There are pros and cons, but a major advantage is that he conserves just enough stamina while probing to assess the opponent's strength and wait for an opportunity.
- For example, Frieza immediately exhausts his stamina as soon as he goes all-out, and both Vegeta and Piccolo suffered repeated stamina depletion during the Androids arc, so raw power tends to have a short duration.
- Goku typically fights at a moderate strength for a long time, then seizes an opportunity to amplify his ki and finish in one go — a very orthodox method.
- Since training under King Kai, Goku has shown more restrained movement; if he weren't floating in the air he'd move in a manner close to boxing or mixed martial arts.
- Goku hardly uses grappling techniques and mainly uses striking techniques. He also has a hidden identity: he has a knack for targeting weaknesses located on the torso, like an opponent's ribs or solar plexus.
- In chronological order, Colonel Silver, Tien Shinhan, King Piccolo, Piccolo Jr., Vegeta, Recoome, Frieza, Cell (anime-only), Jiren, and Moro were defeated by Goku.
- In fact, looking at his record, more often Goku's body blows played a larger role in knocking enemies down than his Energy Wave. Of course, body blows are the easiest attacks to read, so these were often simply confirmation kills when victory had already been decided.
- Goku doesn't create a wide variety of techniques, but he has excellent insight to analyze an opponent's means of attack and find a solution. Vegeta is similar, but Goku stands out a bit more — to the point where "a technique shown once won't work again" because sloppy tricks or low-level moves usually won't fool him more than once. This is a trait: as noted above, rather than inventing many original techniques himself, Goku mostly uses techniques taught to him by others.
- For example, the Energy Wave that became Goku's trademark was taught to him by Master Roshi; the high-risk, high-reward kaio-ken and Goku's ultimate technique, the Spirit Bomb, were taught by King Kai; the convenience-king Instant Transmission and the fusion technique were learned from the Yardrat elder and the Metamor elder, respectively.
- Even Ultra Instinct itself is a technique of the angels that Goku learned and uses. This contrasts with Vegeta, who tends to invent his own techniques.
- The only named technique Goku created himself is "God Bind," and even that is merely a binding technique. Instead, he maximizes the use of existing techniques — for example, firing the Energy Wave from his foot instead of his hand, firing it in the opposite direction to use the recoil to fly quickly, remote-controlling the Energy Wave into a spherical form to confuse the opponent about his position, and so on — creatively applying what he already has.

## 6.1. Childhood:
- Young Son Goku's initial power level was 10. Even at this time Goku only found pistols annoying and already had monstrous strength enough to pick up and throw cars.
- Other characters of the time, except Master Roshi and the Ox-King, were at a level where it was impossible to beat Goku by strength alone. His power level at birth had been 2.
- This is when Goku began serious training with Master Roshi. Alongside Krillin he studied under Master Roshi, developing stamina, strength, perseverance, and basic knowledge.
- Goku, perhaps because he’s a Saiyan, was stronger than Krillin who trained with him, but he ultimately lost to Master Roshi disguised as "Jackie Chun"—even though he nearly won in the World Martial Arts Tournament final.
- After that Goku clashes in earnest with the Red Ribbon Army. At this time he faces Tao Pai Pai but is overwhelmingly defeated by the Dodonpa. Preparing for a rematch, he climbs Korin Tower to meet the hermit Korin and trains to steal the Choseongsu rumored to explode his potential.
- However the rumors about the Choseongsu were false, and instead, thanks to the training he endured to chase after Korin, the now-stronger Goku defeats Tao Pai Pai in the revenge match.
- Goku enters the World Martial Arts Tournament, easily beats Krillin, and reaches the final against Tien Shinhan. Their skill seems evenly matched, but Goku executes a midair body blow on Tien and Tien faints, so at that point the match was effectively Goku’s victory. However, because the angle of their fall was somewhat unfavorable—Goku almost touched down first but, just as he tried to use a momentary energy wave to rebound and win, he was bounced by a suddenly appearing car and was counted out first. In the end, he finishes runner-up again.
- After the tournament Krillin is murdered. Goku, low on energy and hungry, chases after Tambourine in a rage but loses due to his depleted strength. After recovering his stamina he easily wins the rematch.
- Even so, there remained a stark gap between him and King Piccolo, and he was overwhelmingly defeated—even before the rejuvenation.
- He returns to Korin Tower to seek the Choshinsu said to awaken true potential. Overcoming the Choshinsu and powering up, he narrowly defeats King Piccolo by the smallest margin.
- Having defeated King Piccolo, Goku decides to go to the god who created Shenron to restore Shenron and the Dragon Balls. Arriving at the god’s palace, he attempts Mr. Popo’s test, but despite his strength he doesn’t know how to use it and is once again overwhelmingly defeated.
- Eventually Goku restores the Dragon Balls and remains in the palace on the condition that he trains under the god.
- Later Goku enters the tournament three years hence. There he defeats his rival Tien and advances to the fated final against Piccolo Jr. He struggles against Piccolo Jr., who has already grown stronger than Kami, but ultimately draws out his full power and, in a desperate headbutt that mangles his limbs, claims victory. Goku wins his first tournament. At this point Son Goku has ascended to become the strongest warrior on Earth.

## 6.2. Saiyans:
- Son Goku first clashes with his biological brother Raditz. Stripped of his heavy gi his power was 416, and using an energy blast it was at least 924, but Raditz's power was 1500, so he didn't budge even against the combined attack of Son Goku and Piccolo.
- However, due to Gohan's sudden surge in power Raditz is struck once, and taking advantage of that gap Son Goku sacrifices himself so that Piccolo's Special Beam Cannon can hit Raditz and kill him. Son Goku also experiences his first death from accumulated damage and a penetrating wound.
- Clearly Son Goku was the strongest warrior on Earth and had become stronger since the World Martial Arts Tournament arc, but Raditz — who fought to the death alongside Piccolo, an opponent equal to Goku — was not a particularly powerful warrior on a universal scale and was only a low-tier power within Frieza's forces. From this point Goku's power begins to rise rapidly, far beyond what it had been before.
- To prepare for Vegeta and Nappa's visit, Goku trains on King Kai's planet for a year, raising his base power to 5,000 and learning the Kaioken and the Spirit Bomb. When not using Kaioken and pushing his power as high as possible, Goku could exceed 8,000; Kaioken x2 raised him to 16,000, forcing it to x3 brought him to 24,000, and x4 to 32,000.
- Afterwards Goku defeats Nappa and fights Vegeta, but they only exchange blows without Goku being able to prevail, and it is only with the help of Krillin, Gohan, and Yajirobe that Vegeta is finally brought down. Having expended a great deal of energy and sustained heavy damage, Vegeta decides to retreat without finishing his match with Goku.
- Perhaps because of the beating he took from Vegeta, Goku hopes for a revenge match and vows to fight again in the future. At that time Goku was not yet fully accustomed to controlling the secret technique Kaioken, but from then on he rises to a level where he can be active even in space.

## 6.3. Frieza Battle:
- During the several days Goku spends traveling by spaceship to Planet Namek, he trains with the gravity device and as a result acquires the 10× Kaio-ken on a base power level of 90,000; at this point he easily wipes out the Ginyu Force, who had been giving Vegeta, Gohan, and Krillin's group a hard time.
- Afterwards Goku is seriously injured in his fight with Ginyu, and after recovering his power level rises to a base of 3,000,000. In this state he clashes with Final Form Frieza. For a moment it seems they are evenly matched, but Frieza had not been using his full power, and there is a vast gap in strength that even the 20× Kaio-ken cannot bridge.
- Goku's last resort, the Spirit Bomb (Genki Dama), is neutralized and he is about to be defeated, but enraged by Krillin's death he becomes a Super Saiyan and brings down Frieza, the emperor of the universe. From that point on, Goku rises to become one of the universe's top warriors, excepting only beings on the level of gods or a few others.

## 6.4. Androids Battle:
- Son Goku learns Spirit Control at Yardrat Castle and becomes able to use Instant Transmission. Later, he hears from Trunks, who came from the future, that the appearance of the androids will devastate the Earth. Even Trunks, who can transform into a Super Saiyan, could not handle the androids' power.
- Goku vows to prepare for the androids' appearance and trains for three years to power up. However, despite that power-up, he is temporarily removed from the lineup due to the onset of a heart disease.
- With Cell's arrival, and after Super Saiyan Vegeta and Trunks also fail to cope with the androids' strength and are ultimately defeated, Goku begins training in the Room of Spirit and Time together with Gohan.
- Rather than merely changing the Super Saiyan form to power up, Goku develops his ability into a fourth form that maintains the Super Saiyan state continuously, reduces excitable emotions, and allows him to control his power more efficiently.
- Afterwards he becomes roughly capable of fighting Perfect Cell, but still cannot match Cell's power. Goku sacrifices himself to deal with Cell's self-destruction and dies. This becomes Goku's second death.

## 6.5. Majin Buu Battle:
- Son Goku, after steadily training in the afterlife, acquires Super Saiyan 2 and Super Saiyan 3 during the Majin Buu arc.
- Even in Super Saiyan 2 form, Goku demonstrates power greater than Gohan had in the past and fights Majin Vegeta on even terms.
- When Goku first transformed into Super Saiyan 3, he fought Majin Buu (Fat)—who had been unbeatable—at least to a standstill, and later, before the final showdown with Majin Buu (Pure), he admitted that he had been able to defeat Fat Buu.
- Goku also fought on equal terms in the final battle with Pure Buu, but because at that time he had been revived by Old Kai's life, he was limited by the ki constraints of the living world and could not maintain Super Saiyan 3 for long, so in the long run he supposedly could not overcome Pure Buu.
- Later, with the appearance of Mystic Gohan—who surpasses Super Saiyan 3—Goku steps down from his position as the single strongest ally.

## 6.6. After:
- Despite being de-aged and weakened by the Black Star Dragon Balls, Son Goku in his base form without transforming produces power exceeding Super Saiyan 3 from the Majin Buu arc. In fact, he drives General Rirudo—whom Son Goku himself assessed as above Majin Buu—into his base state and, even without transforming, easily subdues the Super Saiyan Oban who continued training according to the setting and the Super Saiyan Ocheon who did not train.
- When Son Goku was restrained by General Rirudo's henchmen, he demonstrated phenomenal power: the shock of transforming into a Super Saiyan alone shattered those henchmen to pieces and blew away a whole planetary area.
- Afterwards Son Goku grows and employs very strategic combat. Representative examples include the mirror trick he used in the fight against the Four-Star General, and feigning an inability to aim despite his vision having been restored to lull the First-Star General into complacency and land a decisive blow.

## 6.7. Techniques Used by Son Goku:
- Most of the techniques Goku uses were taught to him by others, copied after watching someone else perform them, or are various variations of energy blasts.
- The only technique Goku developed entirely originally is the Kwang Fist (Kwang-kwon), and even that is not a deeply developed technique but a one-off fist method improvised on the spot.
- Rock-Paper-Scissors Fist: A technique young Goku learned from his grandfather Son Gohan. He hardly uses it anymore.
- Kaio-ken: A kind of doping technique that controls and momentarily amplifies all the ki in the body. If done properly, strength, speed, destructive power, and durability are said to increase several times.
- Kaio-ken Finish: A technique used on Nappa. He activates Kaio-ken, charges in a Superman pose, stabs the opponent in the back, then lifts and throws them.
- Kaio-ken Attack/Assault: A technique used on Vegeta. He charges with triple Kaio-ken and tracks and attacks flying opponents one by one.
- Berserk Fist (Kwang-kwon): A fist style devised in response to Jackie Chun's "Drunken Fist," literally a technique that rampages like a mad dog.
- Afterimage Fist: It’s not clearly described when he learned it, but he used it when fighting Jackie Chun and afterward is often shown to toy with opponents using afterimages.
- Solar Flare: Tien Shinhan’s technique, which Goku used when fighting Tien at the 23rd Tenkaichi Budokai.
- Martial Technique: First used when fighting Piccolo at the 23rd Tenkaichi Budokai. It did not allow him to fly faster than the Nimbus.
- Ki Glare: A technique where he stares at an opponent to deliver a physical shock. He used it on Frieza, briefly pressuring and stunning him, causing Frieza to clumsily escape just before being thrown into the sea. He used a similar technique when digging a grave to bury Vegeta's body. East King Kai and Majin Buu also used it, and Jiren uses a similar technique which Goku used to counter it.
- Spirit Bomb (Genki Dama): A technique that can only be used by someone with a pure heart and is also Goku’s decisive weapon.
- Instant Transmission: A technique where he places his hand on his forehead to sense ki and then teleports to the desired location. He learned it from the Yardrat elders.
- Fusion: He fuses with Vegeta to become Vegito.
- Ki Blast (Void-like technique): A projectile-like technique Goku used after mastering the unconscious extreme.
- Dragon Fist: Concentrates raised ki into the fist, shapes it like a dragon, and blows the enemy away. Since it’s not an energy-wave-type technique, it cannot be absorbed like energy blasts.
- Dragon Strike Fist: A ki-glare where he thrusts his hand strongly at the opponent. He used it on Vegeta and Pure Buu. (As a side note, the first one he ever used was on Chi-Chi.)
- Flying Headbutt: It may seem like a brutish move unbefitting a master of martial arts, but surprisingly it was used twice as a finishing move on bosses. First, an aerial headbutt used against Tien while firing an energy wave backward; second, a surprise headbutt using a hidden martial technique against Piccolo.
- Mafuba (Evil Containment Wave): Taught to him by Master Mutaito to seal Goku Black and Zamasu.
- Meteor Combination: A technique used on Majunior. He charges, unleashes a flashy leg rush, then jumps vertically and fires an energy wave.
- Meteor Smash: A technique used on Frieza. While evading an attack he lands a powerful body blow, charges with a flying kick, and just before impact or evasion he crosses his opposite leg and snaps it to kick the opponent away.
- Meteor Blast: Forms a dome-shaped explosive hemisphere with both hands. It vaporized Pure Majin Buu’s body in an instant.
- Destruction: Used in the fight against Fused Zamasu. A technique that annihilates the very existence of the target — similar to a God of Destruction’s technique — but it appears difficult to control; unlike Beerus’s instantaneous Hakai which erases a target at once, this one shows a gradual disappearance. It’s unknown when or how he acquired it.
- Ki Giant: Creates a giant in the exact likeness of Goku and uses its arms to hold the enemy so they cannot escape.
- God Bind: A technique that restrains an opponent with ki; it briefly held rampaging Broly while Goku was in Super Saiyan God form.
- Always-on Unconscious Ultra Instinct: Through training with Whis, he acquired the ability to unconsciously dodge without transforming. It functions like a passive that allows dodging without switching to Ultra Instinct, but his mastery is still low, so its accuracy is worse than when he is in the unconscious Ultra Instinct state.
- Biting: You might say "biting" isn’t a technique, but it was a surprisingly handy method he used to get free when bound.

### 6.7.1. Energy Wave:
- Energy Wave: a finishing move used by most characters, including Master Roshi and his school. Originally Master Roshi didn't directly pass the Energy Wave on to anyone except Grandpa Gohan, but Goku saw it once and copied it exactly.
- Super Energy Wave: a technique equivalent to Goku's full-power Energy Wave. He used it as a trump card against Piccolo at the 23rd World Martial Arts Tournament.
- Reverse Energy Wave: a technique that gathers an Energy Wave, fires it in the opposite direction, then charges using its recoil. It can be used with one hand or even with both feet instead of hands.
- Guided Energy Wave: a technique characterized by lifting the Energy Wave and changing its trajectory. Usually they fire it straight to lure the opponent into complacency, then change direction. Also called the bending Energy Wave.
- 3x/4x Kaio-ken Energy Wave: a technique used to counter Vegeta's Galick Gun. Knowing that Kaio-ken x2 wouldn't overwhelm Vegeta, Goku fires it at x3 from the start. He later increases output to x4 to overpower Vegeta, pushing Kaio-ken to its absolute limit and sending Vegeta up into the sky.
- Kaio-ken x20 Energy Wave: a technique Goku tried against Frieza, who was supposedly using only half his power; an Energy Wave fired while using Kaio-ken x20. Its firing speed is relatively fast. Even so, its power was strong enough to bewilder Final Form Frieza despite him using only half his strength, and Frieza actually had a lot of trouble blocking it. A technique using an unorthodox activation timing.
- Wrathful Energy Wave: the technique that finished off Frieza. It uses only one hand, has a different pose, and you don't shout "Energy Wave"—it's just a ki blast—but the media treat it as an Energy Wave.
- Instant Transmission Energy Wave: a technique where an Energy Wave is charged to the maximum from afar, then Instant Transmission is used to appear in front of the enemy and attack. First used during the Cell fight and also used against Beerus.
- x10 Energy Wave: an Energy Wave used in Super Saiyan 4 form.
- Limit-Break Energy Wave: an upgraded version of the Super Energy Wave. An Energy Wave fired in Super Saiyan God form. Because it's used wrapped in godly ki, its output is on a different level.
- God Energy Wave: an upgrade of the Limit-Break Energy Wave. An Energy Wave fired in Super Saiyan Blue form. It is far more powerful than the Energy Wave fired in Super Saiyan God form.
- New Moon Energy Wave: used against Kefla in the Ultra Instinct -Sign- state. It compensates for the previous weakness of being unstable during attacks and thus having reduced damage. Also called the sliding Energy Wave.
- Ultra Extreme Energy Wave: a superior version of the God Energy Wave. An Energy Wave fired in the Ultra Instinct state.
- Father-Son Energy Wave: a joint technique by Goku and Gohan used during the Energy Wave showdown with Cell, who had become a perfect being. With this technique, Gohan wins by pushing back Cell's Energy Wave—which had gathered enough power to blow away the entire solar system—head-on.
- Three-Generation Energy Wave: a joint technique with Son Gohan and Son Goten. The technique that decorated the climax of "The Two Dangerous Super Saiyans Do Not Sleep."
- Final Energy Wave: famous as Vegito's technique, but it was also used as a fusion attack by Vegeta and Goku.
- Galick Energy Wave: used together with Vegeta's Galick Gun in Super Saiyan Blue form. Its shape is slightly different from the Final Energy Wave: while the Final Energy Wave is similar to the Special Beam Cannon in that the Final Flash is wrapped in a spiral, the Galick Energy Wave has the Galick Gun overlaid on the Energy Wave, forming an outer layer of the Galick Gun.
- Cross Energy Wave: a variation of the Father-Son Energy Wave.

### 6.7.2. Transformations:
#### 6.7.2.1. Great Ape:
- The Saiyan's basic transformation and the first form Goku ever transformed into. They transform upon seeing a full moon, and while transformed they lose their sanity and become violent.
- The Great Ape's appearance can be likened almost to King Kong, and transformation is only possible if one has a tail.
- It first appeared during the Pilaf battle; when all the Dragon Balls were taken and Goku was locked in a cell, he saw the full moon and transformed. Its bulk was easily around 10 meters, and it destroyed Pilaf's castle. It rampaged after losing its mind, but Yamcha had Puar transform into a pair of scissors, and when he cut off the tail, he reverted to his original Goku form.
- Later the Great Ape appeared in the 21st World Martial Arts Tournament arc, but Master Roshi destroyed the moon with an energy blast, causing the transformation to end.
- Afterward, Kami permanently cut off Goku's tail so it would never grow back, so the form ceased to appear; besides, it is weaker than later-learned transformations like Kaio-ken or Super Saiyan. Moreover, since it causes the loss of rationality and rampaging behavior, there is hardly any reason to transform into it. In Great Ape form, you can see that the hands and feet have the same shape.

#### 6.7.2.2. Kaio-ken:
- Kaio-ken is a transformation Son Goku acquired by training under the North Kaio he met in the afterlife, after he and Raditz died together early in the Saiyan battle.
- When using Kaio-ken, combat power increases by a specified multiplier and a red aura surrounds the entire body. However, if the multiplier is forced too high, the recoil is severe and places great strain on the body.
- Son Goku demonstrated Kaio-ken at 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, and 20x. The standard Kaio-ken (2x) was shown to Nappa and Ginyu; when 2x proved insufficient in the fierce fight with Vegeta he used 3x, and in the clash between the Galick Gun and the Energy Wave he used up to 4x to secure a decisive advantage.
- Later Goku demonstrated 10x and 20x against Frieza. At the time 10x was the best he could manage, and 20x was likely beyond his limits; having forced a 20x Kaio-ken Energy Wave, Goku’s stamina was completely depleted and he was overwhelmed by Frieza, who had blocked the Energy Wave without taking much damage.

#### 6.7.2.3. Super Saiyan:
- In the mid-to-late stages of the battle between Son Goku and Frieza, Goku used his last resort, the Spirit Bomb (Genki-Dama), against Frieza, who had even withstood the 20x Kaioken Energy Wave. It only caused damage—Frieza did not die—and an enraged Frieza drove Piccolo to the brink of death and blew Krillin apart, killing him.
- Seeing this atrocity firsthand, Son Goku felt overwhelming rage and awakened as the legendary warrior, the Super Saiyan. All his stats, including speed and power, rose overwhelmingly, and as a result he left Frieza nearly dead.
- The Super Saiyan’s appearance features hair standing on end and turning blond, yellow eyebrows, and green eyes. The aura around the entire body becomes golden. Later, higher transformations were introduced and enemies appeared against whom even the basic Super Saiyan level could not compete, making its practicality almost nil; however, it is usually used to gauge or test an opponent’s combat power.
- Above all, putting raw power aside, this form has the best energy and stamina consumption efficiency among the Super Saiyan series.
- Son Goku trained in the Room of Spirit and Time with Oban; entering earlier than the other two, he, like Vegeta and Trunks who had learned the Super Saiyan power-up transformations, acquired those power-up transformations as well, but discovered the problem that while power increased, speed decreased. Consequently, Son Goku chose to remain in Super Saiyan form at all times, adapting the Super Saiyan state to be like his normal state, thereby choosing the method of producing "optimal power" from the "most stable form."

#### 6.7.2.4. Super Saiyan 2:
- Goku, who died preventing Cell's self-destruction, trained in the Other World and acquired it. Goku's combat power is increased to 150 times normal (three times that of Super Saiyan 1).
- The appearance of Super Saiyan 2 features hair that rises more sharply and becomes fuller than Super Saiyan 1; whereas in 1 there are 4–5 strands of bangs that fall down, in 2 the side hair and bangs all rise except for a single strand, creating a cleaner form that shows more of the forehead.
- Also sparks fly from the aura and the hair color appears slightly lighter. Goku's first transformation was in the Majin Buu arc, when he transformed to face Majin Vegeta.

#### 6.7.2.5. Super Saiyan 3:
- Super Saiyan 3 is overall quite different from the previous Super Saiyan forms. The hair becomes very thick and long, extending down to the waist, and the usual trait of turning the eyebrows gold is nowhere to be seen — like Piccolo, pronounced musculature means there are no eyebrows. The eyes turn green and develop dark green pupils.
- Becoming Super Saiyan 3 changes not only the appearance but also the scale of power; the transformation alone, which takes several minutes, depicts a massive increase in energy that shakes the entire Earth.
- Even by simple comparison, it was boldly claimed that Super Saiyan 3 could defeat Fat Buu, whom Super Saiyan 2 Majin Vegeta could not handle.
- However, a serious flaw of Super Saiyan 3 is that its ki consumption is inseparably tied to its power.
- Because of this, Goku — who could stay in the land of the living for 24 hours — had that time shortened so much that a single transformation forced him to return to the afterlife. When he used it after being revived, it consumed such extreme stamina and ki that, while Vegeta was buying time, Goku tried to gather ki but the side effects of overconsumption caused the transformation itself to be undone.
- Goku first used Super Saiyan 3 when he confronted Majin Buu, whom Babidi had resurrected.

#### 6.7.2.6. Super Saiyan God:
- A transformation that reaches the level of a god. Five Saiyans surrounded one Saiyan and performed a ritual to effect the transformation.
- Only the hair color turns red while the original hairstyle remains, and the muscles become leaner. He also looks somewhat younger, and the aura becomes a red aura that blazes like flames.
- The strength of Super Saiyan God vastly surpasses Super Saiyan 3, and it became possible to somewhat contend with Beerus, who had overwhelmed Super Saiyan 3.

#### 6.7.2.7. Super Saiyan Blue:
- A form achieved by layering another Super Saiyan transformation on top of the Super Saiyan God state; its initial name was "Super Saiyan God Super Saiyan," but because that was too long, Whis instead named it "Super Saiyan Blue."
- Super Saiyan Blue's appearance features a blue aura that blazes like the God form and blue hair shaped similarly to Super Saiyan 1. Goku trained under Whis, surpassed Super Saiyan, and reached Super Saiyan Blue.
- Super Saiyan Blue's combat power is significantly increased compared to the God form. As a clear example, Goku was completely trounced by Shin Broly even in his God state, but when he transformed into Blue Broly could no longer maintain the upper hand and they exchanged close-quarters combat repeatedly until Frieza called him.

#### 6.7.2.8. Ultra Instinct -Sign-:
- A transformation Goku acquired in his fight with Jiren. It is the "Sign" form of Ultra Instinct and the transformation obtained during the Tournament of Power.
- Ultra Instinct allows the body to automatically dodge, but, as the name "Sign" implies, it is an incomplete, unstable state. Therefore it has a flaw: at the moment of attacking consciousness returns, so offensive power cannot reach Ultra Instinct's full strength, and the technique cannot be maintained stably, resulting in severe stamina drain.

#### 6.7.2.9. Pinnacle of the Unconscious (Perfected Form):
- Son Goku is in a state of having completely mastered the pinnacle of the unconscious; unlike the previous omens, he fights unconsciously not only when evading but also at the moment of attack, literally not thinking about either offense or defense, thereby attaining an automatic combat sense through his body — the ultimate state.
- As the perfected form of the pinnacle of the unconscious, Son Goku clearly overwhelms Jiren, a fighter whose ability transcends the Gods of Destruction, demonstrating that the perfected pinnacle is unquestionably a realm that surpasses the Gods of Destruction. However, the price of gaining power that exceeds limits is greater than imagined, so the side effects are quite severe for Goku’s body to endure.
- The appearance of the perfected pinnacle of the unconscious, like the omen of the pinnacle, shows certain parts of the hair raised to some extent and turning silver, a silver aura surrounding the body, and the pupils also turning silver.

#### 6.7.2.10. True Unconscious Ultra Instinct:
- Son Goku’s own Unconscious Ultra Instinct — an answer to when Goku told Whis, “It’s hard to always stay calm like you,” and Whis replied, “Angels and you are fundamentally different beings, of course. Find your own Ultra Instinct.”
- The true Unconscious Ultra Instinct has the same eyes as the sign of Unconscious Ultra Instinct, but unlike the original sign, the hair is ordinary black without any shining highlights.
- The original Unconscious Ultra Instinct requires keeping the mind always calm, but this form can exert power beyond the original Unconscious Ultra Instinct without that requirement.
- Unlike the original Unconscious Ultra Instinct, which fought with restrained movements, this form’s combat becomes rougher.
- Even Vegeta, seeing the fighting style of the true Unconscious Ultra Instinct, remarked, “To think fighting like that is Ultra Instinct.” Its strength is at a level that completely overwhelms Gas, who can simultaneously contend with both Unconscious Ultra Instinct and Conscious Ultra Instinct.
- However, it was defeated because Gas grew even stronger by using his lifespan as fuel. It seems this is still only the initial stage of finding his own Unconscious Ultra Instinct. The strain on the body is still considerable.

#### 6.7.2.11 Super Saiyan 4:
- Son Goku transforms through Neba's unlocking of latent potential. Although Son Goku became Super Saiyan 4 for the first time because Neba released his latent potential, it is said that he had been training beforehand aiming for the level of Super Saiyan 3.
- However, until receiving Neba's help he had never transformed and, judging from his saying he wasn't even sure it was possible, he had only established the theory before that; with Neba's help and the experience, it seems he internalized it in a way similar to Super Saiyan God.
- Super Saiyan 4 can use energy waves that emit an orange energy similar to the red energy wave, and has high durability.

### 6.7.3. Other Abilities:
- Son Goku is an ultra-elite master who went back and forth among the best martial arts teachers of his era and received highly systematic training.
- From his adoptive father figure Grandpa Gohan to being a famed disciple of Master Mutaito — who was regarded as a god of martial arts — he inherited various secret techniques even from divine-level figures like King Kai.
- In terms of understanding and applying techniques, he is arguably equal to or even better than Vegeta, who was trained by that king.
- You can consider Son Goku a perfect master with nothing lacking in basics or secret techniques. He never invented as many varied and powerful techniques as Tien, Piccolo, or Vegeta, but his combat sense is so outstanding that his tactical judgment is among the best.
- Thanks to that, even though Goku has no remarkable original techniques, he always improvised and fought on par or better against opponents who had similar power but more varied techniques, and even did fairly well against slightly stronger foes like Cell.
- The energy wave Goku often uses feels, in raw power, somewhat a step below the likes of the Tri-Beam, Masenko, Galick Gun, or Final Flash, but Goku landed effective hits by using many creative applications — body-slamming using the recoil of the wave, changing the wave’s direction, firing the wave with his foot, reinforcing it with Kaio-ken, or combining it with Instant Transmission. He is truly a genius at applying techniques.
- Goku’s instincts are extremely sharp — he can make bold, superhuman judgments in life-or-death situations.
- Goku was once fooled by Raditz’s false promise that he would never invade again and spared him; in a similar later situation he showed no mercy and killed. With Vegeta, however, Goku spared him at the moment when Krillin could have delivered a finishing blow with Yajirobe’s sword, and that act indirectly earned him an ally later (Vegeta received help from a medical machine during the Frieza battle).
- Before Namek was about to explode, Goku made the risky request to Porunga that, after warping everyone except Frieza away, Porunga leave Goku behind on Namek so he could finish the fight — which ultimately led to his ship being wrecked to Yardrat and became the opportunity for him to learn Instant Transmission.
- If Vegeta is the type to put into action a conclusion reached by meticulous, rational, and calm calculation, Goku is the type who makes emotionally excellent judgments that he may not be able to explain rationally.
- Also, Goku’s ability to manipulate ki is on a completely different level from other characters. Techniques like the Spirit Bomb or Kaio-ken depend heavily on ki control, and seeing him freely wield techniques even the creator of Kaio himself never fully perfected shows that his ki-handling ability, along with his combat sense, is top-tier.
- However, compared to his ki-control, he may lack delicacy: Tien learned the Mafuba (Evil Containment Wave), which was written by Master Mutaito, by looking at it once and taking the whole day to learn it on his own.
- Besides that, when Goku searched the Sacred Namek to bring Dende before the Cell fight, he displayed abilities that surprised Piccolo and King Kai, and around the Saiyan and Frieza battles he often assessed an opponent’s capabilities before fighting and predicted the outcome in advance. This measurement ability is excellent, but not perfect.
- Goku also possesses non-combat psychic-like abilities such as placing his hand on an opponent’s head to read their memories, telepathy, and the ability to look down at the lower world from the palace of the gods. He learned telepathy from King Kai, and the other abilities were likely naturally acquired as his mental powers strengthened while he grew stronger.

# 7. Items related to Son Goku:
- Four-Star Dragon Ball: Considered his grandfather's keepsake and emblem. He joined Bulma on her journey partly because she planned to reclaim the Four-Star Ball after having a wish granted. Later he went to train under Master Roshi, but immediately after the World Martial Arts Tournament he set out to recover the Four-Star Ball; after fulfilling the wish to revive Bora, when the Dragon Balls scattered he jumped to grab only the Four-Star Ball. He later attached it to his son Gohan's hat.
- Power Pole (Nyoi-bō): Also his grandfather's keepsake; he had it from the beginning. Later, to reach Kami's Lookout he stuck it into the top of Korin Tower and extended it up to the Lookout. After training with Kami at the Lookout it has remained in that extended position.
- Flying Nimbus: Received from Master Roshi as thanks for rescuing his turtle. It was Goku's only rideable vehicle — he couldn't operate other vehicles and ended up wrecking them. By the way, only kind-hearted people can ride it. So after receiving it from Roshi, Goku had no trouble riding it, but Krillin, who had been a prankster since childhood, fell straight to the ground.
- Master Roshi–style gi: He generally preferred Chinese-style clothing, but he wore the gi he received from Master Roshi for almost his entire life. When it got torn beyond use he made a new one with the exact same design.
- Dragon Radar: Bulma's invention, but Bulma handed it over to Goku because she no longer thought it necessary to collect the Dragon Balls. Since Goku needed to find the Four-Star Ball, he kept the radar and, after the World Martial Arts Tournament, set out on another journey to gather the Dragon Balls. Also, when Bulma didn't accompany the trip, Goku carried it.
- Choseong Water & Choshin Water: He received these from Korin when he went to Korin Tower and drank them. Goku is the only person who drank both.
- Senzu Bean: Likewise received at Korin Tower. A very important and almost unique recovery item; thanks to the senzu bean he escaped death several times.
- Special Rubber Protector: Made by Bulma and worn by him. Piccolo and Tien refused to wear it, saying clothes that Saiyans or Frieza wear feel unpleasant, but Goku praised its excellence and wore it. After wearing it in the Room of Spirit and Time during training it was completely torn and broken, and he changed back into his gi, so he didn't wear it again.
- Spaceships: He owned more spaceships than one might expect. First, there was the Attack Ball he rode when he first came to Earth, which he modified to travel all the way to Planet Namek. That ship was inadvertently left behind on Namek and disappeared, but he returned to Earth aboard the Ginyu Force's ship.
- Potara: He received them from the Supreme Kai. Because the situation was urgent he risked it and fused with Vegeta to become Vegito, but later when fighting Kid Buu he apparently felt the method was cowardly, so he refused and went into a one-on-one fight.
