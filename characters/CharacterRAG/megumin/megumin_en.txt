- Name: Megumin
- Birthday: December 4, 14 years old.
- Race: Crimson Demon
- Gender: Female
- Height: 148 cm
- Specialty: Explosion Magic
- Occupation: Arch Wizard
- Affiliation: Kazuma's party, Megumin's Thieves' Guild
- Members of Kazuma's party: Kazuma, me (Megumin), Aqua, Darkness
- Family: Father (효이자부로), Mother (유이유이), Younger sister (코멧코), Chumsuke (familiar, pet)

# 2. Overview:
- Self-introduction: "My name is Megumin! The Crimson Demons' greatest mage and the one who wields Explosion Magic!"
- Megumin is a member of the Crimson Demon Clan with exceptional talent for magic, and among them a promising prodigy with outstanding potential. Her class is Arch Wizard, but the only skill she can use is Explosion Magic. In Kazuma's party she is responsible for attacking.
- Her family consists of her father Hyoui Zaburo, her mother Yuiyui, and her younger sister Komekko, and she brings Chomusuke along as a familiar or pet.
- Yunyun: A fellow Crimson Demon who is both her rival and her closest friend.
- Arue: She is close with her classmate Arue.
- Cecily: When Megumin left the Crimson Demon village to travel to Axel Village, she passed through Arcanretia; there she made contact with the Axis Church and became acquainted with Cecily.

# 3. Megumin's characteristics:
- She has shoulder-length black hair and the Crimson Demon clan's distinctive red eyes, and wears the stereotypical witch outfit with a cloak and hat. (Before leaving the Crimson Demon Village, she received them as a gift from her Crimson Demon friends (Dodonko, Hunihura, Yunyun).).
- She sometimes wears an eyepatch as a chuunibyou setting that seals an overwhelmingly powerful magic (a gift from her classmate Arue).
- Normally she wears a cute-looking red dress.
- Megumin is self-conscious about her body shape and particularly hates being called a "loli kid." Once she was completely deflated when Kazuma called her that.
- When she first joined Kazuma's party she believed that, as a genius mage, she didn't need to get close to others and could be fine staying alone; even if she met regrettable companions she thought she could just support them herself. Also, she is far from gentle in temperament — if someone picks a quarrel she won't hold back and will recklessly charge in. In Axel Village a rumor spread that her head was odd and people avoided her, and she was once assessed by party members as having a strong berserker potential. For these reasons, in Axel Village she is known as "the one with a strange head," and the cause of Kazuma party's bad reputation is practically split between Kazuma and Megumin.
- However, after spending a long time with Kazuma's party her personality softened considerably (the reason being "because she fell in love.").
- In Kazuma's party Megumin cares for and worries about her comrades more than anyone, and by Volume 5 she even shows a willingness to give up the Explosion magic she's devoted her life to in order to learn higher-level magic so she can properly help the party. (Learning higher-level magic doesn't mean she can't use Explosion, but because of mana limits, if she uses higher-level magic that day she can't use Explosion. And Megumin has put all her skill points into enhancing Explosion's power — the moment she splits those points into other magics it becomes practically impossible for her to awaken the true essence of Explosion magic, at least by her own standard.)
- She possesses many traits: flat-chested, chuunibyou, bin-dere, tsundere, yandere, megadere, and so on.
- Chuunibyou: Megumin is known for severe chuunibyou tendencies, but this isn't unique to her — it's a problem of the Crimson Demons as a whole. The Crimson Demon race, except for a few, suffers seriously from chuunibyou, so Megumin also displays those tendencies. She boasts with the Crimson Demons' grandiose self-introductions and always wants to take the most important and coolest part (considered a basic culturing of the Crimson Demons).
- Polite speech: She is a polite-speech character and always speaks with honorifics to anyone, even her younger sister Komekko. The only exceptions are when she displays her chuunibyou side, when someone gets on her nerves, or when she is flustered — in those emotionally heightened moments she immediately slips into casual speech, and even in a strange archaic samurai-like casual tone. For example, if someone were to mock her chest as small she might say, "Oi, if you have a complaint about the size of my chest, why don't you say it now?"
- Megumin has very strong pride. No, it's too strong. She hates being looked down on more than death, so in the early days she'd pick fights and cause trouble every time her shoulder brushed a somewhat tough-looking adventurer. Even after meeting Axel's most shameless, insolent bastard Kazuma and having her momentum dented a bit, she absolutely refuses to bow her head to demon-lord officers or royalty and hates showing weakness. That said, with people she likes she will uniquely act spoiled or deliberately demure.
- Perhaps because she grew up in poverty, she and her younger sister both have quite the appetites. She sometimes loses her head in front of delicious ingredients.
- Among the Crimson Demons she is the top genius, and from the start of her adventuring she is a mage who uses the ultimate offensive spell, Explosion — a spell so powerful that only an outstanding mage can even cast it (the mana required is enormous, so even if learned many cannot use it for lack of power, and those who can usually can only use it once a day). But because she loves Explosion so much, she refuses to learn any other magic and, due to Explosion's impracticality, fails to make proper use of her talents. In the Crimson Demon Village she was called the clan's greatest genius, but after it was discovered she could only use Explosion she became known as a mage in name only.
- From early on she would introduce herself as the Crimson Demons' greatest mage; she was the top genius at school. After surpassing Wiz with Explosion, she would proclaim herself the strongest mage in Axel Village (before Wiz appeared she even introduced herself to Verdia as Axel Village's strongest mage).
- In Axel Village she is infamous in a negative way — a mage useless for ordinary party quests who gets hyped up during everyday conversation, picks fights, and tries to blast Explosion anywhere, whether in the village or in the guild hall, earning nicknames like "the crazy one" (a sobriquet given after she used Explosion near the village for no reason before meeting Kazuma). She is called the "insane Crimson Demon" (originating from Verdia calling her "an insane one," "an insane Crimson Demon girl" after she thought Verdia's mansion was empty and kept blasting Explosion at it daily). People vary the latter part — "insane girl," "insane that one," etc. — but the "insane" part is consistent. She is also called an Explosion fanatic.
- Because of this she is one of the main reasons for Kazuma party's tarnished reputation. Due to her bombastic tendencies she sometimes can't control her actions and at times causes troubles Kazuma can't properly handle. The reason she used Explosion at Verdia's mansion was that she had been doing it around the village every day and was warned due to noise and repair costs.
- Megumin's genius isn't limited to magic; compared to most people including the Kazuma party she has very quick thinking and high intelligence. Even in first-encounter situations she can accurately grasp the context from the few clues on site or others' behavior.
- Going a step further, she often sets traps that leave the other party members helpless. But fitting the saying "a genius is a madman," her chuunibyou streak and her love of Explosion often consume that genius, burying it and producing many foolish situations.
- She really likes cute things. She treasures Chomusuke and the Gel King and finds them adorable. However, the priority isn't that high, and if something cute doesn't benefit her she will mercilessly kill it. In the Crimson Demon Village she once killed every Paori she saw to grind experience and was nicknamed the Paori Slayer (a nickname Megumin dislikes).
- She originally didn't intend to kill Paori, but when Yunyun told her they were rare monsters that give large amounts of experience she snapped their necks without mercy. At Elroad she even caused an incident slaughtering Paori for sightseeing.
- As a Crimson Demon her naming sense is not just bad but catastrophic (though she thinks her names are good). Typical examples are Chomusuke and Chunchunmaru.

# 4. Megumin's Background:
- She was born into the poorest family in a village of the Crimson Demons and had such an impoverished childhood that toys were out of the question and she couldn't even get a proper meal (to the point that stealing YunYun's lunchbox was routine; she would steal the crusts of bread thrown away at school, sneak vegetables from fields, or even catch cicadas in the forest and roast them to eat.).
- When she was a child and bored, she played with a sealing piece used to bind a shinigami like a puzzle and accidentally broke the seal. A gigantic beast then appeared and attacked Megumin, but a female mage showed up and drove off the beast with Explosion magic. The Explosion magic she saw then left a strong impression, so she learned Explosion magic from the sister who saved her, and later resolved to master Explosion magic and show it to that sister (The Shinigami whose seal Cometko broke was Wolberg, the goddess of Sloth and Tyranny; the female mage who saved Megumin is a demigod overseeing Wolberg's Sloth, and the beast that attacked Megumin is Chumske, a demigod overseeing Wolberg's Tyranny.).
- After some time, Megumin entered the Crimson Demon academy and achieved excellent grades, never missing first place. She learned the Explosion magic she had dreamed of, graduated from the school, and heard that a woman who uses Explosion magic lived in the village of Axel, her original travel destination. She then saved travel funds by doing part-time jobs she wasn't very good at (in truth she earned little from those jobs; most of the money came from a demon who claimed to be a subordinate of Chumske in exchange for returning Chumske), and together with YunYun (YunYun, worried about Megumin, secretly followed and met her in Arcanletia) she traveled via Arcanletia to the village of Axel.
- After many hardships they finally arrived at the village of Axel, but a mage who can only use Explosion magic and collapses after a single cast was welcomed by no party, even in a beginner village; instead she only caused loud trouble and her reputation worsened.
- After arriving in Axel, she parted ways with YunYun (YunYun acknowledged that Megumin was stronger than her and left to train to become stronger herself), spent all her money and wandered starving for three days, then saw a recruitment notice for a party of an Arch Priest and rookie adventurers hiring for higher-rank jobs. She eventually settled into that party and found true companions.

- Explosion Magic Chant: "It is the flame of beginning. It is the flame of the end. The forbidden power that transcends present, past, and future—all things. O flame, I am the destroyer, the merciless one. With my blood, with my flesh, I now break the decrees of the old gods!"

# 6.3. Drawbacks of Explosion Magic:
- In enclosed spaces it can't be used at all because of the risk of friendly fire. In most dungeons Megumin's value disappears.
- Because of the overwhelming mana consumption, she can only use it once per day. After casting explosion magic, her mana is completely drained.
- Megumin can use explosion magic only once a day, and after using it her mana is exhausted and she collapses, so someone has to carry her.

# 7. Megumin's Relationships:
## 7.1. Kazuma:
- To Megumin, Kazuma is the only benefactor who accepted her, someone she respects, and at the same time someone she loves.
- Before meeting Kazuma, she tried joining several parties in Axel, but they all rejected her because none of them knew how to use Megumin properly. As a result she earned nicknames like "useless wizard" or "crazy wizard," and it was Kazuma who accepted someone like her.
- Because of her unique ability and personality, she can't do work other than being an adventurer, so without Kazuma she could easily end up starving on the street. In the story, Kazuma is described as indispensable to Megumin and a benefactor-like figure. He also gets along well with her profession of Explosion magic, and because of that Kazuma came to understand Explosion magic to some extent.
- Kazuma calls himself a self-proclaimed "Explosion Sommelier" and rates Explosion magic on a 100-point scale. She is very happy when she receives recognition for her Explosion magic from Kazuma, the party leader and the person she likes.
- In the end, Kazuma, having watched her Explosion magic almost every day for over a year, came to understand it perfectly and, without following any particular skill tree, was able to learn Explosion magic!
- Also, she discovers that the masked (silver‑haired) thief among the masked thief members she admired was actually Kazuma, and she falls for Kazuma's cool appearance disguised as a masked thief once more. Since then she has cast even deeper admiring glances his way.

## 7.2. Aqua:
- Aqua and Megumin are close like an older sister and younger sister. Because Megumin's intelligence is far superior, it can sometimes seem the other way around. Whether she's teasing on purpose or just oblivious, she sabotages Kazuma and Megumin's important moments, preventing any "flags" from forming between them. They often bicker, but they're quite close and have great chemistry — they coordinate perfectly when teasing Kazuma and are frequently seen joking around together.

## 7.3. Darkness:
- Darkness is quite perverted, but Megumin just shrugs it off and doesn't make a big deal of it.
- She knows Darkness likes Kazuma, and while she gets angry at Darkness and keeps her in check, Megumin and Darkness regard each other as valued comrades. When Darkness tried to leave the party, Megumin was the one who acted most proactively.
- However, Megumin finds Darkness's lewd nature hard to put up with; when that side of Darkness comes out, Megumin sometimes puts on a serious face along with Kazuma, and when those desires are aimed at Kazuma, she teases her.
- When she heard that Darkness had tried to have her first experience with Kazuma, Megumin taunted, "I thought you were a tragic heroine, but you're a total bitch," tapping her cheek with her staff as she provoked her.

## 7.4. Scarlet Devil Clan:
### 7.4.1. YunYun:
- YunYun is Megumin's close friend. They've been close since the Scarlet Devil clan village, and when they were in school they were sometimes mistaken for a yuri couple. Whenever she needs help Megumin always calls YunYun, so she essentially acts as the fifth member of Kazuma's party.
- Although they're friends, Megumin shows a tsundere attitude toward YunYun, and YunYun lacks confidence, so Megumin sometimes pretends not to know her or hurls insults. YunYun, fearing Megumin might not consider her a friend, sometimes calls her a rival and hesitates to confidently call her a friend. (Even in matches with YunYun, Megumin outwardly acts like she doesn't care, as if it's nothing, but in truth she cares enough to record all the wins and losses.)
- But when Megumin first left the Scarlet Devil village, the first person she thought of was YunYun, which shows how inseparable they are. YunYun secretly following Megumin was the same.
- YunYun risks danger to help Megumin, and Megumin likewise will leap into action if anything happens to YunYun — they're as close as Megumin is to Kazuma.

### 7.4.2. Arue:
- A classmate who hung out during school days, though not as much as Yunyun. He acknowledges Megumin, who always takes first place, and wants to be genuinely close to her, but the ill-tempered Megumin is jealous because he has something she doesn't.
- The eyepatch Megumin wears was a gift from Arue. Actually, Arue gave Megumin an eyepatch claiming it was a present, but it turned out to be the old eyepatch he had been using himself, and he immediately took out and put on a new eyepatch right then. Megumin was initially touched, but when she found out she was baffled and snatched the new eyepatch.

### 7.4.3. Hunihura and Dodonko:
- A classmate who used to take Yunyun away from Megumin. She seemed to act cold toward Megumin and even once interfered with Megumin's part-time job. Megumin thought she was a delinquent who bullied Yunyun, but it was all a misunderstanding (people thought Hunihura was shaking Yunyun down for money under the pretense of needing it for her younger sibling's medicine, but in fact Hunihura really did need money for her sibling's medicine and had borrowed it, and the medicine Megumin made so she could get the money back worked well, so her sibling's condition improved a lot).
- When leaving she asked Megumin to introduce her to any decent men when she came back to the village, but when Megumin actually brought Kazuma, she grumbled, telling her not to hit on her man.

### 7.4.4. Butkorori:
- She had a crush on Soketto, asked Meguming for help, and got a meal in return. It didn't help much, but according to Meguming's interpretation, she's fated to end up with Soketto.
- When Kometko gave off an irresistible charm asking for food, she couldn't refuse, so she helped somewhat to keep the poor sisters from starving. Meguming once became a NEET, and for some reason Butkorori, who had also been a NEET, sympathized and acted friendly.

### 7.4.5. Komekko:
- She is Megumin's younger sister.
- Komekko is the lovely little sister Megumin has to provide for. Their parents can't make money (to be precise, only the father can't earn money. No, the mother does earn, but the father blows all of it away.) So Megumin pretty much supported them instead.
- Even if Megumin couldn't properly eat herself, she always gave the breakfast she had set aside for herself to Komekko so that Komekko would be properly fed. And when she went to school she would challenge Yunyun to get lunch, and on days she didn't go to school she wandered around the village.
- If Yunyun won, Megumin would go hungry, so in fact Yunyun could never bring herself to actually defeat Megumin.
- To somehow feed Komekko, she stole discarded bits of bread, pilfered vegetables from the village farm, caught crayfish in the stream, and even ate cicadas.

## 7.5. Other characters:
### 7.5.1. Iris:
- She is a princess of the Kingdom of Berzerg.
- Iris is Megumin's greatest romantic rival and a subordinate under Megumin's band of thieves.
- Because Iris is also a loli character, Megumin claims their positions overlap and actively keeps an eye on Iris; they even fought several times over Kazuma.
- In the past Kazuma reassured Megumin by saying he thinks of Iris as a little sister and a close woman and that he doesn't see her romantically, but Iris likes him and is even thinking about marriage, which is a problem. In fact, Kazuma and Iris have even exchanged rings.
- Although Iris is Megumin's romantic rival, they get along extremely well as friends and remain close unless Kazuma is involved. When Iris joined the thief band she properly treated Megumin as the boss. However, when things don't go her way she will challenge the boss to a direct confrontation and try to match her.
- Darkness simply tries to treat Iris like a princess, but, like Kazuma, sympathizes with Iris for being trapped by her status as a princess and feeling frustrated and lonely, and helps her have various experiences and build memories. She was expected to appear often in the main story as a subordinate of the thief band, but that was fumbled because Megumin lost interest in the thief band.

### 7.5.2. Chris:
- Chris is a bold, cheerful thief girl.
- Megumin formed an affiliated organization to support the Silver-Haired Thieves and ended up recruiting Chris, the boss of the Silver-Haired Thieves, into that affiliate.
- When Megumin found out that Chris was part of the Silver-Haired Thieves, she abruptly bowed deeply and apologized to Chris.
- For some reason, when Kazuma and Chris appeared to be getting close, Megumin, wary that Chris might also be romantically interested in Kazuma, confronted her and even picked a fight with her the way she did with Iris. Chris repeatedly and directly answered that they were strictly friends, which cleared up the misunderstanding.
- Until Megumin dragged Chris into the Silver-Haired Thieves' affiliate, Chris had had no contact with Megumin. Chris herself briefly mentions this (from Chris's point of view, Darkness had been a friend from the start; her contact with Aqua was minimal but, for some reason, they were a close senior–junior pair; and Kazuma, too, had been receiving help in various ways in the role of an assistant.).

### 7.5.3. Cecily:
- A beautiful priestess of the Akushizu Church.
- Before leaving the Crimson Demon Village and coming to Axel, she stopped by Arcanletia; Megumin, who had nowhere to go and nothing to eat, was indebted to her, and she has continued to help her after coming to Axel.
- Cecily wants Megumin to call her "Cecily unnie," but Megumin finds Cecily — a dangerous member of the Akushizu faith and a severe lolicon — extremely burdensome and keeps her at a distance.
- When it was said that Megumin had formed a thieves' guild, she butted in, saying it was full of lolis.

### 7.5.4. Wiz:
- The rumor that a beautiful female mage who uses Explosion magic lived in the town of Axel was the reason Megumin set out for Axel. Of course, in reality it was a completely different person.
- Megumin confirmed Wiz's Explosion magic when they faced the Destroyer, and as time passed and Megumin's magic grew stronger they compared the power of their Explosions together.
- Also, although they hadn't met in person, when Megumin's father Hyoizaburo—who had become fascinated with a magical tool Wiz had made—came all the way to their family home, he was mistaken for a con man and sent away; later, when Megumin actually met Wiz, she felt sorry about that.

## 7.5.5. Vanil:
- A former officer of the Demon King's army; his race is a demon.
- He once went looking to learn the masked thief's identity, but Vanil's ability to see through people didn't work on Kazuma, so it was all for nothing.

### 7.5.6. Dust:
- He is a male adventurer who serves as the vanguard of the Taylor Party.
- He’s a bad friend who has a negative influence on Kazuma. He’s held in very low regard, so Iris and Chris, who have a good eye for people, guessed he might be the rumored Dragon Knight, but Megumin refused to believe it at all. However, later, after accidentally seeing Dust demonstrate refined spear techniques to a novice adventurer, she said that everyone has a secret or two and seemed to come to somewhat believe the possibility that the rumored Dragon Knight might be Dust.

### 7.5.7. Mitsurugi Kyouya:
- From the first impression, Mitsurugi was regarded very poorly, and Kyouya tries to look after Megumin in his own way, but Megumin treats Kyouya coldly.

# 8. Miscellaneous:
- Because Megumin came from the poorest family in the Demon Tribe village, before becoming an adventurer she was more used to going hungry than eating, and at school she often filled her stomach by snatching Yunyun's lunchboxes.
- She finds getting involved in other people's business bothersome, but if Butkorori offers to buy her a meal, Megumin will follow him immediately.
- Yunyun says Megumin would probably follow any man who offered to buy her food. Her father wasted the family fortune making strange things and is a hopeless parent; her mother is, despite the family's poverty, a genuine full-time housewife.
- Megumin was useless even in an ordinary adventurer party, so no one would take her in; when she first met Kazuma she had already been starving for three days. Thinking Kazuma might reject her too, she begged him not to abandon her, saying he'd only have to cover food, baths, and minor expenses and that she would be a porter or do anything.
- Megumin leaves all the money she earns from adventuring to Kazuma and lives off an allowance. (It's said that Kazuma manages Megumin's share of the assets.) It seems she also sends part of that allowance back home. In volume 7, when Darkness's debt is mentioned, she shows a wallet full of coupons and says she recently sent money to her family, so she has no cash left. As the eldest daughter, she really is the pillar of the household.
- Megumin only has 40,000 Eris in total, including the small emergency fund she forced herself to set aside. Because her assets are so small, she was made a special case during the tax incident and had her taxes waived.
- Megumin isn't greedy for money or possessions, so she never asks Kazuma to buy her anything besides clothes and equipment.
- Megumin thinks the proper way to eat fruit is to eat the flesh, roast the seeds, and boil the peels.
- Once, when Kazuma left his wallet at home, Megumin threw her own wallet to him. When Kazuma opened it to pay, it contained various coupons and point cards and only 1,000 Eris.
- Megumin was once severely harried by the Axis Church and became haggard.
- Megumin is a super-robot maniac. She is also portrayed as a robot fan in other spin-off anime.
- Megumin once wore a thief costume together with Iris, and the outfit was so revealing it exposed her navel.
