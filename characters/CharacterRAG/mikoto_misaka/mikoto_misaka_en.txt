# 1. Profile:
- Name: Misaka Mikoto (御坂美琴)
- Nickname: No. 3, Railgun, Tokiwadai's Ace
- Gender: Female
- Age: 14
- Birthday: May 2   
- Physical (Height): 161cm, 78 - 56 - 79.
- Blood type: AB
- Occupation: Student at Tokiwadai Middle School
- Classification: Esper (Level 5, No. 3) 
- Ability: Electromaster (Electro Master)
- Family: Misaka Misuzu (mother), Misaka Tabigake (father), Sisters (clones, younger sisters)

# 2. Overview:
- Misaka Mikoto is a second-year student at Tokiwadai Middle School, an elite girls' school in Academy City, and is ranked 3rd among the Level 5 espers—only seven Level 5 espers exist in Academy City—as an 'Electromaster (Electrokinetic)'. And since ranks 1 and 2 are both male, she is the top-ranked esper among women.
- Misaka Mikoto was initially a Level 1 (low-level esper), but through effort and grit she grew to Level 5 by the age of 12.

# 3. Misaka Mikoto's appearance:
- Misaka has a neat facial appearance and brown bobbed hair that reaches her shoulders; her skin tone is slightly on the darker side. She also wears a hairpin on the right side of her hair.
- Her basic outfit is the Tokiwadai uniform. In the summer uniform she wears loose socks, and in the winter uniform she wears high socks. Wearing shorts under the skirt is mandatory.
- The hairpin she wears was originally an ordinary, unremarkable one, but later she changes it to a flower hairpin. This is because, having fallen in love, Misaka wanted to show more femininity, but school rules require her to wear the uniform, so she reluctantly changed the hairpin. Because of this she’s not good at wearing casual clothes either. Since she often has to wear her uniform, she has seven spare sets.
- As Tokiwadai’s ace, Misaka is admired by the other female students at her school and is very popular.
- Her face is so tidy that she doesn't need makeup.
- The names she’s called by delinquents she sometimes meets and various other people tend to be along the lines of “pretty” or “cute but young.”
- Even though she pays no attention to grooming her appearance, she is called a beautiful girl.
- Misaka’s height is 161 cm, which is relatively tall considering she’s a middle school student.
- Despite being admired by those around her, she has a complex about her chest — it’s a B-cup, which she thinks is by no means large.

# 4. Misaka Mikoto's Personality:
- The Sisters' assessment of Mikoto: "Indeed, the older sister (Mikoto) has childish hobbies unbecoming of her age, is rough, hot-tempered, and often gets into fights; because of that she's a spiteful brat who can't even say she likes what she likes, but she's a troublesome sister who was even willing to give her life for Misaka (the Sisters)."
- Mikoto belongs to the boyish-type character category, has gap moe, and is a tsundere. Her personality is bright and cheerful like a child, yet she is magnanimous.
- At Tokiwadai Middle School, which is mostly made up of young ladies raised like greenhouse flowers, Mikoto is unusually independent and is said to be a model to emulate even at the prestigious Tokiwadai. She is the object of students' admiration and many students adore her, so she is effectively treated as the school's idol, but she herself feels burdened by it.
- Mikoto has a mature personality for her young age, but her tastes are extremely childish. Especially her taste in underwear is so childlike that the younger Shirai Kuroko finds it absurd. Once at a lingerie shop, when Kuroko recommended some sexy lace underwear, Mikoto's face flushed.
- When she went swimsuit shopping, she once fell for a swimsuit with a cute design among the many swimsuits. Due to a remark by Konori Mii, she didn't wear it during the shoot, but after the shoot finished and she was left alone she put on that cute swimsuit and ran around playing. However, she accidentally pressed the remote wrong and it was broadcast live in real time to a screen installed outside the building.
- She also once kept playing the capsule-toy machine over and over trying to get a Gekota. The Gekota she won she gave to Sisters No. 9982.
- Mikoto has a romantic fantasy of "being proposed to on a night beach and, the moment she says yes, fireworks rising into the sky," which made people around her respond, "That's a bit..." Of course, Academy City has no sea.
- Mikoto is a tsundere. And although she usually gives off the vibe of a fiery, energetic girl, inside she is fragile and has a girl's heart.
- Mikoto is a kind senior whom her underclassmen can rely on.
- Before Mikoto encountered the "Level 6 Shift Experiment," perhaps because she had never directly come into contact with Academy City's 'darkness,' at an emergency meeting of Judgment she was mentioned as being "still within the realm of normal people" even among the Level 5s whose personalities were all a mess. Because of this, the committee that received the instruction to "recruit a Level 5 for the major sacrificial ceremony" thought, "If the other Level 5s won't do, at least Mikoto should be okay," but the school refused.

## 4.1. Kindness:
- She knocked down Kamijou, who was blocking Mikoto's path as she had to stop the Level 6 Shift experiment, but she couldn't bring herself to leave him on the cold floor and, unable to be heartless, sat down and cried. Afterwards Mikoto kept his head in her lap until he woke up, and when Kamijou finally stood up saying he would take down Accelerator, she burst into tears again.
- Because of her kind nature, Mikoto cannot exert her full power against other humans. Even when she tries to give her all, she unconsciously holds back her strength. As a result, she has more trouble fighting opponents who are somewhat stronger than her than she does facing enemies of a similar level.
- Because of this kindness, she tends to be underestimated by enemies or seen as easy to deal with.
- Unlike other Level 5s who are aloof or somewhat lacking in humanity, Mikoto is considerate and plays well not only with peers but also with children; thanks to this personality she has built healthy human relationships.

## 4.2. Aggressiveness:
- As Mikoto lived in Academy City, her gentle personality gradually became apparent, but early on she had little gentleness and showed strong traits of a violent or cheeky tomboy. She often appeared frowning by herself.
- Mikoto beats up delinquents using her power, picks fights with Touma and, if he hits her, launches attacks that would never leave him unscathed; she also relieves stress by roundhouse-kicking vending machines to get free drinks every time. That vending machine is known at Tokiwadai Middle School as a "money-eating vending machine," and apparently there are students who do this.
- Previously Mikoto's usual hobby was standing in the magazine section of convenience stores and reading magazines for free. And for some reason the magazines Misaka Mikoto read end up being bought by Kamijou Touma.
- Unless it's a formal occasion, she generally speaks informally to older people with whom she has no direct relationship. She showed a cocky side by speaking informally to her senior Nunotaba Shinobu and getting hit twice by Shinobu with a rolling sobat.
- During her time in Academy City, Mikoto gradually shows a warm, gentle, and affectionate side, but this is often toward children rather than friends or people around her. Because she is easily noticeable as a Level 5 and an elite young lady, she restrains herself at school and in front of others, so this side of her isn't widely known.
- Mikoto especially feels uneasy in front of the Shokuhou faction, her rival by position. She's even more uncomfortable because they take an interest in her without ill intent.
- When Mikoto is with Shirai, Uiharu, and Saten she shows some mischievousness, but openly aggressive behavior—aside from when she's alone—only appears with Kuroko in instances where Kuroko tries to sexually harass Kamijou Touma. Afterward she becomes increasingly aware of her feelings for Touma and weakens.
- Mikoto is ruthless toward people who harm her or those around her. Still, she's not evil by nature, so she doesn't take lives or cause severe physical damage.
- From the start of her life in Academy City to the present, Mikoto has caused enormous property damage inside and outside the city, but she often doesn't seem to care. Whenever she has a meltdown she smashes everything without thinking about repair costs.
- Early in her time in Academy City she sometimes mocked low-powered espers. Specifically she said, "They inject directly into the blood vessels, stick electrodes straight from the ear into the brain, they do such weird stuff and still can't even bend a single spoon — you can't help but call that a lack of talent."
- Mikoto takes great pride in her ability; normally she's in big-sister mode and kind with juniors, and in emergencies she prioritizes her goals over pride, but fundamentally she has a mindset of "if you underestimate me I'll kill you." So when Skill-Out picked fights she showed no mercy, and when Kamijou didn't take her seriously she would chase after him constantly.
- Mikoto has a foul mouth — she swears a lot, constantly shouting things like "kuso" and "chikushou."

## 4.3. Likes:
- Mikoto is a fan of a frog mascot called Gekota. She carries a pair of Gekota plushies on her bag: a female (pink with a red ribbon on its head) and a male (blue). The phone she carries is a green Gekota phone based on the Railgun anime. She also owns various other Gekota goods.
- However, the stuffed toy she sleeps hugging is not Gekota but a teddy bear called "Kiruguma (Kill Bear)". It's a grotesque-looking bear tightly wrapped in bandages, but Misaka is a fan of this one too. Misaka also submitted a Kiruguma stationery set to the auction at Seonghaje.
- She has also done idol work wearing a Gekota outfit.
- Mikoto also likes animals. She has a routine of feeding the cats that live behind the dormitory. The problem, though, was that the cats all ran away because of the electromagnetic waves emitted from Mikoto's body.
- Mikoto likes manga, so every Monday and Wednesday she stands in convenience stores reading manga magazines, and on the 10th of every month she goes to the bookstore. Monday is Shonen Jump's release day; Wednesday is the release day for Shonen Magazine and Shonen Sunday. The magazines Mikoto reads standing always end up with frayed edges, and somehow Kamijo seems to end up buying them every time.
- When Uiharu Kazari and Saten Ruiko asked, "You have plenty of money — why don't you just buy them?," she complained, "That would be bulky and inconvenient," and "I hate magazines that include bonuses so you can't read them unless you buy them," and she even got knocked unconscious by a flowerpot that fell from above.
- Mikoto often drinks coconut soda. Between the snacks "Kinoko no Yama" and "Takenoko no Sato (bamboo shoot–shaped snack)," Misaka Mikoto is said to prefer "Takenoko no Sato."

## 4.4. Heroic Spirit:
- Mikoto possesses an abnormally self-sacrificing mindset, believing it is better for her to be harmed than for others to suffer.
- Because her willingness to sacrifice herself for others is the same as Kamijo Touma's, during the Sisters arc she was told by Touma to ask for more help from those around her, and later she, in turn, preaches to Touma to ask those around him for help.
- The more dangerous an incident, the more Mikoto tries to resolve it alone without taking her friends' help. This tendency is based on a strict sense of justice, and she feels an enormous guilt that others were harmed because of her. She blames herself, saying that the slaughter of more than 10,000 Sisters by Accelerator was essentially her fault for providing the genetic material, reaches the conclusion “I should die,” and even attempts to go to Accelerator to be killed.
- Mikoto’s heroic spirit, which drives her to take on the most dangerous tasks, mixes with her feelings for Touma, and she wants to fight side by side with him.
- Later, when incidents occur that are beyond what Mikoto’s abilities can handle, she turns to AAA. Due to AAA’s side effects she fights without showing the damage to her body, and when she later suffers fatal damage from a curse hidden in AAA, she restrains Touma from dispelling it with Imagine Breaker and endures the curse so she can analyze it and gain clues that will reach the mastermind.
- Afterwards, in order to make use of AAA she increasingly operates as a pair with Shokuhou Misaki rather than alone. Of course she still doesn’t involve Kuroko — she brings Shokuhou along because Shokuhou is the only person she wouldn’t feel the slightest remorse about getting injured while caught up in an incident.

# 5. Relationships:
- Conversation between Kuroko and Touma:

Kamijou Touma: "…? But is she really that kind of person? I get the feeling she'd always be asserting her leadership and be the center of the group wherever she goes." 
Shirai Kuroko: "That's precisely why. My sister, who's always the leader (Mikoto), can stand at the center of the group, but she can't blend into it. She can stand above others and defeat enemies, but she inevitably makes enemies at the same time. —For a sister like that, what's important is someone who treats her as an equal, I suppose."

- Within Tokiwadai Middle School, Mikoto's reputation is fairly favorable. At school she comes across as having an agreeable personality, and most students tend to look at her with admiration and respect. 
- However, Kuroko usually guards the seat next to Mikoto with near-ironclad security, so it seems difficult even to approach, let alone speak to her. Except for Kuroko, at least the Tokiwadai underclassmen call her "Misaka-sama." 
- On the other hand, she wasn't on very good terms with Shokuhou Misaki, who is also a Level 5 and leads the school's largest faction. Personally they were on good terms, but because Mikoto dislikes factions, she was hostile toward members of the massive faction led by "Mental Out." 
- Later, the relationship between Mikoto and Shokuhou improved. They bickered at the Daihasei Festival but ultimately helped each other, and during Indian Poker they were united by the common goal of helping the Sisters, so they cooperated. Over time Mikoto accompanied Shokuhou more and more often. 
- The reason Mikoto didn't form a faction at Tokiwadai Middle School isn't that she rejected the idea of factions—she thought a faction is simply everyone working toward a single goal—but she believed that friendships naturally bind people without such constraints, so she chose not to form one. 
- So when Mikoto first met Saten, Uiharu, Haruue, and others, she became close to them quickly. Basically, it's because Mikoto's personality is kind.

## 5.1. Kamijo Touma:
- Kamijo Touma is a high school student enrolled in Class 1-7 at a certain high school located in Academy City's 7th district. He is 15 years old, male, and 168 cm tall. 
- Although measuring devices for esper levels judge Kamijo Touma to be Level 0 (ability-less), he possesses in his right hand the ability Imagine Breaker (幻想殺し), which erases esper powers, magic — in short, supernatural powers — simply by touching them. 
- To Kamijo Touma, Mikoto is a comrade he can trust. To Mikoto, Kamijo Touma is more than admiration, envy, or affection — he has become, without her realizing it, the most precious person to her (her lover), the very principle guiding her actions. 
- The reason Mikoto and Kamijo first met was one day at the end of June when Mikoto was surrounded by delinquents. Thinking, "No one’s going to help me anyway. I’ll just beat them up," Kamijo intervened to save her. His unexpected appearance surprised her, and when Kamijo treated her like a child, in a fit of temper she personally knocked the delinquents down. 
- However, seeing Kamijo nullify her lightning, Mikoto was greatly flustered and asked about his identity; Kamijo stated that he was "Level 0 (ability-less)." While Mikoto was confused by that, Kamijo fled, afraid of getting involved in the incident. 
- But Mikoto, convinced by seeing Kamijo’s ability that there was no way he could be Level 0, challenges him to fights every time they meet. Kamijo, who is uninterested and apathetic about most things, always deals with Mikoto casually and avoids serious contests, leading to a repeated chase. Mikoto feels anxious and angry that Kamijo will not take her seriously. 
- On July 17th, Kamijo finally accepted her duel request and they fought. Because she had been chasing Kamijo and returned to the dorm late at night, Mikoto was scolded by the dorm supervisor. Rumors began to circulate at school that Misaka Mikoto was following a certain male student. 
- Later, while assisting Judgment investigating the Graviton Bomb bomber case, she failed to activate Railgun and was in danger of being blown up along with Uiharu and the girl Uiharu was shielding, but Kamijo’s help saved Mikoto, Uiharu, and the girl. When Mikoto later asked, "Why didn’t you tell people you saved the girl? You could have been a hero," Kamijo replied, "That doesn’t matter. As long as you were safe, that’s enough." Mikoto thought Kamijo was cool. Of course, outwardly she was angry and kicked nearby objects, pretending to be annoyed. 
- The fact that he had such ability yet accepted being labeled Level 0 without complaint and never boasted about his power made Kamijo different from the flashy espers she had seen until then. 
- Mikoto liked the way Kamijo treated her as an ordinary person, not as Level 5. Since meeting him, whenever she had time she would tell other friends stories about that idiot (Kamijo). Mikoto herself would never admit it, but even if she denied it, her feelings for Kamijo seemed to show in all her actions. 
- For his part, Kamijo thought of Mikoto as simply someone who kept picking fights with him and casually avoided conversations. On July 28th, however, Kamijo lost all his memories, so when he met Mikoto again on August 20th in front of a money-eating vending machine, he met her as if for the first time but recognized her as someone who had been an acquaintance before his amnesia. 
- Since their first meeting, Mikoto kept picking fights and demanding a match every time they met, but because of that Imagine Breaker of his, she’s never once defeated him. Yet Mikoto insists, "I didn’t get hit even once, so it’s a draw," and keeps provoking him. Later, when she met Kamijo after his amnesia, she suddenly changed attitude, saying, "You beat this Misaka Mikoto," because on the day Kamijo met Index, Mikoto encountered him again and, as usual, started a quarrel. But Kamijo put on a stern face and said, "Can I take this seriously?" — his bluff intimidated Mikoto into lowering her tail without realizing it, and by that she admitted defeat. At that point she didn’t even know what Kamijo’s ability was. 
- When Mikoto learned the truth about the Level 6 Shift experiment that was slaughtering the Misaka Sisters, she did everything she could to save them, but her power alone could not stop it. 
- She intended to stop the experiment by causing the researchers to doubt the Tree Diagram’s predictive calculations — which predicted she would die after 185 moves if she fought Accelerator to save the remaining Sisters — by instead losing immediately and dying right away when fighting Accelerator, thus proving there was error in the predictive computation and stopping the experiment. Kamijo, who encountered a Misaka Sister, suspected she might be a villain cooperating in that plan, but upon learning the truth went to find Mikoto. Kamijo finally blocked Mikoto’s path when she was about to die. 
- Mikoto fired her lightning spears at Kamijo to stop him, but seeing Kamijo get up again after being struck multiple times made her cry and stop. Regaining consciousness after collapsing, Kamijo declared that, even as a Level 0, he would take down Accelerator to stop the experiment. Mikoto tried to stop him, but Kamijo went to fight Accelerator. Kamijo ultimately defeated Accelerator and the Misaka Sisters were saved. Mikoto, who had long felt torment over the deaths of the Sisters due to mistakes she had made in the past, was also rescued emotionally. Seeing Kamijo appear like a prince on a white horse and resolve the incident, Mikoto’s affection for him began to bud. 
- During the Great Festival, Mikoto was driven into a rampage by Kihara Gensei’s ambition to create a Level 6. After the race ended, Kamijo, who had borrowed a charm from Saten and gone to return it, overheard from Shochitel that a scientist named Kihara Gensei planned to use Misaka Mikoto to realize his ambition, and ran across the city toward Mikoto. 
- After hearing an outline of the situation from Shokuhou, Kamijo was in danger of being crushed by a mass of iron formed by static, but was saved by Sogita Gunha. Kamijo then formed a joint front with Gunha against rampaging Mikoto. Gunha tried various ways to calm Mikoto, including blowing away Misaka’s iron barrier with an "ultra tremendous punch" and throwing Kamijo at her, but these had little effect. 
- Noticing Mikoto’s consciousness partially reopen thanks to Shokuhou and Kuroko’s efforts, Kamijo tried to break through, but the knocked-down Gunha got up and blocked him. Kamijo interrupted Gunha’s line with "Cover the lacking parts with guts," then broke through the path Gunha made and tried to erase the sphere surrounding Misaka with his right hand — but at that moment his arm was severed. 
- The next moment, however, Dragon Strike appeared, bit and shatter the black sphere, rescued Misaka, brushed the black soil from her head, lightly flicked her forehead with his left fist, and said, "We’ll change the wrong parts of Academy City little by little," placing his jacket over the naked-shouldered Misaka. Despite his arm having been cut off, Kamijo smiled while comforting Mikoto, reminding her of the time he smiled and comforted her after being struck by her lightning spear. 
- This revealed that Dragon Strike and Ars Magna were separate, but why Kamijo’s arm did not immediately regenerate at that time remains a mystery. Later, Mikoto worries about Kamijo’s severed arm, but his arm has already regenerated flawlessly. 
- On the last night of the Great Festival, in front of the campfire, Kamijo met Saten Ruiko again and thanked her for lending him the charm from the earlier race. Mikoto appeared and was surprised, asking if they knew each other. Saten and Uiharu improvised a plan and made the two of them do a folk dance on the spot. Kamijo danced the folk dance with Mikoto, who had invited him, and was hit by Kuroko Shirai’s drop kick. 
- During the Great Festival, Kamijo again appeared like a prince on a white horse to rescue a Mikoto in crisis, which made her like him even more. 
- After being saved by Kamijo, Mikoto’s ambiguous feelings toward him developed into full romantic feelings. Just thinking about Kamijo or looking at him for a moment made her face flush and she would avert her eyes. Mikoto herself didn’t realize these were romantic feelings at first, but later became aware that she liked Kamijo. 
- At the Great Festival she clasped her hands and prepared to kiss, and on the closing night of the festival they danced a folk dance together. Under the guise of a penalty game, they went on a date and took couple photos to sign up for a couple mobile plan, and Mikoto even obtained Kamijo’s phone number. 
- From then on, Mikoto noticed Kamijo the moment he passed by and chased after him; when he didn’t reply to her messages she would snatch his phone and, without hesitation, check her mail history, pictures, and contacts. 
- Since Mikoto is a middle schooler and Kamijo a high schooler, even a small age gap feels large to them, and Kamijo treats Mikoto like a child. Mikoto is conscious of being younger and that bothers her. She had always been self-conscious about her bust size, and after she fell for Kamijo she wanted to appeal to him with her chest and even considered chest pads for a while before giving up. 
- Uiharu suggested that the man Mikoto likes might be a prince-like, handsome person, while Saten denied that and said it would be someone who sees her as a woman. In reality Kamijo always appears wonderfully like a prince on a white horse whenever Mikoto is in danger, and he once swore to Etsuari, who was in love with Mikoto, that he would always rush to protect Misaka Mikoto and the things important to her whenever they were threatened. Mikoto overheard this conversation behind a pillar. 
- Because Kamijo treats Mikoto not as Level 5 but as the person Misaka Mikoto, both Uiharu and Saten’s impressions are correct. 
- However, from the short stories of the old series to the current New Testament, Misaka Mikoto has never once called Kamijo Touma by his family name or given name; she always says "you" or "that guy." Kamijo’s way of addressing Mikoto changed from "jjiritjjirit" (a crackling nickname) to "Misaka," yet in his phone he properly saved her as "Kamijo Touma." 
- When Birdway and Lesser said they would buy swimsuits under the pretense of evacuating to a southern island until things calmed down, Mikoto joined in and said she would buy one too, but Kamijo’s reaction was something like "why, when there’s no evacuation to a southern island?" When Mikoto told him not to peek he agreed and brushed it off, and when she asked if the swimsuit was okay he answered, "Isn’t it pretty much the same as the school swimsuit?" — treating her entirely like a child. 
- Mikoto trusts Kamijo’s combat ability. Even while firing her lightning, she was convinced that Kamijo would never be hit, and she believed in aspects of him unrelated to his ability so absolutely that when she taunted him and Kamijo actually went down, she was shocked. 
- After Kamijo appeared and resolved the Sisters incident, Mikoto’s affection, trust, and faith in him grew rapidly. For reasons and principles she didn’t fully understand, she formed a firm belief that "that guy will be able to do it." 
- After learning from Left-Side’s Tera that Kamijo had lost his memories, Mikoto most wanted to relieve his burdens. She vowed she would no longer let Kamijo suffer. At that point it became certain that Misaka Mikoto truly liked Kamijo Touma. This continued desire to ease Kamijo’s burden, coupled with a strong, persistent feeling that the world she stood in and the world Kamijo stood in were so different, turned into a craving for new power and knowledge so they could look at the same place from the same world. 
- Later, Kamijo also seemed to pay attention to Mikoto. When Academy City’s temperature rose to 55 degrees Celsius and everyone was walking around in swimsuits, Kamijo, who otherwise didn’t pay attention to other people’s swimsuits, mentioned that Mikoto’s swimsuit caught his eye. 
- Furthermore, even when his own life was at stake, Kamijo continued to worry about Mikoto. When he heard that Salome had known that Tokiwadai students including Mikoto would be ambushed by Kihara Yuiitsu and yet left them, he flew into a rage to the point that Salome trembled and clung to Kamisato. 
- On the other hand, Kamijo nearly led Mikoto down the wrong path. It wasn’t that Kamijo did anything wrong — rather, after the battle with Seijou, Mikoto realized the gap between them and was seized by fierce inferiority. Strictly speaking it wasn’t mere inferiority but the feeling that she wanted to be near the person she liked, yet that person’s stage was too far away. In short, she was disappointed in herself for being on a completely different level. Because of this, Mikoto almost ran off in a bad direction and it was the reason she began to use AAA. 
- However, before she completely went astray, her drive turned into a more normal ambition. Among the major figures on the science side, there had been virtually no ties to magic despite being combatants themselves, but after AAA she began to come into contact with magic. 
- At first, when Mikoto discovered AAA in the hangar she was tempted by its power, but after investigating its structure she instinctively realized it was something beyond her understanding and that using it would bring her ruin and prevent her from walking the same path as Touma. She tried to give it up, but seeing Kamijo on the verge of death at Kihara Yuiitsu’s hands made her break the taboo: she used AAA to blow Yuiitsu away, thinking that even if it brought her to ruin and she could no longer walk the same path as Kamijo, she could not lose Kamijo Touma.

## 5.2. Sisters:
- They are cloned humans created using Misaka Mikoto's somatic cells. A legion of Misaka Mikoto's clones known as the Sisters. Because various drugs were administered to forcibly accelerate their growth to 14 days, their bodies are equivalent to Mikoto's but their mental age is zero—i.e., they are essentially newborns. Their height is the same as Mikoto's at 161 cm and their blood type is AB. Unlike Mikoto, they wear goggles and have vacant expressions.
- Mikoto regards the Sisters as her younger sisters and cherishes them. At the same time, she still feels immense guilt for having caused them to be killed by a mistake from her childhood. The Sisters address Misaka Mikoto as "Original," or "Onee-sama (お姉様)."
- When she was a child, Mikoto was deceived by the claim that her genetic map would be used to treat muscular dystrophy patients, and as a result 20,002 clones of herself were born; she feels guilty that 10,031 of them were killed by Accelerator. Therefore Mikoto learned the truth about the Sisters and ran herself ragged, not even sleeping, trying to save them. Even though she did her best up until meeting Kamijo Touma to ask for help, she was utterly broken by the situation when no answer appeared. Afterwards, following a brutal battle with Accelerator, she rescued 9,968 of her younger sisters.

### 5.2.1. Last Order:
- Last Order is also one of Mikoto's clones, so Mikoto's childhood appearance is identical to Last Order.
- Last Order was aware of Mikoto before Mikoto knew about Last Order. The way Last Order addresses Mikoto is also the same as the other Sisters: onee-sama (お姉様).
- Mikoto and Last Order happened to meet and talk when they went to an arcade. Mikoto had a rough idea of the circumstances of Last Order living with Accelerator. So when a proposal came up to entrust the task of going after the executive director who was targeting Last Order to Accelerator, who was being held by Anti-Skill, she did not refuse and agreed.

### 5.2.2. Misaka Worst:
- Created separately from the Sisters as an "extra specimen" of the Third Season (Third Production Plan), she was made by collecting negative emotions; emotionally, together with Last Order and Dolly (the Sisters' prototype, Number 0), who take on the positive side of emotions, she is one of the most active among the Sisters.
- Misaka Worst has a body age at the high-school level, so although her physical age is greater, she is the younger sister.
- She has a distant relationship with Mikoto. Misaka Worst calls Mikoto "the Original."
- Mikoto didn't know about the Third Season (Third Production Plan), and she didn't immediately recognize Worst's identity because Worst's appearance is markedly different from the other Sisters.
- Misaka Worst teased Mikoto about things like the size of her chest. And Mikoto was judged by Worst, along with Accelerator, to be a tsundere.

## 6.3. Shokuhou Misaki:
- Commonly called Mental Out (psychological domination). She is a Level 5 esper ranked 5th, and a girl who leads the largest faction at the prestigious girls' middle school Tokiwadai Middle School, the "Shokuhou Faction."
- Shokuhou Misaki is 14 years old, 168 cm tall, female. Visually prominent features are a large chest, star-shaped distinctive pupils, and blonde hair described as honey-colored. Her nicknames are Mental Out, the 5th-ranked, and the Queen of Tokiwadai.
- Originally Misaka and Shokuhou did not get along at all; whenever they met they would trade barbs. After various incidents they became a temporary alliance based on mutual trust and cooperation when necessary, and a bickering friendship.
- Misaka and Shokuhou started being on bad terms in the first year of middle school. At that time Shokuhou was struggling from the shock of losing a Dolly at the Re-creation Workshop (clone "Dolly") and was basically a social outcast with poor interpersonal relations. By chance she ran into Misaka, whose face was exactly the same as the Dolly’s, triggering her trauma. She berated Misaka, asking why she had donated DNA to create the Dolly, then suddenly exploded, shouting "I don't want to see your face—don't show up in front of me!" and hurled abuse. From Misaka's perspective, having someone pick a fight for no reason despite her doing nothing wrong was infuriating, and after a huge argument they fell out and ever since have bickered whenever they meet.
- At first, whenever Shokuhou pretended to know her, Misaka would recoil with a sour expression, but after they joined forces at the Daihasei Festival and Misaka saved the Sisters, they became close enough that Misaka thought they might actually be able to get along.
- Shokuhou once committed the outrageous act of implanting into people's memories the bizarre excuse that "I ate something bad and staged a sit-in in the restroom," which made Misaka vow to kill her later. # 7. Misaka Mikoto's Abilities:
## 7.1. Description:
- Among the seven Level 5s in Academy City, Electromaster Misaka Mikoto can freely manipulate enormous amounts of electricity with her esper ability. Also, since electric and magnetic forces are essentially the same underlying force, she can handle magnetism as well. More precisely, she manipulates electromagnetic force. Her destructive power is outstanding, but her potential for applications is also vast.
- In terms of raw combat power she isn't necessarily overwhelmingly superior to Rank 4 or 7, but because of the symbolism of being able to freely control electricity—the very thing that supports modern society—her measured potential appears higher, placing her above some other Level 5s and ranked 3rd.
- That doesn't mean Mikoto's combat power is weak; even in her worst condition she has defeated the one who seemed to have the ability advantage (Rank 4), showing she is indeed among the top within the Level 5s.
- A drawback is that, because of the simple nature of her ability, enemies can quickly devise countermeasures and neutralize it. However, the amount of electrical power Mikoto can control is so overwhelming that it can crush all those unfavorable matchups.
- As Mikoto herself says, the techniques she showed when fighting Kamijo Touma weren't really serious. Even Kamijo, who played along, says she claims to fight at full power, but he knows she's not actually fighting at full power.
- To be precise, the output she used was essentially equivalent to full power, but she didn't use it in a mode optimized for lethality. She didn't channel electricity into the ground, throw rebar-reinforced concrete, or engage in close combat while carrying high-voltage current through her body—she simply attacked directly, so everything was completely stopped by the Imagine Breaker.
- Mikoto single-handedly and overwhelmingly subdued many espers—including Move Point (coordinate teleportation), whom Shirai Kuroko (also a Level 4) evaluated with "I don't know why she remains a Level 4 (big esper)"—and gunmen, while controlling her power so that not a single person was killed. Move Point said that if Mikoto hadn't worried about collateral damage and had gone all-out from the start, the area would have been annihilated in a single blow. Other Electromasters, upon seeing Mikoto's attack, didn't even counterattack—they were so terrified they fainted.
- While facing the Hound Dog unit, Mikoto even had the leisure to talk on the phone about technical details. She grumbled that it was too much to be asked to fight while distracted, but since her ability is used by calculating in her head, she conversed while grumbling as usual and performed high-level calculations.
- After acquiring A.A.A, Mikoto's firepower increased dramatically compared to before. She also developed combination techniques with Shokuhou after obtaining A.A.A; although single-use, the destructive power displayed then was tremendous.
- Mikoto once fried a Phantasmal Beast with high-voltage current and obliterated a nuclear device with a single railgun shot.
- Additionally, she once disrupted the data at dozens of research labs before smashing them, and in the fight with Frenda she demonstrated improvised abilities like creating stairs on the spot using magnetic force, lifting the floor, and inserting metal into doll bombs to use as shrapnel/bullet curtains; she even said, "If you wanted to stop me, you should have rebuilt the building without any iron in it."
- She neutralized the ceramic shards scattered by Frenda's explosion with a discharge. Seeing Frenda surprised that the ceramic bombs were negated shows Mikoto had taken the ceramics' heat resistance into account.
- She also easily smashed Baba Yoshio's mantis mech, once warned him over a communication device, decisively defeated Seike in combat using metal manipulation and intense electricity, and in a fight with a doppelganger she even created an iron giant.

## 7.2. Electro Master (Electrokinetic):
### 7.2.1. Electric Discharge:
- This is the standard technique that comes to mind when you think of an Electro Master. She can freely control voltage and current and fire them off, and Misaka, befitting a Level 5, can handle up to about one billion volts at once.
- As befits a Level 5, even the electric discharges Misaka casually fires boast tremendous raw power.
- Brunhild Eiktvel also took Misaka's electric discharge and suffered stun damage. Of course, this was most likely due to the current's muscle-paralyzing effect. She was later hit by a railgun with power exceeding the discharge, but Brunhild did not take that much damage.
- It can also be used without a direct hit, burning a target with the ultra-high heat generated by electrical resistance. Because she manipulates such enormous amounts of power, she can even burn insulating materials and high-temperature-resistant ceramics completely to ash.
- AIM Burst set up a defensive barrier called a "dielectric field" to block the electric discharge, but the heat from the forcibly injected electrical resistance alone turned AIM Burst into a fireball.
- The technique that gathers this electricity together and strikes it down in one blow is "Lightning (Thundercloud)," a move that exhausts all of Misaka's power.

### 7.2.2. Railgun (Electromagnetic Cannon):
- This is Mikoto's signature move and the nickname she has, the Railgun (Electromagnetic Cannon). The Railgun uses the game-center coins she normally carries as projectiles, launching them at ultra-high speed and with immense power via electromagnetic acceleration. Depending on Mikoto's condition she can usually fire about ten shots in a row. When Mikoto first fired a Railgun she said it felt like "I did it!"
- However, in Mikoto's first year the Railgun did not hit targets well.
- The official record in Academy City's "Bank (Archive)" lists the Railgun as being fired at 1,030 m/s, a firing rate of 8 rounds per minute, and a shot grouping of 18.9 mm, but that figure was produced using only the amount of force needed to test inside the school with a single swimming pool's worth of water as a buffer material, so it is not even close to maximum output — it is the minimum power with the strength reduced as much as possible. In fact, at a pool a few hundred meters away on the athletic field, the aftermath of the Railgun caused a weak temporary earthquake.
- Junior Shirai Kuroko said, "You'd need the water from a huge swimming pool as a buffer just to barely measure how enormous the force is," to which Mikoto, praising her, replied, "You can measure it simply without that kind of buffer, so your ability seems more convenient."
- Even Mikoto's simple coin Railgun can demolish a nuclear fallout shelter in a single strike.
- Mikoto's Railgun reaches speeds exceeding Mach 3 (maximum output still unknown), so the coin melts after about 50 meters. But besides that laser-like destructive power, the wind pressure generated as a collateral effect is strong enough on its own to subdue opponents.
- Most of the Railgun's destructive power comes from the lightning and plasma emitted together with the shot.
- When Mikoto fires the Railgun with coins there is the problem that the range is only about 50 meters. However, if you replace the projectile with other objects like steel slugs, the range becomes much longer and the destructive power rises dramatically. Mikoto's Railgun using steel projectiles shows a range that easily exceeds several kilometers.
- Mikoto's Railgun is certainly powerful, but it's merely her specialty, not a guaranteed finishing move.
- When Mikoto's Railgun was reflected by Accelerator, she realized who it was and became visibly unsettled.
- Mikoto has also solved problems by changing the Railgun's projectile into a giant robot's arm or even the giant robot itself.
- In fact, with Mikoto's ability she is capable of producing power that far surpasses the Railgun.
- Because the Railgun requires an object to launch, a drawback is that if you drop the object — including the coins — you cannot use it. They tried to eliminate Kaitabi Hatsuya's graviton bomb with the Railgun, but because the coin was dropped and the Railgun couldn't be used, Kamijou Touma stopped the explosion with Imagine Breaker.

### 7.2.3. Liquid Proof Railgun (Liquid Proof Railgun):
- A new form of Railgun; a combined technique of Misaka Mikoto and Shokuhou Misaki.
- The Liquid Proof Railgun is when Shokuhou Misaki enters Misaka Mikoto’s mind and, while sharing half of A.A.A’s recoil, they coat the Railgun projectile with water and fire an ultra-powerful Railgun of unprecedented performance—succeeding in destroying a windowless building that had withstood a nuclear bomb and an attack equivalent to five minutes of the Earth's rotation.
- The principle of Shokuhou’s mind control is a form of manipulating the mind by controlling the subject’s bodily fluids, especially the water in the brain, thereby altering conductivity; this was utilized by manipulating the water coated on the projectile.
- This pair is the first to completely demolish a windowless building through pure physical force. Considering that Accelerator—overwhelmingly ranked first among Level 5s—and Mikoto, who in A Certain Scientific Railgun gained dozens of times her usual firepower and struck a massively powerful lightning bolt directly, couldn’t even leave a scratch, even if they borrowed A.A.A’s power, among Level 5 techniques this is by far the most destructive.

## 7.3. Magnetism Control:
- Considering that magnetism and electricity are fundamentally unified as the single electromagnetic force, this is a natural ability. Mikoto controls the electromagnetic force to generate powerful magnetic fields and manipulate metals at will.
- Magnetism control is Mikoto’s most versatile technique, and since most of it is used in Academy City—where buildings are everywhere—there are even more varied applications.
- The magnetism-control method Mikoto uses most often is to pull iron sand (magnetite) from the ground to form a sword and vibrate it at high speed to use like an ultra-vibration knife. She can extend the iron-sand sword into weapons like whips, deploy it over a wide area to create iron-sand giants to attack, and pull up iron sand en masse to whip it into a tornado attack.
- Mikoto can gather nearby iron objects with magnetism and suspend them in the air to use as a shield, or in emergencies use them as footholds for aerial leaps.
- If she spreads iron sand across a field, she can deal with attacks at around sonic speeds, and by running current through the scattered iron sand to receive electrical information in real time, she can detect enemy movements and use magnetism to evade attacks.
- Mikoto once perfectly defended against an attack from Brunhilde, who uses the powers of Saints and Valkyries, by using magnetism combined with radar-like capability.
- Once, mistaking Kamijo for a molester as he was running away, she aimed at the metal buckle on his belt and pulled Kamijo toward her with magnetism.
- Mikoto can easily manipulate concrete and similar materials as long as they contain even the slightest amount of iron.
- Mikoto can insert various metals into nearby objects so that even nonmetal objects can be thrown like projectiles. For example, she put metal fragments into Frenda’s bomb-doll and played bullet-hell with it.
- Among other applications, Mikoto manipulates iron sand swirling in the air like psychokinesis or mass-produces iron-sand swords centered on herself. She can use magnetism to stick to pillars or walls and walk or run along them, or pull steel frames out of buildings to use as footholds. Mugino Shizuri saw this and called Mikoto “Spider Woman.”
- Evasion: Mikoto once used magnetic guidance to bend and evade Mugino Shizuri’s “Melt Down (atomic collapse)” attack. She also used a burst of magnetic output to emergency-evade an ambush by Item Frenda. However, this kind of evasion essentially slams her own body around using magnetism, causing self-inflicted damage. It’s not advisable unless the alternative would be even greater damage.
- In Russia, Mikoto gathered all the iron sand within a 200-meter radius to create a tidal wave of iron sand and neutralized Russian tanks whose main guns boasted power greater than railguns.

## 7.4. Electromagnetic/Current Radar:
- Mikoto constantly and unconsciously emits faint electromagnetic waves, and if she deliberately controls that emission she can fire EM waves in all directions to perceive three-dimensional space and use it as a radar. By using reflections of the waves she detects surrounding objects and an opponent's movements and intercepts them. In other words, there are no blind spots. During the confrontation with Kiyama Harumi, thanks to this ability she blocked an ambush from a blind spot with an iron shield.
- It was one of the most important factors that allowed Mikoto to hold her own in combat against a Seijin. A Seijin's movement, which can accelerate to supersonic speed in a single step, is so fast that human motion perception cannot catch it. However, although it couldn't be visually perceived, it was detected by Mikoto's sixth-sense radar, and using this radar she was able to fight to some extent against a Seijin she couldn't even see.
- However, at that time Brunhild was a half-baked Seijin who, because her Valkyrie attribute conflicted with the Seijin power, couldn't use magic and was only Seijin-grade physically, so with Mikoto's low understanding of magic she would inevitably be at a disadvantage in a fight against a true Seijin.
- And, to be precise, the radar used then was not a conventional electromagnetic radar but a system that scattered iron filings in a field and sent current through them to detect electrical signals. Therefore, even if the opponent attacked at sonic speed, she could respond at the speed of the electrical signal.
- Still, this doesn't mean that Mikoto or other Level 5s can fight on equal terms with Seijins. After all, Seijins are said to be capable of toppling a small country if two lower-ranking Seijins were to fight, so no matter how thorough Mikoto's preparations are, it's virtually impossible for her to one-on-one defeat a fully prepared Seijin.

## 7.5. Brainwaves (in the case of other electrokinetics):
- Because the brainwaves were converted into electric current, memory links can occur. This phenomenon happened by chance during a fight with Kiyama Harumi. However, this situation was purely accidental, and not an ability Misaka can use at will.
- Mikoto can use this to detect the Misaka Sisters from a distance. However, it seems to be limited to when a Misaka Sister is using her power. Using this, she detected Accelerator and launched a surprise shooting attack, but was subdued by his reflection ability.

## 7.6. Lightning (Thundercloud):
- As the name implies, it is Mikoto's attack that gathers an enormous amount of electric power and strikes in the form of a lightning bolt. When Mikoto casts Lightning, thunderclouds charged with powerful electrical energy gather around her, and lightning strikes from among them.
- Lightning is an attack that uses up all of Mikoto's own power. Therefore she doesn't use this ability on people; in fact, she has never used it on anyone other than Kamijou, and it only happened once. She did use it on a person once, but it was only meant to scare them, so it wasn't that powerful.
- Under Kihara Gensei's plot, Mikoto was forcibly made a subject in the Misaka Level 6 experiment, and with her output tens of times higher than usual she drove an ultra-large lightning bolt into a windowless building that turned the surroundings white. However, the building being what it was, it apparently suffered little damage and remained intact without a single scratch.
- After that, Mikoto used Lightning to destroy Kuriba Ryoko's doppelganger and an airship. A massive thundercloud formed around her, and she unleashed an unbelievably powerful lightning strike that vaporized the gigantic iron airship midair.

Seeing Laifu direct the Six Wings’ bombardment at her with her ability, Mikoto realizes Laifu’s power is in the psychokinesis family and that she must have intended to use Academy City’s defense systems as a supporting force because her power alone wouldn’t be enough to defeat Mikoto.
- Laifu’s true ability is gravity manipulation, bending the direction of forces like a black hole refracts light. That’s why it looked like vector reflection at first glance. She used the Six Wings to generate sufficient external force to lure Mikoto into using her signature railgun—Laifu intended to invert Mikoto’s Level 5 strength and use it against her. When Mikoto does fire the railgun, Laifu waits near a helicopter and bends the railgun shot back with gravity to injure Mikoto.
- However, Mikoto senses something odd and adjusts the railgun’s output, so she isn’t seriously injured. Laifu, fearing she’ll lose the advantage, pulls out a claw hidden in her sleeve to finish the fight up close.
- Laifu threatens Mikoto with illusions using light refraction, but Mikoto had prepared iron-sand bait, so Laifu loses her last chance to strike and is hit by a line of electricity at close range. The electrical damage knocks out her ability computation and shatters the claw; her efforts to keep struggling end in futility. Mikoto kicks Laifu’s head and sends a thin stream of electricity into her chest, knocking her unconscious.
- Before rendering Laifu unconscious, Mikoto tells Laifu—who had earlier called Mikoto a hero—that the real hero Mikoto knows is the kind of fool who won’t be satisfied unless they save not only troubled people but villains like Laifu too, and that Mikoto, already overwhelmed just taking care of the people around her, cannot be that kind of hero. Still, she says she’d be willing to listen to that guy if he behaves like that.
- Later, when Uiharu and Saten bait Kimi and disable an AIM jammer in part of the detention center, Mikoto seizes the chance to infiltrate and fires a point-blank railgun at Kimi, who happens to be there. Kimi pulls out the dragon to block, but the surprise strike isn’t perfectly defended and blood runs from her forehead. Kimi still pressures Mikoto with the dragon, but Mikoto, having understood the dragon’s mechanics to some extent, uses cover to approach and engages in hand-to-hand combat. Kimi believes a middle-school self-defense move can’t match her battlefield-trained experience and tries to knock Mikoto down with one punch, but is surprised when Mikoto counters strongly, even stepping on her foot.
- Kimi quips that it’s uncouth fighting so dirty for someone from Tokiwadai, and Mikoto replies that she’s seeing the real thing right in front of her and will shatter those illusions—then launches a broken security robot by her own power. Kimi duplicates Mikoto’s railgun and sends the robot back, and that robot punches a hole in the detention center wall. The escape-retardant liquid flowing from that hole blocks the exit Saten and Uiharu were trying to use.
- Mikoto attacks Kimi with a centipede-shaped security robot, but Kimi disassembles the machine to fire its parts as railgun projectiles. Mikoto counters with her own railgun and wins the exchange, striking the dragon.
- Mikoto then attacks the dragon with the remaining fragments, but even if it’s weaker than Touma’s Dragon Strike, it’s still a Dragon Strike, and the attacks have no effect. Floating in midair and about to be struck by the dragon, Mikoto uses her brains: she redirects the dragon’s attack into a wall, then fires the railgun to destroy the wall so Uiharu and Saten can escape.
- Mikoto then makes a large number of iron-sand decoys and tries to flee, but is attacked and knocked down by the dragon. She gets up quickly and confronts Kimi, accusing her—who hates people who protect others—of actually having trauma from being protected by someone. She presses that suspicion on Kimi.
- As they continue to fight, Uiharu interferes with Kimi’s field and the dragon’s saltification ability is sealed, leaving only its skeleton; Mikoto destroys the dragon skeleton with her railgun.
- Mikoto knocks Kimi down with punches and lectures her to get stronger. Kimi, seeing that Mikoto’s sermon contains sincere things and saying it sounds like something she would tell herself, prods Mikoto. Mikoto recalls Touma’s injuries during the Level 6 Shift experiment and admits that a heroine who must be protected is a hundred times more tormented than a hero, acknowledging that fact.

## 8.5. The Psychological Domination of a Certain Science:
- Mikoto is seized by the Shokuhou faction to clear up a misunderstanding that she supports Gaoin Tsukasa, a candidate in the Tokiwadai Middle School student council election. Mikoto had been caught in a trap that used the ultra-rare Gekota Junko Hokaze owned as bait, as if hunting some kind of beast. All the Shokuhou faction members, who had been incredulous that a Level 5 would fall for such a trap, were left speechless.
- At that time, she asks Shokuhou Misaki, “It’s unexpected that you’d run for the student council,” and, “You don’t really like this kind of noisy environment, do you?”
- It was true that Mikoto supported Gaoin, because Gaoin had found the lost Gekota strap Mikoto misplaced after she got caught up in fights between factions trying to recruit her. It was the strap she’d gotten from the couple-plan service with Touma. She valued it so much that when she realized she’d lost it she completely lost her composure, and when the Kamino faction forged a fake strap and pretended to have found it, Mikoto begged, “I’ll join any faction or whatever, just please give it back.” Of course the fake was spotted as soon as she saw it, so she didn’t actually join.
- After that, Mikoto suddenly sees hair that had discharged her ability, asks the maids to dry her hair, then is struck by a mysterious attack and ends up fighting Gokucho, the fourth-ranked member of Tokiwadai’s factions.
- She defeats Gokucho and, heading toward Shokuhou Misaki inside Tokiwadai Middle School, sees Kozaku Mitori, asks if she’s her friend, and offers advice. After taking Shokuhou to the hospital, she mocks that it was an expression worth seeing in a long time.
- Mikoto eavesdrops on a conversation between Gaoin and Hokaze and accompanies them to question Gokucho, confronting him, but Gokucho warns that it would be bad if Tokiwadai’s dorm supervisor discovered that she’d used her ability to wreck parts of the school. They apologize to each other while trading barbs and speculation, and then get trapped inside an omnipotent crystal.

## 8.6. A Certain Magical Index: Endymion's Miracle:
- Mikoto, while in a café listening to Meigo Arisa's music with Saten, is caught by Kuroko in a wheelchair; when Kuroko keeps pestering her, she ends up sending an electric current through her.
- Mikoto claims to be Meigo Arisa's manager, gets noticed by a female PD, and, wearing stage outfits together with Saten and Uiharu, performs decently. Later Mikoto is discovered by Kamijo Touma, but she is completely unaware that Kamijo is watching her.
- In the latter part, Mikoto smashes the robot that was blocking Anti-Skill's access with a railgun and storms the underground alone. Later, due to a radio message from the British Puritan side, Mikoto cooperates with Accelerator's group and Stiyl's group to carry out an unprecedented joint operation between the magic side and the science side.
- Afterwards, with the help of her sisters, Mikoto becomes the first among the three to destroy Endymion's pillar.

# 9. Miscellaneous:
- Mikoto has the nickname "Railgun (Railgun)" taken from her finishing move, which shoots coins by vibrating them.
- Kamijo Touma used to call Mikoto "Biribiri (Jjirit jjirit)." Now he calls her Misaka.
- Mikoto graduated from kindergarten and came to Academy City from elementary school onward.
- Misaka Mikoto's family includes her mother Misaka Misuzu and her father Misaka Tabigake.
- The vending machine in the park near the school is famous at Tokiwadai Middle School as the "money-eating vending machine." When Mikoto entered Tokiwadai Middle School she didn't know this and put in a 10,000-yen bill which got swallowed. Kamijo also didn't know and put in a rare 2,000-yen bill—nowadays hardly seen—which also got eaten. Because the fixing bolt is a bit loose, one of Mikoto's specialties since then is to shout the Tokiwadai-grandma method of restoring broken machines by a 45-degree strike, "Chaser!" and kick in a roundhouse to get a drink out. The downside is the drink comes out randomly. While pulling one out she sometimes says, "What kind of cutting-edge science city is this when something this shoddy exists?"
- She's burdened by the over-the-top attentions of her junior Shirai Kuroko. After the Level Upper incident, when her batteries literally ran out and she couldn't move, Kuroko took the opportunity to tend to her all over.
- Young Misaka has an ahoge (a silly tuft of hair), inherited from her mother. Even now, as she’s older, she occasionally unconsciously sticks it up. In the story where Mikoto learns about the Radio Noise Project, when she sneaks into the lab or controls guard robots with electromagnetic waves, she uses her ahoge like a conductor's baton. She also often raises it when she senses Kamijo.
- Thanks to Tokiwadai's school rules, she's always in her uniform when going out, so it's quite hard to see Mikoto in civilian clothes. Saten said, "Seeing Misaka-nee in civilian clothes is rare," and Uiharu said, "I want to see Misaka-nee in civilian clothes."
